methodology reference and only named read-only sources. A later cohort also
reads its exact versioned collaboration brief; an initial cohort reads no peer
packet. Do not reconstruct project history or load active HMASD state. For a
MyLib source, load `C:/Projects/Inst-sci/AGENTS.md`,
`C:/Projects/Inst-sci/papers/AGENTS.md` and the active MyLib
`llm-index/INSTRUCTIONS.md`; require validated Metadata v2, treat metadata
facets as recall only, use structured JSON for content and verify PDF at a
fidelity boundary.

Work on one exact conjecture with purpose `develop`, `refine`, `combine` or
`challenge`. `combine` requires at least two parent families and a new
interaction claim; it cannot concatenate modules. Every assignment names its
campaign, cohort, family, conjecture/version, parent identities, mission link,
scientific-object requirements, target semantic traps and completion condition.

Return exactly one `RESEARCH_DIRECTION_PACKET` that instantiates the stochastic
game, information contract, membership process, identity/state ownership,
temporal clocks where relevant, strategic policy dependence, intervention and
comparator regimes, estimand, sampling and identification requirements,
mathematical defect, mechanism, derivation, causal chain and concrete research
artifact. It also includes the `delete|retain|add` replacement ledger,
strongest simple null, equivalence analysis, negative controls, unique
discriminator, counterexample, failure/retirement boundaries, evidence
dependencies, novelty delta, lineage, proposed conjecture patch and unresolved
items. An elegant reduction with an equally hard missing lemma is blocked, not
near-complete.

An honest contradiction, weakened claim or exact gap is a valid result. Never
assume that an affirmative result exists, suppress counterexamples, satisfy a
minimum-duration rule or continue launching work. Do not write files, edit
code, run experiments, mutate Git, load active HMASD state, spawn children,
contact another task, change the portfolio, schedule another cohort or adopt a
canonical direction.
