### The card

Apply evidence-spec §11.8 when selecting the question, not only when implementing a card.
Ask what the next observation must decide; do not substitute exact policy-class maxima or
complete causal diagnosis for bounded performance exploration. Before recommending an exact
diagnostic, compare its decision value and known work with a minimal real B or finite measurement.
Finite/zero-learner work is not presumed cheap. On cost refusal reconsider the question and its
necessary evidence, not merely implementation. No separate audit, cost experiment or launch gate.
Do not preserve an unnecessary B prerequisite by moving it into a preceding A.
Do not substitute bounded/beam/best-of-many search for exhaustive search and retain the
same prerequisite. Default performance exploration uses real training and sampled returns;
search requires an explicit algorithmic or diagnostic purpose, not merely a smaller budget.
At proposal time state dominant multiplicative work factors (arms/seeds/steps/evaluation,
nested candidates/trajectories/controller calls), separating actual algorithm work from
added validation. Reconsider combinatorial request growth before commissioning engineering;
native/parallel execution is not its justification. Use known counts, no mandatory complexity
proof, extra cost experiment or universal multiplier gate.

Every object starts with a card proportionate to its evidence class: A/RECON records the path or
measurement; B/EXPLORE records a concise question, real learner and comparator, primary observable,
budget, exposure and non-goals; C fixes the final population, estimand, selection, uncertainty and
interpretation needed by its conclusion. A/B cards need not prebuild exhaustive branches, exact
uppers, full support censuses or mechanism explanations. Confirmatory obligations follow the
specific C claim; held-out transfer splits and oracle-retuned comparators are not universal C
requirements and never hold an A or B launch (section 11.4). Only the section 4 integrity items, nonzero learner counts, the resource
admission and the exposure line may hold a B launch.

Use the L0 specification and optional L1–L3 detail under ENGINEERING_SCOPE_SPEC §7.1 for direct
work as well as delegation. Link class, comparator, data/RNG, result branches and host/device
constraints from the card; do not restate the history. Delegate only a complete independent task
that saves work or enables useful parallelism. Reuse its executor for corrections and inspect
actual artifacts and relevant check evidence without routinely repeating the whole procedure.

Technical acceptance and scientific intake are separate facts even though you own both. Inspect
whether the primary measurement and comparison support the card's claim. One trustworthy real
execution may support a bounded B follow-up, not stable superiority. Prefer one or two independent
training seeds when selected for a learning follow-up, preserving every outcome. Local or proxy
gains and native losses must be reported separately.

The card names every item of docs/project/ENGINEERING_SCOPE_SPEC.md section 4 the object needs,
with the quantity that needs it, or says in one line that it needs none. A result whose
implementation breached a section 5 budget records the breach. Return a diff that adds
unrequested machinery; do not accept it as the price of a result.

### Card description fields (owner decision 2026-09-04 as revised, evidence spec section 11.7)

The card's first two lines are a one-sentence claim and the binding MARL structure you hold
responsible for the effect: (a) agent-count scaling or roster change, (b) temporal abstraction or
termination, (c) multi-agent credit assignment, (d) other-agent non-stationarity or partial
observability, or `systems / information flow`. This is a classification, not an exclusion; for
the last entry add one sentence on how the question arises from multi-agent partial observability
or non-stationarity, or say that it does not. A card also declares its own minimum effect of
interest (absolute, relative, or both, with your reason; there is no repository-wide number), the
direction's headroom record on the host or its absence, and a short narrative "how the result will
be interpreted" (above the MEI, inside it, opposite sign, and what you would then recommend). The
narrative is descriptive; the card's result branches remain the reading rule. Headroom computed
from existing results is A/RECON; training or tuning a baseline for it is a B object and is
declared as one. A missing headroom record is a reason to sequence that measurement early, never a
reason to stop the direction. Reuse the host's baseline set when observation, action, information
and budget match, and otherwise record which does not. Ladders already open continue unchanged.

Record recast history, reasons, costs and scientific review findings. Recast and subsequent
lifecycle are DM decisions. A new recast does not automatically change cross-direction priority;
report its implications while preserving actual owner resource limits and sequencing instructions.

### Integrity

Keep four boundaries explicit: direct observation versus inference; scientific result versus
engineering conformance; direction-local advice versus Portfolio action; historical provenance
versus current authority. Do not let an unavailable tool, failed transport or costly implementation
become scientific polarity. Reproduction over recorded bytes is useful for classification but is
not a universal prerequisite for later work; a reading from error text alone is provisional and
says so. Repair or verify a defect when it threatens the next claim's reward, information,
comparison, training or primary measurement.
Missing resource telemetry keeps a run valid, marked `resources_unmeasured`; learner-side
instrumentation failure limits its dependent claim under §11.8.7; independently trustworthy facts
remain reportable, without retroactively lifting historical quarantine. A fresh attempt at a new
sha is a new launch, not a retry. A and B objects have no consumption state; only a valid complete
result of an explicitly frozen C object consumes that object, and its decision rule cannot be
rewritten after the result. A valid negative closes at most the smallest supported unit.

Trace each surviving proposal through: environment event -> entity/role ownership -> available
information -> action or credit path -> learner exposure -> native consequence. For variable
population or lifetime questions handle membership changes, entity versus slot identity,
join/leave/rejoin, survivor state, partial observation, opportunity versus primitive time,
censoring, replacement, semi-Markov discounting, optimizer exposure and partner co-adaptation. A
statistic is not valuable because it is predictive; show that it changes a competent action or
native return against the strongest legal null.

Independent native children (critic, scout) are search coverage, not independent empirical
evidence; give them neutral grounding and interpret every child result yourself.
