# Open algorithm inspiration campaign

```text
campaign_unit=one_broad_research_direction
owner_modes=direction|portfolio
direction_owner_scope=one_named_direction_without_sibling_preload
portfolio_owner_scope=explicit_named_direction_set_only
owner_task_result=conclusion_first_canonical_capsule
evidence_level_runtime_orthogonal=true
resource_model=observed_resource_vector_and_conflict_set
resource_conflict_serialization=only_named_dependency_or_observed_vector_or_mutable_conflict
owner_task_exact_inputs=canonical_inputs_named_by_assignment
owner_task_write_paths=canonical_write_paths_named_by_assignment
owner_task_result_destination=canonical_result_destination_named_by_assignment
source_first=true
fixed_first_wave_count=forbidden
phase_order=corpus_lock|source_absorption|innovation|principles_review|adversarial_review|portfolio_update
portfolio_shape=multiple_retained_directions
```

## Research object

Each campaign phase runs in a same-level owner task. Direction mode remains
one named direction without sibling preload; portfolio mode integrates only an
explicitly named direction set. The task assignment is self-contained and
returns one conclusion-first canonical capsule for archival.

This campaign is for a fuzzy but bounded direction such as variable skill
period or variable agent population. It is not a contest among prewritten
ideas and not a closed proposition proof. The source corpus supplies results,
mechanism primitives, empirical patterns and failed assumptions that can be
absorbed, transformed and combined into HMASD-relevant candidates.

Keep four epistemic layers distinct:

1. `source_result`: what the paper claims or experimentally supports;
2. `transferable_primitive`: the information, temporal, optimization or policy
   principle abstracted from that result;
3. `adaptation_hypothesis`: why and how the primitive might transfer;
4. `algorithm_candidate`: a new advisory mechanism that still needs evidence.

Metadata and titles support recall only. Source-result claims require the
registered JSON/PDF evidence contract. An authoritative corpus is not an oracle.

## Source absorption

The Explorer versions one corpus manifest. Each source or coherent cluster has
one exact owner assignment and one terminal `SOURCE_RESULT_PACKET` or exact
operational failure. Logical assignment count equals the work required by the
manifest. Current native capacity affects scheduling only.

The Explorer merges terminal packets into a `SOURCE_ABSORPTION_BRIEF` with:

- established results and evidence fidelity;
- mechanism primitives and learning signals;
- information, action and temporal structures;
- non-transferable assumptions and failure boundaries;
- same-concept/different-terminology links;
- complementary and conflicting results;
- transfer candidates and cross-paper questions.

No innovation assignment begins before this barrier closes.

## Innovation and dynamic directions

The first innovation roster sees the same absorption brief but no peer packets
or favored answer. Later assignments may receive named parent packets and a
versioned collaboration brief. Valid purposes are `adapt`, `develop`,
`combine`, `refine`, `split` and `challenge_dependency`.

Maintain a directed portfolio graph. Nodes are source results, primitives,
candidates and subdirections. Edges are `derived_from`, `transfers_to`,
`combines_with`, `corrects`, `splits_into`, `supports`, `conflicts_with` or
`ready_for_validation`. Preserve parents when adding a combination or split.
Do not use a single-winner field.

## Constructive RL-principles analysis

Before adversarial criticism, analyze each retained candidate or connected set
through the relevant information-theoretic, statistical, game-theoretic,
stochastic-process, RL, MARL and exploration/exploitation lenses.

At minimum identify:

- nominal versus effective state, observation and action spaces;
- the directed exploration driver and exploitation driver;
- whether randomness reaches useful states or joint policies;
- information, gradient and temporal credit flow;
- primitive, decision, termination, renewal, membership, update and credit
  clocks when relevant;
- individual versus joint exploration and partner-policy dependence;
- the simplest explanation that could produce the same observation;
- constructive simplifications, combinations and validation requirements.

For VAE, mutual-information or bottleneck mechanisms, record the random
variables, variational objective, preserved and removed information, behavioral
role, leakage, posterior collapse and why the objective changes useful behavior
rather than reconstruction alone.

## Adversarial review after construction

Criticism follows principles analysis. It checks whether the empirical story is
actually distinguishable from passive noise, capacity, optimizer exposure,
ordinary recurrence, information reorganization or partner co-adaptation.
It proposes the smallest practical discriminator and actionable corrections.
It does not require a theorem, routinely demand a constructed counterexample or
retire unselected directions.

## Opportunity-driven continuation

After portfolio update, generate a `NEXT_CYCLE_OPPORTUNITY_MAP`. Each item uses
exactly one kind:

- `new_mechanism`;
- `transfer`;
- `combination`;
- `important_correction`;
- `subdirection_split`;
- `cross_direction_inspiration`.

Every item binds source or parent identities, a material delta, expected
portfolio effect, required role, exact planned assignment and completion
condition. A transfer includes source/target contexts and changed assumptions.
A combination includes at least two parents and a new interaction. A split
names a distinct assumption, learning driver or prediction. An important
correction records whether it is applied, rejected with reason or parked with a
reactivation condition.

## Convergence

Convergence requires complete corpus absorption, completed principles analysis
for every retained direction, completed adversarial review for every recommended
direction, closed actionable corrections and no material unprocessed item in
any of the six opportunity classes. The Explorer records the evidence for each
predicate; the mechanical gate validates only record completeness and identity.

If an assignment/resource boundary prevents required work, return
`PARTIAL_CAMPAIGN_RESOURCE_BOUND`. If the next step needs a new source boundary,
code, compute or formal adoption, return that exact external boundary. Neither
case is convergence.
