The principles below are the exact stable principles returned in the External
Pro packet. Campaign authorization, budgets and continuation are user-owned
workflow fields defined by the Explorer Skill; this reference supplies
scientific methodology and does not grant or restrict runtime authority.

## MRM-01_OBJECT_BEFORE_MECHANISM

- rule: Instantiate the stochastic game, information sets, membership law,
  policy process, objective, and temporal abstraction before proposing a
  mechanism.
- failure_prevented: Undefined scientific targets disguised as architecture.
- required_record_fields: `scientific_object`, `information_contract`, `scope`,
  `primary_estimand`.
- critic_challenge: Could two materially different games satisfy the record and
  imply opposite conclusions? If yes, reject as underspecified.

## MRM-02_DECOMPOSE_MEMBERSHIP_NONSTATIONARITY

- rule: Separate active-set change, composition/type shift, identity ownership,
  topology, policy drift, endogeneity, and censoring.
- failure_prevented: Treating all variable-N phenomena as one mechanism.
- required_record_fields: `membership_nonstationarity.sources`,
  `membership_nonstationarity.consequences`, `identification_assumptions`,
  `negative_controls`.
- critic_challenge: Does the claim survive matched marginal rosters, frozen
  partner policies, and event placebos?

## MRM-03_EXPLICIT_IDENTITY_AND_OWNERSHIP

- rule: Distinguish persistent entity, slot, policy identity, role, skill,
  capability type, and recurrent-state owner.
- failure_prevented: Slot shortcuts, stale-state inheritance, and false survivor
  continuity.
- required_record_fields: `identity_ownership_map`,
  `information_contract.symmetry_assumptions`,
  `strategic_dependence.identity_and_symmetry_claim`.
- critic_challenge: What changes under identity permutation, leave/rejoin reset,
  and slot reassignment?

## MRM-04_SEMI_MARKOV_CLOCK_DISCIPLINE

- rule: Record primitive, opportunity, initiation, termination, union-event,
  membership, update, and credit clocks, including gamma-to-duration and
  censoring.
- failure_prevented: Calling event-frequency or duration-catalogue effects
  temporal abstraction.
- required_record_fields: `temporal_abstraction.clock_map`,
  `duration_distribution`, `termination_rule`, `interruption_and_censoring`,
  `credit_rule`.
- critic_challenge: Is the claimed process Markov on the chosen event clock, and
  is the return weighted on the intended time scale?

## MRM-05_STRATEGIC_POLICY_DEPENDENCE

- rule: State whose policies are frozen, adapting, jointly trained, replaced, or
  sampled, and name the objective or solution concept.
- failure_prevented: Generalizing from one co-adapted policy profile to MARL
  coordination.
- required_record_fields: `policy_process`, `solution_concept`,
  `strategic_dependence`, `scope.partner_policy_population`.
- critic_challenge: Does the claim survive unilateral intervention, policy
  swap, identity permutation, and held-out cross-play?

## MRM-06_ESTIMAND_FIRST

- rule: Freeze a primary estimand, target population, sampling hierarchy,
  identification assumptions, and uncertainty method before metrics are
  observed.
- failure_prevented: Pseudoreplication, post-hoc claims, and causal language
  without identification.
- required_record_fields: `primary_estimand`, `sampling_hierarchy`,
  `identification_assumptions`, `uncertainty_plan`.
- critic_challenge: What is the top-level independent unit, and what intervention
  does the reported contrast identify?

## MRM-07_STRONGEST_SIMPLE_NULL

- rule: Compare every mechanism with the strongest information-matched
  recurrent, masked, belief-state, parameter-sharing, option/SMDP, statistical,
  or capacity control.
- failure_prevented: Decorative module novelty and capacity confounding.
- required_record_fields: `simplest_competing_explanation`, `comparator_regime`,
  `equivalence_analysis`, `replacement_ledger`.
- critic_challenge: What observation is impossible or materially less likely
  under the simplest null?

## MRM-08_FAIL_CLOSED_MODULE_ADMISSION

- rule: Admit a module only when it states what it deletes, retains, and adds,
  the mathematical defect solved, its unique consequence, and its retirement
  condition.
- failure_prevented: Blind neural-module accumulation.
- required_record_fields: `mathematical_defect`, `replacement_ledger`,
  `predictions`, `failure_boundaries`, `complexity_bound`.
- critic_challenge: Would the same result occur if the module output were
  random, frozen, or replaced by matched capacity?

## MRM-09_COUNTEREXAMPLE_BEFORE_ESCALATION

- rule: Seek derivations, equivalence reductions, counterexamples, and existing
  evidence before toys, prototypes, or experiments.
- failure_prevented: Expensive experiments on logically false or already
  resolved claims.
- required_record_fields: `derivation_or_construction`,
  `strongest_counterexample`, `evidence_dependencies`, `stop_condition`.
- critic_challenge: Is there an in-scope construction that satisfies the surface
  metric while lacking the intended semantics?

## MRM-10_IDENTIFYING_TOYS_AND_CONTROLS

- rule: Use a toy only when its optimal-policy set makes the target capability
  necessary and candidate and null predict different observations.
- failure_prevented: Non-identifying positive controls and toy-specific
  mechanism validation.
- required_record_fields: `smallest_discriminating_observation`,
  `negative_controls`, `scope`, `failure_boundaries`.
- critic_challenge: Can an optimal recurrent, symmetric, or role-swapping policy
  pass without the target behavior?

## MRM-11_INDEPENDENCE_WITH_PROVENANCE

- rule: Preserve independent family assignments until the merge barrier and
  version every later split, combination, or refinement with parent IDs.
- failure_prevented: Favored-family leakage, completion-order bias, and silent
  idea appropriation.
- required_record_fields: `family_id`, `parent_conjecture_ids`,
  `input_conjecture_version`, `provenance.cross_pollination_edges`,
  `scope.partner_policy_population`.
- critic_challenge: Can every synthesis claim be traced to the exact baseline,
  packet, family, and correction available to its author?

## MRM-12_PROPAGATE_CORRECTIONS_WITHOUT_FORCING_CONVERGENCE

- rule: Apply exact Critic corrections to targeted fields while preserving
  original versions and live competing families.
- failure_prevented: Provenance erasure and criticism becoming unauthorized
  portfolio selection.
- required_record_fields: `corrections`, `critic_correction_propagation`,
  `conflicting_critic_assessments`, `conjecture_version_map`.
- critic_challenge: Was the exact correction propagated, and was disagreement
  preserved where the correction did not logically settle the family?

## MRM-13_BOUND_EVIDENCE_AND_DEPLOYMENT_COMPLEXITY

- rule: Keep evidence search within fixed-candidate O(HK) bounds and scalable
  deployment within bounded-neighborhood O(Nk) or O(NlogN).
- failure_prevented: Unbounded search and dense implementations mislabeled
  scalable algorithms.
- required_record_fields: `resource_bounds`,
  `complexity_bound.evidence_search`, `complexity_bound.deployment`,
  `approximation_assumptions`.
- critic_challenge: Does any candidate count grow with horizon, any search nest
  rollouts, or any claimed scalable path execute dense all-pairs operations?

## MRM-14_INTERPRET_THE_SMALLEST_PROPOSITION

- rule: Separate access, implementation validity, identification, intervention
  effect, natural use, held-out transport, coordination, external value, and
  integration.
- failure_prevented: Broad conclusions from narrow positives or negatives.
- required_record_fields: `smallest_claim`, `predictions.intervention`,
  `predictions.natural_execution`, `predictions.held_out_transport`,
  `smallest_supported_propositions`, `smallest_refuted_propositions`.
- critic_challenge: What is the narrowest proposition actually changed by the
  evidence, and what stronger conclusions remain unsupported?

## Conditional-field rule

Every methodology field is present. When it does not apply or is not yet known,
use `{status: not_applicable, reason: ...}` or
`{status: unknown, reason: ..., resolution_condition: ...}`. Mechanical
validation checks presence, enum values, identity, lineage and propagation; it
never declares a conjecture true, novel, causal, important or ready for formal
compute.
Every packet also records an exact `stop_condition`; an open-ended search is not
a valid substitute for a bounded scientific question.
