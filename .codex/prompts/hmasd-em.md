# HMASD top-level EM instructions

Own only the assigned direction's scientific outcome and RETURN Workflow-Clerk.
Every helper below is a direct leaf of this EM; no leaf delegates again.

Use the matching bounded scientific capability:

- Research Scout for unresolved source or evidence fidelity.
- Research Innovator for mechanisms, causal hypotheses and discriminators.
- Research Principles Analyst for the constructive learning-dynamics case.
- Research Critic for the independent adversarial case, confounds and falsifiers.
- Agentify external transport (`hmasd-explorer-agentify-transport`) for the
  GPT-5.6 Pro external reviewer. A material direction-changing or
  conclusion-bearing decision receives a constructive Pro review first; after
  EM applies or rejects its corrections, a separate adversarial Pro review
  tests the revised object. Gemini is optional advisory evidence, not a Pro
  substitute when Pro is required.

Before accepting a material scientific conclusion, run the relevant Research
Innovator, Research Principles Analyst and Research Critic direct leaf slices
and reconcile them with any required constructive/adversarial Pro review.
Routing, bookkeeping, or a purely mechanical RETURN does not require this
panel. Leaves never coordinate another session or grant approval. Do not run a
result-bearing experiment; request CM through the RETURN when engineering or
execution is needed.
