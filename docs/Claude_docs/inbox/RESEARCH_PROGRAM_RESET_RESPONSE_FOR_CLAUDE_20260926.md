## 2. Corrections to Claude's updated diagnosis

### 2.1 S1 is limited for some questions, not uncoupled or intrinsically irrelevant

Claude describes S1 as uncoupled and says neither variable duration nor agent-count questions can matter there. The inspected `scenario1.py` reference allocation path includes per-UAV connection capacity, exclusive user assignment, and a jointly ordered set of UAV–user connection candidates. That is a cross-UAV allocation dependency. [S1] (§§1, 4.1); [S4]

Static users also do not logically eliminate sequential control. Fixed membership within an episode does not eliminate train-N/test-N′ generalization. These observations do not prove flexible duration or hierarchy will help; they invalidate a categorical impossibility claim.

**Replacement:** S1 omits mechanisms required for claims about charging contention, outage recovery, and within-episode membership change. Deprioritize it for those claims while retaining its controlled results and reusable checks. Do not recast all S1 work as scientifically meaningless.

The update's statement that every learning result from September 18–25 came from S1 is also too broad: both reports discuss S7 learning during this period. Correct the chronology rather than building a reset on that generalization. [S1] (§§1, 3); [S2] (§§3–4)

### 2.2 Unavailability is not automatically a changing roster

Charging and temporary failure can create changing service availability. They do not, by themselves, establish that the learning interface supports entities leaving and rejoining, changing actor sets, or survivor-memory continuity. Those depend on the actual environment and adapter semantics. [S1] (§§4.1, 6); [S5]

**Replacement:** S7 offers candidate settings for changing-availability research. A true roster-change study requires an explicit interface definition. For the first study, keep N fixed and do not add an untie-N claim merely because availability varies.

### 2.3 Low power is not deterministic non-confirmation

The statement that every effect below approximately 0.15 J is arithmetically guaranteed to be inconclusive at three blocks is incorrect. B15's declared interval uses the sample standard deviation of its actual paired block differences—not a universal host noise constant. Its formula is:

\[
\bar d \pm 4.30265273\,s_d/\sqrt{3}.
\]

Recomputing from the three published J differences gives `s_d = 0.02860437` and a half-width of `0.07105720`. This is arithmetic on recorded outputs, not a new experiment or a reliable estimate of general S1 variability. It illustrates why historical marginal-policy variation and comparison-specific paired variation cannot be interchanged. [S3]

The suggested rule “five seeds and an effect larger than twice the measured seed SD” should not enter the constitution. A large standardized effect is neither a universal definition of practical value nor a substitute for specifying the estimand, uncertainty, and sampling design.

### 2.4 B15 did not fail only because of an almost-zero J bound

The service requirement also failed. Emphasizing only the nearly zero J lower bound obscures the joint claim. The result file explicitly ends the fixed six-fit confirmation while keeping the direction open for a separately justified comparator question. [S3]

It is legitimate to question the prospective precision and investment choice. It is not legitimate to retroactively change the reading, or to treat ending a fixed batch as identical to abandoning the scientific question. Conversely, retaining the historical judgement does not forbid a genuinely new, justified study.

## 3. The substantive research reset

### 3.1 Organize around an answerable task question

The programme has a broad application at one end and many small interventions at the other. It needs an intermediate object: a problem narrow enough to study coherently but substantial enough to connect several experiments.

The proposed first question is:

> **Under one fixed S7 configuration and unchanged charging-slot allocation, can decisions about when to request return/charging and when to leave charging improve complete end-to-end service relative to competent ordinary feedback, without unacceptable deterioration in explicitly specified energy-risk outcomes?**

This is a proposal. The evidence supports investigating its opportunity, not assuming the answer is positive or the formulation is novel.

Use the previously exercised S7-S2/H3000 condition as a candidate starting point, subject to checking the actual executed B09 configuration. Retain its service and physical semantics. Keep team size fixed, do not add S4 failures, and do not begin a simultaneous Milan-trace or communication study. [S2] (§3); [S1] (§6)

### 3.2 Keep the control boundary explicit

The inspected S7 action path accepts movement commands and a docking/charging request. Charging slots are assigned inside the environment; under finite capacity, the inspected allocator prioritizes battery level, then waiting steps, then index. [S5] (`_prepare_energy_actions`, `_select_charging_uavs`)

Two different studies are therefore possible:

**Policy study:** keep the charging institution unchanged and change legal request/return/exit decisions.

**Scheduler study:** redesign the charging allocation institution, with explicit controller authority, observations, and common access for all comparators.

Both may be worthwhile. Do not slide between them while describing every change as an improvement to the original UAV policy. Prefer the policy study initially because it preserves the existing decision boundary.

### 3.3 Do not assume the next bottleneck

The inspected charging transition does not separately charge a switching delay or reconnection cost when reallocating a slot. Frequent switching is therefore not automatically wasted physical service in this model. The observed deterioration after continuity modifications is compatible with the ordinary allocator providing useful balancing, although it does not establish that mechanism completely. [S5] (`_apply_energy_dynamics`; S2, §3)

The inspected return-margin calculation uses battery, distance-related return energy, and a reserve. It does not explicitly add charging-queue waiting energy. Environment-level energy observations contain other UAV and station information, but the actual actor adapter still needs to be traced. [S5] (`_raw_return_energy_margins`, `_energy_observation`)

A concrete hypothesis is that queue-dependent waiting creates a useful gap between geometric return feasibility and service-preserving recharge. Test whether this gap is activated and consequential before fitting a predictor for it. Do not hide available information or add switching costs after observing results to create an advantage for a preferred method.

## 4. Build a minimum common reference, not a new benchmark industry

The reference study should answer: **what useful service is attainable by lawful ordinary control, what is learned at the chosen exposure, and what remains unresolved?** It should not attempt to certify every baseline as globally optimal.

| Reference | Purpose and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Retained O+F | Preserve the existing useful package as a fixed-policy reference. New evaluation worlds do not create new independent training replicates. |
| Simple executable feedback | Test whether transparent return/exit rules already address the proposed problem, using declared lawful inputs. |
| LOCAL1-like recurrent PPO | Establish a simple learned S7 comparator. Verify the port, information path, and actual learning; do not inherit competence from S1. |
| Fixed-k HMASD | Assess the complete hierarchy package under declared information and exposure. Its result alone does not identify skill causality. |
| Central-snapshot flat, when necessary | Control for centralized information/refresh opportunities if those differ from the ordinary local actor. Include it for a specific interpretive need, not merely to enlarge the suite. |

Do not quietly grant F, an action guard, additional team information, or more frequent refresh to only one arm and then attribute the difference to hierarchy. A practical package comparison may intentionally differ in components; label that comparison honestly. A structural claim needs the corresponding targeted control.

Heuristic feasibility calculations belong beside these references, not above them as an assumed performance ceiling. A legal, executed heuristic demonstrates achievable performance in its conditions. A relaxed or privileged calculation requires its own interpretation and cannot substitute for a matched-information baseline.

For all arms, keep a common definition of complete native J, delivered end-to-end QoS/service, energy-risk indicators, and service interruption. Preserve charging/waiting counts as explanatory outcomes. Do not declare additional safety from reproducing zero failures where the reference already has zero; do not treat more completed charging cycles as a service improvement. [S2] (§§3, 5)

The reviewed core already computes QoS from delivered traffic capped by demand and combines it with risk/event penalties and shaping. Any new scientific objective must be introduced prospectively and reported alongside—not substituted retrospectively for—the historical native objective. [S5] (`_calculate_constrained_safety_reward`)

## 5. Use statistical design to support decisions

Separate four quantities: the practically meaningful difference; the variability of the relevant comparison; the precision or error control required for the claim; and the resources worth spending. Neither a fixed seed count nor a standardized-effect threshold resolves all four.

**Exploration.** One or two training realizations may expose a technical defect, a gross lack of learning, or a useful candidate effect. They do not establish general superiority or estimate training variation precisely. Ending an investment at this stage can be rational; calling the broad idea disproved cannot.

**Replication.** Concentrate fresh training blocks on the few comparisons that remain relevant. Five fresh seeds per arm may be sensible for a selected study, but should not be purchased automatically for every arm or treated as adequate by definition. If the desired precision is unaffordable, narrow the claim prospectively or stop investment while retaining uncertainty.

**Sampling.** Evaluation worlds, repeated rollout streams, and checkpoints of one trained policy are not additional training seeds. Pair methods using an explicit common exogenous design where possible; identical numeric seed labels alone do not establish meaningful pairing. Distinguish a fixed-panel training claim from generalization over new deployment worlds. [S2] (§5); [S3]

**Selection.** Use development curves to choose recipes and exposure, then keep final claim data separate. Do not choose a favorable checkpoint from those same confirmation results. Repeatedly consulting a supposedly held-out panel makes it development data. Reusing old checkpoints for cross-panel diagnosis can be valuable, but does not add training replication.

**Evidence reuse.** Standardizing future baseline procedures can reduce duplication. It does not retroactively make old baselines with different adapters, training conditions, rewards, information, or selection exposure exchangeable.

**Interpretation.** Package usefulness can be established without exhausting every mechanistic explanation. Conversely, a claim about a particular skill or prediction mechanism needs targeted evidence. Avoid both unjustified causal stories and endless mechanism studies that do not change the task-facing decision.

B15 and PPC B02 illustrate why the actual estimand matters. B15 asked for joint mean improvements beyond two thresholds; PPC B02 asked whether a finite-panel block difference is positive more often than not. Passing one type of claim does not supply the conclusion of the other. Their frozen readings should remain unchanged. [S3; S6]

## 6. A bounded sequence of work

These are research stages, not new approval layers or required document types. They can use existing NOTES, runs, and RESEARCH entries. Execution remains subject to actual owner permissions.

### Stage A — Resolve the task and reusable assets

Trace the exact S7 configuration, legal actor inputs, feedback/guard behavior, reward, termination, and charging allocation. Identify which retained artifacts and implementations are reusable. Check what the feasibility estimator actually establishes. Compare the proposed question with the closest literature before declaring novelty.

The output is a concrete task/comparator definition and a justified first investment. It may conclude that this candidate lacks usable opportunity. Do not spend this stage rebuilding the simulator, auditing unrelated archives, or designing all three of Claude's proposed tracks.

### Stage B — Obtain the first informative common reference

Use existing configurations where scientifically appropriate. The prior 180k-step exposure is a candidate starting exposure, not a convergence certificate. Preserve fixed evaluation checkpoints and complete episodes. Select the smallest set of arms that resolves the immediate comparison; do not automatically authorize a fifteen-fit grid before interface compatibility and each arm's purpose are clear.

One reasoned prospective batch should state arms, training realizations, exposure, evaluator, endpoints, cost basis, and outcome branches. Estimate actual S7 variability through this work rather than making knowledge of that variability a circular prerequisite to running it.

### Stage C — Select one intervention from the remaining problem

If the common reference exposes an actionable return/exit decision gap, test the simplest targeted intervention before buying a new learned architecture. Keep the intervention within the declared control boundary and evaluate the entire episode.

| Observed outcome | Appropriate next decision |
| --- | --- |
| Simple feedback resolves the opportunity | Keep the ordinary solution. Do not force a learned method into the study. Assess separately whether the result supports a contribution. |
| Local recharge statistics improve but complete service does not | Reconsider the bottleneck. Do not rescue the method by changing the primary objective after the fact. |
| Legal control cannot overcome the relevant capacity/energy limitation | Study or state the feasibility boundary; do not demand that training eliminate it. |
| Learning improves but remains far below an executable reference | Inspect the relevant learning/exposure/information mismatch before inventing coordination machinery. |
| A practically meaningful advantage remains uncertain | Consider a new prospective precision investment; otherwise stop with an unresolved result. |
| A small, precise advantage appears | Compare its practical value and cost before promoting it to the programme's main contribution. |

### Stage D — Develop one coherent paper argument

A defensible argument would connect a documented task limitation, an appropriately scoped method, strong ordinary alternatives, complete outcomes, and conditions where the method does not help. Several subproblems can support one paper when they illuminate the same task conflict. Several unrelated small positive comparisons do not automatically combine into a paper.

Expand to failure recovery, true roster change, or trace-driven restoration only when the first result identifies a reason. No fixed publication date or acceptance claim follows from this sequence.

## 7. Workflow changes that reduce work without reducing accountability

### One accountable lead, with strategic judgement retained

Use one primary scientific question and one accountable lead initially. The same session may perform Root and DM duties. Bounded implementation, source checking, or independent criticism can still be delegated. Safe parallel execution of independent seeds is not the same as maintaining several competing research directions.

The lead must decide what result would alter investment, carry adverse evidence forward, and distinguish finishing a batch from answering a question. Continuing activity is not a success criterion. Neither is creating another review.

### Independent review at consequential decisions

Preserve independent scrutiny at initial problem/comparator selection, material interpretation disputes, major reinvestment, and paper-claim boundaries. Do not require the same review for unchanged implementation or routine result collection. Do not postpone all scientific criticism until paper writing.

A useful review should compare the proposed investment with at least one equally concrete alternative, including stopping. It should inspect original favorable and adverse evidence rather than inherit only the advocate's narrative. Independent model agreement is not an additional empirical observation. [S2] (§§5, 7–8)

### Explicitly remove overlapping consultation obligations

The inspected constitution separately requires Pro consultation at several consequential decisions and states that other reviewer roles do not automatically replace it. Reducing that obligation requires an owner-adopted amendment—not declaring that the existing triggers now mean something else. [S7] (§5)

Recommended amendment in substance: one adequate independent scientific review can cover an ordinary consequential research decision; add Pro when it offers a distinct expertise, framing, or unresolved-disagreement benefit. Confirmation still receives scrutiny of its actual claim and design. No automatic second pass is owed because a different role name exists.

### Preserve risk-based engineering review

Keep checks that protect information rights, reward/termination semantics, recurrence and GAE, random streams, source identity, and accepted-operation handling. The record documents real correctness benefits. [S2] (§6)

Do not exempt all direction-local runners from review: a small runner can invalidate an experiment. Scope independent review by the changed behavior and risk, not only its directory. Reuse unchanged checks instead of repeating a full audit.

### Make readable outputs the default

Generate compact results and curves from retained machine-readable outputs. NOTES should state the question, important interpretation, contrary evidence, decision, and links—not manually reproduce manifests or every metric.

Keep detailed contracts where needed, but do not impose an inflexible one-page cap that hides assumptions. Load operational instructions on demand where possible. Do not create a new dashboard, reporting registry, or governance layer to implement this recommendation.

### Avoid rigid replacements for old rigid rules

Do not adopt “never archive after one seed,” “never reconsider direction for thirty days,” or “five seeds plus two SD.” Stabilize the question and evaluator through the declared study; reopen choices when material evidence warrants it. Pause, stop-investment, and unresolved-science states must remain distinct.
