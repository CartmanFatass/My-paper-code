## 2. Why this question, with the contrary evidence retained

Two complete exact-recipe instances give final T−G DOWN/WITHIN and no above-MEI
point gain; T−F is adverse beyond0.01 on all six panels. This is the strongest
reason to require a competent fixed-short F comparison for any changed learned
law. It is not evidence of a uniquely diagnosed credit error.8703 T approaches
G, improves along its curve, and beats hover by0.062103492173288755 at the final
endpoint. F/G also changes sign across instances. Preserve all these facts,
unexplained incomplete8702 and A01's non-independent prefix measurement.

The proposed law changes a real competent action: at each own renewal it can
alter whether the sampled velocity persists into a second primitive tick.
The first sent velocity/reward is common to both labels, so a conditional suffix
target avoids charging that immediate action-insensitive reward to the binary
choice. Separate PPO ratios also prevent that duration factor from sharing a
single clipping product with its sampled velocity. A small learned correction
to V_old conditions the duration baseline on recorded private history/velocity.

This is an untested package. Removing one reward may be weak in a256-step task;
the learned correction may increase error, and added parameters/gradient clipping
competition may offset any benefit. No new observation proves lower variance,
better credit or better return. The proposed native endpoint against F is what
would test usefulness, without a preceding diagnosis, policy search or pilot.

We considered allowing termination to inspect the next observation after the
first step. That changes the decision time and commitment law, as well as credit,
and adds an unnecessary ambiguity to this immediate design. It is not selected.
The proposed commitment remains at own expiry with exactly the old legal input.
The card specifies when label effects begin, current versus future information,
separate likelihoods, detached old predictions, zero-credit horizon cases,
primitive denominators, recurrent ownership and complete accounting.
