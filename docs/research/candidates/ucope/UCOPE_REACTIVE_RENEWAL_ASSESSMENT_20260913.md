## One concrete alternative to PARK

Call the unimplemented candidate **R, feedback-conditioned one-step continuation**. Retain the current five UAV identities, 256 primitive ticks, original native reward and legal private recurrent history. At an initial or forced renewal, sample a velocity and execute it for one tick. Before the next primitive tick, update the owner's private history from the already allowed fresh observation, then choose between keeping its actual previous velocity for one additional tick or ending and sampling a fresh velocity for the current tick. After a keep, the following tick must renew. Thus each command persists for at most two ticks. Other UAVs keep their own boundaries; no joint barrier is introduced. All private memories update every primitive tick.

This changes **when the keep/end decision receives feedback**. Old T and L choose duration before the first movement, conditional on its sampled command. R makes a keep/end choice after that movement and its free observation. R is therefore a new, outcome-informed policy/control package, not the stopped L recipe with another name. It adds no sensing fee, privileged observation, reward transformation, roster change, new host or L suffix residual.

A direct primitive-step stochastic-policy formulation is the starting learning proposal. On an eligible row the joint log probability is the keep/end log probability plus the fresh-velocity log probability only when ending. Forced-fresh rows have velocity probability only. A keep/end choice can change the **current** sent velocity and reward, so the old L argument for omitting the first reward must not be reused. Use full remaining native return and the existing raw critic/PPO approach, with masks representing actual stochastic choices.
