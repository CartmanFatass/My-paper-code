## 1. Question

The chain to date has separated three things on the linear `FT-XF-BC` tail objective:

| Established by | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Instrumentation check (2026-09-02) | the frozen 5-term basis represents the true tail value **exactly**, at `beta* = (0.31, 0.60, 1.35, -1.08, -0.891)`, and a policy carrying it is recorded as competent |
| Training-target diagnostic (2026-09-03), branch `D1` | at `n = 10,240` tail rows per policy the *empirical* optimum sits `0.060`–`0.223` from `beta*`; pooled over 61,440 rows it is `0.021781`, within `1.22` standard errors — so the objective is right and the per-policy **variance** is the problem |
| Optimiser-and-conditioning object (2026-09-03), branch `O-B` | whitening removes `82.2 %` of the median learner gap at the base budget and all six policies close below `0.10` at the ten-fold budget; the raw arm at ten-fold closes for **none** — so **conditioning** is the dominant cause and the budget a secondary one |

Conditioning and variance now each have a remedy. **With both handled — the whitened learner, at the
ten-fold budget, at an `n` chosen so the exact solve itself recovers the oracle root vector — does
the learner reach competence on this host?**

## 3. `n` selection — computed 2026-09-03, before any learner exists

**Outcome-free.** Only the exact normal-equation solve was used: no learner, no optimizer, no
competence claim about any learner. Runner:
`scripts/run_ucope_competence_whitened_n_selection.py`; record (gitignored):

Resource admission before the calculation: `13,033,611,264` bytes physical and effective, passed.
Wall `133.7 s`.

**Index law.** Rows are generated by the frozen host at episode indices `i = OFFSET + j` with
`OFFSET = 1_000_000` and `j = 0 .. m-1`. Every published UCOPE run draws from `0..5_119` (B1, both
exposure ladders, both R02 rungs) or `0..319` (ASSESS), so the ranges are disjoint; and because every
host draw is counter-addressed on `(namespace, seed, episode_index, counter)`, disjoint indices give
disjoint draws with **no overlap with any published run's rows**. `OFFSET` is a multiple of 20, so
the frozen 10-episode behaviour stratum (`host.behavior_stratum`, `index % 10`) and the 20-episode
fold alternation (`host.group_fold`, `(index // 10) % 2`) start in exactly the phase they have at
index 0. The runner asserts fold balance, behaviour-stratum balance and `K_train` period balance on
the fresh range directly, because `host.validate_population` is written for the index range
`0..episodes_per_context-1`.

`m` episodes per context give `2m` tail rows per policy (a policy's tail rows are the complementary
fold's PROBE rows) and `4m` root rows. Both conditions are evaluated on all six policies:

| tail rows per policy | episodes per context | max `d_objective` | all six `d_objective <= 0.10` | oracle root vector recovered | both hold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10,240 | 5,120 | 0.203088 | no | 5 of 6 | no |
| 20,480 | 10,240 | 0.155826 | no | 4 of 6 | no |
| 40,960 | 20,480 | 0.145172 | no | **6 of 6** | no |
| **81,920** | **40,960** | **0.046433** | **yes** | **6 of 6** | **yes** |
| 163,840 | 81,920 | 0.070938 | yes | 6 of 6 | yes |

Per-policy `d_objective` at the chosen point: `0.011638, 0.046433, 0.030209, 0.023380, 0.037303,
0.013271`. The Gram is unchanged in shape — `kappa = 726.199`, `lambda_min = 2.620428e-03` at the
chosen point — confirming that `n` buys variance, not conditioning; the two remedies are independent,
which is why both are applied.

**Fixed here, before any learner runs: `n = 81,920` tail rows per policy, `m = 40,960` episodes per
context**, the smallest grid point at which both conditions hold for all six policies. Two honest
notes: the criterion is monotone in expectation but not in realisation — the 163,840 point has a
*larger* `max d_objective` (`0.070938`) than the chosen one, which is sampling noise — and the rule
takes the smallest passing point as specified rather than the smallest observed value. And the two
conditions are **necessary, not sufficient** for competence: they do not include `C_even`'s regret
and tail-agreement gates. Branch `C-D` exists precisely for the case where they turn out not to
suffice.

## 4. Arms

All at `n = 81,920` tail rows per policy, the three B1 seeds `ucope-scout-r01-b1-fresh-{00,01,02}`,
both group-disjoint folds, the frozen `FT-XF-BC` initialisation, batch 256, `lr 3e-3`, and the
ten-fold budget: **1,600 tail updates and 3,200 root updates** (ten times the frozen rung-1
`160`/`320`).

1. **`WHITENED-10X`** — the whitened linear learner. Both stages are treated identically within the
   arm: the tail design and the root design are each whitened from **their own training rows only**,
   at float64, under the same contract the conditioning object used — `max |L L^T - G| <= 1e-10` and
   `lambda_min(G) > 1e-6`, checked before any optimizer step exists. Parameters are recovered to raw
   coordinates by `beta = L^-T beta_tilde` before every reported statistic. *Specification decision,
   recorded because owner decision D.14 named the arms by their tail treatment: whitening is applied
   to both stages, since "the whitened linear learner" is the learner, not one of its two heads.*
2. **`RAW-10X`** — the control: identical in every other respect, no whitening. This is the published
   path at the ten-fold budget and the larger `n`.
3. **`EXACT-SOLVE`** — the ceiling: tail solved exactly, its coefficients fed through the frozen root
   target package (`probe_primitive + max over K_TRAIN of Q_tail`), root solved exactly. Outcome-free
   reference; `d_learned = 0` by construction, no optimizer trajectory, excluded from the exposure
   line.

## 5. Differentiating measurement

- The whitening check numbers per policy and per stage: `kappa`, `lambda_min(G)`,
  `max |L L^T - G|`.
- Gradient ratios `g_learned / g_star` in raw coordinates for both training arms — descriptive,
  deciding nothing, for the reason section 6 of the conditioning result gives.
- One machine-generated **exposure line** over arms 1 and 2: per-coordinate displacement of the
  recovered vectors from the frozen initialisation of the same seed and fold.

## 6. Mechanisms

**(i) Conditioning and variance were the whole story.** With the coordinate system fixed and `n`
large enough that the exact solve itself recovers the oracle root vector, the whitened learner lands
within `eps_L` of an optimum that is itself competent, and therefore **reaches competence** on this
host. Prediction under (i): `WHITENED-10X` is competent in all six policies, `RAW-10X` in
substantially fewer, and `EXACT-SOLVE` in all six.

**(ii) Something the chain has not measured still binds.** Three candidates, none of which any object
in this chain has varied: the **root learner's own optimisation** (every result so far measured the
tail head; the root was only ever solved exactly or trained without being examined), the **gradient
clip** at norm `1.0`, and the **batch order** — contiguous cyclic windows of a list sorted by
`(episode_index, context_id)`, never shuffled, with the behaviour period a deterministic function of
the episode index. Prediction under (ii): `EXACT-SOLVE` is competent in all six and `WHITENED-10X` is
not, which localises the remaining obstruction to the learner rather than to the objective, the
geometry or the sample size.

## 7. Reading rule — written before data, branches ordered by effect size

Thresholds, all fixed here. `C_even` is the frozen predicate and introduces no new threshold.

Branches, evaluated in this order; exactly one applies.

- **`C-A — WHITENED_LEARNER_COMPETENT`.** `WHITENED-10X` satisfies `C_even` in **all six** policies.
  Reading: mechanism (i). On this host, conditioning plus sample size were the whole obstruction, and
  the first competent policies of this direction exist.
- **`C-B — WHITENED_MAJORITY_CEILING_CLEAN`.** Not `C-A`, but `WHITENED-10X` is competent in **at
  least four** of six **and** `EXACT-SOLVE` is competent in all six. Reading: mechanism (i)
  substantially, with a residual per-policy obstruction that the ceiling does not share. The gap
  between the arm and the ceiling is the next object's subject.
- **`C-C — CEILING_COMPETENT_LEARNER_NOT`.** `EXACT-SOLVE` competent in all six, `WHITENED-10X`
  competent in fewer than four. Reading: mechanism (ii). The objective, the geometry and the sample

  selection, not a result about any learner.
- **`C-E — UNCLEAR`.** Any other combination. Reading: report every number, name what is
  unexplained, propose no successor on the strength of a `C-E`.
