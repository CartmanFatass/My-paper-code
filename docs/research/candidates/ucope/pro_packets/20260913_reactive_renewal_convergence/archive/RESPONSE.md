## Why I favor this observation over PARK

The relevant research choice is whether to develop **feedback-conditioned persistence**, not whether another label can be attached to a duration head. In the proposed R, an observation obtained after the first movement can change the command sent on the next tick. The tested T/L rules could update memory after that movement, but their decision to execute a second held tick had already been made. R can therefore avoid an unwanted second tick or retain a useful one using information already available at that time. That is a concrete control difference, not new sensing.[^assessment]

The plausible route is: **a command has just executed once → fresh legal private observation and continuing memory → KEEP the actual command or END and sample a new one → current joint motion and service → subsequent observations/actions → primitive-return learning and the final native endpoint**. Moving teammates make persistence versus renewed action consequential, but they do not guarantee that the learned branch identifies the better choice. R may overreact, learn the branch poorly, or lose the exploration and optimization advantages of simpler persistence. G can already adapt every tick and may perform all useful feedback control without this additional branch.

A single fresh R/F/G/H instance can change a practical next recommendation. A useful R−F margin would support the new package relative to the attained holding alternative; R−G would reveal whether that local advantage also survives ordinary feedback. ## Material design findings and proportionate corrections

### 1. Specify the per-owner control phase, not just a generic renewal mask

The proposal's timing is coherent, but a later learner definition must distinguish a forced fresh command from a feedback-eligible continuation decision. My formalization of the proposed semantics is:

| Current per-owner situation | Actual stochastic choices and sent command | Next primitive tick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Episode start, or the tick after a KEEP | Sample a fresh velocity only; no KEEP/END draw | Eligible to choose KEEP or END after receiving the new observation |
| Feedback-eligible, choose KEEP | Draw the branch; copy the recorded previous command; no new velocity draw | Forced fresh command |
| Feedback-eligible, choose END | Draw the branch, then sample and execute a fresh velocity on this same tick | Eligible again after that new command has executed once |

In particular, **END must start a new command's first tick**. It should not force an additional fresh draw on the next tick merely because it was called a termination. Conversely, allowing another KEEP immediately after KEEP would permit commitments longer than the proposed two-tick maximum. These distinctions follow from the stated control law; they are not observed implementation failures.[^assessment]

The owner updates its private recurrent state from the current observation before the decision in every row. The previous command and the owner's own control phase are known internal state, not a new sensor. The later description should state exactly how they enter policy inputs and masks instead of silently reinterpreting F's remaining-hold feature. A partner's decision cannot reset that state, trigger a team barrier or provide the partner's new action to the actor. The critic remains predecision and may use only its declared training information, not the just-selected branch or future reward.

KEEP means the command actually sent previously, in the established command representation. It does not mean a newly recomputed policy mean, a resampled velocity, or physical displacement inferred after environment clipping. The branch is selected before sampling a replacement velocity; conditioning KEEP/END on an unexecuted newly sampled candidate would be a different policy. Forced renewal means a new selection event, not a requirement that its numerical command differ from the previous one.

These are small but decision-critical clarifications. They preserve the proposed new response opportunity without inventing a new observation channel or importing a general asynchronous-control framework. The listed foundations explain why memory updates, command persistence, boundary decisions and optimizer steps must be kept separate; none requires resetting recurrence at an option boundary.[^foundations][^hierarchy]

### 2. KEEP is a real policy choice even though no fresh velocity is drawn

The proposed branch-plus-conditional-velocity log probability is an appropriate starting formulation. Let x denote the current legal conditioning history, stored previous command and control phase; let q denote the KEEP/END distribution and p the fresh-velocity distribution. For a recorded action on the corresponding branch, my recommended explicit likelihood convention is

\[
\ell_\theta =
\begin{cases}
\log q_\theta(\mathrm{KEEP}\mid x), & \text{eligible KEEP},\\
\log q_\theta(\mathrm{END}\mid x)+\log p_\theta(v\mid x,\mathrm{END}), & \text{eligible END},\\
\log p_\theta(v\mid x), & \text{forced fresh}.
\end{cases}
\]

This is a likelihood for the **recorded branch and, when present, the fresh velocity**, not a Gaussian density fitted to every sent command. The copied command has no new velocity-density term on KEEP. A forced-fresh row has no artificial branch probability. The transformed-velocity density and its existing change-of-variables treatment belong only to genuine fresh draws. The branch label must be retained rather than reconstructed by testing whether successive command values happen to be equal.[^assessment][^comparator]

The important masking risk is that the existing fresh-velocity mask cannot also define all R policy-credit rows. **An eligible KEEP has branch-policy credit despite having no velocity sample.** Reusing a mask that zeros the entire surrogate when no velocity was drawn would remove the very learning signal for KEEP. That would materially change the proposed method. It is a prospective risk to check, not a bug found in supplied R code.

For the minimal comparison, I recommend retaining the existing per-owner compound-PPO approach: form the new/old ratio from the complete applicable row log probability, apply that owner's clipped surrogate, sum owner contributions within each primitive row, and retain the all-primitive-row denominator. Do not silently use L's separate velocity/duration clips, divide by the number of fresh draws, or count a carried action's original velocity likelihood again on KEEP. Those would introduce additional learning changes beyond the direct primitive-policy formulation being proposed.

Old branch labels, sampled velocities, behavior log probabilities and control phases remain recorded data during the existing recurrent replay. New probabilities are recomputed for those recorded choices and histories, not for newly sampled replacement actions. The stored previous command is data, not a pathwise link through a recomputed earlier velocity. Recurrent gradients through current replayed features may still follow the declared recurrent-PPO approximation; detaching a stored command must not be confused with freezing private memory or the entire actor.

**Claim limit:** the native action law now includes an explicit history-conditioned copy branch together with a fresh continuous-action branch. Sharing the same legal velocity range with G is not the same as sharing the same parameterized action distribution. A favorable result could reflect this action representation, changed exploration, branch-credit exposure, capacity or co-adaptation. It would not identify the isolated value of fresh feedback or prove that ordinary feedback cannot express useful repetition. No extra attribution arm is required for the proposed whole-package B; the attribution claim must simply remain unavailable.[^assessment][^empirical]

### 3. Include the current reward, including on an eligible final tick

The assessment correctly rejects L's omitted-first-reward argument for R. This distinction needs to be carried through the final-tick masks, not just the main prose. With primitive reward r_t, the relevant remaining native return is

\[
G_t=\sum_{\tau=t}^{255}r_\tau,
\]

not G_(t+1). R chooses KEEP versus a fresh command **before the current tick's environment step**, so that choice can change r_t. At an eligible t=255, it can still change the last sent velocity and r_255. Thus **eligible final-tick KEEP/END decisions retain policy credit**. Copying L's rule that retains the last duration draw but excludes its credit would be wrong for this different decision timing. A forced-fresh final tick still has only velocity credit.[^assessment][^prior]

There is no step at t=256, terminal carryover, extra reward, or bootstrap beyond the fixed horizon. Rewards and observations from all executed primitive ticks remain in the training trajectory. The action's current and later consequences are not converted to a reward rate, divided by duration, or discounted once per variable renewal event. The raw critic and existing advantage treatment should be specified in those same native units.

For the minimal R comparison, retaining the F/G recipe's zero explicit entropy coefficient avoids adding an unmotivated entropy intervention. That is a recommendation, not a statement that R's entropy treatment was already fixed. Likewise, its gate inputs, architecture, initialization, trainable parameter count, recurrent-gradient convention and private random-stream domains still need explicit description. The assessment acknowledges those open items. Their absence does not refute the concept or demand a preliminary scientific experiment, but it prevents claiming a complete accepted R implementation or an exact compute match today.[^assessment][^comparator]

These three findings can be addressed in one concise method definition and proportionate changed-behavior/primary-output checks if the DM proceeds. They do not require a full support census, another theoretical paper, a cross-platform replay, a new reviewer approval tier, or repeated verification of unchanged baseline code. No such check is performed or allocated by this consultation.[^spec]
