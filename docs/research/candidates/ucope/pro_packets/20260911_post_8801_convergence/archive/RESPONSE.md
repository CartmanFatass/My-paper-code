## The stopped package and its actual control and credit boundaries

The binding MARL structure is temporal abstraction/termination in a fixed, partially observed, co-adapting team. Five UAVs retain their identities and private recurrent histories throughout each 256-tick episode. Their 108-component actor inputs contain the existing local observation, own last command and remaining-hold feature. The predecision global critic remains separate. Private recurrence updates every primitive tick, including held ticks; neither an owning-agent expiry nor a partner's renewal resets that history. Parameters are updated on the rollout/optimizer clock, not whenever a hold ends.[^design][^hierarchy]

At an owner's expiry, velocity is sampled first. The learned duration law then chooses END_AFTER_ONE or CONTINUE_ONE_MORE using the current private recurrent feature and that sampled command, before the first environment step. Both labels initially send the same velocity; one allows feedback next tick and the other retains that velocity for the second tick. A holding teammate draws neither a new velocity nor duration. There is no new observation channel, sensing fee, roster change, simultaneous decision barrier, time rescaling, or post-first-step observation available to revise the already selected duration.[^design]

The native route is **own expiry amid team movement → legal private history and sampled velocity → one/two-tick persistence → actual movement and joint service, including opportunities lost while holding → later free observations and owned decisions → native return and finite actor/critic learning**. A held second step can preserve service or prolong a poor velocity while partners co-adapt. A duration component earns scientific value through that complete native comparison, not through its name, movement, timer operation or prediction accuracy.

L is the whole changed learning package, not merely a relabelled T. It uses separate PPO clips for velocity and duration, raw gamma-one suffix credit, the old critic, and a detached history/velocity-conditioned **2209-parameter residual**. Its duration advantage is defined from the observed suffix **G(t+1)** minus the old critic and old residual, divided by the same raw-advantage scale used by velocity. It is not independently centered or standardized. Old predictions and targets remain fixed across four epochs; the policy surrogates retain the all-primitive-row denominator. The training-only residual uses recorded detached acting history/velocity rather than the selected duration or future information as input.[^design][^design-intake]

At the final tick, L retains the duration draw and its RNG effect but assigns it no duration-policy or residual-regression credit, because no later action is observable. Final velocity still receives its native reward. The record reports **6797** such final-tick training draws, preserved rather than deleted. Horizon censoring does not create another step, bootstrap, reward-rate conversion or episode carryover.[^card][^result]

F retains its complete initial **2242-parameter duration head frozen at half/half physical {1,2}**, while its velocity actor and critic train freshly under the old compound-PPO recipe. It is not a historical fitted tensor substituted for a comparator, and it receives no privileged acting information. Shared initialization and exogenous world pairing do not imply identical trajectories or shared action draws. L has **70762 trainable parameters**; F has **66311**, with its frozen duration parameters retained separately. Changed capacity, regression and gradient allocation remain part of the package difference.[^design][^card]
