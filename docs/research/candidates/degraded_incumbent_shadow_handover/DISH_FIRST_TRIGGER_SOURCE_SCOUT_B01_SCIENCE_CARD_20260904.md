## 1. Question, claim ceiling, and non-goals

At the first application-valid handover proposed by one finitely trained STRUCTURED policy on a
small declared panel, does promoting the pre-warmed standby shadow state improve 100-tick recovery
over copying the incumbent state, and is either owner transfer better than shell-matched retention?

The claim ceiling is a preliminary source-selection signal or counterexample on the sixteen
declared rows of `RIDGE-BEND-HOT-STANDBY-RELAY-2UAV-v3` after 64 learner updates. A positive branch
means only that SHADOW beat COPY on the observed first-trigger rows at this budget. It does not
establish stable superiority, expected return, natural trigger prevalence, an optimal policy,
unique information, necessary shadow state, arbitrary `k` or `N`, transfer, safety, deployment, or
flight value. A negative closes at most this panel, budget, checkpoint process, and intervention.

## 2. Prior evidence and live explanations

R02 produced no result activity and therefore no COPY/SHADOW polarity. The accepted TEST-only
transaction substrate directly materializes three equal-cost first-trigger branches, while the
native recurrent trainer and evaluator already exercise the real host, policy, PPO learner, and
native transition path. Evidence-spec section 11 makes the former R02 total-map, prevalence,
formal-repair, and complete-production obligations inapplicable as B launch gates.

Live explanations:

1. **Shadow preparation is actionable.** The standby's pre-trigger causal history makes its warm
   hidden state better than an incumbent-state copy immediately after ownership changes.
2. **Generic remap is sufficient.** COPY improves over RETAIN, but SHADOW adds no recovery value.
3. **Replay/replan containment.** A competent deadline-bound replay could reconstruct the useful
   shadow state; it remains the strongest unimplemented containing explanation and limits any
   positive claim to source selection on this runner.
4. **Checkpoint/source co-adaptation.** The policy is trained with shadow preparation, so a SHADOW
   advantage may reflect that training convention rather than unique environment information.
5. **Exposure or trigger insufficiency.** Sixty-four updates may not make the commit head competent,
   or the declared rows may supply too few first-valid triggers.
6. **Panel or optimizer heterogeneity.** A source contrast may depend on seed, package, schedule,
   speed, owner, reflection, or finite-budget optimizer noise.

## 3. Treatment, comparators, and causal cut

All branches share the seed's trained STRUCTURED checkpoint, normalization state, complete prefix,
first-valid origin action, pre-transaction native state, and future exogenous tape. The fork occurs
after the policy emits the origin action and before the application-tick policy forward.

- **Treatment — `TRANSFER_SHADOW`:** apply the common atomic owner/actuator transaction and promote
  the recipient's pre-warmed shadow hidden state.
- **Strongest live same-information comparator — `TRANSFER_COPY`:** apply the identical transaction
  and promote a copy of the incumbent active hidden state. It sees the same observations, prefix,
  checkpoint, action law, transaction bytes/cost, and future tape; only the promoted source differs.
- **Shell-matched control — `RETAIN`:** consume the same intent and transaction shell/cost but retain
  ownership and hidden-state placement. `COPY-RETAIN` is generic remap value on this shell, not a
  natural no-transaction effect.

The absent replay/replan arm is recorded as a live explanation, not a launch blocker or a fourth
outcome. Exact replay containment, if later observed, would remove unique-information wording but
could not erase the directly observed COPY-RETAIN contrast.

## 6. Observables and estimands

Every seed summary records nonzero training transitions, learner updates, optimizer steps,
evaluation prefix ticks, and branch consequence ticks, plus trigger counts and every panel row.

For each triggered row and branch record:

- `recovery_return_100`: sum of native service over the 100 consequence ticks;
- `worst_20_tick_service`: the minimum service sum over all contiguous 20-tick windows;
- `recovery_delay_10`: first consequence tick ending a run of ten service-positive ticks, capped
  at 100 when absent;
- total-energy change from the post-transaction branch boundary;
- counts of invalid commit, token gap, dual owner, dual payload, buffer-clear, command-slew, and
  separation events; and
- first-trigger tick, owner before/after, package, schedule, speed, slot, and branch receipt facts.

Primary paired estimand per seed:

`Delta_shadow = mean_triggered(recovery_return_100[SHADOW] - recovery_return_100[COPY])`.

Supporting paired estimands are the analogous `worst_20_tick_service` and recovery-delay
differences. Generic-remap value is
`Delta_copy = mean_triggered(recovery_return_100[COPY] - recovery_return_100[RETAIN])`.
All per-row differences and seed values remain visible; no panel row is weighted by trigger time.

A seed has **usable trigger support** when at least four of sixteen rows trigger and the triggered
set contains both packages. A seed has **SHADOW nonharm** when SHADOW has no more total hard-event
ticks than COPY and mean SHADOW energy is at most `1.05 *` mean COPY energy on its triggered rows.

**Minimum effect of interest:** an absolute mean SHADOW-COPY difference of five service-positive
ticks in the 100-tick recovery horizon. Native service is binary per tick, so this is five percent
of the maximum recovery return and a concrete recovery difference larger than a one-tick edge.
The same scale is used descriptively for COPY-RETAIN. Relative return alone is unsuitable where
COPY can supply little service. The original sign-based `FTS-*` rule is not replaced by this MEI.

**Headroom:** no tuned same-information generic baseline with the B01 64-update process and
sixteen-row first-trigger population has been observed. Earlier R06 checkpoints/panels and the
FOLR B04 host differ in observation, action, information or budget and do not fill this slot.
COPY is the immediate legal source-selection comparator; replay/replan remains a stronger live
containing explanation for any positive source-specific result. No compatible host baseline
package has been identified, and this absence does not hold the launch.

**How the result will be interpreted:** an improvement above five ticks, repeated across usable
seeds with the original tail and nonharm checks, would motivate a further B discriminator of
source preparation versus replay/replan or training co-adaptation. A difference inside five ticks
would leave a small finite-budget effect possible but would not justify a practically substantial
source claim, even if the original sign rule says `FTS-BS`. A difference below minus five ticks
would favor COPY on this population and motivate inspection of shadow co-adaptation or source
mismatch. Insufficient trigger support still follows `FTS-B0` and cannot be read as source equality.
The original branches remain the reading rule, and no direction disposition follows locally.
