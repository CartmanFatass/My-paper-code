## Decision now

The accepted family asks whether the recurrent state source at an ordinary legal
handover changes native recovery. B01 never exposed a source fork. A03 restored
information and incumbent service, but no legal handover; A04 identified two failed
certificate conjuncts, and A05 reproduced a synthetic interface boundary. Another
diagnostic is useful only if its answer changes the next intervention. A trustworthy
native package-performance comparison can be worthwhile before component attribution
or a source-effect comparison is possible.

The owner's latest steering asks us to reassess experiment design itself after the
specification calibration: distinguish real learning work from exact diagnosis,
upper/census/replay/publication burdens, and propose the smallest useful real B with
honest cost. An over-cap projection is not a measured inflation factor against a
minimal adequate design. No such comparative factor has been established for N3.

## A concrete candidate for the node to accept, narrow or reject

Candidate claim: at a small, matched training budget on the declared ground-terminal
host, a forecast-consistent learner/interface package may improve mean whole-episode
native service over the existing same-information learner/controller.

Binding structure: other-agent partial observability and state ownership during
handover. Physical vehicles, active/shadow recurrent copies and causal messages stay
distinct. The two-agent host has no new variable-population or join/leave claim.

The proposed package applies a sigmoid to the existing service-logit native interface
and trains the existing prediction mean/Cholesky output jointly with Gaussian negative
log likelihood in the native prediction coordinates, using the existing next-state
labels. It replaces that component's mean-only MSE at the same auxiliary coefficient;
the existing service-Q target, masks and the other PPO/auxiliary terms remain matched.
This is a prospective joint algorithm treatment, not a reporting correction and not a
promise of calibration. In particular, covariance changes affect both certificates and
snapshot feedback. The node must decide whether this specific package is a meaningful
next question or choose a cheaper concrete alternative. No implementation is accepted.

The control is the actual currently executable learner/controller with its original
mean-only objective and raw service interface, on the same new host and training budget.
It is a legal matched control, not a demonstrated tuned competent optimum. A stronger
currently supported same-information control can be selected by the node; lack of a
tuned upper pair does not itself stop this initial package comparison. State-copy,
retention and deadline replay remain alternatives for any later source-specific claim.

Proposed smallest initial allocation: one new training seed paired across two arms,
sixteen complete updates per arm at the existing 32 lanes by 128 ticks, four epochs
by eight minibatches. This is 65,536 training transitions and 512 optimizer steps per
arm, 131,072 transitions and 1,024 optimizer steps in the pair. It is an economical
first allocation, not established sufficient exposure or a universal new minimum.
No success-conditioned extension, favorable-seed replacement or repeated smoke.

Use the final sixteenth-update checkpoint, with no checkpoint/configuration search.
Evaluate both packages and both schedules at speed 4, slot 0: four existing-coordinate
combinations, each with at most 1,200 native ticks. Pair exogenous evaluation randomness
between arms. This is at most 4,800 evaluation ticks per arm. Preserve terminal failures,
all four row outcomes, training curves, actual legal handover counts and service before
and after any transfer. Those four rows are not four independent training samples.

Primary proposed observable: mean native service ticks per 1,200-tick episode, together
with the paired per-row differences. A proposed minimum effect of interest is +24 ticks
(+0.02 episode service fraction, 2.4 seconds of service) against the matched control:
a small tangible recovery margin for deciding whether to buy independent seeds. This is
a new candidate scale, not A05's diagnostic margin or a rewrite of B01's source MEI.
Energy and hard-event counts remain visible so a service gain bought with harm is not
described as unqualified superiority. No change to legal thresholds, ownership rules,
available actor information or source readiness is included.

One trustworthy improvement of this scale would justify considering one or two new
independent training seeds with this same comparison. Inside-MEI, adverse or mixed
results would be reported without claiming stable equivalence or general failure; the
next adjustment would need a concrete reason. The proposed package can gain entirely
through incumbent control or permissive learned forecasts. Neither calibration nor
SHADOW-COPY value follows, even if the service signal is positive. All previous outcomes
and the outcome-informed motivation of this candidate remain disclosed.

## Honest workload and cost

Let U be updates per arm, N = 32 * 128 * U ordinary training transitions, E the number
of service-label-eligible transitions, and H the actual sum of their consequence steps
(0 <= H <= 20E). The retained native training path executes:

`ordinary steps N + next-label steps N + delay steps 2E + consequence steps H`.

Thus the complete native training work is `2N + 2E + H <= 24N`, before evaluation,
recurrent/critic forwards, PPO replay/backward, initialization, compilation and publication.
At the proposed U=16 this upper is 1,572,864 native step calls per arm, not 65,536.
The coefficient 24 is a source-derived work bound, not an observed wall multiplier.
Eligibility and early termination determine the actual clone work.

Completed B01 per-seed runner walls were 1,068.1103, 977.5006 and 995.6142 seconds
for 64 updates plus sixteen evaluation rows; all met their 1,800-second original cap.
The 3,041.2250-second figure is the sum of three seeds, not one invocation. Current
A05's full process was 2.40 seconds, with a separate 3.29-second verification process.
These facts do not show an N3 runtime explosion. Historical engineering complexity and
source-scope refusal are different observations from measured runtime.

Scaling the old B01 wall by 16/64 is not a justified projection: the host, package and
eligible clone fraction change; startup/evaluation/publication are not proportional.
No complete matching-node wall or aggregate-CPU measurement exists for this candidate.
Proposed spending boundary is at most 1,800 seconds per complete arm/seed invocation,
including preparation, learning, checking and publication, with no inherited A05 allowance.
It is a proposed cap, not a forecast or a launch already admitted. The node may choose a
smaller allocation or reject the package if its actual workload cannot fit economically.
CM must use the chosen runner's work law and actual-node facts to state an honest bound;
there is no blanket requirement for a new pilot or extra exact-validation round.

Existing policy tensor batching and native lane batches are reusable; the C++ lane loop
is currently serial. Dividing measured wall by core count would invent a speedup. Any
fixed-team/native change must preserve the selected treatment, RNG and complete cost,
and be separately bounded by the current runtime specification. No scheduler, service,
generic worker pool or new guard is part of this proposal.

Consultation exposure: zero new models, native states, transitions, backwards, optimizer
steps, tests or experiments. Machine-generated prior exposure remains in the listed B01
seed summaries and A05 formal summary; read-only source arithmetic is not new exposure.
New result-bearing work would remain remote-first, committed/pushed and detached, with
fresh actual-node memory admission. This note requests only the Direction decision.
