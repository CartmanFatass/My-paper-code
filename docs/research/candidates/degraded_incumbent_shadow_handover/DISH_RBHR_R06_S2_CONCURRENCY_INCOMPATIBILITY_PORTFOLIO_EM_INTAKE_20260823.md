## Conclusion

The exact S2 thread scheduler is technically incompatible with its mandatory
concurrency and overhead gates. This changes no r06 science, but it invalidates
the active S2 continuation and every future-request assumption based on the
thread implementation.

Current evidence supports **one assessment-only, result-blind isolated-process
scheduler cost/feasibility decomposition** before either a finite repair
envelope or no-current engineering decision. It does not support a finite
isolated-process implementation cap: the packet names the required process
architecture but supplies no low/central/high engineering cost or accepted
resource model for it. The 21 unused days under the stopped 24-day S2 hard cap
are neither transferable nor process-repair authority.

No-current engineering investment is also premature. The failure is sharply
localized to the thread substrate, while the immutable panel remains the sole
current direct-UAV, strongly identified, branch-complete discriminator. A
read-only process-architecture decomposition is the smallest fact that can
separate a finite proportionate repair from an unbounded, infeasible or low-
value continuation.

This artifact recommends assessment only and creates no implementation,
resource, lease or allocation authority.
