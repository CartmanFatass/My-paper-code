## Conclusion

Recommend that Portfolio invest in the finite, non-replenishable r06 process-
repair envelope: release **P1 now** at `21` managed / `35` hard engineer-days,
and reserve **P2 only conditionally** at `15` managed / `24` hard, subject to
the complete P1 release gate and an absolute `59`-day residual total. The
separate 20 S1/S2 days remain sunk actual and neither enlarge nor reduce this
forward envelope.

This is preferable to no-current engineering because r06 remains the sole
direct-UAV, strongly identified, branch-complete variable-`k` discriminator,
and P1 is now an exact bounded test of the only material remaining feasibility
risk: whether isolated workers plus semantics-preserving per-worker repair can
restore ordinary CPU/wall feasibility. P2 is not released merely because P1
was funded or because a process pool exists.

This intake recommends, but does not enact, the Portfolio decision. It grants
no source, TEST, runtime, lease, identity, activity or partial-result authority.

## Scientific decision value and identifiability

The residual rebase exposes no science-bearing ambiguity. R06 retains its
Pro-closed treatment, strict-containing comparators, deterministic 11,520-tape
population, complete 256,513-unit indivisible panel, paired mask views,
first-valid REAL/SHAM fork, common-anchor inference, fifteen exhaustive
branches, one future nonreplaceable identity and complete-result firewall.
The thread failure and cost assessment provide no efficacy evidence.

The full panel remains unusually decision-dense. It can separate STRUCTURED
from FLEX, NEVER, IMMEDIATE and HYSTERESIS; actual actuation value from
REAL/SHAM behavior; and the learned mechanism from simple-rule sufficiency,
while jointly enforcing competence, witness, support, headroom, precision and
nonharm. No panel shrink or cheaper isolated subset answers that same causal
question, and no other current object supplies the same direct-UAV bridge.

P1 therefore has high scientific option value even though it produces no
scientific result: success unlocks a uniquely aligned empirical object;
failure at its explicit resource or semantic gates prevents the larger P2 and
full-panel costs. Engineering absence remains distinct from scientific
failure.

## Residual cost, ordinary-CPU risk and opportunity cost

The CM's `20 / 36 / 59` estimate is decision-grade for the present bytes. It
retains the native/data-plane flows, deterministic task graph and most atomic
contracts; adapts journals, cache and CLI for process safety; replaces the
thread pool, shared Torch runtime and shared data plane; and discards the
thread concurrency result for production eligibility. Approximately 70–80%
of the r06-specific experiment/data-plane bytes remain, while approximately
45–55% of current scheduler/atomic implementation is salvageable into the
process design.

The 20 days already spent are not prospective cost and must not be used to
justify continuation. They matter only as evidence of implementation risk and
of which bytes now exist. The forward comparison is the residual central `36`
and absolute hard `59` against r06's remaining information value.

That cost is substantial, especially with four empirical objects already in
the Portfolio. The ordinary-resource risk is also real: the comparable
`10.672341100056656`-second update implies about `364.28` training core-hours
and about `379.47` core-hours after the existing nontraining component, before
process overhead. This exceeds the unchanged 320-core-hour ordinary target.
An update of approximately `8.93` seconds or faster is required even before
overhead; the true accepted threshold must be lower enough for all complete-
command work.

Those facts argue against releasing the whole residual envelope now, but not
against P1. P1 explicitly prices process isolation, process-safe atomicity,
per-worker CPU repair and result-blind self-audit. Its `21` managed / `35` hard
limit converts the CPU uncertainty into an early discriminator before the
`15/24` P2 acceptance, remeasurement and request-preparation spend. The
process-safe scheduler and observation surfaces also have reusable value
within DISH. That reuse is engineering option value only; it transfers no
evidence, resource, claim or authority to SGSP or another direction.

## Strongest alternatives and unchanged claim ceiling

The strongest decision alternative is **no-current r06 engineering**. It is
credible because the residual central/high cost is large, the current one-core
baseline already misses the ordinary CPU target, and competing empirical work
creates opportunity cost. It becomes the recommended disposition if P1 cannot
reach its resource and semantic release gates inside `35` days, if P2 can no
longer fit within its `24`-day hard ceiling and `59`-day total, or if the
incremental direct-UAV decision value is displaced before P2 release.

The strongest scientific alternative to a favorable STRUCTURED outcome
remains finite-budget restriction or learnability: STRUCTURED may train more
easily within 1,024 updates than strict-containing FLEX without establishing
that FLEX freedoms are intrinsically harmful or that structured handover is
uniquely causal. The strongest population-specific alternative remains
insufficient recoverable headroom in the frozen speed/geometry population.
Scheduler or CPU behavior is not a scientific alternative.

The maximum claim remains finite-budget exact-host evidence on
`RIDGE-BEND-HOT-STANDBY-RELAY-2UAV-v3` that one shared two-UAV policy improves
direct service or tail robustness at one common registered speed across every
claim schedule, remains noninferior and nonharmful across the other registered
speeds, and passes every competence, witness, support, REAL/SHAM, FLEX,
simple-rule, energy and continuity qualification. No outcome can establish
arbitrary or continuous `k`, arbitrary route speed, variable `N`, natural
prevalence, convergence, unique mediation, transfer, safety, certification,
deployment or flight.

## Invalidation and revisit boundary

The envelope is invalid and engineering returns no-current before further work
if any of the following occurs:

- P1 would exceed `35` days, P2 would exceed `24`, or P1 actual plus P2
  forecast would exceed the non-replenishable `59`-day residual hard ceiling;
- result-blind effective concurrency remains below six or overhead exceeds
  30%;
- more than eight total cores, any GPU, a different native substrate, or an
  enlarged hard resource ceiling is required;
- P1 cannot project complete-command CPU at or below 320 hours and wall at or
  below 65 hours, or any unchanged hard resource gate cannot be satisfied;
- deterministic order, persistent optimizer/model/recurrent state, sole
  checkpoints, paired mask/fork lineage, complete reducer, result blindness,
  failure atomicity, the immutable panel or one-identity rule cannot be
  preserved; or
- any treatment, comparator, population, inference, branch, claim or result-
  firewall change is proposed.

After such a return, revisit only on a new result-blind process-worker fact
that makes the complete ordinary CPU/wall case supportable within eight cores
and unchanged hard ceilings, together with a fresh bounded residual-cost
assessment for the then-current bytes. Completion of competing empirical work
may change opportunity cost, but transfers no evidence or resource authority
into DISH and does not itself reopen the envelope.

R05 remains terminal, consumed and no-current. The withdrawn r06 lease remains
unlaunchable. The current cut remains
