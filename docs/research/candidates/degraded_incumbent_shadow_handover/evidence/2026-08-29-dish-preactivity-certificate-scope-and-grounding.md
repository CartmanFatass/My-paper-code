## Smallest discriminator and decision branches

The primary discriminator is an implication proof or a counterexample pair.
For each proposed certificate `C`, require either:

- a result-blind theorem `C -> B` for every named complete-object gate and
  first-match branch `B`, including the block, speed, schedule, regime,
  common-anchor, support, precision, endpoint and nonharm semantics; or
- two conforming complete R06 completions with identical `C` but different
  registered opportunity/headroom or comparator branches.

The second construction proves that `C` is not sufficient; it need not and may
not instantiate an R06 identity or reveal an empirical result. If no proper
certificate survives, the honest scientific result is that the complete panel
is the smallest currently justified decision object.

- A sufficient certificate preserving direct opportunity/headroom and
  competent containing-rule separation supports `CONTINUE` toward a separately
  frozen staged engineering slice.
- A sufficient certificate retaining only generic redundancy or a deployed
  simple timing-rule question supports `NARROW` to that exact direct-service
  question.
- A proof of absent physical opportunity/headroom, or exact absorption by
  competent containing controls, supports `PARK` of the current scientific
  object.
- Irreducibility with no static scientific refutation preserves the empirical
  question and returns the full-panel purchase. Static bindability or cost may
  stage or block that purchase but cannot create a lifecycle recommendation.
- A technical bindability failure returns its exact reentry while preserving
  scientific priority.

## Early stop and scope invalidation

Stop searching for a smaller certificate when a general conforming-completion
counterexample defeats the audited certificate family, when the only surviving
object is the full registered panel, or when a proposal weakens a claim-bearing
gate. Stop the technical branch when the smallest faithful observation would
create result-bearing R05/R06 activity or require changed semantics.

competence; treating function-class inclusion as trained-policy equality;
