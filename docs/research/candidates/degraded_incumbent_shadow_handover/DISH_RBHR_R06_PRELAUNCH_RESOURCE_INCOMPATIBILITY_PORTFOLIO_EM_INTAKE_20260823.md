## Why assessment must precede another implementation envelope

The scientific value supports continued investigation, but not unpriced
implementation. A deterministic failure-atomic scheduler must preserve master-
addressed order, sole checkpoints, paired mask/fork lineage, crash/resume and
one-identity frontier semantics while scheduling at most eight independent
block-arm training jobs and evaluation batches. It must then pass independent
reacceptance and produce actual aggregate CPU, wall, RSS, scratch, durable and
I/O measurements from the exact parallel command.

Those obligations may be reusable within compatible DISH revisions, but the
packet does not quantify their engineering cost or updated reuse fraction. The
observed serial wall proves a scheduler is necessary; it does not establish
how many engineer-days a correct scheduler requires or that the repaired full
chain will satisfy ordinary 65-hour or hard 110-hour wall bounds. The prior
E2 caps and prior optimistic wall estimate cannot be repurposed as this new
cost evidence.

The smallest high-information purchase is therefore the bounded assessment.
It resolves whether the remaining scheduler work is finite and proportionate
to the unique direct-UAV discriminator before Portfolio incurs implementation
or another lease cycle.
