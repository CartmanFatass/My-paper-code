envelope, Portfolio should request one bounded, result-blind, exact
low/central/high cost decomposition from the same DISH CM, with no
implementation.

it no current engineering investment now would be premature. But the only
established engineering-cost fact is that the five-flow data plane exceeds 30
experienced engineer-days. No defensible central estimate, upper estimate or
finite expanded boundary is yet available. Authorizing option (a) now would
therefore require inventing a numerical ceiling unsupported by the evidence.
The bounded decomposition is the cheapest next fact capable of distinguishing
a finite, proportionate expansion from a no-current decision.

This recommendation does not authorize construction, source work, a lease,
identity creation or execution. It preserves the applied r06 scientific
investment while Portfolio obtains the missing cost information.

## Opportunity cost and why decomposition dominates now

Exceeding 30 experienced engineer-days is material. An open-ended expansion
could consume engineering attention that Portfolio may prefer to allocate
elsewhere, and the five mutually dependent flows may have different risk,
reuse and integration profiles. Conversely, declining the direction solely on
the threshold crossing would discard the only current direct-UAV discriminator
without knowing whether the overage is modest and bounded or substantially
larger.

The current evidence cannot support either extreme:

- option (a) lacks an evidence-backed finite envelope;
- option (c) lacks evidence that the full cost is disproportionate to the
  discriminator's high, branch-complete scientific value; and
- option (b) directly resolves the missing decision variable without crossing
  the activity boundary or consuming the larger implementation cost.

This direction-local recommendation does not rank RISP, RCLE or SGSP, import
their evidence or impose a direction quota. Portfolio may later compare the
returned cost against its portfolio-wide opportunity costs using each
direction's own provenance.
