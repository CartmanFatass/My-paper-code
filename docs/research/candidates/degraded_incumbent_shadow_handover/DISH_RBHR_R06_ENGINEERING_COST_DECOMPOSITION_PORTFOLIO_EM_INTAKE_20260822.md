## Scientific value and identifiability

R06 remains the sole current direct-UAV empirical variable-`k` object. Its
question is directly aligned with the project destination: whether one shared
two-UAV policy can preserve responder-to-UAV-to-base service when externally
fixed or switched `k` changes renewal duration, using prepared structured
shadow control and at most one certified first handover.

The scientific decision value is high because the complete panel can select,
narrow or delete the mechanism family rather than merely report an aggregate
score. It jointly distinguishes:

- STRUCTURED from strict-containing FLEX, NEVER, independently trained
  IMMEDIATE and HYSTERESIS;
- full-package value from actual first-application-valid REAL-over-SHAM
  actuation value;
- adaptive structure from deployable simple-rule sufficiency;
- target competence, recovery witness, adaptive support, headroom, precision,
  nonharm and endpoint failure; and
- fixed-only, schedule-specific, regime-specific and common-anchor variable-
  `k` value across fifteen exhaustive first-match branches.

Identifiability is unusually strong for a construction-stage object. The
11,520-tape population is deterministic and arm-independent, has zero
scientific-admission failure probability, retains every unfavorable tape and
requires a common speed anchor across schedules and regimes. Paired mask views,
all five arms, the REAL/SHAM fork, 24 independent blocks, 99,999 joint
resamples, the complete 6,990-estimand family and result firewall address the
main shortcut and multiplicity risks. No cheaper partial panel, TEST fixture or
single comparator can answer the same joint causal question.

Every possible complete branch is decision-relevant: it can retain structured
handover, prefer the containing FLEX family, select a simple rule, isolate a
nonactuation package effect, identify target-specific nonidentification or
remove the present direct-UAV value claim. The high construction cost therefore
buys a high-information discriminator rather than a weakly identified trial.

## Strongest alternatives and unchanged claim ceiling

The strongest alternative to a favorable STRUCTURED result remains finite-
budget restriction or learnability: STRUCTURED may train more easily within
1,024 updates than its strict-containing FLEX comparator, without showing that
FLEX's broader freedoms are intrinsically harmful or that structured transfer/
timing is uniquely causal. The strongest population-specific alternative
remains insufficient recoverable headroom in this fixed speed/geometry
factorial. Generic two-vehicle redundancy, favorable standby geometry, simple
timing, message traffic, recurrent-state capacity and event/role/token
shortcuts also remain possible; the retained controls narrow but do not prove
unique mediation.

The claim ceiling does not change with engineering investment. At most, a
positive complete result supports finite-budget evidence on
`RIDGE-BEND-HOT-STANDBY-RELAY-2UAV-v3` that one shared two-UAV policy improves
direct service or tail robustness at one common registered route speed across
all three claim schedules, remains noninferior and nonharmful across the other
registered speeds, and passes every competence, witness, support, REAL/SHAM,
FLEX, simple-rule, energy and continuity qualification.

It cannot establish arbitrary or continuous `k`, arbitrary route speeds,
variable `N`, natural prevalence, convergence, unique mediation, other
geometry/terrain/sensor/radio laws, aircraft transfer, safety, certification,
deployment or flight.
