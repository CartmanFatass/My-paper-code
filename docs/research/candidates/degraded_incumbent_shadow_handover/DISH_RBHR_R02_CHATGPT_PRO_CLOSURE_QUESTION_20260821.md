## 1. Question, value and isolation

The exact question is whether a two-UAV tracking/relay team can preserve direct
physical service across an externally fixed or switched skill period `k` by
preparing a second causal controller state while the incumbent continues to
serve, then transferring the single service-owner token atomically after a
deployable readiness decision.

The treatment is useful only if its **real first handover**, not merely its
second state, extra compute, event marker, transaction shell, simpler threshold
rule or favorable standby geometry, improves service. The experiment therefore
separates the first real-versus-sham actuation effect from the complete learned
package and compares the structured policy with a strict-containing flexible
shadow family and two deployable simple handover rules.

This is a new direction. It is not a successor, rescue, fusion or reinterpretation
of any previous HMASD object. It imports no result, threshold, seed, coordinate,
acceptance or claim from another direction. The two physical packages below are
supported only as prospective task primitives: temporary visual occlusion can
remove target observations and degrade tracking, while terrain obstruction can
remove relay line of sight. No source establishes DISH treatment value or
external-`k` robustness.

## 2. Exact physical host

simulator with sealed stochastic tapes.

The complete generator is frozen in the companion manifest reproduced below. That manifest is part of
revision 02: it fixes the route and terrain equations, visibility/radio laws,
all distributions, independent degradation and `k` clocks, initial-state and

below is interpretive; the manifest controls any discrepancy. No host parameter

- Primitive time step is `0.1 s`; every episode lasts `120 s`. After a physical

### 2.1 Two and only two degradation regimes

Every claim-bearing panel reports the regimes separately.

1. `TARGET-VISUAL-OCCLUSION`: a mapped ridge spur removes the incumbent's
   target view for `2–4 s` while the target executes a sealed `25–45 degree`
   turn. The standby has a prospectively balanced clear, marginal or inferior
   view. The base-radio path remains physically viable, so the primary degraded
   quantity is the incumbent target belief rather than relay loss.
2. `TERRAIN-RELAY-LOS`: the incumbent retains target view while the ridge-bend
   makes its payload path to the base lose Fresnel clearance for `2–4 s`. The
   standby has a prospectively balanced superior, near-equal or inferior
   overlap corridor. The primary degraded quantity is payload delivery rather
   than sensing.

Within each regime, positive, near-zero and negative standby-advantage strata
occupy one third of sealed evaluation tapes. The stratum is evaluator-only and
is never supplied to training, observations or the gate. This prevents a task
in which handover is always beneficial. No combined-occlusion third regime,

### 2.2 Independent degradation opportunity

## 3. External skill period

A high-level motion/protocol command is held for `k` primitive ticks. One
parameter vector, normalization state and checkpoint per arm is shared across
all `k` schedules. The current `k`, its epoch number and primitive ticks to the
next ordinary renewal are observed by every arm; the future schedule is hidden.

- Training mixes fixed `k=4`, fixed `k=12`, `4->12` and `12->4` schedules.
- Claim-bearing evaluation cells are held-out fixed `k=8`, `4->12` and
  `12->4`. For every switched cell, degradation onset is independently uniform
  on `{42,54,66} s`, switch time independently uniform on `{36,48,60,72} s`,
  and their Cartesian product is balanced. Neither clock is computed from the
  other. Initial renewal phase is independently uniform and balanced; relative
  switch/onset offset is analysis metadata only.
- Fixed `k=4` and fixed `k=12` fresh evaluation cells are competence and
  calibration cells, not held-out generalization claims.
- A switch never truncates the command already in progress. The old command
  finishes; the new `k` takes effect at the next ordinary renewal. No recurrent
  state, target track, packet buffer or optimizer state resets at a `k` switch.
  A readiness certificate bound to the old `k` epoch expires and must be
  recomputed.
- Reward, discounting, opportunity windows, service, outage, recapture and
  costs are defined in primitive physical time, not decision count.

No per-`k` policy, head, fine-tuning, recalibration, checkpoint selection or
evaluation adaptation is legal.

## 4. Shared observation, model and action law

All learned arms use the same tensor shapes and full forward/backward graph:

- one role-shared observation encoder with two `128`-unit hidden layers;
- a `128`-unit GRU state for each UAV's incumbent copy and an equal-sized
  weight-tied shadow copy;
- role-shared motion, prepare and commit-intent heads;
- a centralized critic used only for training, with identical privileged
  causal-past input in every learned arm; and
- identical normalization, auxiliary one-step target/link prediction, optimizer
  calls, communication budget and full dual-state computation.

Deployable observations are current and causal only: local/partner geometry,
camera observation or missingness, target belief, radio margin, packet age,
energy, separation, owner and service epoch, causal messages, current `k` and
renewal phase. True regime label, standby-advantage stratum, target future,
future `k`, future radio state, evaluator ceiling, counterfactual return,
remaining episode time, seed and hidden ground truth are excluded.

Both recurrent copies update once per primitive tick. Only the incumbent copy
may drive payload or motion before a valid atomic commit. The shadow may send
the same bounded readiness/snapshot messages in every arm; those bytes and
their energy/latency are charged in every arm.

The five separately trained learned arms—STRUCTURED, FLEX, NEVER,
IMMEDIATE-ELIGIBLE and FIXED-HYSTERESIS—use exactly `1,024` PPO updates,
`4,096` primitive transitions per update, AdamW learning rate `3e-4`, primitive
discount `exp(-0.1/20)`, GAE `lambda=0.95`, the same minibatch/epoch count and
one fixed checkpoint immediately after update 1,024. The auxiliary prediction
loss weight is `0.1` and is identical. There is no handover bonus or
treatment-specific shaping. No budget, checkpoint, architecture, threshold,
arm or optimizer search is allowed.

## 5. Structured treatment

`PHYSICS-CERTIFIED-SHADOW-HANDOVER` maintains a single-writer service token.
Before handover the incumbent alone carries payload; the standby shadow may
move, sense, predict and exchange readiness messages but cannot count as
service.

At an ordinary renewal after a deployable degradation indication, the incumbent
sends a canonical snapshot

```text
(service_epoch, payload_sequence, target_mean, target_covariance,
 link_state, timestamp, k_epoch)
```

and the standby builds a shadow state from that snapshot plus its own causal
observations. A commit is eligible only when all frozen certificate predicates
hold:

1. owner, service epoch, payload sequence and current `k` epoch match;
2. snapshot age is at most `0.5 s` and the shadow has warmed for at least
   `1.0 s` physical time;
3. incumbent-transfer and standby-local two-dimensional target predictions have
   Mahalanobis disagreement at most `5.99` under their summed covariance;
4. the standby's causal two-second service predictor has lower confidence bound
   at least `0.60`, while the incumbent can maintain its present command until
   the next commit boundary;
5. the fixed action-slew and `15 m` separation predicates hold; and
6. the shared policy emits `COMMIT-INTENT`.

The atomic arbiter compare-and-swaps `(owner, service_epoch)` at the next
primitive boundary. A rejected commit leaves the incumbent fully active. A
successful commit moves the canonical payload sequence and target/link belief
without clearing or duplicating any buffer; the old owner stays active until
that boundary and the new owner alone is active afterward. No blackout, empty
token, dual payload, action interpolation, extra decision instant or episode-
clock reset is permitted. Revision 01 allows at most one successful handover
per episode.

The certificate is an interlock, not an automatic solution: the policy must
prepare, position the standby and issue commit intent using deployable history.

## 6. Comparators and controls

### 6.1 `FLEXIBLE-SHADOW-CONTAIN`

The strict-containing arm has the identical inputs, tensors, communication,

- shadow initialization residual `Delta I`;
- projected incumbent/shadow state blend `alpha`;
- a residual on the readiness score; and
- a one-boundary action-blend residual.

The constrained treatment is reproduced exactly at
`Delta I=0`, `alpha=1`, readiness residual `0` and action-blend residual `0`.
Those tensors exist and are evaluated in every learned arm; the constrained
arms mask their behavioral output to these constants. Before any scientific
activity, CM must demonstrate exact action/state emulation on identical causal
histories and at least one causal history on which an unmasked FLEX setting
changes the reachable post-renewal action. Hard eligibility and safety

Because FLEX strictly contains the treatment, a treatment advantage over a
competent scratch-trained FLEX can support only finite-budget learnability,

### 6.2 `NEVER-HANDOVER`

This separately trained arm executes the same dual-state encoder, shadow
motion, snapshots, readiness computation, messages, bytes, energy charges and
optimizer work, but masks effective token transfer to zero. The incumbent
remains sole payload owner. It controls extra compute, a second vehicle/state,
standby movement and auxiliary learning.

### 6.3 Matched `SHAM-HANDOVER`

SHAM is not a separately trained model and is never yoked to a post-treatment
real trajectory. At the first valid structured commit intent, the simulator
clones the complete pre-trigger physical/controller state and the sealed future
exogenous tape into two branches:

- `FORK-REAL`: performs the registered atomic transfer to the prepared shadow;
- `FORK-SHAM`: performs the same comparison, communication, latency, bookkeeping,
  accounting debit and epoch transaction but promotes an exact incumbent-state
  clone, leaving the incumbent vehicle as service owner.

The pre-trigger history is identical. Both branches run a fixed `10 s`
post-trigger horizon, prohibit a second transfer and retain every failure or
non-recapture. This paired first-trigger assay is the primary causal estimate
of applying the prepared shadow rather than merely executing the transaction.
The full-episode package comparison is separate and includes every tape,
including no-trigger tapes.

### 6.4 Deployable simple rules

- `IMMEDIATE-ELIGIBLE` is independently trained end-to-end from matched
  initialization/tapes with the same architecture, full dual-state graph and
  1,024-update budget. Its commit head is hard-masked during training and
  evaluation to transfer at the first common-certificate-eligible renewal after
  one deployable degradation-indicator tick.
- `FIXED-HYSTERESIS` is independently trained under the same law, but its commit
  head transfers only after the indicator persists for `0.5 s` physical time.
- The visual indicator is camera-target missingness; the relay indicator is
  incumbent radio margin below the registered `6 dB` service threshold. Both
  are causal inputs already supplied to all arms. Neither rule receives the
  regime label, standby-advantage stratum, future switch or evaluator ceiling.

Both use the same token protocol, physical host, state transfer, costs and
fresh evaluation tapes. Post-hoc overrides of a STRUCTURED checkpoint may be
reported only as diagnostics and cannot satisfy a result-map branch. If either
independently trained simple rule matches the learned structured policy, the

## 8. Direct endpoints and inference

Every statistic is first reduced to one value per replicate block; primitive
ticks are never treated as independent samples.

`STRUCTURED-FULL`, NEVER, FLEX and the two independently trained simple-rule
contrasts use the 20-second event window from exogenous degradation onset on
all tapes. `FORK-REAL` minus `FORK-SHAM` uses only the frozen 10-second window
after the first-trigger clone. These populations and windows are never pooled.

1. `MEAN-SERVICE`: mean primitive-time valid-service fraction, higher better.
2. `TAIL-SERVICE`: CVaR at the worst `10%` of episode-level valid-service
   fractions, higher better.
3. `SERVICE-DEFICIT`: integral of `1-valid_service` in service-seconds, lower
   better.
4. `RECOVERY-DELAY`: zero when no invalid-service tick occurs in the relevant
   window; otherwise elapsed time from the first invalid tick to the first
   subsequent `1.0 s` consecutive valid-service run. Nonrecovery receives the
   full window cap and remains in the block mean.
5. `COST`: total two-UAV propulsion energy, protocol bytes, invalid commits,
   token-gap/dual-owner ticks, command jump and minimum separation.

One predeclared blockwise randomization max-statistic family simultaneously
covers every endpoint, contrast, physical regime, external-`k` schedule and
standby-advantage stratum invoked by the ordered map for superiority,
noninferiority and no-materiality. Randomized arm-to-substream assignment and
the first-trigger fork supply the assignment law. All intervals are 95% family-
wise bounds. An at-least-one-endpoint branch may fire only from these
simultaneous bounds; simple-rule noninferiority uses the same endpoint-specific
margins with contrast direction reversed where needed.

Material direct-value margins are:

- `+0.03` absolute MEAN-SERVICE;
- `+0.05` absolute TAIL-SERVICE;
- `-0.50` service-seconds SERVICE-DEFICIT; or
- `-1.0 s` RECOVERY-DELAY.

For `FORK-REAL` minus `FORK-SHAM`, the 10-second-window margins are `+0.03`
mean, `+0.05` tail, `-0.25` service-seconds deficit or `-0.5 s` recovery delay.

A value route requires at least one margin and simultaneous noninferiority of
the others: no worse than `-0.01` mean, `-0.02` tail, `+0.25` service-seconds
deficit or `+0.5 s` recovery delay. Every direct-value contrast is reported by
regime x schedule x standby-advantage stratum. A positive route requires a
material benefit in POSITIVE and simultaneous noninferiority on every direct
endpoint in NEAR-ZERO and NEGATIVE. No pooled contrast overrides a stratum.
Propulsion energy must be no more than `3%`
worse; protocol bytes are reported separately; token gaps, dual payload,
buffer clear, command-slew breach and separation breach must be zero. These are
non-harm constraints, not a safety or certification claim.

## 1. Coordinates and time

- Primitive step `dt=0.1 s`; episode ticks are `t=0,...,1199`.
- World coordinates are metres. Ridge-Base is fixed at `(-600,0,20)`.
- Both UAVs have fixed altitude `z=90`. Their horizontal state follows

  `p[t+1]=p[t]+dt*v[t]`

  `v[t+1]=clip_norm(v[t]+dt*(a[t]+w[t]),18)`

  with action `clip_norm(a,3)`. A command is zero-order held until its ordinary
  `k` renewal. The common wind follows

  `w[t+1]=clip_component(0.95*w[t]+0.05*eta[t],-1.5,1.5)`,

  where each component of `eta` is standard normal from the sealed WIND stream.
- A physical collision/separation breach is any tick with horizontal UAV
  distance below `15 m`.

## 2. Terrain, visibility and radio

The terrain height in metres is

`H(x,y)=135*exp(-(x/75)^2-(y/220)^4)`

`       +55*exp(-((x-90)/35)^2-((y+40)/85)^2)`.

For endpoints `q0,q1`, the registered ray samples exactly `j=1,...,127` at
`q(j)=q0+(j/128)*(q1-q0)`. A camera ray is clear only when every sampled ray
height exceeds `H(q_x,q_y)+5 m`. Camera range must also be at most `500 m`.

When clear, a UAV receives target position plus independent zero-mean normal
noise with `2 m` standard deviation per horizontal component. When blocked it
receives a missingness bit and no target position. No synthetic interpolation
is inserted by the host.

For a radio hop of three-dimensional length `d`, let `blocked=1` if any sampled
ray height is below `H+8 m`, else `0`. Its margin in dB is

`M=30-20*log10(max(d,1)/100)-35*blocked+eps`,

where `eps` is independent normal noise with `1 dB` standard deviation from the
sealed RADIO stream. A hop is service-valid at `M>=6 dB`.

## 3. Ground-responder route and degradation onset

Each tape draws speed `v_g` uniformly from `{4,6,8} m/s`, turn magnitude
uniformly from `{25,35,45}` degrees and turn sign uniformly from `{-1,+1}`.
Degradation onset `tau_d` is uniform on `{42,54,66} s`.

The responder begins at `(-v_g*tau_d,-120,0)` and moves east so it reaches
`(0,-120,0)` exactly at `tau_d`:

- for `t<=tau_d`, `g(t)=(-v_g*tau_d+v_g*t,-120,0)`;
- for `t>tau_d`, `g(t)=(v_g*(t-tau_d)*cos(theta),
  -120+v_g*(t-tau_d)*sin(theta),0)`.

The target route, `tau_d`, turn, wind, sensor noise, radio noise and every `k`
choice are generated before any policy action.

## 4. Regimes

The same terrain and route are used in both regimes.

### `TARGET-VISUAL-OCCLUSION`

From `tau_d-1 s` through `tau_d+3 s`, the incumbent camera additionally has a
mapped-spur mask exactly when its line segment to the target intersects the
vertical prism `x in [-20,30]`, `y in [-155,-85]`, `z in [0,120]`. The shadow
camera uses the ordinary terrain ray only. Radio physics is unchanged.

### `TERRAIN-RELAY-LOS`

Camera physics is ordinary for both UAVs. From `tau_d-1 s` through
`tau_d+3 s`, the incumbent-to-base radio hop additionally receives the same
`35 dB` terrain-block penalty when its ray intersects the vertical prism
`x in [-30,45]`, `y in [-80,80]`, `z in [0,130]`. The shadow radio uses the
ordinary terrain ray only.

The extra masks are deterministic functions of geometry and time, are visible
only through ordinary missingness/margin observations, and never expose a
regime label to a policy.

## 5. Initial state and identity balance

At `t=0`, let `g0` be the responder position. Draw `u_x` uniformly from
`{-80,-40,40,80}` and `u_y` from `{-180,-120,120,180}`. The two UAV candidate
positions are

`q_A=(g0_x+u_x,g0_y+u_y,90)` and

`q_B=(g0_x-u_x,g0_y-u_y,90)`.

Both start at zero horizontal velocity and equal normalized battery `1.0`.
The incumbent label, vehicle name, reflection `y -> -y`, and assignment of
`q_A/q_B` are crossed exactly and balanced within each replicate. Sensor and
radio-noise laws are identical before geometry acts.

## 6. External-k law

Training and evaluation both use fresh tapes from the same sealed schedule law;
only fixed `k=8` is held out from training.

- Fixed schedules are `k=4`, `k=12`, and evaluation-only `k=8`.
- Switched schedules are `4->12` and `12->4`.
- For a switched schedule, switch time `tau_k` is independently uniform on
  `{36,48,60,72} s` and is crossed as the Cartesian product with every
  `tau_d`. It is never computed from `tau_d`.
- Initial renewal phase is independently uniform on `{0,...,k_initial-1}` and
  balanced. Relative `tau_k-tau_d` is analysis metadata only.
- A command already in progress finishes. New `k` applies at the next renewal;
  no state/buffer reset occurs and any old-epoch readiness certificate expires.

## 7. Standby-advantage strata

Before learned-arm activity, every candidate sealed tape is evaluated with a
future-blind scripted pair from the identical state at the first common
certificate-eligible renewal after `tau_d`:

- `FORCED-SAFE-TRANSFER` atomically transfers the owner token and then applies
  the same proportional geometry controller for `5 s`;
- `SCRIPTED-RETAIN` keeps the incumbent owner and applies that same controller.

Neither controller reads future noise, evaluator labels or learned outputs.
Let `A` be forced-transfer minus retain valid-service fraction in the fixed
five-second window.

- `POSITIVE` iff `A>=0.10`;
- `NEAR-ZERO` iff `abs(A)<=0.02`;
- `NEGATIVE` iff `A<=-0.10`.

Intermediate tapes are outside the registered population. The generator uses
prospective rejection sampling from the STRATUM stream until each
regime/schedule/replicate contains exactly 16 tapes in each stratum. The
selection is complete before learned weights, actions or outcomes exist. All 48
accepted tapes remain in the panel. The stratum is evaluator-only.

## 8. Random streams

All draws use a counter-based generator keyed by the UTF-8 tuple

`DISH/RBHR/R02/<purpose>/<replicate>/<regime>/<schedule>/<tape>/<tick>/<field>`.

`purpose` is exactly one of `INIT`, `TARGET`, `WIND`, `CAMERA`, `RADIO`,
`K-SCHEDULE`, `TRAIN`, `EVAL`, `STRATUM`, `ARM`, or `SHAM-FORK`. The master is
future fresh material created only after a later activity authorization. A
purpose key is never reused across training, evaluation, arms or the first-
trigger fork. Numeric seeds, masters and coordinates do not exist at the
definition stage.

## 9. Forbidden generator changes

No CM may tune the height field, route, masks, onset/switch sets, advantage
thresholds, rejection law, noise, initial geometry, service thresholds or RNG
keys for competence, support, headroom, result balance, cost or runtime. A
change to any of them is a new science revision requiring same-direction EM
authorship and Pro closure before activity.
