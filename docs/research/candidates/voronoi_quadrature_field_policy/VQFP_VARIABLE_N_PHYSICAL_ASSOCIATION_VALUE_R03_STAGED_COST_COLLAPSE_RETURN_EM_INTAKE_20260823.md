## Decision

The same-direction EM accepts the exact Stage-R01 technical return as the
predeclared **branch 2** outcome and recommends **no current r03 construction
under the unchanged total ceiling**.

This is a cost-and-opportunity disposition, not a scientific failure. Exact
r03 remains Pro-closed and retains its direct variable-`N` decision value: a
single roster-independent treatment would still test held-out roster sizes,
matched conditional-residual comparators and full reassociation under the
unchanged claim ceiling. Stage-R01 produced no frozen candidate score,
question-relevant output, efficacy evidence, result or partial value, so it
does not weaken or strengthen that scientific claim.

The stage did answer the investment discriminator. All six selected
non-frozen native slices conformed, result-blind measurements are finite, and
the principal slow-path, scaling, reducer and canonical-I/O uncertainties are
now measured or conservatively bounded. The revised cumulative low case fits,
but managed engineering is `23.75` days, `3.75` days over the unchanged
`20`-day ceiling. The cumulative high case is `37.75` days, `3514.003` CPU
core-hours, `154.503` wall-hours, `48 GiB` RSS, `200 GiB` scratch and
`38.001 GiB` durable storage. Every non-GPU high quantity exceeds its
unchanged ceiling. There is therefore no evidence-supported path from the
accepted stage to full construction inside the selected envelope.

Per the frozen return law, branch 2 makes no further construction eligible,
does not authorize a repeat Stage-R01 and does not silently enlarge any
engineering or resource ceiling. Portfolio should consume the temporary
enabling-construction allocation and record r03 as scientifically retained but
with no current construction.

## Revisit condition

R03 may be reconsidered only on one of these complete new facts:

1. a genuinely new production-grade exact-arithmetic, reducer, parallel or
   canonical-I/O capability, accompanied by independent result-blind evidence
   sufficient to establish that the **unchanged cumulative high case** is
   pointwise inside the existing `20 d | 1800 CPUh | 72 h | 32 GiB RSS |
   120 GiB scratch | 24 GiB durable | GPU none` ceiling; or
2. a separately authorized new cost or scientific object with a complete new
   total-cost boundary. Any science-bearing revision must be frozen by the
   same-direction EM and returned to the existing ChatGPT Pro conversation for
   a new `CLOSED` ruling before production selection.

An optimistic low or central estimate, reuse of the present benchmark, a
repeat Stage-R01, unpriced engineering, or a silent ceiling expansion is not a
revisit fact.
