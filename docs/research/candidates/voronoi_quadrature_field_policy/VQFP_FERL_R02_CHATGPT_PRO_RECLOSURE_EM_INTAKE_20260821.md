180-statistic vector undefined and conflicts with the intended event-
nonanswerability disposition.

noninferential sentinel when `M=0`, set `event_sufficient=false`, and forbid the
sentinel from every interpreted D90 predicate.

### 2. Event answerability occurs after D90-dependent competence
