Conclusion

A proof-sized exact association-value certificate exists in principle, but the proposed 6/12/18-row ladder is not yet an exact surviving object.

There is no structural impossibility from conditional FREE containment, MASS, half-cycle reassociation, or largest-remainder projection. In fact, a single rational row in G can make the treatment attain the global integer oracle while clearing every named 1/500 comparison. That one-row witness refutes both “MASS must absorb treatment” and “integer projection must erase the association” as universal theorems.

It does not establish the retained target, because the retained target also requires all six fields, training at N={4,8}, unchanged evaluation at N={6,12}, and one globally selected coefficient tuple. The smallest target-preserving finite object is therefore the six-row rung per roster, meaning 24 geometry–field rows across the four rosters, assuming each of the six registered fields appears once per roster.

The controlling defect is that the proposed ladder remains a schema: it does not specify the nine coefficient values, exact site vectors, row aggregation, deterministic coefficient tie law, or the quantifier on the nonalias witnesses. Consequently, neither survival nor a finite-ladder null follows from the supplied evidence.

There is also one exact fork:

If the 1/500 treatment-versus-control requirement is rowwise or minimized over fields, the construction is impossible because the uniform field makes treatment exactly equal to MASS for every treatment coefficient.

If it is an equal-weight six-field mean at each held-out N, no such obstruction exists. The first rung remains viable in principle, but its efficacy is presently unproved.

The repository’s analytic result establishes exact measure, legality, universal treatment-in-FREE containment, reassociation semantics, and a globally exact diminishing-marginal oracle, while expressly declining any efficacy or held-out-N conclusion. The historical cost-collapse stage likewise produced no question-relevant result and left association value, held-out-N value, FREE noninferiority, and reassociation deletion unknown.
