observations before PAUSE:

1. **The population law is not uniquely inherited.** The source target card fixes the marginal
   uniforms of `v`, `y` and `phi` but does not fix their copula or state mutual independence. Both
   the product density and

   ```text
   f_theta(u_v,u_y,u_phi) = 1 + theta(1-2u_y)(1-2u_phi),  0 < |theta| < 1
   ```

   have the registered uniform marginals and positive full support, while
   `E_theta[u_y u_phi] = 1/4 + theta/36`. Choosing the product law is therefore a new population
   completion, not a law uniquely derived from the antecedent task authority.

2. **Cross-fitting removes the old direct plug-in reuse defect but changes the target.** Conditional
   on a selected action, the disjoint evaluation fold is unbiased for that finite-sample selector's
   value. Fold swapping and symmetrization therefore estimate a two-tape selector functional
   `D_2`, not in general the protected oracle contrast

   ```text
   D_star = 0.5*sum_q max_A mu_q(A) - max_A 0.5*sum_q mu_q(A).
   ```

3. **The strongest counterexample is a common-optimum false positive.** The complete Innovator
   response supplies a bounded three-action, two-tape construction in which `a0` is the unique
   optimum under both latent graphs and the best common action, hence oracle `Q_star=D_star=0`.
   With tape type `R` having probability `0.2`, tape type `C` probability `0.8`, and the other 15
   actions fixed at `0.5`, its exact action values are

   ```text
             q0: a0   a1   a2       q1: a0   a1   a2
   R tape        0  0.9  1.0           0.3  0.9  0.6
   C tape      0.8  0.3  0.1           1.0  0.0  0.6
   true mean  0.64 0.42 0.28          0.86 0.18 0.60
   ```

   `Q=192/625=0.3072`, `D=153/2500=0.0612` and
