## Formal structural object

### Three support layers

1. **Registered ideal support**

   \[
   \Omega_{\mathrm{ideal}}=\{-1,+1\}^{364\times3},
   \qquad |\Omega_{\mathrm{ideal}}|=2^{1092}.
   \]

   The three signs at each tick multiply `.003`, `.002`, and `.004`. Under the registered iid
   fair-bit law, every complete word has mass `2^-1092`. One word is shared across all six
   graph/action counterfactual cells.

2. **Native-consumable tape**

   A 1092-bit word whose magnitudes, length, coupling, and tick order are accepted by the exact
   native input contract and independently replay to unique terminal endpoints under the frozen
   actor/runtime. This is an execution-semantics property, not a probability or HMAC property.

3. **256-bit HMAC image**

   A tape for which an exact 256-bit master is exhibited and the committed addressed HMAC map
   reproduces all 1092 signs. The image contains at most `2^256` words and cannot equal the
   `2^1092` ideal hypercube. A theorem over only this image does not prove registered ideal-support
   positivity. An ideal-support/native-consumable adverse witness need not have a HMAC preimage;
   if HMAC reachability is claimed, the preimage is an additional checkable obligation.

### Endpoint and integer gaps

For tape `b`, graph `g`, and first action `a`, define the exact terminal numerator

```text
N(g,a;b) = 364-dock_tick   on safe dock
           0               on failure or timeout.
```

Thus `U(g,a;b)=N(g,a;b)/364`. A safe dock at tick 364 also has zero utility. Define

```text
K_A_RH = N(HR,A_HR)-N(HR,A_RH)
K_A_HR = N(RH,A_RH)-N(RH,A_HR)
K_COMMON = N(RH,A_RH)+N(HR,A_HR)-N(RH,COMMON)-N(HR,COMMON).
```

The frozen gaps are `G_j=K_j/728`. The complete-support strict-positivity theorem is

\[
\forall b\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ideal}}:\quad
K_{A\_RH}(b)\ge1\;\land\;K_{A\_HR}(b)\ge1\;\land\;K_{COMMON}(b)\ge1.
\]

The adverse-tape complement is

\[
\exists b\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ideal}}:\quad
K_{A\_RH}(b)\le0\;\lor\;K_{A\_HR}(b)\le0\;\lor\;K_{COMMON}(b)\le0.
\]

The theorem and counterexample form an exhaustive finite dichotomy only after the exact actor,
native numerical semantics, tie rules, support/coupling, and termination order are fixed and total.
