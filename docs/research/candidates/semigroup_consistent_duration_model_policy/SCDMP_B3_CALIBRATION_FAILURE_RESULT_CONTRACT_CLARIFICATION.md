## Exact clarification

After all 24 arm-seed update-zero calibration cells have been attempted and
retained under the frozen rule, a failure in one or more cells is itself
question-relevant scientific output. CM shall install a technically accepted
atomic calibration-failure packet at the requested result path and return it
to the named same-direction EM as the complete discriminator terminal for the
calibration-nonidentification branch.

That packet has `question_relevant_output_exists=true`. It contains:

- the exact revision, frozen configuration and activity record;
- the complete 24 attempted calibration cells, including each available raw
  endpoint and auxiliary norm, coefficient or absence thereof, finite-value
  checks and frozen validity predicates;
- the fresh-coordinate and unchanged-initial-state identity needed for
  technical acceptance;
- every failing cell and the exact registered predicate it failed; and
- material anomalies and retained artifact locators.

No training, checkpoint or downstream support/audit/scored panel is produced
after this branch fires. Their absence is required by the treatment and does
not make this calibration-failure packet partial. No repair, recalibration,
reseed, replacement batch, coefficient clamp/substitution, training,
evaluation, adverse inference, null/equivalence inference, automatic retry or
other interpretation branch is authorized.

If and only if all 24 cells are valid, the training path is entered. On that
path, question-bearing output requires the complete atomic package of all 24
final checkpoints and every registered downstream panel. A partial trained
package remains nonselectable.
