## Three support notions that must not be conflated

### Registered scientific support

One assay tape contains three signed disturbance components for each of 364 ticks. Under the
prospectively registered ideal-PRF/iid-fair-bit abstraction, a tape is therefore a vector in

```text
S_ideal = {-1,+1}^{3*364} = {-1,+1}^{1092}.
```

Every member of this finite set has probability `2^-1092`. This is the full support used by the
frozen distribution-free scientific claim.

### Native-admissible support

`panel.py` validates every component only by exact membership in its two registered magnitudes and
then supplies a complete tape to the native host. Thus the typed tape validator admits every sign
vector in `S_ideal`. This is an admissibility fact. It does not by itself make an arbitrary vector
reachable through the guarded production RNG/runner.

### Production HMAC image

Production maps one 256-bit master through addressed HMAC calls. For any fixed tape index,
the image of all possible masters has cardinality at most `2^256`, strictly smaller than the
`2^1092` ideal tape space. The frozen science explicitly treats HMAC as an ideal PRF abstraction;
it does not prove information-theoretic equality between the HMAC image and `S_ideal`.

Consequently:

- an `S_ideal` tape accepted by the native transition contract is enough to refute a theorem stated
  over the registered full scientific support;
- that tape is not a production-HMAC reachability witness unless an exact 256-bit master, tape
  index and address recomputation produce it; and
- a theorem over `S_ideal` is stronger than a theorem over the HMAC image, whereas an adverse
  HMAC-preimage witness refutes both.

Any new object must choose and name one support notion prospectively. Silent switching between
them would change the scientific meaning.

## Exact grid lemma and logical compatibility with `.3`

The endpoint utility is zero for failure/timeout and `1-dock_tick/364` for a safe dock, with integer
`dock_tick` in `[1,364]`. Every cell utility is therefore on the `/364` grid. Each frozen gap is
one half of one integer-grid contrast or one half of the sum of two such contrasts, so all three
gaps lie on the `/728` grid.

It follows that a genuine theorem

```text
for every tape b in S_ideal and every frozen gap j: G_j(b) > 0
```

would sharpen automatically to

```text
min_{b,j} G_j(b) >= 1/728.
```

Such a theorem would imply positive population gaps for every probability law carried by that
support. This would be a conclusion of the new proof object, not an inference from the 562 observed
contrasts, not a retroactive `.3` pass, and not adapter or SCDMP-reentry authority.

### Static mismatch lemma and four-lane reduction

The native source supplies one nontrivial result-blind partial invariant. In `HR` with mismatch
`A_RH`, and symmetrically in `RH` with mismatch `A_HR`, one cable has `a=2`, positive graph load
coefficient `b_i=1`, and no compensating share `r_i=0`. During the forced first hold,

```text
tau_i >= 0.38 + 0.12*2 + 0.16*2 = 0.94
z_i(t+1) >= 0.84*z_i(t) + 0.06.
```

Starting at `z_i(0)=0`, the real-arithmetic lower bound after seven primitive ticks is

```text
z_i(7) >= 0.06 * sum_{r=0}^{6} 0.84^r
       = 0.264341... > 0.25.
```

The native cable-overload predicate therefore fires within the 13-tick forced hold, independent of
the tape; the lane cannot reach `x=24.5` in seven ticks under the registered velocity cap. The
strict numerical margin is large, but a formal object must still bind this lemma to exact IEEE-754
evaluation with a directed-rounding or bit-replay check.

Subject to that exact numerical check, both mismatched utilities are zero for every tape. The first
two frozen gap integers can therefore never be negative; they are zero exactly when the
corresponding matched lane also has zero utility. A strictly negative structural counterexample can
occur only through the balanced COMMON gap. Complete pointwise positivity reduces from six outcome
lanes to the following four-lane obligations for every tape:

```text
U(HR,A_HR) > 0
U(RH,A_RH) > 0
U(HR,A_HR) + U(RH,A_RH)
  > U(HR,COMMON) + U(RH,COMMON).
```

This is a useful proof/search reduction, not the missing theorem: matched and COMMON trajectories
still depend on the exact `.3` actor, all tape-driven state branches and terminal semantics.

The valid `.3` nonpass is compatible with both structural branches:

- If all support gaps are positive but commonly smaller than the frozen KL/IUT critical resolution,
  a valid finite panel can be all-positive and still nonpass, exactly as observed.
- If at least one rare support tape has a zero or negative gap, 562 sampled tapes can miss it; that
  witness alone would refute pointwise positivity but would not establish a nonpositive population
  mean.

The current evidence therefore does not select the theorem or counterexample branch.
