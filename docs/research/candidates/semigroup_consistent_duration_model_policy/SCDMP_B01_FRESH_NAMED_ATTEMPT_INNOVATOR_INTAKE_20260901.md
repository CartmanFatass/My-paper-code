## Theoretical and methodological support

An exploratory B family is a distribution-and-comparison-level object whose named runs are
reproducible executions. One realized stochastic coordinate becomes constitutive only within the
attempt to which it was committed. Preserving that coordinate across a retry prevents redraw and
selection. It does not turn an invalid, permanently unread attempt into a family-wide fixed
realization or import C-style consumption semantics.

The containing integrity rule is therefore two-sided:

1. within one named run and evidence attempt, root, master, `q`, stochastic coordinates, scientific
   manifest and atomic frontier cannot change; and
2. after invalidity, a fresh outcome-blind named attempt may receive a new root/master/`q` only by
   preserving the registered distribution and every scientific comparison factor, permanently
   quarantining the prior attempt, and forbidding result-aware selection or pooling.

This separates reproducibility from stochastic-realization fixation while preserving the strongest
legal anti-redraw null.
