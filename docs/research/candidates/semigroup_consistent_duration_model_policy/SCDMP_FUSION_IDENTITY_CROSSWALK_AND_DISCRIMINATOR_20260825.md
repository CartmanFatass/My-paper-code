## Frozen fusion discriminator

The two object-local results named by `DIRECTION.md` cannot be conjoined by
role name. The narrow source-grounded discriminator for any later fusion object
is:

```text
I_native = one prospective task-local authority binds the task law, roster,
           event/order coordinate, observation, action catalogue, external-k
           registry, foundation, opportunity estimand, controls, endpoints,
           thresholds, first-true map, workload, and fresh stochastic identity;

C_native = that same object's complete order-erased foundation competence gate
           passes before any order-aware parameter exists;

O_native = with that exact competent foundation frozen, that same object's
           complete direct foundation-conditioned order-opportunity gate passes;

FUSION_PREREQUISITES_ESTABLISHED = I_native and C_native and O_native.
```

This discriminator is sequential and fail-closed:

1. invalid or incomplete required evidence yields no fusion conclusion;
2. a valid `C_native` nonpass terminates before opportunity or adapter work;
3. a valid `O_native` nonpass terminates before adapter work;
4. only `I_native and C_native and O_native` can make a later adapter
   implementation scientifically eligible; and
5. adapter value, non-harm, retention, decline, or nonidentification still
   requires its own prospective controls and complete final inference family.

Under the current frozen evidence, `I_native` is absent: opportunity and
foundation are explicitly in separate task-bound objects. The `OA` opportunity
role cannot make `O_native` pass for `FF`, and the `FF` foundation role cannot
make `C_native` pass for `OA`. Consequently the conjunction is not established.

This artifact freezes the discriminator shape, not a new task, architecture,
numeric gate transplant, or treatment. Those choices would be new science and
require a new meaning-complete direction-science authority before source work.
