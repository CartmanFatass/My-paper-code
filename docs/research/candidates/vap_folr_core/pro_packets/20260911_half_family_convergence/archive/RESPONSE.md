# Pause the exact fixed-half recipe; retain generic RETAIN

**Pause the fixed 0.5 public-event survivor-state attenuation versus RETAIN family on the present easy Traffic Junction / 5,000-training-episode / 128-final-episode recipe.** Retain ordinary generic RETAIN as the usable baseline. Select neither a third unchanged pair nor another coefficient or redesigned mechanism in this decision. The reason is limited marginal value of further work on this particular recipe after its initial useful observation and an actual independent recurrence comparison with a substantial matched loss—not proof that attenuation is generally harmful or that two training instances settle its performance distribution. [Scientific brief, §§1–4][brief]; [HALF-B01 intake, §5][i1]; [HALF-B02 intake, §8][i2].

This is a reversible, direction-local pause, not permanent retirement of survivor-memory research, a true RECAST, a C-class result, or a Portfolio lifecycle or priority change. I narrowly prefer it to the strongest alternative, one more independently trained unchanged pair. The available evidence permits that alternative as ordinary B work; I judge its present decision value insufficient to select it now. Repeatability remains unresolved by choice, rather than being declared resolved by the pause. No numerical or implementation allocation follows. [Fixed task, requested decision and scope][task]; [empirical specification, §§5.2,6.1,7,11.8–11.9][spec].

## What the state intervention did—and what the results do not identify

The comparison has a real multi-agent action and reward path. Actual native departures and activations, including removal and refill of the same slot within a step, determine physical-trip ownership. A true survivor is `C = A_before & A_after & ~D & ~B`; the completed public event is `E = any(D | B)`. HALF supplies `0.5 * h_in` for a true survivor at that event boundary, before the GRU processes the next input. RETAIN carries the incoming survivor state. Common fresh entrant, inactive-slot and episode handling takes precedence, and legal survivor previous-action features remain common. A reused slot is a new trip, not a resumed owner. [HALF-B01 card, §2][c1]; [HALF-B02 card, §2][c2].

Both actors have the same permitted native local-attention information, public event bit and own-birth cue; neither receives a new privileged future or other-agent state. This is equality of the information interface, not identity of their realized observation histories. Once actions differ, traffic, membership and global NumPy consumption can differ. Shared-parameter teammates also co-adapt. The same arm-specific state law is used in acting and both online and target recurrent replay during training. Terminal controller passes are computational positions, not subsequent native control opportunities, and an individual departure does not terminate the cooperative episode. [HALF-B02 card, §§2–3][c2].

The accepted summaries record 48,079 training and 1,350 final HALF attenuations in B01, and 46,657 training and 1,409 final attenuations in B02. HALF made no full survivor resets; RETAIN made neither survivor attenuations nor full survivor resets. These counters establish that the intervention was applied, not that a particular memory component changed or retained a measurable amount of information. The counter definition includes an eligible application even when the incoming vector happens to be zero. Thus an entirely untriggered law does not explain the records, while effective memory-content changes remain unmeasured. [HALF-B01 summary, `arms_results`][s1]; [HALF-B02 summary, `arms_results`][s2]; [HALF-B01 card, §6][c1].

The host and learner are substantive common parts of the tested package: easy five-slot/five-action, vision-1, 7×7, twenty-tick Traffic Junction with add rate 0.1 and native reward/side effects; entity-attention GRU64/FlexQMixer, double Q, 32-complete-episode replay samples without replacement, RMSprop learning rate 0.0005, updates from episode 32, target copies every 200 episodes, and the accepted exploration schedule. Training uses gamma 0.99; the final observable is undiscounted native episode return. Equal interaction and optimizer counts do not imply equal gradients, parameter movement, sampled data or optimization success. [HALF-B01 card, §§2–3][c1]; [HALF-B02 card, §§2–4][c2].

The live explanations include retained useful context, representation and optimization differences, action-dependent data, joint attenuation and partner co-adaptation, together with conditional evaluation variation. None has been isolated. A scalar multiplier does not select stale teammate information, preserve half the useful information, or interpolate the returns of independently trained RETAIN and full-EVENT policies. The nonlinear recurrent updates between events also prevent reading repeated attenuation as a fixed fraction of a common unchanging memory. Ownership of a hidden-state record does not make its informational ancestry strictly self-only. [Prior Convergence response, “Why the changed question is worth one observation”][prior]; [accepted Convergence intake, §§2–3][prior-intake]; [FOUNDATIONS, §§2–4][foundations].

The empirical unit matters equally. The 128 final rows per arm are conditional observations of fitted policies, not independent training instances or paired traffic worlds merely because their indices and seed labels match. Recorded conditional standard deviations are approximately 5.6003/6.8335 for B01 RETAIN/HALF and 9.89218/6.96587 for B02. They do not estimate training-population variance or a population contrast interval. Two matched pairs leave substantial uncertainty about recurrence and expected performance. I do not turn their signs into a seed-success probability. [HALF-B01 intake, §5][i1]; [HALF-B02 intake, §8][i2]; [04_EMPIRICAL, randomness hierarchy and comparison targets][empirical].

These foundational distinctions change the judgment's scope: they rule out a causal or population verdict but do not dictate continued spending. The retained CAMA recurrent-history and Sable acting/training-boundary grounding supports a competent generic comparator and consistent reconstruction, not a prediction of which multiplier wins. No new literature-dependent performance or corpus-coverage claim is needed here. [HALF-B01 intake, §2][i1]; [HALF-B02 intake, §3][i2]; [accepted Convergence intake, §3][prior-intake]; [FOUNDATIONS, §6][foundations].

## Why pause rather than repeat or change another constant

A third unchanged pair would be legitimate and genuinely informative. It would add another training-level observation under the same rule, rather than merely reduce rollout noise for existing checkpoints. A further above-MEI HALF point would strengthen the case for keeping this rule under active consideration; another adverse point would strengthen the contrary evidence; a within-MEI point would leave practical separation unresolved in that fit. None would automatically settle the population or justify a fixed majority vote. The existing positive result is sufficient to motivate such a B proposal without prior significance, positive replication or a located defect. [Empirical specification, §§5.2,11.8.2–11.8.4][spec].

That is the strongest case against my selection, especially because the implementation already exists and the latest native pair took about twenty-five minutes within its measured native scope. I do not dismiss replication as unoriginal, require a new mechanism before another run, or claim that discussion is cheaper than learning. The outstanding recurrence uncertainty is real and could justify a different investment judgment. [Current exposure/cost record, `additional_pair_unallocated`][cost]; [scientific brief, §§4–5][brief].

I nevertheless select the pause. The prior decision purchased a concrete intermediate-law observation rather than another full-erasure result. That observation was obtained, and its positive point has already motivated an actual independent repetition. The repetition produced a substantial matched native loss against an attained, usable generic baseline. On the present question, the additional value of refining this exact multiplier's mixed recurrence record does not, in my judgment, outweigh leaving it inactive and retaining ordinary RETAIN. This is a limited choice under uncertainty, not a statement that the expected HALF effect is negative or that replication has become scientifically inadmissible. [Prior response, selection and outcome meaning][prior]; [HALF-B01 intake, §§5–6][i1]; [HALF-B02 intake, §§8–9][i2].

The distinction from the previous selection is important. Previously, no trained nonzero intermediate event law had been observed. Now this particular rule has been observed twice, with opposing above-MEI results. That changes the marginal question being purchased; it neither retroactively invalidates the earlier decision nor makes its positive outcome disappear. The pause rests on the present value of further exact-recipe work, not on defending the earlier recommendation or recovering a desired sign.

I also do not select a different multiplier, a new host, selective attenuation, or a state-partition redesign merely because B02 was adverse. Such changes can be valid outcome-informed B work, but no particular replacement is justified here as preferable to the usable baseline and pause. Choosing another constant automatically would risk a sequence of substitutions without a distinct decision purpose. This is not a demand for proven mechanism attribution before making a change; it is a refusal to manufacture a change in a task that asks whether further work is worthwhile. No true mechanism recast or specification exception is necessary. [Scientific brief, §4][brief]; [empirical specification, §§11.3,11.8.2,11.9][spec].
