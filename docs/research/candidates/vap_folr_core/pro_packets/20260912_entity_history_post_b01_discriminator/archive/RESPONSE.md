## 2. Why this use is worth considering

The practical choice is **whether to keep the already trained BANK controller as an optional executable reference in development on this exact public-information host**, alongside an actual learned Generic alternative, or to use Generic alone for that purpose. Reference retention here means inclusion in the development candidate set, not deleting or retaining evidence archives. It is not a deployment-readiness judgment or a conclusion about which training algorithm is generally better.

The accepted BANK artifact makes that choice concrete, while Generic's missing endpoint leaves it unresolved. A new equally informed Generic fit supplies a meaningful alternative; fresh evaluation of both policies avoids treating BANK's already observed sample mean as a population constant. Keeping the BANK weights fixed also avoids changing the subject of the question in response to its −4.32609375 result. This is a prospective use of known evidence, not a claim that the evidence selected itself without regard to outcomes. [FACTS, retention and scientific_reading][facts]; [E intake, strongest support and contrary evidence][intake].

**The strongest opposition is conservation.** BANK has not demonstrated comparative value, the twenty-tick host may offer little useful additional history, and current local features plus adaptive Generic recurrence may already suffice. N3 B04 previously obtained the same 0.98828125 mean final return from typed and generic learners, with a typed-minus-generic AUC increment of 0.0026041667 inside its 0.05 MEI. That is serious contrary evidence against assuming typed organization pays. It is not a score for this host or an upper bound on BANK. A new Generic training history is nontrivial work, and this conditional comparison will still leave BANK's training variability unresolved. Declining further spending on this particular reference-use question would therefore be scientifically defensible. [DIRECTION, Accepted N3 B04 science and entity-history scope][direction]; [E intake, strongest alternative][intake].

I nevertheless prefer one such comparison because it supplies the strongest directly grounded legal alternative at an actual native action endpoint, without retraining the available reference or substituting a deliberately weakened null. This is not continuation because money has already been spent, because a missing comparison must always be repaired, or because a positive result is required before stopping. The marginal question is whether this retained controller has useful comparative standing for a specific development use. My preference is qualitative; no expected gain, success probability or measured information-per-second ranking is available.

A new two-fresh-fit BANK/Generic study is the strongest alternative empirical design. It would answer a different, broader exploratory question about a newly generated pair of training outcomes and permit declared matching of compatible initialization tensors. I do not select it now because the chosen use is conditional on the existing BANK controller. Avoiding a new BANK fit relinquishes fresh BANK-learning evidence and matched-pair interpretation; it does not claim that those would be worthless. Conversely, a retained-BANK-only ablation or predictability statistic would not establish its standing against competent Generic and is not substituted for this comparison. [TASK, retained versus fresh unit and proportional burden][task]; [evidence specification §§5.2,11.8–11.9][spec].
