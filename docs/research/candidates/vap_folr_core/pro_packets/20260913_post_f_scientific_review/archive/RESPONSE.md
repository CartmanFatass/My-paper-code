The proposal's option name “C” does not make it a class-C study: its adequate class is ordinary **B / EXPLORE**. No exact maximum, full support census, theorem, positive pilot or C-level confirmatory protocol is needed to ask it. [Intake, C: coherent continuation scope][intake]; [specification, §§5.2,11.4,11.8–11.9][spec].

The first complete block would provide two fresh fits and one new within-block package contrast. The important gain over F is a new BANK learning realization rather than another observation of its old fixed weights; it also supplies a new Generic comparison under the same planned exposure. That makes it a direct investigation of the still-unresolved finite-learning question, not merely administrative completion of E. An unchanged comparison has that purpose without a new architecture or customer. Re-evaluating old policies more often could improve conditional precision but would not supply this information. [Intake, C and minimum one-block discussion][intake]; [prior response, §§2–3][prior]; [empirical note, randomness hierarchy][empirical].

The proposed common seed scheme must be interpreted through actual matching. Compatible same-role tensors and RNG consumers can be matched where the accepted construction supports that; differently shaped BANK and Generic actors cannot have identical complete initial parameters. Neither equal labels nor contemporaneous execution implies matched traffic trajectories after actions diverge. Each arm needs its own fresh optimizer, replay, recurrent state and full episode histories, with one final checkpoint and all 128 prescribed final outcomes. No old E prefix or F checkpoint becomes fresh training evidence. These are already material protections in the proposal, not an extra reviewer-designed experiment. [Intake, C: coherent continuation scope and engineering interface paragraph][intake].

A practical fresh BANK gain would weaken a broad pessimistic inference from the particular old BANK and provide a new bounded reason to consider the learning package. It would **not reverse F's conclusion about its old reference**, prove expected BANK superiority, or identify memory causality. A fresh Generic advantage would add contrary evidence from a different learning realization; an inside-MEI point would retain its sign without equivalence. Missing or damaged endpoints would limit their dependent contrast, not turn into an adverse BANK result. Two completed blocks could describe some between-block variation, but would still not establish a stable population ordering or settle other contexts. This alternative would not by itself distinguish BANK training variability from Generic training variability as the cause of a changed contrast, or isolate memory organization from width/readout/optimization. [Prior response, §§2–3,6][prior]; [E0, complete Generic result][e0]; [intake, C and cost anchors][intake].

Thus the final preference is **informed judgment under uncertainty, not a consequence uniquely entailed by F and not an established methodological error**.
