It supports considering Z for development within this design, not concluding that persistence is generally harmful, Z is memoryless, or Z outperforms unaugmented Generic. The missed prediction and all adverse outcomes remain material. [Result evidence, Rule applied verbatim and Deviations][e0]; [PAIR_READBACK, primary and exposure][pair].

**I recommend preparing the proposed fresh Z–G comparison rather than requiring another A–Z or A–G block first.** It supplies a presently missing, action-relevant fact: whether the current-only augmented program offers a practical native-return increment over the actual adaptive Generic alternative. ## 3. The intervention is real, but its interpretation is narrower than “memory hurts”

The inspected source implements the intended contrast. On every primitive step it first applies observer/subject continuation to entity state. For Z it then replaces that state with `torch.zeros_like` **before** the GRU call and before the visible-versus-unseen selection. Visible Z entries receive the current call's output; unseen entries select zero rather than an old learned value. Inactive pairs are masked to zero. This avoids the concrete failure mode of zeroing only the GRU argument while accidentally carrying old hidden state through unseen steps. A instead carries its lawful continuing entity history. [model.py, AugmentedActor.forward, entity-state update][model].

Both arms keep the Generic64 recurrence, its own-lifetime cuts, and the same visibility/seen/age and public lifecycle metadata. Unseen current physical/action values are sanitized before learned encoders; entity features enter the final Q head rather than Generic's recurrent input. Z therefore removes persistence from one learned stream, not all history from the policy. Bookkeeping and Generic recurrence remain historical information paths, including metadata-only tokens for previously seen but currently unseen subjects. “Current-only” must continue to qualify the **entity hidden-state input**, not the complete actor. [Model, masking, metadata and Generic update][model]; [card, Programs and legal information][card].

Equal module names, initial weights and external constructor RNG make this a more targeted design intervention than old replacement BANK-versus-Generic. They do not hold the learned Generic subpolicy, trajectories or optimization fixed. With zero incoming entity state, the hidden-to-hidden weight matrix receives no gradient through a nonzero prior-state input; current-input weights, gates and biases can still learn. Removing recurrence also removes that stream's temporal credit path. Those are consequences of the declared intervention, not an accidental defect or a reason to invent dummy parameters. **The observed difference belongs to the complete training-and-execution state law, including its optimization and policy-induced data consequences; it does not isolate the value of remembered physical information.** [Card, initialization and temporal-capacity limits][card]; [model, zero-state branch][model]; [technical review, recurrent and gradient findings][technical].

Two reporting refinements matter. Describe 192,741 as the common **registered parameter count**, not proof of equal effectively trained capacity. Describe Z as **temporally simplified**, not a smaller network, a faster implementation or a wholly nonrecurrent agent. The current card already qualifies temporal capacity, and the E0 reports both timings; these are safeguards for downstream shorthand rather than findings that invalidate the result. An explanation based on easier optimization remains plausible, not empirically diagnosed by the count or one return contrast. [E0, bounded meaning and timing table][e0]; [intake, surviving alternatives][intake].

The same-information comparator protection is substantive. It does not establish that the idealized public announcements are available in an unchanged original-CAMA or deployed system, nor that the supplied information is sufficient for optimal decisions. The foundation distinction between information, representation and successful finite learning is precisely why an unfavorable persistence instance cannot close the broader history question. ## 5. Why the proposed fresh Z–G comparison is the better next question for this decision

The DM's stated choice is whether the temporally simplified augmented program deserves development **beyond the available Generic alternative**, not whether every persistence effect is understood. A new Z–G block directly addresses that choice. It is not justified by assuming Z must beat G because Z beat A. It is justified because that comparison is missing, its outcomes have different development consequences, and both candidate and legal baseline have concrete executed learning paths. [Intake, Concrete next question and known work][intake].

The strongest opposition is worth more than a generic warning about few seeds. Z was prioritized after one favorable internal contrast; the observed difference could depend substantially on this complete realization. Choosing it over A risks following a noisy local winner when A, unlike Z, already has one direct positive comparison against G. A fresh A–G could test repeatability of that known program increment, while a fresh A–Z could test repeatability of the internal preference. Neither objective is obsolete. I nevertheless prefer Z–G for the explicitly posed **simplified-candidate value** decision: repeating A–Z still leaves its relation to G unknown, and repeating A–G does not test Z at all. This is an outcome-informed exploratory prioritization, not a finding that Z is the superior learning method or that the alternatives have low expected value.
