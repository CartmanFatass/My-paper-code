## 3. One concrete bank, not an architecture menu

I narrow the proposed treatment to a **shared 16-coordinate GRU cell per observer–subject entry**, with zero initial state, followed by one observer-local attention readout and a feedforward Q head. Sixteen is the single untuned reference width already offered for sizing, now used to make this candidate concrete; it is not a tuned optimum, width search or frozen experiment allocation. The bank replaces the pooled recurrent stage in this reference treatment; it does not secretly add a second pooled GRU on top. The comparator retains its ordinary 64-coordinate pooled GRU. This is therefore a whole memory-organization comparison, not a capacity-matched component ablation. [Question, §2][question]; [FACTS, proposed_sizing_only][facts].

Let m(i,j,t) denote **observer i's** recurrent summary of its own observations of physical subject j. The bank includes the observer's self entry. Parameters are shared across entries, agents and time; state is not. Let c(i,t) and c(j,t) be continuation of the observer and subject lifetimes across the preceding transition. Let v(i,j,t) mean that both trips are active and j is visible in i's own local mask. The selected state law is:

`m_bar(i,j,t) = c(i,t) c(j,t) m(i,j,t−1)`;

`m(i,j,t) = GRU16(phi(y(i,j,t)), m_bar(i,j,t))` when `v(i,j,t)` is true, and `m_bar(i,j,t)` otherwise.

Here y contains only current permitted subject features and its explicit label, and phi is a shared pointwise embedding before any multi-subject pooling. No other bank entry or already aggregated partner representation enters this cell update. The equation states the proposed operation; no cell has been constructed or executed in this consultation. [Question, observer-owned bank and lifetime rules][question]; [current pointwise embedding and aggregation order][model].

This law has specific consequences:

| Boundary or observation | Selected bank behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Continuing observer sees a continuing subject | Update that entry from the newly visible local features and its own previous entry. |
| Subject becomes unseen but continues | Carry the entry unchanged; do not advance it with fresh hidden features, a fabricated zero-observation transition, or a predicted physical trajectory. |
| Subject reappears in the same trip | Resume updating the same observer-owned entry. |
| Public subject end or replacement | Clear that subject entry and its seen/age bookkeeping for every continuing observer before any new-trip observation is ingested. |
| Observer birth, replacement or episode reset | Clear its entire bank and bookkeeping; current visible observations may then establish fresh entries. Inactive observer rows remain zero. |

At first visible observation the entry starts from zero; never-seen subjects have no learned historical token. Maintain an observer-local seen flag and elapsed primitive steps since its last sighting, resetting them at the same lifetime cuts. Age is a record of the observer's own information staleness, not a measurement of unseen motion. A replacement can start a fresh visible cell in the same controller call without inheriting its predecessor. Temporal state derivatives stop at both relevant lifetime cuts; carrying an unseen continuing entry does not gratuitously detach its legitimate same-lifetime history. Shared parameters may still learn across different trips. [Question, §2][question]; [environment.py, physical continuation][environment].

For action selection, form a subject token from its memory, label, local visibility/seen/age indicators and **current visible features through a direct current-feature path**, zeroing that path while unseen. Current features are not forced to pass exclusively through the 16-coordinate bottleneck. An active observer uses one query based on its own current input/self entry to attend only to its own live, previously seen subject entries. The readout may use a carried unseen entry, explicitly marked as stale, but cannot read another observer's bank. Public facts about never-seen or departed slots remain accessible through the common metadata input, not invented historical tokens. Use conventional learned attention projections and a feedforward 64-coordinate-to-five-Q readout; there is no recurrent communication sweep across actors. These readout limits complete the selected candidate rather than commission an implementation. [Question, bank/readout proposal][question]; [source attention and Q-head reference][attention][model].

Clearing a subject-indexed entry is not a theorem of erasing every causal trace of that subject from the system. Other trips' legal observations may have been affected by earlier interactions, and shared learned parameters reflect training across trips. The claim is ownership of recurrent records and their update inputs, not strictly-self ancestry or complete causal forgetting. This distinction also prevents relabelling public replacement metadata as proof of useful-memory selection. [DIRECTION, accepted public-lifecycle scope][direction]; [FOUNDATIONS, §§3–4][foundations].
