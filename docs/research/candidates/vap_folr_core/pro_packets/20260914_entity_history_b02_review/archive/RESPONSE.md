## 2. Interpretation: the current limits are necessary and largely already correct

**Equal permitted information is a meaningful comparison protection, not equal capacity or sufficient state.** The unchanged host is five-slot H20 easy Traffic Junction, vision 1, with native actions, reward and transitions. Both actors receive the complete public activity/birth/departure/initial-occupancy table and their own permitted physical, visibility, seen and age information. BANK's observer–subject state updates visible data, carries only that observer's permitted past while unseen and cuts at either lifetime boundary. Generic keeps adaptive own-trip recurrence without forced peer-event amnesia. Neither receives another actor's hidden state or unseen physical values. The ideal public announcements remain an explicit task assumption, not original-CAMA or deployment evidence. [Card, Protected mechanism and legal comparator][card].

Compatible constructor matching does not make unlike-shaped actors identical. Separately resetting the same seed also does not create independent arm draws or matched post-action traffic worlds. The card preserves the shared environment/replay NumPy stream, so policy-dependent data and draw consumption can differ. The reported per-policy SEs must remain conditional; neither pairing nor training-level precision can be inferred from the labels. A fresh block is useful evidence about realized learning outcomes, but one block cannot estimate the distribution of their differences across training. [Card, Fixed exposure and RNG][card]; [Summary, uncertainty][summary]; [Empirical note, 随机性有层级][empirical].

The parameter-count, recurrence and readout differences are legitimate parts of this **whole-package** comparison. They do not isolate memory organization, capacity, useful-history content or lifetime cuts as causes. Both learners retain complete-episode online/target reconstruction, double Q, training-only FlexQMixer and the specified optimizer/discount. Their actual movement and updates do not prove optimal learning. No capacity-matching arm or causal ablation is needed for the stated B claim; omitting them relinquishes attribution, not information or reward integrity. [Card, Protected mechanism][card]; [E0, Analysis limits][e0]; [Specification §§4,11.8.4–11.8.7][spec].

Generic's negative mean and 73 negative episodes cannot be presented as demonstrated good service. Equally, **not demonstrating competence is not proving incompetence**: no absolute competence criterion is established here, and zero return is not declared such a threshold. Negative-return counts are not collision counts or failure probabilities. The current intake appropriately claims only the better observed package. H20/common cues might reduce incremental history value, but no horizon, information-channel or memory intervention identifies that explanation. [Intake, Observation and prediction][intake]; [E0, Analysis limits][e0]; [Foundations §§2,4][foundations].

The reported improving curves are changing-policy training-return summaries, not repeated greedy-policy evaluations. They establish neither convergence nor inevitable further improvement, and cannot predict a ranking after more training. Keeping budget dependence unresolved is correct; inferring that longer training is necessary, sufficient or likely to rescue BANK would not be. [E0, Analysis and interpretation limits][e0]; [Empirical note, 先分清在比较什么][empirical].

## 4. The next-information judgment and its strongest opposition

The DM's argument has a sound empirical starting point: the first missing contrast is now observed, its practical result favors Generic, F's separate reference-use outcome remains adverse, and another complete block entails real work. The intake expressly acknowledges a second block as legitimate and potentially informative, rather than treating a negative first sign, expired permission, missing headroom or unchanged architecture as a prohibition. **I identify no concrete methodological defect in that qualified preference.** [Intake, Changed scientific value assessment and alternatives][intake].

My strongest challenge is to any implication that **“first comparison completed” itself proves sharply diminishing information value**. One block leaves training variation unidentified. Depending on how a new gain, near-tie or further loss would change development advice, another block could still have substantial decision value. The absence of a current reason to prefer the observed BANK controller is not identical to absence of a reason to investigate BANK learning. The current intake largely respects this distinction; its lower-value assessment remains a preference under uncertainty, not an estimated probability of future failure or expected information value. No quantified value-of-information calculation is required to express that preference. [Intake, P rationale][intake]; [Specification §§11.8.2–11.8.3,11.9][spec].

The strongest direct alternative remains **one new, unscreened same-host fresh BANK16/Generic64 block**, with the unchanged information, learners and 5,000-training/128-final endpoints. Its question is whether another training realization materially strengthens or weakens the case for developing the BANK package. It requires two new fits, 205,120 native ticks, 9,938 updates and 256 final episodes—not more evaluation of the existing checkpoints. Ordinary **B/EXPLORE** is adequate; a second block is neither a seed quota nor sufficient for stable superiority. [Intake, option B][intake]; [Card, exposure][card]; [Specification §§5.2,11.8.3][spec].

A practical new BANK gain would demonstrate heterogeneity in observed package outcomes and could justify renewed bounded development interest; it would not reverse B02 or restore F's rejected historical reference. Another Generic advantage would strengthen the finite adverse evidence without proving universal harm. An inside-MEI result would retain its sign without equivalence; missing endpoints would limit only their dependent contrast. This possible information is the reason to reconsider the block, not a requirement to run it. Any outcome-informed decision to acquire or omit it should remain visible. [Card, result narrative][card]; [Specification §§5.2,11.8.2–11.8.7][spec].

Longer learning and a more history-demanding host are coherent but different questions about exposure dependence and context, respectively. The improving training curves and short horizon leave them open; neither identifies an obligatory repair. The listed record does not support ranking those alternatives by expected gain or demanding them before another same-host B. A positive pilot, novel mechanism, external customer, exact maximum or exhaustive diagnosis would not resolve the current replication question more directly. I select none of these alternatives and impose no new execution requirement. [Intake, option L][intake]; [Specification §11.9][spec].
