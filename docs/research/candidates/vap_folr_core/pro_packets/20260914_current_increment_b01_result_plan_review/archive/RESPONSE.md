**The strongest case for one block is substantial.** It provides a new complete A–G contrast at half the acquisition work. Alongside the old A–G result it could already show agreement, a near tie or reversal; two new blocks are not required merely to observe heterogeneity. **I nevertheless narrowly prefer the two-block preparation here.** The important gain is a second fresh comparative realization in the same outcome-independently bounded study, rather than deciding again from only the next point after a sequence of comparator changes.
