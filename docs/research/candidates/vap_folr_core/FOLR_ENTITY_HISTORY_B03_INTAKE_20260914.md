## A concrete scientific alternative to another identical block

Reading the actual accepted Actor.forward reveals a material structural asymmetry.
Generic applies its own-trip GRU64 **after** fusing current legal observations and
public/local metadata. It can recurrently process temporally changing relations
across subjects. BANK instead updates each observer-subject GRU16 from that subject's
visible embedded physical/action features; it fuses the resulting memories only in
the current readout. The BANK branch replaces the generic fused recurrence rather
than adding entity history to it. The direct current-feature bypass preserves current
information, but does not make these temporal representations equivalent. Actor
parameter counts also differ. This is a source fact and a plausible explanation
space, not a newly found integrity bug or a diagnosed cause of the measured loss.

The surviving hypothesis is: explicit observer-subject history may be useful as an
additional structured state while a learned generic recurrent policy remains
available. DM currently favors one bounded comparison targeting that hypothesis
over a third unchanged replacement BANK16 block. The proposed pair would retain
the same Generic64 current-attention/metadata/own-trip recurrence in **both** arms,
and add identically parameterized subject-bank and fusion readout modules to both.
Treatment carries lawful observer-subject GRU16 state with the existing lifetime
cuts/visible-only updates. Control sets that branch's hidden input to zero at every
step while evaluating the same cell and readout; its common Generic64 recurrence
continues normally. Both retain the same legal public/observer-local input channels,
current bypass and metadata. Their Q readout would use the generic recurrent features
and bank features in the same declared fusion architecture.

This proposed comparator is not memory-free: generic recurrent history remains in
both arms, as do truthful seen/age metadata. It asks whether additional persistent
entity-indexed state improves finite learning relative to a current-only bank branch
on top of that common generic policy. Parameterization could be identical, but
functional temporal capacity intentionally differs. A positive contrast would be a
local performance effect of this declared training program, not proof of a unique
physical-membership mechanism or population superiority. It would not establish
that either augmented policy beats unaugmented Generic; the historical B03 score
cannot be used as a fresh comparator. That narrower claim is intentional, and review
should challenge whether omitting a contemporaneous unaugmented arm sacrifices
information necessary for this specific first question.

A possible bounded design is2fresh fits, each5000training episodes/100000ticks/
4969updates then128final greedy episodes/2560ticks, on the same H20 host and native
reward. Primary would be persistent-minus-current-only final mean, MEI1. No seed is
yet selected or screened, and no frozen successor card exists. Both constructions,
RNG consumers, initial fusion, lawful reset/visibility behavior and complete exposure
would be made explicit before implementation/launch. This proposal changes the
model, not old results, and does not revive the stopped scalar LEARNED_EVENT family.
Whether the change is best treated as within-family redesign or recast belongs in
the later Portfolio direction report; naming it does not reset history or cost.

## Strongest alternatives and why the recommendation is tentative

The strongest reason to stop this path is substantive: repeated adverse exact-package
results, a short H20 host, reobserved current cues, public lifecycle signals and the
now-demonstrated ability of at least one generic fit to obtain better returns may
leave little practical role for an extra entity memory. Adding a branch can make
optimization harder while the generic policy already processes the same legal
history. The proposed control may reveal no useful gain and cannot guarantee that
this host contains enough action-relevant hidden history. Complexity is a real cost,
not evidence of novelty or a reason to protect the proposal.

Longer learning of unchanged BANK16/Generic64 is also a legitimate direct B option.
For example10000episodes/9969updates/128final per arm would require405120native ticks
and19938updates for two fits. The latest curves do not prove it unhelpful, but another
budget answers exposure sensitivity while preserving the structural asymmetry just
identified. A third unchanged5000-episode block costs roughly the scale of B03 and
would add realization information, but is currently less likely to resolve the
remaining design question than the proposed controlled addition. These are value
judgments under uncertainty, not probabilities inferred from two signs.

A history-demanding native host or longer horizon could be informative without a
positive pilot or mandatory causal diagnostic. It would change task demand and some
credit/learning conditions, so it answers a different question. DM does not require
an exhaustive opportunity census, exact optimum or mechanistic proof before any
such B. The present proposal first holds host and legal comparator information fixed
while retaining generic recurrence. Independent review may identify a better
proportionate alternative or a concrete defect, and DM will respond substantively.
