  those changes is the proposed mechanism or a differentiated prediction for its value.
  The paper establishes neither that this context is harmful nor that a private branch is
  empty. This is a scientific-design choice left unselected here, not a demand for an exact
  ancestry proof, positive pilot, baseline tuning or finished adapter before future B work.
- **Redirect to handover-state sources.** DISH P67 already returns no-ready while preserving
  the distinct COPY/SHADOW/own-history question and P62's narrow decisions. Its persistent
