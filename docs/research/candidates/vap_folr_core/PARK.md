## Why the direction is parked

The direction asks whether organizing a continuing agent's legally available
entity history under physical membership change improves finite-learning native
coordination over generic recurrence. The latest completed object answered a
narrower practical question: whether to keep a particular historical BANK policy
as an optional executable development reference. That use is now resolved in
favor of Generic-only.

DM considered a first complete fresh/fresh learning comparison on the same H20,
common-lifecycle-information host. It would provide genuinely new information,
but DM judged its present marginal value modest relative to the necessary new
learning work. The adverse available BANK reference and a serious equally informed
Generic comparator inform that judgment. Short horizon and common cues are only
plausible background explanations, not identified causes or proof that memory is
unnecessary. No quantitative probability of a future BANK advantage or value of
information was estimated.

PARK is therefore a reversible research-value judgment, **not** empirical futility,
family closure, an exhausted-permission finding, a reviewer veto, or a hard elapsed-
time rule. The old 6900 s fresh-block envelope and 120 s cleanup allowance were
planning inputs, not unavoidable costs or owner-only renewal gates. No generic
600 s stop or investigation-time threshold supplies a reason to keep FOLR parked.
The underlying entity-history family remains scientifically OPEN; the direction
does not occupy a slot while parked. No replacement or cross-direction disposition
is selected here. See the [recorded P decision](FOLR_RETAINED_REFERENCE_USE_B01_INTAKE_20260913.md#dm-lifecycle-decision--2026-09-13-full-direction-delegation)
and [runtime-policy reconsideration](pro_packets/20260913_post_f_scientific_review/INTAKE.md#owner-resume-and-runtime-policy-readback--2026-09-13).

and strict `< -1` for Generic-only/BANK-worse.

The reusable lesson is to distinguish an executable-reference choice from an
algorithm-learning comparison. Equal legal information makes the named use
comparison meaningful; it does not match model capacity or training history and
does not turn a fixed-policy evaluation panel into independent training evidence.
A recurrent Generic policy can adapt its actions with fixed parameters; this
observation does not establish that its state is sufficient or that it has reached
an absolute task-competence threshold.

  histories remain explicit. The 128 episodes per policy describe conditional
  execution variation, not 128 independent fits. Generic's 62 negative episodes

  and the need for persistent history are not separated. Other observation
  interfaces, lifetimes, longer recovery sequences and membership patterns remain
  untested, not ruled out.

  within its MEI and equal mean final learned returns on a different replacement

  universal negative. The narrower post-B03 LEARNED_EVENT/5000/128 hold and older
  recast history remain unchanged. See [B04](DIRECTION.md#accepted-n3-b04-science--2026-09-04)

## Scientifically meaningful conditions for reconsideration

The following are alternative reasons to revisit the value judgment, **not a
cumulative checklist, automatic launch trigger or present authorization**:

1. **An action-changing fresh/fresh learning question.** A bounded, clearly defined
   comparison could decide whether the BANK learning package merits further
   development against the equally informed Generic package. It must distinguish
   independent training variation from more episodes of an old policy, retain all
   outcomes/failures and state the limited inference. The unchanged host is eligible;
   no architecture novelty, favorable pilot or proven mechanism is required.
2. **A concrete information/lifetime contrast.** A specified recovery sequence,
   observation interface or membership pattern could make preservation of an
   observer's entity history meaningfully different from generic recurrence.
   State the decision it could change and maintain a fair legal-information
   comparison. Changed task semantics define a new object and cannot be used to
   rewrite F or to claim that its original BANK reference was beneficial.
3. **A material new evidence or work-cost fact.** A trustworthy new learning result,
   a concrete primary-integrity correction, or a credible simpler measurement of
   the same unresolved question could change its expected usefulness relative to
   the required work. Unknown cost, elapsed-time multiples and larger arbitrary
   allowances alone are not evidence of either scientific value or futility.

An explicit owner request to reconsider can also reopen the discussion without
manufacturing a favorable result. Exact headroom, an external customer, complete
mechanism diagnosis and a positive seed are not universal prerequisites. Any
future proposal must identify what remains unresolved and how a result would
change the next action; repeating the already-resolved fixed-reference use adds
no such information by itself.
