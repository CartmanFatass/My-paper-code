## Direct observation

The proposed endpoint arithmetic is conditionally correct. With service
measured after movement, 24 sectors at speed three reaches the new dock at
post-event offset seven, giving `tau=7` and `U=7/32`; 48 sectors reaches at
offset fifteen, giving `tau=15` and `U=15/32`. Carry serves at offset zero,
giving `(tau,U)=(0,0)`. Balanced expansion and contraction preserve exact dock
demand, so the fixed three-tick and 0.03 numerical margins, recovery-censor
headroom, and service-support headroom pass algebraically.

The scientific opportunity proposition does not pass. The make-before-break
event leaves the old target assignment feasible, exact-demand, zero-travel,
and pointwise service-optimal. Both the frozen `PUBLIC-MIN-SWITCH` rule and an
ordinary public current-position `NEAREST-FEASIBLE` exact-demand rule reproduce
carry. `CANONICAL-REPLAN` is worse only because its roster-size convention
forces every target 24 sectors away. `OPPOSITE-BREAK` doubles the stipulated
transit and therefore proves endpoint sensitivity, not that churn creates a
need for plan persistence.

The counterexample route also tested a smaller host in which churn genuinely
requires four local six-sector repairs. A maximum-overlap carry repair has
small nonzero recovery and service loss, but a matched public min-travel replan
selects the identical repair, leaving a zero continuity gap. Adding public
target-progress or setup state does not separate the packages because a
matched-information controller can retain that state; erasing it makes reset,
not continuity, the causal intervention.

This yields the bounded containment lemma relevant here: when comparator and
carry share the public state and objective, and the flexible policy class
contains the carry policy, an endpoint-competent physical controller can select
carry. Strict physical-only superiority cannot be forced without comparator
weakness, unequal information, a forced reset/erasure intervention, or a
finite-budget optimization constraint. The latter remains outside this
physical-only cycle.

Several secondary specification defects reinforce the stop: the phase-to-
anonymous-assignment map, reflection/orientation law, phase byte under
minimum-switch, exact unmet-demand normalization, and cloned-state tuple are
not fully defined. Repairing those semantics would make the deterministic
calculation executable but would not repair the comparator mismatch.
