## Frozen scientific question

Can any finite roster-churn host give a public CARRY continuation strictly
better direct physical recovery or service nonharm than a competent public
REPLAN class that:

1. receives the same complete legal public event history and current physical
   state;
2. has the same future exogenous law, objective, action support, clock, and
   randomization;
3. is free of finite compute, optimization, or estimation limits; and
4. strictly contains the complete CARRY continuation policy, including its
   public maximum-overlap/minimum-travel repair when churn makes literal old
   claims infeasible?

First derive the containment theorem or identify its precise failed
assumption. Only a failure that preserves public information, matched objective
and actions, true functional containment, and competent endpoint selection may
open the finite counterexample family. If containment holds, the cycle is
decision-complete without CM or learning.

## Public state, policy, and causal objects

At an event boundary, the public history `h` contains the unordered current
physical roster; public survivor/newcomer/departure status; all current
positions and resources; public targets, demands, and service geometry; the
pre-event public plan and assignment; every earlier public roster and plan
event; deterministic public tie laws; the complete future exogenous dynamics
law; and the observation, action, and service clocks. Ephemeral public ordering
may break a current tie but cannot encode a persistent identity or private
fact. There is no hidden plan, private setup state, undisclosed departure, or
arm-specific history.

A continuation policy maps public histories after `h` to distributions over
legal joint physical actions. The treatment `pi_CARRY` first retains every
still-legal public claim and, if exact post-event feasibility requires a
change, uses one prospective public repair: maximize retained survivor claims,
then minimize total physical travel, then use the public lexicographic tie law.
The repair and every subsequent action are part of `pi_CARRY`; CARRY is not a
command to preserve an infeasible assignment.

The comparator class `Pi_REPLAN` contains every legal public-history
continuation allowed to CARRY plus additional reassignments. Functional strict
containment means `pi_CARRY in Pi_REPLAN` for every legal history and at least
one other replan policy induces a different legal joint action distribution on
some public history. REPLAN is evaluated as a class or by a competent selector
that minimizes the same registered physical objective over that class. A
fixed convention that must change a target, forgets history, discards the old
plan, uses a shorter horizon, or cannot instantiate the CARRY repair is not the
containing comparator.

All policies share the same cloned event state, transition kernel, action set,
horizon, service law, and potential exogenous/random draws. There is no
training, gradient, parameter budget, inference latency, or search cost in this
physical-only object.

## Endpoints, objective equivalence, and estimand

For a finite post-event horizon `H`, normalized unmet public demand after the
physical transition at offset `t` is `u_t in [0,1]`. Recovery time is the first
offset beginning two consecutive zero-unmet-demand ticks, censored at `H` if
none exists:

```text
tau = min {t in {0,...,H-2}: u_t = u_(t+1) = 0}, else H.
U = (1/H) * sum_(t=0)^(H-1) u_t.
```

Lower `tau` and `U` are better. The primary physical objective is the
lexicographic loss `(tau,U)`; the complete Pareto-minimal endpoint set is also
reported so a scalar tie choice cannot manufacture a conclusion. For churn
path `p`, define

```text
Delta_tau(p) = tau_REPLAN^*(p) - tau_CARRY(p)
Delta_U(p)   = U_REPLAN^*(p)   - U_CARRY(p),
```

where `REPLAN^*` ranges over the exact endpoint-optimal comparator set. A
positive value favors CARRY. The treatment-bound proposition requires, in
both one-agent expansion and one-agent contraction,

```text
Delta_tau >= 1 physical tick
Delta_U   >= 1/64,
```

with CARRY headroom `H - tau_CARRY >= 2` and `1 - U_CARRY >= 1/32` in both
directions. These are fresh prospective margins. They do not reuse a prior
host's thresholds or endpoint realizations.

The theorem-level estimand is stronger and margin-free: whether the REPLAN
attainable endpoint set can include the exact CARRY endpoint for every public
history. If it can, strict direct physical superiority of CARRY over the class
is impossible regardless of the numerical margins.

## Frozen containment conjecture

For every public cloned event history `h`, let `L(pi;h)` be the registered
expected physical loss under the common transition and exposure law. If
`pi_CARRY in Pi_REPLAN`, then

```text
inf_(pi in Pi_REPLAN) L(pi;h) <= L(pi_CARRY;h).
```

For the vector endpoint formulation, the attainable REPLAN endpoint set
contains the CARRY endpoint. Consequently a competent REPLAN can select CARRY
exactly, or select an endpoint that is better under the shared objective.
CARRY cannot have direct physical recovery/nonharm value over the containing
class. Equality remains possible. A realized finite learner or fixed replan
algorithm may underperform CARRY, but that is not a physical class-containment
effect.

The proof obligation includes randomized policies, public common randomness,
joint multi-agent actions, path-dependent public history, churn-forced repair,
and both event directions. It must distinguish existential class containment
from the behavior of one arbitrary selector.

## Prospective failure conditions

A theorem failure is potentially in scope only if it survives all matched
objects above. Candidate boundaries to audit are:

1. CARRY's repair is not actually a legal member of REPLAN;
2. CARRY receives plan/history/setup information absent from REPLAN;
3. the policies face different actions, dynamics, clocks, randomization,
   horizon, or public future law;
4. the comparator is forced to change, erase, refresh, canonicalize, or use a
   weaker/myopic objective;
5. a decentralized REPLAN lacks common randomness or joint action capability
   available to CARRY;
6. equilibrium or tie selection changes the objective or feasible joint-policy
   set;
7. finite compute, optimization, data, parameterization, learning exposure, or
   selector error prevents discovery of the contained policy; or
8. a private or partially observed physical commitment is causal.

Items 1--6 defeat the stated containment or matching premise unless a genuine
same-class mathematical exception is shown. Items 7--8 are legitimate future
objects but are explicit non-goals here. Naming a boundary is not a
counterexample.

## Strongest nulls and alternatives

- **Containment null:** direct class value is impossible because REPLAN can
  instantiate CARRY exactly.
- **Selector artifact:** a named replan rule underperforms even though the
  containing class does not; this identifies selector competence, not plan
  persistence.
- **Information artifact:** the old plan or setup state is unavailable to
  REPLAN; this is unequal information or partial observability.
- **Optimization regularization:** a restricted learner finds CARRY more often
  under finite budget; this may motivate a separate generic regularization
  object but is not physical-only value.
- **Objective/commitment change:** a penalty for breaking commitments or a
  time-inconsistent objective can favor persistence by definition; it changes
  the shared objective.
- **APFI/DISH axes:** event-order identification and controller handover can
  interact with persistence but remain distinct directions unless a future
  prospective interaction is explicitly frozen.
