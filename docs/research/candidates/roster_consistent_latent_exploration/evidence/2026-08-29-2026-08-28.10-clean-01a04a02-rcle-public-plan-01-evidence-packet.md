## Frozen question and gate order

On one new 80-sector, four-beacon rotating-service ring, can a public-state
common plan carried unchanged through held-out physical roster changes
`5->10` and `10->5` have direct recovery-time value with integrated-unserved-
demand nonharm over a competent learned controller whose policy class strictly
contains the keep-plan treatment?

The prospective result map required the following order.

1. The deliberately fragmented public assignment had to establish
   bidirectional assay sensitivity over coherent carry.
2. Public identity-free CARRY and REPLAN scaffolds had to control all four
   held-out cells and beat an independent-nearest policy.
3. A same-state CARRY-versus-REPLAN fork had to establish a physical
   assignment-hysteresis opportunity.
4. Only then could Stage B train `KEEP-COMMON` and `FLEX-EVENT`, establish FLEX
   competence, and compare direct recovery/nonharm.
5. Only after a direct KEEP win could an evaluation-only same-checkpoint FLEX
   clamp localize event-rekey application versus training geometry.

Failure at an earlier gate prospectively made every later stage unnecessary.
No branch permits post-result repair of the host, perturbation, roots,
thresholds, endpoint, comparator, budget, or checkpoint.

## Treatment, comparator, and claim ceiling

`KEEP-COMMON` draws one public-state four-dimensional plan at episode start and
carries it unchanged to survivors and newcomers. `FLEX-EVENT` shares the same
manager, actor backbone, tensor inventory, information, optimizer, data, and
pre-event law, but at a churn event may add common and agent-specific plan
updates. Zero event-update outputs exactly recover every KEEP policy, while
nonzero connected outputs yield policies outside KEEP. The comparison is
therefore strict policy-functional containment, not representational
necessity.

The maximum possible claim concerns only this finite toy, training rosters 6
and 9, held-out rosters 5 and 10, one event at tick 20, the exact 256-block
score-function law, sixteen roots, the registered simultaneous inference
family, and the exact finite-budget packages. It cannot establish separate
commonality or persistence effects, mediation by allocation fragmentation,
arbitrary roster transfer, optimizer-independent causation, UAV value,
safety, or deployment.

## Competing readings for Convergence

1. **Park active target-bound investment.** The cycle deliberately required a
   valid physical assay before paying for or interpreting learning. It failed
   in one churn direction, the public scaffold was also weak descriptively,
   and no learned evidence exists. Neither active RCLE investment nor a
   generic-regularization narrowing is supported by this object.
2. **Treat the outcome only as a host-design failure.** The expansion recovery
   endpoint was near its censor under coherent carry, so failure may reflect
   insufficient endpoint headroom or the exact two-claim perturbation rather
   than absence of continuity value. This preserves a genuinely new host
   question but cannot repair the observed cycle.
3. **Use the descriptive CARRY/REPLAN result as evidence against persistence.**
   REPLAN was faster and no worse in service loss on both churn paths. Against
   a strong conclusion, the prespecified map forbids downstream attribution
   after assay failure, and the scripted laws are not the learned treatments.
4. **Proceed directly to the learned comparison despite Stage A.** This would
   discard the prospectively registered protection against an insensitive or
   uncontrollable host and would change the estimand after observing the
   result. It is not an admissible interpretation.
5. **Narrow to generic finite-budget regularization.** Strict containment and
   event-head search geometry make that mechanism possible in principle, but
   Stage B never trained either arm. The present evidence contains no finite-
   budget ordering to narrow.
