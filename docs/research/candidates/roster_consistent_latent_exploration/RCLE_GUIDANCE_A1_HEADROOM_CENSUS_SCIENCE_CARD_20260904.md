## 1. Question, claim ceiling, and non-goals

On RCLE's current repository-mapped rotating-perimeter roster-churn host, do the accepted records
already contain both:

1. a stated numeric upper native-return reference on a declared population; and
2. a valid, tuned, competent generic learner baseline with the same actor-visible information,
   legal action support, native-return definition, evaluation population, and recorded work and
   model-selection exposure,

so that the unthresholded current-host quantity

```text

```

can be transcribed without constructing or running a learner?

The maximum claim is a repository-bound evidence-availability or raw-measurement fact. If both
members exist and match, this object may report their already-recorded difference. If either is
absent, it may name the exact deficit. Even a numeric gap would concern only the declared finite
host, population, information set, native metric, and recorded budget. It would not establish an
RCLE mechanism effect, learnability, package superiority, general roster value, transfer, UAV,
safety, flight, deployment, or a lifecycle consequence.

Non-goals: do not train, tune, select, or evaluate a new learner; materialize future TBCFV
coordinates; relaunch or repair PCPV, B1, B2, CPC, or TBCFV; substitute a scripted physical
