## Smallest meaning-complete no-learner A/RECON boundary

The smallest safe next card is a **local one-step legal-action headroom census**, not an oracle and
not a full-horizon policy comparison:

- one prospectively addressed deterministic native fixture for each `N={6,9,15,21}`;
- a 12-step baseline trajectory whose joint actions are drawn once from a prospectively addressed
  `UNIFORM_LEGAL` tape;
- immediately before each baseline step, snapshot the exact native state;
- for each entity, restore that same snapshot and execute each *other* legal focal action while
  every partner action remains the baseline joint action; do not continue an alternative branch;
- restore once more and execute the baseline joint action to advance the sole trajectory; and
- report raw one-step primitive and endpoint deltas. No learned parameter, optimizer, gradient,
  critic, MEI statistic, ACTIVE-set selection, or threshold is part of the object.

The direct work law removes the already observed baseline focal action from each entity's replay.
WEST and EAST surveyors each contribute two alternatives and RIDGE relays three. With equal role
blocks this is `12 * (7N/3)` alternative transitions plus 12 baseline transitions per roster:

| roster | alternative transitions | baseline transitions | total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 6 | 168 | 12 | 180 |
| 9 | 252 | 12 | 264 |
| 15 | 420 | 12 | 432 |
| 21 | 588 | 12 | 600 |
| **total** | **1,428** | **48** | **1,476** |

The future card should cap the invocation at ten wall-minutes, require a fresh 4 GiB admission,
and carry the exposure line `learner=none; optimizer_updates=0; parameter_displacement=N/A`.
It needs none of engineering-scope specification §4's default-prohibited machinery.

## Raw fields the card must retain

For every baseline step and focal alternative, retain enough direct state to reconstruct rather
than infer the gap:

- fixture identifier/root and source digest; roster, slot, entity, role, 22-field observation,
  legal mask, baseline joint action, baseline focal action, and tested focal action;
- pre-step snapshot digest and the native pre-step state quantities that drive legality and
  consequence: event times/time-to-expiry, FIFO occupancy/content, pending arrivals,
  previous-action, previous-success, and delivered state;
- post-step snapshot digest, terminal flag, `D_W`, `D_E`, radio actions, waste actions and fraction,
  timely deliveries, expired arrivals, duplicate arrivals, collision loss, empty-radio actions,
  and the recomputed endpoint `J` for baseline and focal action;
- signed per-action deltas for every primitive and `J`, then per `(fixture,N,slot,entity)` raw
  `max(J_action)-J_baseline`, `J_baseline-min(J_action)`, and
  `max(J_action)-min(J_action)`; and
- optional `1-J_action` labelled only `algebraic_ceiling_slack`, never feasible-policy regret or
  attainable headroom.

These are raw local causal spans under one fixed partner joint action. They do not identify a
globally optimal joint action, a feasible full-horizon upper policy, tuned-generic regret,
learner exposure, value after co-adaptation, or the cause of B01 nonactivation.

## Strongest alternative explanation

Even if a future local census finds wide immediate action spans, event timing, FIFO occupancy,
role legality, and fixed partner actions may explain them without implying useful credit or
trainable policy headroom. Conversely, a small local span would not rule out delayed multi-step
headroom. Generic Adam/K0 alignment and insufficient exposure therefore remain live explanations;
this read-only map supplies no polarity among them.
