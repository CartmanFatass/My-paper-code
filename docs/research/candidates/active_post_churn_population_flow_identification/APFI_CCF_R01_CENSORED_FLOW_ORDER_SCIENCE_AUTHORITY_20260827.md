## Frozen latent family and common prefix

The finite latent family is `(Z,B) in {0,1} x {0,1}`, with uniform support.
`Z` is a hidden source-flow mode and `B` is a balanced sham bit. Both are
sampled after a common law-independent prefix ending at `t=0`. The prefix has
identical roster, positions, energy, inspection age, legal masks, identifiers,
clock, actions, disturbances, and transaction outcomes in every cell.

No mode, scenario name, template label, seed, hidden queue value, or future
admission is policy-visible. `B` affects only a matched sham presentation and
is independent of the source flow.

## Hidden offers, censoring, and public history

There are four source slots `k=1..4` before the decision at `t=10`. At each
slot the source creates one hidden offer. A deterministic capacity/service gate
and a one-place staging queue either admit the offer or reject/hold it. The
learner sees only an admitted membership event (join or exit) and its public
time; rejected offers, queue occupancy, service attempts, and source causes
are censored. Censoring is performed before the policy interface and has no
arm-dependent marker.

The source is generated by a causal two-state automaton, not by a scenario
label. Its hidden mode chooses one of two balanced template pairs:

```text
Z=0: 0011 or 1100
Z=1: 0110 or 1001
```

The pair member is chosen by an independent fair source coin. Each template
contains two admitted joins and two censored/held offers. The gate emits the
four public join/exit times corresponding to the `1` positions and emits the
same compensating exit schedule in both modes. Thus every individual slot
event is Bernoulli-balanced across `Z`, and the total admitted-event count is
always two. The order language is the only intended mode information:

```text
L0 = {0011, 1100}
L1 = {0110, 1001}
```

The source automaton retains its hidden mode and queue phase after each slot;
the policy receives neither. A fifth hidden offer is generated and censored in
every world to prevent event count or end-of-stream timing from acting as a
mode label.

## Matched state and later opposite-action witness

After the fourth event, the environment performs a fixed, law-independent
state restoration. At `t=8`, all worlds have the same unordered active roster,
positions, energy, inspection ages, legal mask, queue-free staging state,
clock, and physical state. Histories differ only in the ordered public
membership-event record. At `t=9` the same roster slot exits in every world.

At `t=10` the sole remaining decision is `EXPAND` or `HOLD`. At the start of
`t=11`, the hidden source mode controls whether a prepared replacement can
enter the time-critical spur; no other future variable is policy-visible.
The frozen gross reward table is:

| hidden mode | `EXPAND` | `HOLD` | unique optimum |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `Z=0` | 14 | 6 | `EXPAND` |
| `Z=1` | 6 | 14 | `HOLD` |

The templates are balanced, so a mode-blind policy has gross value `10` and a
correct order-state policy has gross value `14`. These are analytic design
values, not results. The immediate response channel is absent in this draft;
an optional legal non-actuating response may be added only if it is proven
independent of `Z` and cannot reveal the ordered history.

## Policy-visible interface and controls

The policy receives unordered roster features, public clock, prior legal
actions, and the ordered record of public join/exit events and times. It does
not receive hidden offers, queue state, source mode, template identity,
future admissions, reward lookahead, or a scenario label. Every action owner
receives the same record before `t=10`.

The definition requires these exact controls under matched support and work:

1. `RESPONSE-ONLY`: no population history;
2. `SINGLE-EVENT`: the most recent event only, with no cumulative state;
3. `SIMPLE-COUNT`: total admitted-event count only (always two);
4. `ORDER-STATE`: the registered finite anchored state that updates from the
   full ordered event record;
5. `RNN-GENERIC`: unrestricted recurrence without the anchored block; and
6. `RNN-CONTAIN`: an exact-containing supernetwork with the `ORDER-STATE`
   block embedded and a generic block of at least equal dimension.

`RESPONSE-ONLY`, `SINGLE-EVENT`, and `SIMPLE-COUNT` are required negative
shortcuts. `RNN-GENERIC` and `RNN-CONTAIN` are policy-class upper bounds, not
proof that the anchored representation is necessary; if either matches the
anchored policy under the frozen finite budget, any APFI-specific
representation claim stops.

## Causal interventions and discriminator contract

The prospective audit must enumerate all four `(Z,B,template)` worlds and
apply paired interventions:

1. **History-order swap:** hold hidden `Z`, physical state at `t=8`, future
   slots, disturbances, and pre-`t=8` execution fixed; replace only the public
   ordered event record with the paired opposite-language record, then permit
   `t=10` to respond.
2. **Action clamp:** clamp the `t=10` action through termination and verify all
   non-message descendants match within each world.
3. **Censoring swap:** hold accepted events fixed while swapping hidden
   rejected/queued offers; no policy-visible or reward change may follow.
4. **Mode swap:** hold the complete common prefix and all one-event marginals
   fixed while swapping the causal template language; only the ordered-history
   pathway may change the optimal action.

The constructive discriminator is valid only if the full order record changes
the unique optimal action, while each single event, the count, and any legal
immediate response remain insufficient. A useful history witness alone is not
APFI-specific evidence; the exact-containing controls must be reported and a
finite-budget advantage must remain answer-changing after all shortcuts.

## Failure and stop boundary

The candidate is invalid or stops without a positive interpretation if any of
the following occurs:

- a pre-prefix mode, template, label, queue, or future variable is visible;
- a hidden rejected/queued offer changes policy-visible state or reward;
- event marginals, count, timing support, or common prefix are not exactly
  matched across modes;
- the restoration fails to match the complete `t=8` state;
- the response channel or any side channel carries information about `Z`;

  correct action on the declared support;
- the history-order swap changes a non-message descendant or fails to change
  the permitted action pathway;
- `RNN-GENERIC` or `RNN-CONTAIN` matches or exceeds `ORDER-STATE` under the
  frozen finite budget, removing anchored-representation value;
- the reward table, admission order, or opposite-action uniqueness is not
  deterministic and fully specified; or
- any implementation, experiment, provider, runtime, or result is presented
  as evidence for this definition.

efficacy or against all censored-flow policies. A null result, shortcut match,
