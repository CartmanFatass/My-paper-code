## Objective and value

Determine whether one natural dynamic-roster task contains two complete
pre-probe-information-identical situations with different persistent future
population-flow laws and different optimal legal actions, and whether one safe
matched probe reveals that hidden law. A pass can justify an active
information-acquisition/shared-flow-state algorithm family; a failure can
delete it before training. This fills the episode-internal dynamic-`N` gap and
is not a repair or evidence successor of RCLE, VNFC or another direction.

## Frozen finite registry

The EM may examine exactly these four operational scenario families and may
not add a fifth without a new Portfolio decision:

1. persistent wildfire or plume-front monitoring with exogenous UAV failure
   and replacement launch;
2. aerial communication-relay coverage with relay loss and replacement/arrival;
3. disaster search-and-rescue sector coverage with responder withdrawal and
   later dispatch/arrival; and
4. persistent infrastructure inspection with battery-driven exits and
   scheduled replacement availability.

The EM must instantiate at most one named, source-grounded host from this
registry. Lack of existing code is not a rejection; lack of a coherent natural
task law, legal action or value endpoint is.

## Pass contract

One host passes only if a prospectively frozen, zero-training packet establishes
all of the following without looking at learned-policy efficacy:

- two histories identical in current roster, physical state, legal actions and
  all policy-visible pre-probe information;
- different persistent future source/sink laws;
- different task-value-maximizing downstream legal actions, not merely
  different values under the same action;
- one safe physically responsive legal probe;
- one sham matched in cost, timing, useful work and actuation shell;
- a response that distinguishes the flow laws without post-treatment leakage;
- real/sham, history-swap, response-swap and downstream-action-clamp controls;
- a full event-history RNN with the same observations and probe action as the
  exact-containing generic comparator; and
- a credible search/relay/replacement UAV mapping of observations, actions,
  failure mode and value endpoint.

The EM must explicitly test two fatal alternatives: the containing RNN can
infer the same sufficient belief without an anchored flow representation, and
the probe helps through direct physical work/state displacement rather than
information.

## Claim and stop boundary

A pass supports only named-host constructibility, insufficiency of passive
pre-probe information, and the existence of a legal information-bearing probe.
It does not support APFI efficacy, necessity of the anchored belief,
superiority to the containing RNN, general dynamic-`N`, UAV performance,
safety or deployment.

If no registry member satisfies every pass clause, return a complete stop
milestone and do not train, simulate, request CM work or contact a provider.
Revisit requires a new named natural host satisfying the same complete clause
set and a new Portfolio decision.

## Allowed research and return

Allowed: read-only repository evidence, primary/public technical sources,
paper-and-pencil task-law construction, causal/control analysis, and bounded
research-specialist support inside this direction. The final return must state
the exact sources and separate sourced operational facts from proposed toy
semantics.

Return either:

1. one complete proposed R01 host-witness science card and exact direction-
   scoped Pro/Gemini question purposes for Portfolio review; or
2. one complete no-host conclusion with which clauses failed for each registry
   member and the exact revisit condition.
