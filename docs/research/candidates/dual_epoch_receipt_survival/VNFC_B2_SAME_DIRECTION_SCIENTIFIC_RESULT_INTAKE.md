uses retained payload, this common no-probe/low-return regime is directly
mechanism-relevant. The experiment successfully measures three weak final
packages but does not establish an access-competent learned reference through
which carrier semantics can be isolated.

The strongest alternative is therefore:

> At the frozen PPO budget and host, the shared learned policy converged to a
> low-performing fact-insensitive/no-probe strategy. The typed carrier may have
> exposed valid retained facts, but the policy did not turn them into a material
> task advantage; reset did not learn reacquisition; and raw did not learn a
> materially better history strategy. Independent training and the failed `C3`
> regret-equivalence control leave generic optimization and learned-policy
> differences sufficient to explain the small arm contrasts.

This explanation is stronger than either “retention has no value” or “the surface
is saturated.” It does not rescue the typed package: the registered package result
