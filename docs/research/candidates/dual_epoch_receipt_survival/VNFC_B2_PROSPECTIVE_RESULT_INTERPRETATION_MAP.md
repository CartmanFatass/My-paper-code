## Interpretation order

Apply these questions in order. Later favorable numbers cannot cure an earlier
non-identifying condition.

1. **Did question-relevant activity begin?** All three learned arms for at least
   one paired seed must have final checkpoints and every cell in all four panels,
   fresh-oracle counterfactuals, stale-state instrumentation, and all four
   row-order replicas. If not, there is no B2 scientific result. Missing execution
   or output remains unchanged-science CM work.
2. **Is the named contrast technically and scientifically intact?** A wrong hard
   lifecycle mask, any typed hard stale-state error, material row-order dependence,
   split or world contamination, missing cell, changed schedule, or incomplete
   paired output makes the affected contrast non-identifying. Unaffected complete
   contrasts may remain descriptive; no treatment claim may borrow the broken one.
3. **Is there registered room to distinguish recovery?** On the joint holdout,
   at least one prespecified learned arm must meet either the frozen `0.05` return
   or `0.10` regret oracle-headroom rule with a positive 95% paired-`t` lower bound.
   If none does, the surface is saturated at the registered resolution and cannot
   support the treatment-level pattern.
4. **Does the full typed-versus-reset cell pattern hold?** Conditions 1--4 in the
   card require positive replacement-controlled `D_E` and `D_ER`, material typed
   benefit on the same metric in both `C1` and `C2`, practical typed/reset
   equivalence in `C3`, zero typed hard stale-state errors, and a defined
   eight-seed SCR contrast with no material increase separately in `C2` and `C3`.
   These conditions jointly support selective retention; no individual contrast
   substitutes for the pattern.
5. **Is typed factorization separately distinguished from learned history?** Only
   after the typed-versus-reset pattern is supported, apply the named typed/raw
   `C2` materiality rule and the raw `C0` ordinary-memory equivalence check. This is
   a stricter attribution question, not a prerequisite for saying that retention
   beat reset-all.
6. **What generalizes?** The project-facing conclusion is determined on the joint
   holdout `N=5, S*`. The seen-size, size-only holdout, and schedule-only holdout
   panels localize why that conclusion did or did not transfer; they cannot replace
   it.

## Metric-specific claim wording

- If only `RR3` qualifies, claim reduced three-step post-event recovery regret, not
  improved whole-episode task return, and only if `RR3` qualifies separately in
  both `C1` and `C2`.
- If only `J` qualifies, claim improved episode-average external return. Do not say
  the effect was concentrated in recovery unless the reported `RR3` supports it;
  `J` must qualify separately in both `C1` and `C2`.
- If both qualify, both bounded descriptions are available; neither implies UAV
  safety or arbitrary-churn robustness.
- A mixed pair—`J` qualifying in one retention cell and `RR3` in the other—does
  not satisfy support condition 2.
- `SCR` is a safety outcome and not proof that a particular hidden state caused an
  individual action. It is computed per arm/seed/cell/panel as pooled stale-command
  count divided by pooled eligible focal service decisions across registered worlds
  and row-order replicas. Zero denominator is undefined rather than zero. Zero hard
  stale-state errors establishes only compliance with the declared runtime masks.

## Strongest alternative under a positive result

The host gives the typed arm a deterministic cache of two task-sufficient private
bits while the raw arm must learn to store, preserve, and selectively overwrite
the same facts in a finite recurrent state. The XOR rule, explicit lifecycle
relations, probe cost, two-role ontology, and separately initialized learners may
favor this decomposition. The raw `C0` check, entity-safe registry, replacement
cell, equal capacity/budget, and oracle constrain that explanation but do not turn
a positive result into proof that recurrence cannot represent the solution or that
all UAV memory has exactly two ownership scopes.

the access-adequate raw comparator activates the card's one next discriminator: a

hard `always restore authorized state` against a learned `reuse versus refresh`
gate that can reject valid-but-aged state but cannot override an invalid mask.
