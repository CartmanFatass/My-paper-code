## Cost ledger

Two nonnegative deterministic costs are frozen:

- `c_ret >= 0`: one retention cost for carrying `b_old` across the change boundary. Every controller that possesses retained `b_old` pays it once per unit, before the `t=0` action. Bit-deleted reset pays zero.
- `c_ver >= 0`: one verification cost when a controller's action depends on the designated OWNER-currentness predicate. The gated rule and any raw-history emulation that branches on that predicate pay it once per unit. A policy whose action is OWNER-independent, including fixed ungated use or fixed probing, does not pay it.

These costs are charged to terminal return and are not rewards, learned quantities, random variables, latency aliases, security costs, or claims about a deployment resource. Retention is sunk within a retained-information action comparison but not against bit-deleted reset. Verification is charged by actual dependency: a containing raw controller may not be forced to pay it when selecting an OWNER-independent action. Changing who pays either cost, charging by a different clock, changing `kappa` or `lambda`, adding noisy verification, or adding another action changes the scientific object and stops this cycle.
