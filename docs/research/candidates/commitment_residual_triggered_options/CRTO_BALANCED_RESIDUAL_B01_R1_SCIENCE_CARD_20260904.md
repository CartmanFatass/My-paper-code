## 1. Question and non-goals

On a transparently constructed, KEEP/REPLAN-balanced set of full reset-to-boundary K8 histories,
does the aligned calibrated residual reduce finite-budget native action regret relative to the
strongest same-information RAW learner, and is any reduction alignment-specific rather than a
generic residual-preprocessing effect?

The maximum claim is a preliminary finite-budget inductive-bias observation on the exact selected
rows below. The selection is deliberately outcome-informed: the rows were chosen after the valid
2026-08-31 support census revealed their native G16 advantages. Therefore this object cannot
estimate natural K8 prevalence, a seed/address population, or out-of-selection performance. It
cannot establish information gain, function-class superiority, stable performance, full-policy
return, variable `K`, variable `N`, general MARL/UAV value, transfer, safety, deployment, or a
theorem.

The consumed natural K8 pilot and census remain closed at their original meanings. This object
does not read or instantiate confirmation namespace `2026083001`, lower the old material threshold,
reinterpret its MIDDLE rows as old-object KEEP support, or rerun the support-census object.

## 2. Prior evidence and live explanations

The valid support census at namespace `2026083192` observed 512 complete K8 first-boundary rows.
At the old material threshold `0.02`, none was KEEP-material. On the same recorded rows, however,
48 high-cost rows have

```text
A = max_legal_replacement G16 - G16(KEEP) <= -0.01
```

and 102 high-cost rows have `A >= +0.01`. This supplies a finite, explicitly selected two-sided
surface without changing the old object's result or making a prevalence claim.

The treatment explanation is **aligned residual geometry**: whitening and calibrated coordinate
ranking expose a low-complexity relation between commitment-origin prediction error and the native
KEEP/REPLAN action label, so the aligned representation should need less optimizer exposure than
the containing RAW packet. The strongest same-information null is **RAW**, which receives the
target, predictor mean, and Cholesky factor from which the residual is deterministically computed.
The principal alternative is **generic preprocessing**: any residual-shaped packet, even when
assigned to the wrong row, may optimize more easily. `CALIBRATED_DERANGEMENT` tests that alternative
while preserving the residual-packet multiset within split/K8/elapsed/cost cells.

Other live explanations are the outcome-informed row selection, one-initialization optimizer
variation, predictor error, event/onset cues in the history, insufficient RAW competence, and a
scientific/numerical/RNG/work-parity defect.

## 3. Environment-to-consequence trace

Each selected member is a complete `ScenarioTape` executed from reset in the real four-agent
service-relay host under the frozen deterministic script. The environment event, cost, onset,
physical arrivals, capacities, option uniforms, and rate uniforms determine the target agent's
continuous commitment history. One environment slot owns the active option; there is no entity
replacement or slot reassignment. At the first eligible discretionary review, the gate sees the
same 42-vector history plus exactly one 52-vector packet, then chooses KEEP or a legal replacement.
The host charges the chosen replacement once, advances all four agents for 16 primitive steps on
the immutable exogenous tape, and produces native discounted reward plus terminal potential.

The action label and regret use all legal printed actions. The learner does not create the history
or choose the selected population. Membership is fixed within every episode; join, leave, rejoin,
replacement, censoring, survivor state, semi-Markov lifetime, and partner co-adaptation are not
present. Primitive host time, review opportunity time, commitment age, predictor exposure, gate
optimizer updates, processed-example exposure, and evaluation rows are reported separately.

## 5. Treatment, comparator, control, and exposure

`CommonHistoryGate` parameters and use the same 48 TRAIN rows, cyclic example order, FP32


The paths are:

1. **RAW — strongest comparator.** The 52-vector concatenates the current target, predictor mean,

2. **TRUE_RESIDUAL — treatment.** Existing whitened residual, clipped coordinate, calibrated rank,

The evaluation panel is never used in predictor fitting, calibration, gate optimization, example
ordering, checkpoint choice, or derangement construction for TRAIN.
