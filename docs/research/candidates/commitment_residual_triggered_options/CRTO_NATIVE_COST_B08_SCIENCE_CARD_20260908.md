## 3. The one objective intervention

For TRAIN row i, legal actions A_i, existing constant native labels y_i(a), and eight model logits z:

```text
p_i(a) = legal_softmax(z_i)(a), with temperature 1 and illegal probability 0
c_i(a) = (max_{b in A_i} y_i(b) - y_i(a)) / 0.01
L = mean_i sum_{a in A_i} p_i(a) * c_i(a)
```

Use a numerically stable legal softmax, equal row weights, and no gradient through labels or
max G16. The .01 scale is the existing population's minimum material advantage, not a new reward
or tuned parameter. Outputs are logits, not calibrated G16 estimates. No entropy, auxiliary MSE,
objective mixture, temperature tuning, label clipping or sweep is selected. Gate evaluation still
uses the deterministic legal printed-order argmax; a lower softmax TRAIN loss alone is no result.

The per-row logit gradient is p(a)[c(a)−sum_b p(b)c(b)]. This design directs probability away
from actions with above-average native loss. It differs from B07's centered score regression,
including its equal-row rather than pooled-legal-element weighting. A positive would concern
this objective package; it would not uniquely identify cost weighting, removal of regression,
row weighting, gradient scale, clipping or Adam. No factorization experiment is prerequisite.
