## Material findings and proportionate corrections

**Prefit freezing is scientifically coherent, but its guarantee is narrow.** For fixed pre-action histories, independently sampled simultaneous actions, and a critic fixed independently of this rollout's targets, the focal baseline is constant in the focal sampled action. Consequently, the unnormalized score identity gives `E[grad log pi_i(a_i|h_i) * b_i | histories, a_-i] = 0`. An inaccurate Q does not itself invalidate that baseline identity. Fitting on current returns before constructing the baseline could destroy this independence; the proposed ordering correctly avoids it. Explicitly pair random draws across arms, not correlate agents' action draws within a team. Keep critic context pre-action and replace every focal action field during enumeration. [Assessment, “Frozen baseline and learning”][assessment].

Sampled `G-b_i` retains realized return rather than replacing it with estimated factual Q. It nevertheless subtracts extrapolated predictions: factual regression does not establish counterfactual accuracy, especially with omitted policy histories and changing policies. Rollout-wide normalization introduces action-dependent centering/scaling; clipping and recurrent replay further separate this algorithm from the score identity. Detached, once-standardized advantages held four epochs are appropriate implementation requirements, not an unbiased-PPO, causal-credit or variance-reduction theorem. No exact-credit diagnostic is needed for this package comparison. [Assessment][assessment]; [FOUNDATIONS §§2–4][foundations]; [MARL, credit assignment][marl].

**The V comparator is specified, not yet demonstrated competent.** The adapter supplies the declared central packing and summed native team reward; `returns_to_go` implements complete-episode, gamma-one targets without bootstrap. These support the proposed comparison, not the assertion that either critic learns adequate values. Raw long-horizon targets remain unnormalized even when actor advantages are standardized. Four epochs and a .5 critic-gradient cap could leave substantial underfit; no result currently establishes that they do. Retain prefit V residuals and separate critic movement/norms in the already-planned rollout records, alongside both arms' absolute returns. These are observations from selected learning, not a separate competence pilot or tuning sweep. A broken or accidentally frozen V defeats the intended comparator; a weak but correctly trained finite V remains visible with a narrower budget-specific interpretation. [environment.py, named interface helpers][environment]; [learner.py, `returns_to_go`, `update`][learner].

**Categorical and recurrent semantics require an actual adaptation.** The inherited actor has Gaussian heads; the learner's optional masked reduction sums agents. Specify stored categorical probabilities as `[E,T,N,7]`, selected-action log probabilities and ratios as `[E,T,N]`, and broadcast G before subtraction. Average the surrogate over agents, episodes and time; neither multiply agents' ratios nor retain the old agent-sum reduction. Pin population-standard-deviation normalization once. Preserve commanded-action likelihoods despite physical boundary aliasing. Private episode resets and correctly ordered chunk-start hidden states are essential; replay from stored, detached states is truncated recurrent optimization, not exact current-policy full-history replay. [geometry.py, `NativeGeometryActor`, `build_pair`][geometry]; [learner.py, `clipped_policy_loss`, `recurrent_outputs`][learner]; [assessment][assessment].

Separate .5 actor/critic clipping corrects the inherited global clip's cross-block scaling. One Adam call is acceptable with disjoint parameters and no critic-to-actor gradient path. Copying all common critic weights and biases and zeroing the action columns implies initial Q outputs—and hence baselines—match V, subject to numerical tolerance. It does not equate later learning or capacity: Q adds 4,480 parameters. Keep these initial-output, shape, freeze and recurrence checks within the proposed focused implementation review, not another scientific run. Raw108 actor information and private GRU64 must remain unchanged in both arms. [Assessment, “Critics” and “Work, engineering scope and stopping”][assessment]; [geometry.py, `DenseResidualEncoder`][geometry]; [Engineering §§4–5, 7.1–7.3][engineering].

## Work, alternatives and the strongest contrary case

The supplied arithmetic describes two arms, one matched training pair, H=256, two episodes per rollout, four epochs and one final 32-episode panel per arm:

| Training episodes per arm | Pair native ticks, including finals | Pair Adam calls | Treatment baseline rows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 147,456 | 1,024 | 2,293,760 |
| 512 | 278,528 | 2,048 | 4,587,520 |

These are prospective counts, not measured throughput. Scalar Q fits one factual row per tick per epoch and evaluates 35 baseline inputs once before fitting each rollout; there is no `7^5` environment search. Native stepping, actor collection/replay, backward passes, nonlinearities, publication and support remain material. V's old-baseline inference is not separately itemized: state where it is counted or reused. The 11.69 MiB input figure excludes temporaries and is not peak memory. Neither batching nor the 900-second/arm planning estimate establishes feasibility. [COUNTS.json, `256`, `512`, `cost_limits`][counts]; [assessment, work section][assessment].

The masked multi-output alternative is not merely faster scalar Q. It reduces baseline inputs from 35 to five but fits five focal rows per tick, with potentially inconsistent estimates of the same factual joint action. At 512, supplied baseline MACs fall from 176.16 to 26.09 billion while fitting-forward MACs rise from 20.13 to 104.35 billion, before backward costs. Whole-run speedup is unknown. I favor the simpler shared scalar estimator first, without claiming computational dominance. [COUNTS.json][counts]; [assessment, option 3][assessment].

The strongest case against my 256 preference is that it may observe immature Q learning while implementation/review costs are largely fixed; a later fresh 512 comparison could duplicate substantial work. The strongest case for 256 is its smaller known exposure for the initial development question. Neither endpoint is established necessary or sufficient. A 256 null cannot stand in for a 512 result or automatically trigger extension. Reversible PARK is also defensible if expected information does not justify complete implementation cost—not because a theorem, tuned headroom or positive pilot is missing.
