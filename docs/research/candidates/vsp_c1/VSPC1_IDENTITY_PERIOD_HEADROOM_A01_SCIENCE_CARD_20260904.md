## 3. Intended host population and binding MARL structure

The intended discriminator preserved in provenance is a complete paired
`2 identity x 2 exogenous-period x 2 partner-stratum` fixed-boundary utility probe. The
identity-period training rectangle withholds the fourth identity-period corner, and the partner
axis includes held-out partners. Identity is the renewal action; period is an exogenous treatment;
the primitive consequence is context-congruent native utility over a common micro-horizon.

The proposed partner axis tests whether identity utility survives partner change rather than being
explained by partner co-adaptation. That is a MARL-relevant alternative explanation, not yet a
demonstrated binding MARL constraint: no current VSP-C1 artifact traces a partner-owned action,
multi-agent credit path, non-stationary learner exposure, or native team-return consequence. The
current evidence therefore supports only this trace:

`fixed-boundary identity/period/partner design provenance -> no bound executable host event -> no`
`entity/role ownership -> no observed decision-time information path -> no learner action/credit`
`exposure -> no native return`.

A future B card would have to show which of temporal abstraction, partner-induced partial
observability/non-stationarity, or multi-agent credit is actually binding against a competent
single-agent/generic solution. A01 neither decides nor opens that future object.

## 4. Treatment, comparator, and live explanations

### Stated upper reference

The admissible upper reference is the best legal native-return decision rule explicitly stated for
the complete intended population and its fixed aggregation weights. It may use privileged cell
knowledge, but its information advantage and evaluation population must be stated. A kernel
prediction, formal capacity, or unexecuted proposed oracle is not a numeric upper return.

### Strongest competent same-information generic baseline

The admissible comparator is the strongest tracked, tuned, competent generic learner that receives
exactly the decision-time information available to the VSP-C1 learner, chooses from the same legal
identity/action support, uses the same fixed-boundary native-return evaluator and population
weights, and reports its tuning/model-selection exposure separately from evaluation. A historical
proposal for a fully capacity-matched recurrent period-only controller is a comparator design, not
an observed baseline, unless a tracked result supplies all of those facts.

### Other live explanations

- The proposed identity factorization may be contained by an unfactorized generic learner.
- Apparent fourth-corner value may be capacity, label, age, period, support, exposure, or recurrence
  leakage.
- Apparent identity value may be partner co-adaptation rather than reusable semantics.
- The current production-host absence may be only an engineering/access fact and has no scientific
  polarity for the bounded toy.
- Historical FSBS containment remains a distinct normalized-interface observation; it does not
  numerically answer VSP-C1 headroom.
