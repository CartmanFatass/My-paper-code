## Fit-level uncertainty and the conjunction reading

For fit i and paired world e, write d(i,e,r)=J_F(i,e)−J_r(i,e), with r=C or dwell. The proposed estimators are

$$
s_r^2=\frac1{4}\sum_{i=1}^{5}(D_{i,r}-\widehat\mu_r)^2,\qquad

$$

$$
