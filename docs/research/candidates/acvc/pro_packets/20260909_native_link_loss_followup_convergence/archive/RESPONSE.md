**End the instantiated learned selective-retrace comparison, with no successor selected.** This boundary covers the tested structured and containing-generic gates on the outcome-informed, frozen DENSE/8201 proposer, with the unchanged heads, objective and 512-episode training budget. ## The actual information, action and learning path

The tested host has five fixed UAVs, fifty static users, 256 one-second primitive steps and unchanged native team reward. Each actor uses its private observation and actual previous sent command. It retains one explicitly bound user coordinate from the previous observation: the lowest-SINR nonpadding observed row, not a persistent sorted-slot identity. An unambiguous absence from the current nonempty, unsaturated list of 1–19 users establishes loss of that UAV's own link eligibility under the stated matching exclusions. Empty, saturated, ambiguous and reset cases create no opportunity. [B01 card, §2][card1]; [B02 card, §2][card2].

An opportunity also requires the current sampled base proposal to point horizontally away from the remembered anchor. The alternative action is the clipped reverse of the UAV's realized preceding displacement divided by the 30-metre one-step motion scale. This is retrace of actual movement, not negation of a possibly boundary-clipped proposal. Simultaneous teammate motion changes interference and assignment, so returning one UAV approximately to its previous position does not restore the previous joint state. Own-link loss may accompany a beneficial handoff; it is not an observed global service failure. [B01 card, §2][card1]; [direction record, “Native link-loss B01/B02”][direction].

The native consequence remains team service and connected-link quality: the retained reward is 0.7 times the served fraction plus 0.3 times mean clipped connected-link quality. Thus the relevant chain is simultaneous motion/interference, private bound observation loss, an owned apply/retrace decision, new joint geometry and assignment, then complete native return. There is no actor access to global user IDs, service assignment, full SINR, current teammate commands or a newly supplied reward channel. Centralized critic inputs and team return are learning-only. [Original response, “What the native source actually establishes”][original]; [B01 card, §§2–3][card1].

C always applies the sampled frozen proposal. F deterministically selects retrace on an opportunity, but its underlying base proposals remain sampled. T learns the structured private recurrent gate. G includes T's entire trainable path and adds a same-information recurrent residual, receiving all of T's raw and derived features. Both learned arms have the same training exposure; G is not weakened to offset its additional capacity. Setting its residual output to zero contains T, while deterministic C/F are limiting constant gate policies rather than exact finite sigmoid endpoints. Functional containment is not a finite-budget optimization guarantee. [B01 card, §3][card1]; [B02 card, §2][card2].

Every arm follows its own actual trajectory, with separate private recurrent states and actual-command feedback to its frozen base. Gate-only PPO evaluates the collected proposal rather than resampling it. The team advantage uses primitive gamma-one return-to-go; eligible Bernoulli terms are masked within the unchanged all-primitive-row objective. All five gates share parameters within an arm and co-adapt through the joint trajectory. These are tests of fixed-roster partial observability and other-agent non-stationarity, not variable membership, semi-Markov duration credit or independently manipulated teammate behavior. [B01 card, §§2–3][card1]; [B02 intake, §§1 and 3][b02].

Importantly, **F already uses retained history**, and even the common DENSE proposer is recurrent. F−C is not a pure history-versus-no-history intervention. It combines the explicit anchor predicate, the retrace action and the subsequent joint-trajectory consequences. Neither its positive return nor ending learned selection identifies the necessity of memory, the anchor's isolated contribution, retrace versus dwell/geometry, or partner-response effects. Those remain distinct untested hypotheses, not automatically selected experiments. [B01 card, §§2–3][card1]; [B01 intake, §3][b01]; [B02 intake, §3][b02].

## Genuine learning exposure, limited independent evidence

These were not absent learners. B01 T/G had 51,834/50,076 eligible training choices and relative gate displacements 0.3087580/0.1512762. B02 had 50,137/49,920 choices and relative displacements 0.1773413/0.1708186. Each arm completed 1,024 Adam calls per instance, with eligible rows in every recorded update. The zero-initial gate projections also moved. These observations exclude zero opportunities, zero updates and zero gate movement as descriptions of the completed attempts; they do not establish successful optimization or identify a credit, representation, initialization or entropy explanation. [B01 intake, §3][b01]; [B02 intake, §4][b02]; [consultation facts, `training_gate_opportunities` and `relative_gate_displacement`][facts].

Different opportunity and retrace counts arise on different arm trajectories. They cannot be treated as randomized doses, used to infer that more retracing monotonically causes greater return, or substituted for the native primary. C's opportunity count is unmeasured by design because it performs no matching; a zero bookkeeping entry is not evidence that it encountered no relevant events. Nonzero exposure makes the adverse comparisons interpretable as results of real training, without eliminating optimization uncertainty. [B01 intake, §3][b01]; [B02 intake, §4][b02].

There are **two matched training instances**, not 64 independently trained policies, four independent replications of a T−G effect, or 256 training samples supplied by final evaluation. Each instance has its own 32-reset paired joint-episode panel, common within-instance exogenous resets and separate action streams. Both training and evaluation randomness change between instances, so the change in T−G cannot be attributed uniquely to either. Within-panel paired SEs remain useful conditional summaries, but pooling episodes does not yield a training-population interval. More evaluation of these checkpoints would not add independent training evidence. [Consultation facts, `independent_unit` and `measurement_limit`][facts]; [B02 intake, §3][b02]; [evidence specification, §11.8.3][spec].

The same DENSE/8201 checkpoint was selected with the earlier DENSE outcomes visible; it was the better of the two original DENSE panel means. Keeping it frozen now controls the proposer across these comparisons but does not remove that selection exposure. The conclusions are conditional on this asset and the specified native panels, not evidence across independently selected proposers. No tuned same-information current-host headroom record exists. F is an attained control, not the upper half of a headroom certificate. [B01 card, §§3–4][card1]; [consultation facts, `headroom`][facts].

## Why ending is preferable to another selected B here

The strongest stop case concerns the proposed incremental value of learning selection, not whether correction ever helps. The first result left a genuine uncertainty: T beat G while both lost to F. The separately allocated second instance directly addressed recurrence of those two observations. It again put T and G below F and did not repeat T's generic-gate advantage. Consequently, the specific case for retaining the unchanged learned selector is weaker than at the first-instance boundary, while the useful fixed control remains available. This supports ending the instantiated package without certifying that F wins in a training population. [B02 card, §§1 and 4][card2]; [B02 intake, §§3 and 6][b02].

The strongest continue case is not dismissed: both learned packages have above-MEI native gains over C, and the fixed retained-observation/retrace package does still better. The original low-confidence rationale that selective handling could avoid unnecessary retraces during useful handoffs remains possible. A different learner could improve its approximation or learn valuable exceptions to F. Neither two fits nor G's containment proves otherwise. This is the strongest surviving scientific alternative to a broad negative conclusion. [Preparation, §3][prep]; [original response, opening and “Controls, containment”][original].

What does not follow is that this alternative selects a worthwhile next intervention. Merely making a learned gate behave more like F would address recovery of an already attained fixed behavior; it would not, by itself, establish the extra value of selecting exceptions to F. Conversely, choosing a different objective or representation solely because the present one lost would supply an untested possibility without a specific reason to expect a more useful answer than retaining F and ending this comparison. This is my assessment of the present question, not a claim that such changes are illegitimate or must have uniquely diagnosed causes before they can be tried.

Another unchanged matched instance would provide additional variation evidence and remains scientifically legal. I do not select it because its marginal value for this bounded next-action decision is lower after the targeted follow-up, not because two is a universal stopping count, because a sign failed to replicate, or because the evidence must first become significant. Failure to repeat the gain is an observation relevant to this decision, not a new prerequisite for any future B. The absence of a nominated changed candidate also does not imply that no such candidate can ever be justified. [Evidence specification, §§5.2, 11.8.1–4 and 11.9][spec].

No exact maximum, complete support census, exhaustive causal account, baseline tuning, positive seed, bounded/beam policy search or cost pilot is requested before a direct B. Removing those additional questions relinquishes optimality, mechanism-localization and exhaustive policy-class claims; it does not invalidate the observed native comparisons. There is no specification exception in ending this narrow package. A global rule that two adverse instances close all related mechanisms would exceed both the evidence and the authorized scope, and I do not adopt one. [Evidence specification, §§4, 5.2, 11.4 and 11.8–11.9][spec].
