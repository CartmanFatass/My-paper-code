## Partition reasoning

For k=2 or24, b=2*floor(D/(2*k))+1 differs from D/k by at most one. Each of the first k-1
numerators b+2*(H mod (2*r+1)-r), r=floor(b/(8*k)), is odd. Its perturbation has magnitude
at most b/(4*k), so it is positive. The residual equals D minus those k-1 odd numerators
and is odd, since D and k are even. Its distance from D/k is bounded by (k-1)+b/4;
for the fixed large D at k=2 or24 this is less than D/k. Thus the residual is positive
and less than D. The exact sum is D by construction. All atoms have canonical denominator
