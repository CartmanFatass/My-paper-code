## Conclusion

The recovered Pro response contains a design-changing causal criticism. Under
the B1 toy's conjunctive packet reward and additive per-agent renewal cost, the
current `COORD` execution rule cannot create a positive joint-change surplus by
waiting for an earlier local renewal so that it occurs with a later local
renewal. With fixed local requests it is return-equivalent when it pairs at the
later local time and harmful if it waits longer. A positive B1 `COORD-LOCAL`
contrast can instead arise from partner information changing the learned
request process, better observation accumulation before skill instantiation,
readiness synchronization, or packing false/prophylactic renewals into one
otherwise serviceable tick.

This diagnosis is accepted as a constraint on B1 interpretation. It does not
change the registered B1 treatment, arms, seeds, budgets, activity boundary,
decision calculations, or currently running/recovering execution. The B1
result may still answer whether the complete adaptive `LOCAL` or `COORD` bundle
improves mean or worst-cell return over the registered fixed baseline. It cannot
by itself identify a positive same-information joint-execution surplus, and a
positive adaptive result cannot by itself identify task-change evidence rather
than readiness/age/budget scheduling.

The proposed `EBCR-TCR` mechanism, new comparators, corrected oracles, scarce-
budget setting, and proof-sized toy are prospective successor science. They are
not added as post-hoc B1 gates and no successor experiment is authorized here.

## Accepted causal diagnosis

Let a joint latent change occur at time `tau`, let both post-renewal skills be
correct, and let the independent local execution times be `u_T <= u_R`.
Between `tau` and `u_R`, the conjunctive packet is already unavailable because
at least one role is stale or renewing. Moving the tracker renewal from `u_T`
to `u_R` therefore creates no additional successful packet, and both schedules
still renew two agents and pay the same additive renewal cost. Pairing has zero
pure execution surplus at `u_R` and negative surplus if joint readiness delays
it further. The statement that asynchronous renewal necessarily loses two
packet ticks while simultaneous renewal loses one does not hold after a true
joint change when the still-stale partner already blocks service.

Accordingly, the registered B1 contrast combines at least three objects:

- partner invalidation/readiness bits as extra observations;
- the change those observations induce in the learned hazard/request process;
- the pending and joint-execution transducer.

`COORD-SHUFFLE` and `COORD-YOKED` remain useful tests against marginal renewal-
count and period-distribution explanations. `SHUFFLE` can change the joint event
mask and simultaneous-renewal packing; `YOKED` transfers a complete source-
episode schedule but does not match that schedule's relationship to the
destination episode's readiness classes. Passing them does not isolate pure
joint execution.

Two additional shortcuts are accepted as material:

1. Registered fixed policies request renewal exactly at age `k`, whereas the
   adaptive arms can wait for readiness and avoid the nonready-renewal penalty.
   Thus an adaptive gain can reflect readiness-aware execution without task-
   event detection.
2. The ordinary cap is large enough to admit the age-four schedule. In many
   episodes the token shadow price may be nearly zero, so time and remaining
   budget can act as a schedule clock rather than demonstrate scarce-budget
   allocation.

The three-observation majority also makes the earliest-boundary
`STAGE-ORACLE` non-monotone as a headroom object. Immediately after a change,
two of the three observations can still describe the old phase. An oracle that
knows the boundary may rationally wait for content accuracy. Its failure would
not establish lack of exploitable variable timing.

## Prospective mechanism repair

Pro proposes temporally coded, budget-priced corroborated renewal (`EBCR-TCR`).
Its useful principle is accepted provisionally: cooperation must be able to
advance a weak but compatible partner decision, rather than only delay a strong
local decision.

The proposed local object is a calibrated renewal-advantage score

`D_i(t) = Q_i(renew or commit now | h_i) - Q_i(continue | h_i) - lambda_i(t,b_i)`,

where `lambda_i` represents the marginal value of retaining a scarce renewal
token. A deterministic soft/hard quantization replaces the sampled Bernoulli
exploration action as the message semantic. Over two ticks, the existing one
invalidation bit can encode no support, a soft proposal, or a hard commitment;
the readiness bit remains unchanged. A hard commitment executes locally unless
a one-tick, positive-surplus corroborated opportunity exists; hard-plus-soft may
advance the soft partner; soft-plus-soft does not force renewal; safety and max-
age overrides remain unilateral.

This preserves decentralized execution and the two-bit-per-agent-per-tick
channel. Before it can become a treatment, the temporal code still needs a
causal framing/cooldown rule, exact erasure behavior, threshold calibration,
and a definition of the bounded waiting-loss test. The budget shadow price and
ordered innovation statistic also remain to be defined. Pro's suggestion is a
mechanism family, not a frozen algorithm.

## Prospective comparators and shortcut controls

The following future objects are scientifically well motivated:

- `READY-FIXED-BEST`: issue a fixed-age request but pass it through the same
  readiness, pending-expiry, max-age, safety, and cap envelope as the adaptive
  arms. This tests whether readiness alone explains the fixed-policy gap.
- `ABR-LOCAL`: a capacity/training-matched adaptive arm with role, age,
  readiness, time, remaining budget, and pending state, but no task
  disagreement or partner invalidation. `LOCAL-ABR-LOCAL` estimates the value of
  task evidence beyond adaptive scheduling.
- `INFO-LOCAL`: expose exactly the partner information available to `COORD` but
  retain local execution. It is necessary to separate partner information from
  a coordinated execution design.
- `JOINT-MASK-YOKED`: preserve the complete singleton/simultaneous renewal mask
  and readiness category while breaking association with task changes. This is
  a stronger false-alarm-packing and readiness-synchronization control than
  matching marginal period multisets alone.
- optionally `BLIND-COORD`, giving a masked/live partner-information by
  local/joint-execution factorial.

A separately trained `INFO-LOCAL` and `COORD` comparison estimates the effect of
two designs with matched information; it is not literally the same-policy
execution surplus because training and induced state histories can differ. A
pure execution claim therefore still needs either a frozen-policy
counterfactual with carefully defined state handling or the exact finite toy
below. The future science must not over-label a separately learned contrast.

The future headroom panel should distinguish a content-aware constrained phase
oracle from an allowed-information Bayes oracle. The first asks whether timing
has value in the host; the second asks whether EBCR's actual information can
recover it. Neither replaces the registered B1 oracle after activity has begun.

## Hazard-credit and coordination diagnosis

The primary candidate failure is a second-order joint-credit barrier, amplified
by delayed request resolution, pending-state aliasing, forced-action censoring,
and `gamma=0.99` crediting an undiscounted reported return. For a local joint-
stale state, construct the common-randomness counterfactual table
`Q00,Q10,Q01,Q11`, where the bits are the two discretionary state-changing
requests and every branch is followed through request resolution without safety
or max-age intervention. Define

`Xi = Q11 - Q10 - Q01 + Q00`.

- If `Q11 > Q00` and `Xi > 0` while both unilateral margins are nonpositive,
  the mechanism has joint value but independent hazards face a structural
  coordination/credit barrier. A centralized critic alone does not remove it.
- If `Q11 <= Q00` in matched genuinely joint-stale states, the current joint
  renewal action is uninformative under the costs; more PPO training is not the
  remedy.
- If the table has useful joint structure but learned hazards do not rank it,
  optimization or credit assignment is implicated.

This proof-sized table is the smallest accepted diagnostic separating an
uninformative renewal mechanism from a learning failure.

## Highest-information small discriminator

The discriminator has an analytic current-protocol cell and a prospective
corroboration cell, each with four scored ticks and one ordinary token per
agent.

In the current-protocol cell, a joint change precedes tick one, tracker readiness
is available at ticks one and two, relay readiness begins at tick two, and both
requests are valid. Local execution renews on ticks one and two; current pairing
renews both on tick two. With correct renewed content, conjunctive service, and
additive per-agent cost, their return difference is exactly zero. In the
otherwise identical no-change world, pairing gains one service tick solely by
packing two unnecessary renewals. This separates desired joint-change
correction from the dominant false-alarm-packing alternative without a large
run.

The prospective corroboration cell crosses four worlds: joint change with
hard/soft evidence, singleton change with hard/no evidence, no change with
no/no evidence, and a rare no-change hard/soft false alarm. A valid `EBCR-TCR`
should gain by advancing the soft partner only in the joint hard/soft world,
remain neutral in singleton and no-change worlds, and expose its bounded loss in
the false-alarm world. With renewal cost `c`, the proposed exact contrast is
`+1/4` in the joint world and `-(1+2c)/4` in the false-alarm world, so the net
mechanism is favorable only when

`p_joint_HL > (1 + 2c) * p_false_HL`.

Cross-world or cross-episode yoking of the hard/soft/no-message frequencies
then separates message information from corroborated execution. This is the
highest-information prospective discriminator because it can delete the
current pairing story, support distributed sensing only, or justify the TCR
successor before a larger learner run.

## Toy-to-UAV bridge

The binary-to-continuous bridge is strengthened, not accepted as evidence. The
primary invariant is monotone calibration: higher renewal-score bins must have
higher measured counterfactual value for renewing now rather than continuing
under the same locally available information. The cooperative invariant is a
positive same-information execution surplus on the continuous surface.

That surplus must come from a real UAV mechanism, such as earlier restoration
of a jointly useful tracker-relay configuration or a genuine shared replanning
blackout. It cannot rely on the toy's all-or-none packet conjunction. The next
surface should preserve fixed low-level options, ordered filter innovation and
option age, independently controllable joint and singleton changes, separate
per-agent and shared replanning costs, certified local readiness, erasure-safe
messages, and immediate unilateral safety override. It should retain the
readiness-only, same-information, and joint-mask controls.

The following toy effects do not transfer: packing false renewals into one
binary packet-loss tick, dependence on perfectly simultaneous binary phase
flips, and dependence on the majority-of-three discontinuity.

## Decision triggers after B1

No branch is selected before the complete B1 science packet arrives.

- A positive B1 adaptive result supports only the registered bundle-level
  variable-period claim. Inspect readiness, event alignment, renewal masks,
  false-alarm intervals, and joint-versus-singleton cells before choosing a
  successor. It does not license a pure coordination claim.
- If adaptive arms fail but the registered earliest-event oracle wins, first
  test content-aware oracle and allowed-information headroom; do not assume
  that more training is the answer.
- If neither a content-aware constrained oracle nor readiness-matched adaptive
  scheduling has headroom over readiness-matched fixed requests, further EBCR
  mechanism enlargement is scientifically low value in this host.
- If `ABR-LOCAL` would match `LOCAL`, task-event evidence is not doing useful
  work. If `INFO-LOCAL` would match or beat coordinated execution, cooperation
  is information-only or nonbeneficial. If positive effects concentrate in
  no-change/false-alarm intervals or require near-perfect joint coupling, the
  general tracker-relay mechanism is unpromising.
- A failed PPO checkpoint alone is not evidence against EBCR when the exact
  counterfactual table or allowed-information oracle shows positive headroom.
