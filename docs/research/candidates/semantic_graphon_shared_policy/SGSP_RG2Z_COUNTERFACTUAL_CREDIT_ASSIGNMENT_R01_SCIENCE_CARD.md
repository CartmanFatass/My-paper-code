## 4. All-legal unilateral one-tape continuation return

For a focal factual decision `(e,t,i)` and every action
`a` in the focal role's legal set, define

\[
\widehat Q^A_{s,u,e,t,i}(a)
=J\!\left(\operatorname{Continue}_{\theta^A_{s,u}}
[\operatorname{do}(a_{i,t}=a),a_{-i,t}=a^*_{-i,t};\Omega_{s,u,e}]\right).
\]

The object is exact over the finite current legal-action set and exact for the
declared simulator under one coupled continuation tape. It is one Monte Carlo
sample over future stochasticity, not an exact expectation `Q^pi`, a dynamic-
programming oracle, a variance-free target or a joint-action enumeration.

For `a=a^*_{i,t}`, `Qhat(a)` is by definition the factual episode's terminal
return and is reused without a second simulation. For every other legal action,
restore the exact branch snapshot, replace only the focal current action and
hold every teammate's current-slot factual action fixed. Then:

1. resolve the complete current slot under the exact radio, collision,
   detection, FIFO, acknowledgement, expiry, waste and horizon laws;
2. at `t+1`, build branch-specific observations, including the forced prior
   action and every branch-specific buffer and outcome;
3. continue every agent from the copied post-GRU hidden state;
4. from `t+1` through slot 11, recompute all messages, summaries, recurrent
   states and legal distributions on that branch and sample all agents from
   their ordinary policies using the common future action uniforms; and
5. return the exact full-episode terminal `J`, retaining the common prefix's
   deliveries and waste accounting.

Future factual actions, factual observations or factual recurrent states may
not be replayed after the intervention. A branch never recursively creates
training examples or additional counterfactual branches. Empty-buffer and
apparently ineffective legal actions remain included. Illegal actions are
neither evaluated nor weighted.

The factual-action identity `Qhat(a*)=J_base` is a semantic invariant. Any
implementation whose factual action under the same snapshot and tape would not
reproduce the factual suffix defines a different object and is structurally
invalid.

## 5. Counterfactual actor advantage and retained critic nuisance

Let `pi^A_{s,u,e,t,i}` be the factual floor-mixed legal distribution. Define

\[
\widehat B^A_{s,u,e,t,i}
=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_{role(i)}}
\operatorname{sg}[\pi^A_{s,u,e,t,i}(a)]
\operatorname{sg}[\widehat Q^A_{s,u,e,t,i}(a)],
\]

\[
\widehat A^A_{s,u,e,t,i}
=\operatorname{sg}[J^A_{s,u,e}-\widehat B^A_{s,u,e,t,i}].
\]

The actor loss retains the r03 sampled-log-probability geometry:

\[
L_{actor}^A=-E_e\left[\frac1{12N_e}\sum_{t,i}
\log\pi^A(a^*_{i,t})\widehat A^A_{s,u,e,t,i}\right]
-0.01E_e\left[\frac1{12N_e}\sum_{t,i}H(\pi^A_{s,u,e,t,i})\right].
\]

The complete loss preserves the r03 critic as nuisance computation:

\[
L^A=L_{actor}^A+0.5E_e\left[\frac1{12}\sum_t(V^A(g_t)-J^A_e)^2\right].
\]

`V` is not used in the actor advantage. Its network, initialization, target,
loss, Adam state, gradient and contribution to the joint global-norm clip are
otherwise exactly r03. This preserves trainable parameter count,
initialization draw opportunity, optimizer grouping and clipping geometry
between the inherited and new actor-credit definitions as far as possible
without adding an unauthorized trainer factor.

The entire Q vector, baseline weights, branch simulator state, branch recurrent
state, branch policy distributions, branch action sampling and branch returns
are stopped. Backpropagation traverses only factual-trajectory log
probabilities, factual entropy and the unchanged factual critic loss through
the ordinary 12-slot recurrent unroll. There is one backward call per arm and
update. An all-action differentiable policy-gradient loss, branch BPTT,
additional actor sample, auxiliary objective or replay path is a different
science-bearing trainer and is forbidden.

The baseline is action-independent for the focal factual draw because the full
Q vector and its stopped policy weights exist for every legal action and the
focal current action uniform is independent of the future tape. The centering
identity `sum_a pi(a)[Qhat(a)-Bhat]=0` holds on the sampled continuation tape.
The estimator assigns unilateral marginal credit conditional on teammates'
realized current actions; it is not Shapley credit, joint-deviation credit or a
claim of unique individual causation.
