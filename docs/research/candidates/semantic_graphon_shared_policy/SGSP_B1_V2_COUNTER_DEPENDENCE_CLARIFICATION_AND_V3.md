## Why this choice

This rule preserves the already displayed address and changes less science
than inventing a new common super-roster. It maintains exact arm/cut/common-
random-number pairing where causal contrasts are computed, while making each
held-out roster an independent draw from the same declared population law.
Direct variable-`N` meaning comes from one unchanged parameterization and
common distributional support, not from sharing individual potential agents
across roster sizes.
