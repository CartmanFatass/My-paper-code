Finding: no materially cheaper meaning-preserving gate

I inspected CartmanFatass/My-paper-code at the exact origin-reachable commit 2b8f1ae0ea8c514bd333c45fe11275a2fabfbb81, and confined the scientific inspection to the named science card and seven RSCF contract surfaces.

Under the stated ceiling, the largest valid reduction is a veto-only structural and reachability screen. It can legitimately stop the exact object only if it proves one of two things:

the proposed comparison cannot satisfy its own structural semantics; or

the narrow and containing learners cannot possibly diverge under the frozen optimizer and projections.

It cannot give a failure-valid answer about attained comparator competence, held-out direct value, or symmetric-cut attribution without either evaluating the update-512 learners or proving global optimizer-and-return bounds that are essentially as difficult as solving the full object.

Accordingly, there is no materially cheaper meaning-preserving scientific gate for the five-class investment decision. A small fixture package can demonstrate that the host and policy class permit the relevant phenomena, but that is a sufficient support witness, not evidence of PHY-TRUST value. Failure of such witnesses would remain inconclusive.

Why the remaining classes do not admit cheap failure-valid predicates

The closed object makes the outcome-bearing claims properties of a complete stochastic training-and-evaluation map. It uses one shared recurrent actor, 512 updates, a retained critic and common global clip, one evaluable checkpoint, 24 seed blocks, and adaptation-free evaluation at N={6,21}. The sampled full-suffix score is unbiased only before clipping; the science card expressly states that Adam, clipping, critic gradients, and the resulting finite-budget trajectory remain part of the object.

The exact final meanings are:

Comparator competence: at both N=9 and N=15, the simultaneous lower bound for EDGE−UNIFORM must exceed 0.08, while both seen-roster PHY−EDGE intervals must lie wholly inside [-0.04,+0.04].

Direct answerability: all A_dir, A_interaction, and A_zone lower bounds must exceed zero at both held-out rosters.

Component answerability: all A_cut, A_atten, and A_TV lower bounds must exceed zero at both held-out rosters.

Attribution value: C_PHY>0.05, action TV V>0.08, and differential attenuation I>0.03 at both held-out rosters.

Direct value: held-out direct advantage d>0.04, held-out-minus-seen interaction c>0.03, and worst-basin advantage z>0.02 at both held-out rosters.

The analyzer implements these as a 28-quantity simultaneous family. Structural failure gives no scientific relation; prerequisite failure gives NONIDENTIFIED; component or direct nonretention is allowed only after all prerequisites pass; retention requires the entire conjunction.

That separation matters. A cheaper test of “some policy can respond to the cut” is not a relaxation of “the trained PHY policy responds more than the trained competent EDGE policy at both held-out rosters.”

The central obstruction: the comparison is about optimization, not expressibility

EDGE-FLEX literally contains PHY-TRUST. Therefore a PHY advantage cannot be explained by PHY possessing a policy that EDGE cannot represent. It can arise only through finite-budget learning effects such as projection regularization, optimizer path dependence, recurrent preconditioning, sampled-origin covariance, or interaction with the retained critic and common clipping.

The science card preserves all of those alternatives and explicitly refuses to interpret a positive as kernel truth, representational necessity, credit causality, or general physical-prior superiority.

This produces a no-shortcut result:

A class-capacity witness can show EDGE is capable of competence, but not that the specified learner attains it.

An environment witness can show legal actions can affect delivery, but not that the role-sampled optimizer encounters and exploits those states.

A cut-sensitive parameter witness can show the architecture is not invariant to the cut, but not that the learned PHY policy has larger cut dependence than learned EDGE.

A short optimizer probe can show local gradient geometry, but neither local failure nor local success has a registered one-way implication to update 512.

A finite fixture suite and an early optimizer prefix can also be observationally identical for two admissible continuations that later encounter different on-policy states and gradient sequences. The named contract supplies no monotonicity, basin-preservation, or contraction result that would make early failure irreversible.

Fixture-observer plan

The packet should have three independently inspectable layers.

Fixture producer. Declarative arrays only: roles, active masks, FIFO contents, pending deliveries, events, factual actions, and complete future tapes. Fixture labels contain no seed-, action-, result-, state-, branch-, or checkpoint-like identity tokens.

Branch executor. A fresh test-only harness using the exact shared actor and complete pre-transition carriage. It never invokes the historical production launcher, continuation lineage, checkpoint machinery, update-154 state, or result artifacts.

Independent observer. It receives only compact facts:

snapshot and parameter digests;

focal/teammate action digests;

transition and future-policy-round counts;

common-tape digest;

delivered-event mask and basin counts;

terminal return and waste;

intact/cut legal-simplex probabilities;

factual identity flag.

It should recompute terminal return from the physical delivery-and-waste ledger rather than trusting the branch’s reported scalar. It should retain no Q vector, branch history, model, or scientific coordinate.

Negative fixtures should deliberately introduce one defect at a time: focal action leakage, teammate action mutation, branch-salted tape, hidden-state replay, incomplete action enumeration, asymmetric cut, parameter mutation, and within-role identity leakage. Each must fail the corresponding exact predicate.

Coarse cost of the veto/witness packet

A fresh implementation, independent fixture observer, proof sheet, and negative tests plausibly fit narrowly within:

engineering: 4.0–5.0 workdays;

CPU: approximately 2–5 core-hours, dominated by independent cross-checks rather than the fewer than 5,000 native suffixes;

elapsed: roughly 20–75 minutes with bounded parallelism;

peak RAM: under 2 GiB;

scratch: under 250 MiB.

These are design estimates, not measured benchmarks.

A tight global competence bound, exact policy-class optimization, or a prefix sufficient to make remaining-step optimizer reachability decisive would likely break either the engineering ceiling or the compute ceiling. More importantly, such work would cease to be a substantially smaller scientific object.

Decision-valid ceiling

The only defensible short-gate claim is:

The exact SGSP-RSCF comparison is structurally implementable and not statically proved arm-identical; the finite host contains full-suffix unilateral and symmetric-cut support witnesses at the registered rosters.

That statement provides no evidence that PHY-TRUST has value, no evidence that EDGE-FLEX will be competent, no reason to prefer a physical prior, no latent or kernel necessity claim, and no exclusion of ordinary finite-budget regularization or optimizer geometry.

Therefore the historical package should not be resumed, and a fresh short optimizer probe should not be purchased as a scientific surrogate. The exact comparison must be treated as scientifically indivisible unless the narrow projection-reachability or another genuine impossibility theorem closes first.
