`EOCIV-B9-RECEIVER-ADDRESSED-CREDIT-EDGE` package. The parent base revision is
provenance only. Production readiness and the registered lifecycle require an
explicit committed candidate revision equal to a clean checked-out HEAD.

The candidate reads no predecessor checkpoint, optimizer history, run
artifact, result, or reconstructed ledger. Its sole estimand is the immediate
shared-policy total effect of addressing detached team-credit score terms to
the authenticated receiver rather than the authenticated distinct source. It
does not claim receiver-local weights, unique mediation, sustained learning,
generic gain, direction retention, or scientific acceptance.

Six ordered temporary readiness phases exercise interfaces, artifact
validation/reload, formulas, and branching with zero native episodes and zero
scientific optimizer calls. Only `run-registered` may exclusive-create the
repository-relative reserved result claim and start the 312-episode full.
