## 1. Question, ceiling, and non-goals

receiver-content semantics more when each detached team-credit score term is addressed to its
authenticated receiver than when the same term is addressed to its authenticated distinct source?

Claim ceiling: one preliminary, immediate one-step total-effect signal, null, or counterexample on
the declared two-anchor, three-profile, eight-root toy population. A valid result does not establish
receiver-local mediation, sustained learning, stable performance, generic credit-assignment value,

transfer, safety, deployment readiness, or general MARL superiority.

multi-update training curve; no valve, reward shaping, critic update, global clipping, checkpoint

## 2. Scientific trace and live explanations

Trace: sibling-environment critical event -> authenticated distinct source and receiver identities ->
validated receiver-owned content available to the shared recurrent actor -> endpoint-selected
score-function credit path -> one empty-state Adam actor update -> native CORRECT/SWAPPED critical
reward consequence on matched held-out tapes.

Treatment: `RECEIVER_ADDRESSED`, selecting the authenticated receiver row for every one of the 24
ordered critical score terms per trajectory.

Strongest competent same-information comparator: `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE_ADDRESSED_CONTROL`, selecting
the authenticated distinct active source row from the same full score tensor, using the same
trajectory, detached normalized GAE scalar, actor initialization, parameter set, optimizer and
single update. Endpoint `0` is the unchanged anchor.

Other live explanations:

- a receiver step creates only a generic two-body gain rather than semantic separation;
- the source control is harmed, making receiver-minus-source positive without receiver value;
- either update damages absolute CORRECT reward;
- signs are anchor-, profile-, or root-sensitive;
- the one-step displacement is too small for the native evaluator despite a nonzero gradient;
- earlier content/credit improvements affected representation or optimization diagnostics without
  stable absolute semantics.

## 4. Observable, estimand, and decision rule

For every anchor/profile/held-out-root cell and endpoint `x in {0,R,S}`:

`phi_x = Y_CORRECT(theta_x) - Y_SWAPPED(theta_x)`

where `Y` is the mean native reward across the 24 critical steps. Define:

- `Delta_R = phi_R - phi_0`;
- `J = phi_R - phi_S`;
- `receiver_correct_vs_anchor = Y_CORRECT(theta_R) - Y_CORRECT(theta_0)`;
- `receiver_correct_vs_source = Y_CORRECT(theta_R) - Y_CORRECT(theta_S)`;
- `source_correct_vs_anchor = Y_CORRECT(theta_S) - Y_CORRECT(theta_0)`;
- `receiver_two_arm_generic_gain` as the mean receiver-minus-anchor change across CORRECT and
  SWAPPED.

Retain the global mean, each anchor mean, all three leave-one-profile means, all eight
leave-one-root means, and every underlying cell. Apply the following precedence verbatim:

1. `INVALID_ATTEMPT`: a common-integrity failure, nonfinite required observable, learner-side

2. `B9R1_RECEIVER_ADDRESSED_SEMANTIC_EDGE`: `J > 0` and `Delta_R > 0` in the global, both-anchor,
   every leave-one-profile and every leave-one-root aggregate, while global
   `receiver_correct_vs_anchor >= 0` and `receiver_correct_vs_source >= 0`.
3. `B9R1_GENERIC_OR_SOURCE_HARM_ONLY`: the semantic branch fails and at least one holds:
   global generic gain is positive; global source-vs-anchor, receiver-vs-anchor, or

4. `B9R1_RECEIVER_NOT_SUPPORTED`: branches 1--3 fail and `J <= 0` for both anchor means.
