### Question, contribution and contrary evidence

Given the same radio-aware destination-generation rule, can experience produce
useful joint motion between destinations beyond a competent ordinary trajectory
planner under the complete native service objective? The object is simultaneous
versus staged motion and intermediate waypoints, across the full team. The intended
contribution is empirical understanding and possibly a useful learned controller.
Central PPO, finite path primitives and neural selection are established methods;
their names do not establish algorithmic novelty or learning necessity.

The conjecture is that finite experience with actual guard, association, moving
users and energy consequences can improve complete transition choices beyond
a short-horizon ordinary predictor. The strongest simpler explanation is that
ordinary joint search handles the useful choices already, or that this path
support has little usable advantage after native execution. Neither explanation
is diagnosed by an endpoint score or a blocked-action count.

Relevant [RESEARCH topics 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8](../../RESEARCH.md#研究背景与共享认识)
at the published revision above change the design concretely: preserve native
J and risk, give O the same full-team path rights, use actual exposure and
executed motion readings, retain strong complete R/P references on the new
worlds, and interpret one fit conditionally. The structure is a central team
policy over a factored joint action, with shared transition and service
externalities; it is not a decentralized-information or credit-estimator claim.
The generator's state evolves with each policy's realized positions. A common
generation **rule** therefore does not imply common future numeric targets.

### Host, information and the actual executable decision

Keep native S7-S2, eight UAVs, thirty moving users, H3000, original charging
capacity/allocation, reward, cutoff/depletion, termination and physics. Source:
[fixed evaluator](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/308f3825e/experiments/candidates/energy_relay_benchmark/b01/evaluation.py#L86),
[guard and joint update](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/308f3825e/envs/pettingzoo/relay/routed_core.py#L3294),
[energy preparation](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/308f3825e/envs/pettingzoo/relay/energy_aware.py#L1617).
The interface is an `[8,4]` action array, xyz velocity plus dock request.
The guard checks each proposal against old dependent paths, then positions
update jointly. This is a coupling fact, not a measured dominant bottleneck.
Returning/charging members remain radio participants above native cutoff.

L/O/R all call the unchanged R destination generator at t=0,30,...,2970,
using their own carried targets and realized state. All receive current central
user/BS xy at those clocks, team own xyz/energy/availability, the public static
S2 configuration/stations and the resulting R targets. Current own positions
and production F are read every real step only for common execution. No live
route table, serving-set history, hidden user-motion goal, RNG state, future
trajectory or simulator-copy privilege enters either L or O. The critic gets
the same decision information as the actor. No communication channel is added.

The reusable R controller supplies both targets and its exact direct command.
At each clock define start xyz p_i and target xyz d_i. A normal, available
member can receive one of four modes, in fixed order:

| Mode | First fifteen real steps | Last fifteen real steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D | Follow R's direct target command | Follow R's direct target command |
| W | Hold the clock-start xyz | Follow the same R target |
| B+ | Go toward a left intermediate waypoint | Follow the same R target |
| B- | Go toward a right intermediate waypoint | Follow the same R target |

For B+/B-, waypoint xy is `(p_xy+d_xy)/2 +/- 150*n`, with n the left unit
normal to `d_xy-p_xy`; use n=(0,1) at zero horizontal displacement. Waypoint
height is `(p_z+d_z)/2`. Clip to the original map and altitude bounds. These are
attempted fifteen-step visits, with no teleportation or promise of arrival.
Use R's 30 m/s horizontal and 5 m/s vertical caps and dt, recomputing velocities
from actual observed positions every step. D returns the existing R command
without arithmetic reimplementation. At phase switch, use the original d_i;
at the next clock regenerate destinations. Intermediate waypoints never replace
R's carried-target memory. All members' modes execute simultaneously.

Thus the joint support is up to `4^8=65,536` choices, including staging groups
and simultaneous intermediate paths, rather than a single donor or one hold.
Availability/F at the decision clock forces D for that member; remaining
members still choose. New F entry overrides the proposal immediately through
the unchanged production shield (entry 0, exit .05) and cancels that member's
intermediate mode for the rest of the window. If it exits before the next
clock it follows the existing R target, as D would. No learned dock request,
station choice, return timing, charge dwell or redeployment commitment is added.

The finite modes can alias: short distances, clipping, guard stops or F may
make different labels execute identically. Keep the four nominal labels for
eligible members; do not retrospectively remove worlds or change this law.
Record nominal-path aliases, requested modes, post-shield commands and physical
displacement. These readings diagnose paid exposure, not an admission threshold.

### Competent ordinary O and model rights

O searches the same joint support. Start all-D, perform two coordinate sweeps
over all eligible members (first order starts at clock_index modulo eight,
second reverses it), testing the other three modes for each member. Then visit
all unordered eligible pairs in lexicographic order. For each pair test the
four D/W combinations against the current complete joint plan; other members
retain their incumbent modes. Accept the best joint plan and continue. This
pair pass can move beyond a single-member local optimum for staged movement.
Detours enter through both coordinate sweeps and remain in other members of
each pair test. It is a bounded local optimizer with full-team output support,
not exhaustive search over 65,536 choices or a global optimum.

Every scored joint plan uses three separate fresh-association native radio
snapshots at projected steps 10,20,30, with current users/BS frozen. Construct
the scoring environment independently from public S2 config, never by copying
the live environment. The existing R/P static delivery helper supplies the
native access, interference, association and widest-path semantics.

Precompute each eligible member's four capped, two-phase nominal thirty-step
paths and propulsion depletion using the native power function on nominal
per-step velocities. A joint candidate assembles those arrays. Existing F or
unavailable members stay at current xyz/current battery in this predictor,
as the inherited static model does; the real environment continues moving and
charging them normally. For eligible members, projected battery deducts native
propulsion consumption with no predicted charging. Model prediction does not
change F or execute native transitions. Record any projected crossing of F,
reserve or cutoff separately from actual crossings.

Score each joint plan by
`10 * sum_tau(QoS_tau - lambda_return * capped_return_cost_tau)`,
tau=10,20,30. Compute the native worst-member return deficit from projected
positions/battery and public stations, with the unchanged native coefficient,
scale, reserve and cap. Scores use float64; require improvement >1e-8 native
reward units, then prefer shorter projected xyz travel with 1e-6 m tolerance,
then keep the incumbent. This is a declared ordinary surrogate, not full native
J: it omits future users, association history, guard interventions, charging/F
transitions, first-crossing event penalties and graph-potential changes. The
complete actual evaluation retains every omitted consequence. The decision
to purchase this approximate O belongs in the same selection review; its
limitations cannot later be relabeled as a diagnosed cause or silently repaired.

L has the same access to this public model and path support. It uses the cheap
per-member kinematic/depletion arrays as features but makes **zero joint
transition-radio queries** at inference. O spends queries on its ordinary
search. Their actual information, clocks and action rights match; their finite
computational programs differ deliberately. This is a deployment-value/cost
package comparison, not equal-FLOPs or a component causal estimate.

### Learned L and initialization reference

Use established SB3 PPO with one central two-layer 128-unit tanh actor and
critic, outputting eight four-way categorical choices. All current team modes,
xyz, energy fields, R targets, current users/BS/stations and the per-member
nominal positions/batteries at 10/20/30 are supplied with fixed physical scales;
include normalized episode time. No train-fitted observation/reward normalizer
or recurrent state is introduced. Factorized sampling has full product support;
each member's logits depend on the entire current input. This can represent
coordinated deterministic choices but supplies no guarantee of learning them.

Initialize output weights to zero and each eligible member's probabilities
to `(D=.8, W=1/15, B+=1/15, B-=1/15)`. Forced-D rows have probability one.
With eight eligible members, initial probability of any non-D is
`1-.8^8=.83222784`; at least two is `.49668352`. This intentionally broad
training exposure is explicit, including potentially poor trajectories.
Evaluation is deterministic per-member argmax with D-first ties for both the
initial and final policy. Therefore the initial deterministic policy is exactly
full R by construction. Verify this equivalence in focused correctness checks;
R serves as the complete initialization reference without a fifth panel arm.

Train once on 64 full H3000 worlds: 6,400 thirty-step macro decisions and at
most 192,000 native steps. Macro reward is the unmodified sum of native step
rewards; gamma=1, GAE lambda=.95, final native endpoint has zero bootstrap.
PPO clip=.2, learning_rate=.0003, n_epochs=4, batch_size=100, ent_coef=0,
vf_coef=.5, max_grad_norm=.5, normalized advantages, no KL early stop.
Two lanes collect 100 macro steps apiece per rollout, 32 rollouts, giving
256 optimizer minibatch updates and 25,600 macro sample presentations if all
worlds complete. Preserve native termination and actual shorter exposure;
never fill missing scientific episodes with replacement seeds. Store requested
actions/log probabilities, not later shield replacements. A within-window F
override keeps the selected macro's actual complete reward. No new surrogate
gradient-masking intervention is introduced.

Use only the final endpoint, no intermediate outcome-based selection, warm
start, teacher data, validation tuning, seed sweep or automatic retry. Proposed
world seeds are 62092801-62092864; initialization/policy seed 62092871. Final
worlds are 62102801-62102808, with no earlier exact seed-token use found in
current candidates/runs on 2026-09-28; recheck identity before execution.
These are proposed inputs for selection, not an accepted launch. The fit may
fail or be uninformative; 64 worlds do not certify adequate learning.
[PPO's primary method](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347) supplies the conventional
optimizer, not a native-UAV benefit or finite-sample guarantee.
