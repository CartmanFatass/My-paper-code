## 2026-09-27 — Declaration at question-setting (no code, no run)

**Question.** For a hierarchical MARL coordinator that assigns skill labels to agents in a fixed order (HMASD's autoregressive SkillCoordinator: z_1, …, z_K sampled with prefix conditioning) and is trained on one shared k-step team return, which per-agent learning signal lets it learn non-additive role structures — relay chains where the team's service depends on the *joint* presence of a relay and its servers — and in which regimes (redundancy of roles, strength of chain binding, reward noise) does each signal win or fail?

**Contribution sentence if fully successful.** "For fixed-order skill coordinators with non-additive (chain-type) team rewards, we give a regime map showing where the exact removal difference credit D_i = R − R(−i) beats the learnability-optimal prefix-conditional baseline (COSAC's Sequential Aristocrat Utility) and where it collapses (role redundancy), and we extend the sequential aristocrat baseline from the one-step bandit to k-step skill segments executed by a learned, non-stationary low level." The map is the shared insight: the relay host (`energy_relay_benchmark`) measures its operating point (relay-hop share, D sparsity) and reads the method to use on its T-prime substrate.

**Novelty label (each idea, after first-hand reads on 2026-09-27).** Difference rewards / aristocrat utility: TRIED (Wolpert & Tumer 2001; Castellini et al., *Difference Rewards Policy Gradients*; LIDR, AAMAS 2023 — learned decomposition, team + difference mixture). Sequential prefix-conditional credit for fixed-order teams: TRIED (COSAC, Deshmukh, Subramanian, Addanki & Vlassis, arXiv 2604.17693v2, May 2026 — SeqAU, ridge-fitted additive decomposition, fictitious continuations; contextual bandit; bias grows with non-additivity; MDP extension left open). Programme record: `learned_counterfactual_agent_credit` adverse on S1 (Q − V −.018, learned critic); `expressibility_gated_renewal_credit_relay` (two-agent renewal microhost credit study, B1 complete, not advanced); RESEARCH.md line 524 (COMA-style baselines must respect the autoregressive sampling structure); chain-attributed reward on the reserve list. NEW (provisional, not found after these reads): the regime map D-versus-SeqAU on non-additive chain rewards with role redundancy, and SeqAU on k-step segments with a learned low level.

**Host (stage A, controllable, exact ground truth).** `chain_bandit`: K agents (K = 4 and 8) choose labels in fixed order from {IDLE, RELAY, SERVE_1 … SERVE_C} (C = 2 or 3) with prefix visibility (agent i sees x and z_<i), softmax policies with a shared trunk as in HMASD's decoder (tabular or small linear per agent). Context x per round: cluster demands d_c ∈ (0, 1], per-agent service quality q_ic ≥ 0 (a capability mask makes some agents useless for some clusters), a direct-link backhaul b_0 and a relayed backhaul B > b_0. Team reward R = Σ_c min(d_c, access_c, backhaul_c) + ε, with access_c = Σ_{i: z_i = SERVE_c} q_ic (capped by d_c, so doubling a server beyond demand adds nothing — the redundancy dial), backhaul_c = B if any agent chose RELAY else b_0 (the chain-binding dial is B − b_0; a stronger AND variant requires a RELAY among agents whose mask allows it), ε Gaussian noise (the noise dial). The microhost learner uses exactly this form (one shared policy, one joint update, per-agent advantages, joint normalisation), not COSAC's one-agent-at-a-time update; otherwise an estimator's advantage on the bandit could be an artefact of the update regime. ## 2026-09-27 — Correction after the peer's independent review (Root, commit f3d7cd49b; the same review reused, no new grid): the "D carries no gradient under AND coupling" claim is withdrawn

**Claim withdrawn.** The neighbours entry above (D++ paragraph) says that under AND coupling "D is exactly zero for every agent while the coalition is incomplete … so D carries **no gradient** there" and that "the first cell's exact-gradient reading will show this directly (D's expected update vanishes on those contexts)". The second half is false as a general proposition. The reviewer's counterexample is accepted and verified: two independent Bernoulli agents with logit parameters, R = a1·a2, removal to idle (c_i = 0) gives D1 = R − 0 = R on every sample, so the expected logit gradient under D1 equals the one under R, p1(1−p1)p2 > 0 (numerically at p1 = .3, p2 = .6: E[D1·∂ log π1] = E[R·∂ log π1] = .1260). Zero credit on the sampled joint actions where the coalition is incomplete is not zero expected gradient: the complete-coalition samples carry R to every pivotal member.

**What is true, restated.** Under pure AND coupling with removal-to-idle, D coincides with the shared return on every sample (every present member is pivotal), so D loses its *advantage* over the shared return — no variance reduction and no per-agent separation — not its gradient. Under m-of-K over-completeness, D gives 0 to the non-pivotal (redundant) members and R to the pivotal ones; that is the intended shaping. In the chain host's binding configuration, D_i is the chain mass that i's presence enables; on samples where the chain is incomplete every member's D is 0, and the expected gradient is what the policy's sampling of complete chains makes it. D++'s m = 6 result (G and D learn nothing, D++ learns) is a sparsity-of-pivotal-samples failure under a coevolutionary learner's random exploration (complete 6-coalitions are rare, so both fitness signals are almost always 0), which D++'s added copies remove; it says nothing about the expected policy gradient. The "third named mechanism" recorded in the D++ paragraph is therefore retitled from "D's expected update vanishes under AND" to "D degenerates to the shared return under AND coupling (no advantage) and to a sparse signal when pivotal configurations are rare under exploration". The first cell's primary reading measures D's gradient bias and per-sample variance against the exact ∇J at the three snapshots and reports them; it predicts nothing about a vanishing update. Three items stay separate, as the reviewer asks: the fixed-order suffix dependence through z_{>i} (COSAC's indirect effect; the first cell reads it as the D-vs-oracle-D gap with z_{>i} resampled), the joint normalisation of advantages (the predeclared triple calibration), and PPO clipping (not modelled in the first cell; a stated omission of the microhost → HMASD transfer).

**Consequence for the declaration.** No change to the first cell's arms, contexts, configurations or cost bound. The expectation for the high-binding × non-substitutable configuration is rewritten: D's bias is expected small and its variance no lower than the shared return's on the pivotal set, so any D advantage must come from the substitutable configuration's de-duplication signal; whether SeqAU already captures that is what the cell measures.
