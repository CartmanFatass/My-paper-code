## Short summary

- **Recommendation (W): a per-agent waypoint-commitment MARL deployment learner on the unchanged S7-S2/H3000 host.** Keep the B02 SET learner (shared-parameter MAPPO actor with the k = 10 central snapshot, same seed/worlds/exposure of 1.2 M native env-steps), but replace its per-step velocity action with a bounded next-position target (disk radius 300 m around own xy, i.e. exactly one commitment's reach at cruise 30 m/s × k = 10 × 1 s) chosen once per k = 10 native steps and executed by the ordinary planners' own straight-line go-to executor (cruise 30 m/s, 100 m height controller) under the unchanged production shield and backhaul guard; dock component unchanged; macro reward = unscaled sum of the 10 native team rewards; γ_macro = 0.99^10 (matched effective horizon); no time-limit bootstrap at H3000. The swarm learns *where each member should be next* (layout/redeployment) while *how it gets there* stays ordinary — the complement of Root's `uav_joint_transition` (fixed destinations, learned/ordinary transitions) and disjoint from `uav_persistent_service` (return admission/dwell).
- **Decision bought:** component attribution of the {waypoint + k-hold + ordinary executor} bundle against the velocity interface (matched control: SET c06 unwrapped, or C_SW under the SW wrapper if b05 L(c06) ≥ 0), with a package-reuse reading against **H_central@10** (.769 dev, same executor, same 10-step central cadence, planner-generated k-means + Hungarian targets) and a zero-fit **random-target floor** (uniform target in the same disk each k, same executor + shield).

No second seed in the first declaration.

## 3. Decision exposure

- **Eligibility.** Every agent at every k boundary, except while the production shield holds it: `apply_feedback` (`experiments/candidates/uav_service_auxiliary/b06/feedback.py:122–175`) submits (0,0,0,1) within the 160 m docking radius and overrides during return/charge (enter margin 0.0, exit margin 0.05). Dock/return requests to a valid station bypass the guard under source conditions (`NOTES.md:765`).
- **Output → choice mapping.** Actor outputs a 2-D vector in [−1,1]², scaled by r_max = 300 m and norm-clipped to the disk; target = own xy + Δ, clipped to the area; the executor computes velocity = Δ/dt capped at cruise 30 m/s, vertical toward 100 m capped at 5 m/s (`b01/heuristic.py:318–331`; cruise/height/dt at `:70–71, 78`; `configs/config_1.py:37–38` max_speed 30, time_step 1.0). Dock component: unchanged SET semantics (`envs/pettingzoo/relay/energy_aware.py:1617–1657`), repeated for the 10 steps of the commitment. DERIVATION: r_max = 300 m is the minimal non-redundant parameterisation — a farther target executes identically within one commitment, and a larger disk would make most exploration samples full-speed moves, the C_SW_FULL failure mode (saturated share .959, return cost 110.95, charger input 581 Wh, min battery .015, cutoff .25/episode; `NOTES.md:6640–6648`). Hold = Δ ≈ 0.
- **Fallback.** Shield/guard/wall substitutions are executed by the env; PPO stores the *requested* macro action and its log-probability (as in active_sensing B01, `uav_active_sensing/NOTES.md:369–372`), never the substituted one.
- **Duration.** 10 native steps = 10 s; H3000 has no early termination in S7 (`uav_active_sensing/NOTES.md:585–586`), so every commitment completes; the learner's episode is 300 macro steps; `terminated=True` at H3000 with no bootstrap.
- **Requested-vs-executed readings (declared before running).** Per commitment: |requested Δ|, executed displacement / requested, wall-clipped share, guard-blocked share (reference levels: H_central 1129/14620 checked, H_central@10 1037/14345, N 1949/11031, `NOTES.md:2517`; C_SW 920, C_SW_FULL 1074, `:6646`; SET F-mode/guard shares .18–.23 / .066–.091, `:3258`), shield-mode share, hold share (|Δ| < 30 m), target persistence (autocorrelation of successive Δ), heading concentration R_map / R_spawnrel at t = 0 and by phase (b04 readers; row A: e_ROT 158°/146°, `NOTES.md:5797–5991`), by-corner split, speed decomposition, first service with the −1 fix, served-within-60/120.
  - *Nonactivation:* median |requested Δ| < 30 m or shield-mode share > .5 — the interface was barely exercised; no claim about the interface.

  - *Active adverse intervention:* executed/requested > .8 and guard-blocked share ≤ SET's, yet QoS < control with return cost ≫ 1.5 or depletion > 0 — the executor bundle drains energy (C_SW_FULL pattern) or the layout choices are worse; the energy readings separate the two.
