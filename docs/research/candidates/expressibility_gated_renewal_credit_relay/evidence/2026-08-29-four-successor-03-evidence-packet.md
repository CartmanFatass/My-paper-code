## Frozen question

Do any of three prospectively fixed multi-waiter, multi-step finite host
families establish all prerequisites for an association-aware relay
comparison, or does a competent same-information generic conditional-Q or
Rao-Blackwellized estimator absorb the decision-relevant association content?

The three families, in fixed order, are overlapping many-to-one contention,
serial two-hop complementarity, and a censored substitution chain. Each may
receive only one smallest balanced construction. The first full survivor would
end the search; otherwise all three are rejected without tuning or a fourth
family.

## Prospective contract

Every host has exactly three persistent agents, two eligible waiters within one
generic information stratum, at least two post-source physical transitions,
balanced ordered-role marginals, bounded external utility, and a scarce
decision evaluator. Ordinary GAE, a competent generic pair-aware conditional-Q
or Rao-Blackwellized arm, and the association candidate share action-time
information, raw trajectories, edge keys, downstream objects, policy/model
capacity, useful work, preconditioning, trust map, update count, and evaluator.

If the candidate consumes an edge locator, event key, decomposition,
transformation, model prediction, or sufficient statistic, the generic arm
receives the same object and may condition or Rao-Blackwellize it. No target may
encode identity as reward, use a privileged counterfactual, weaken a critic,
or hide additional work.

A survivor requires all of the following:

- controlled consequence-distinct waiter support that collapses under a
  source-path sever;
- competent shared representation and comparator controls;
- normalized orthogonal association-update residual at least `0.10` after the
  common preconditioner, plus a different actual scarce decision;
- association-minus-GAE and association-minus-generic favorable-allocation and
  normalized-utility gains each at least `0.10`, with positive exact balanced-
  panel lower bounds; and
- native-minus-severed association-versus-generic utility excess at least
  `0.05`, again with a positive exact lower bound.

Generic-target equality, update collinearity, evaluator-margin failure, or
failed localization stops before relay and sampled training.

## Record-wise equality under review

For action record `r`, let `S_r` be its policy score and let `C_r` be the exact
scalar used by the association update after every admitted binding,
decomposition, transformation, normalization, or routing operation. With the
same downstream objects and transformation available to the generic arm,
define

`Cbar_r = E[C_r | H_r, A_r, permitted conditioning objects]`.

The finite-tape tower-property claim is

`E[sum_r S_r C_r] = E[sum_r S_r Cbar_r]`.

If accepted, this equality holds before shared-parameter summation and common
preconditioning. Identical preconditioned updates produce identical trust-map
parameters and scarce decisions, so the association arm cannot pass the
noncollinearity, allocation, utility, or localization gates against the
generic arm.

The strongest objection is comparator overreach: if `C_r` is deliberately
biased to exploit finite-step curvature, conditional-Q of external utility
need not have this direction. The frozen response is that the generic arm may
Rao-Blackwellize the same transformed target and receives the same edge and
causal objects. The reviewer must decide whether that is a legitimate
same-information generic comparator under the prospective contract or whether
it silently turns the generic arm into the association method itself.

## Three canonical finite surfaces

All constructions use all six ordered source-waiter assignments equally,
binary source action at behavior probability `1/2`, balanced content sign
`c in {-1,+1}`, and minimal source features `(1,c)`. Persistent identities
never directly pay reward.

### Overlapping contention

An activated source record writes a pending job into its true waiter; that job
and a background record compete for one later queue slot. For `c=+1`, activation
changes utility from `0` to `1`; for `c=-1`, it changes utility from `1` to
`0`. The pathwise contrast and exact external conditional-Q contrast are both
`c`, producing update `(0,1/4)` in both candidate and generic arms.

Claimed exact comparison: orthogonal residual `0`, different scarce decisions
`0`, allocation gain `0`, normalized-utility gain `0`, and native-minus-
severed excess `0`.

### Serial two-hop complementarity

Activation first marks the true waiter; the waiter's later open/block action
then permits or prevents a third agent's downstream commit. Utility is paid
only after the ordered two-hop path. Its balanced utility table is again
`0 -> 1` for `c=+1` and `1 -> 0` for `c=-1`. Pathwise contrast and exact
conditional-Q contrast are both `c`, yielding `(0,1/4)`. Contributions from
later shared action records are identical across arms.

Claimed exact comparison: orthogonal residual `0`, different scarce decisions
`0`, allocation gain `0`, normalized-utility gain `0`, and native-minus-
severed excess `0`.

### Censored substitution

After activation the original waiter persists, the record expires, or the
third agent substitutes as holder, with all three modes equally weighted and
unknown at action time. Activation has effect `c` under persistence or
replacement and `0` under expiry. The pathwise target is heterogeneous, but
its action-time conditional mean is `(2/3)c`; candidate and generic updates
are both `(0,1/6)`.

Claimed exact comparison: orthogonal residual `0`, different scarce decisions
`0`, allocation gain `0`, normalized-utility gain `0`, and native-minus-
severed excess `0`. The conditional target also removes lifetime-mode variance
retained by the pathwise target.

## Competing readings for independent review

1. **Exact generic absorption.** The tower-property equality and frozen parity
   contract are valid. All three families contain real physical associations
   but none supplies decision content beyond competent generic conditional
   credit; no relay or CM is warranted.
2. **Comparator absorbs the treatment by definition.** Rao-Blackwellizing the
   transformed association target may make the generic arm association-aware
   in everything but name. If so, the frozen comparison is invalid or tests a
   vacuous closure rather than specialized credit.
3. **Biased finite-step coordination is a survivor route.** A relation-indexed
   surrogate can choose a better finite-radius direction than the true
   gradient in a nonconcave host. This reading must explain why the effect is
   association-specific rather than generic optimizer geometry or task-
   specific shaping, why the same-object generic arm cannot reproduce it, and
   how all `0.10`/`0.05` gates pass.
4. **Exact analysis suppresses the intended advantage.** Association
   factorization might help a finite-data or capacity-limited learned critic.
   That is scientifically plausible but is a different approximation-
   efficiency question; weakening the generic comparator inside the present
   exact cycle would manufacture the gap.
5. **Canonical instances are too minimal.** A larger topology might create a
   nonseparable interaction. The bounded search intentionally admits only one
   smallest construction per family; a larger or tuned construction is not a
   repair and any future one must still survive comparator closure.

## Common dependencies and claim ceiling

The result depends on exact finite enumeration, complete permitted event
histories, publicly shared edge keys, one population update, a common trust
map, fixed three-agent membership, and competent generic conditioning. The
common failure risk for finite-host credit research is to mistake a causally
meaningful decomposition for additional decision information. Expressibility,
large path effects, balance, and clean severing can all coexist with generic
Q-sufficiency.

At most this cycle can determine whether these three smallest balanced finite
topologies admit a pre-relay certificate under the frozen exact-population and
parity contract. It cannot establish general impossibility, learned-estimator
or finite-data equivalence, multi-update behavior, variable-population value,
relay value, fixed-`k` superiority, transfer, UAV or continuous-control
relevance, safety, deployment, or general credit.
