## Frozen question

Can a prospectively defined fixed-population host with exactly three persistent
agent identities first establish both of the following, without fitting or
inspecting any identity-relay result?

1. balanced, consequence-distinct eligible waiter identities within one
   generic pre-action information, cue/sign, lag, propensity, and action
   stratum; and
2. positive fixed-token allocation or bounded-utility headroom for a
   prospectively specified oracle that uses exactly the identity information
   available to ordinary GAE?

If either prerequisite is absent, the answer is a bounded host null and the
executable branch stops before any cue-preserving identity-only relay
comparison. If both are present, this cycle may establish only that the host is
informative enough to justify a later relay discriminator; it does not test or
support relay superiority itself.

## Candidate scientific object held neutral at scope freeze

The smallest candidate is a three-agent balanced-crossover versioned-handoff
microhost. Each persistent identity appears equally often as the later joiner.
For each joiner, each of the other two identities appears equally often as the
eligible earlier waiter under an otherwise identical generic pre-action
stratum. A prospectively fixed directed compatibility schedule may make one
ordered waiter-joiner pair favorable and the other unfavorable for that
joiner, provided the complete schedule balances every physical identity's
favorable and unfavorable marginal counts. Thus neither waiter identity,
joiner identity, generic cue, lag, propensity, nor request frequency alone can
carry the consequence sign.

The actor-visible information must be identical for the oracle and ordinary
GAE and must include the ordered physical identity pair if that pair is the
purported association. The oracle may use only a mapping frozen before outcome
generation and expressible by the same policy head; it may not see realized or
counterfactual consequences, a hidden compatibility flag, a future suffix, or
an outcome-derived label at action time. Ordinary GAE must receive the same
features, actions, rewards, training opportunities, update count, and
evaluation choices. A headroom result is invalid if it is manufactured by
withholding pair identity from GAE, giving the oracle a more expressive policy,
using an untrained or deliberately incompetent GAE comparator, or selecting
the compatibility map or thresholds after observing results.

The proposed evaluation unit is a fixed-token choice between two eligible
ordered waiter-joiner opportunities that match on every generic observable but
differ in physical waiter identity and prospectively assigned consequence.
The same token count, renewal count, common evaluation worlds, and bounded
utility definition apply to both oracle and GAE. Identity support is assessed
from the complete counterfactual consequence table before learning; headroom
is assessed from actual token allocation and bounded utility, not probability
movement alone.

This construction is a candidate to be scrutinized, not a favored route. It is
rejected if its pair consequences are arbitrary label injection with no
ordered waiter mechanism, if balance fails, if the generic stratum changes, if
the oracle has extra information or capacity, or if a competent same-information
ordinary GAE has no prospective allocation/utility gap.

## Competing explanations and predictions

1. **Association-support exists.** Ordered pair identity changes the earlier
   waiter's bounded consequence after the later joiner's action, while all
   generic observables and identity marginals are balanced. The complete
   precommitted consequence table contains within-stratum sign or magnitude
   differences and a legal identity-only permutation.
2. **Identity is merely a relabeled generic cue.** Compatibility can be inferred
   from a cue, global identity quality, role frequency, or outcome leakage. It
   predicts that balancing identities or conditioning on the generic stratum
   removes the apparent pair effect, or that the oracle succeeds only because
   it sees extra information.
3. **Ordinary GAE already exhausts the same information.** Given a policy head
   that can express the ordered mapping and a competent frozen learning
   protocol, ordinary full-return GAE ranks the fixed-token choices as well as
   the oracle. It predicts zero oracle-minus-GAE allocation and bounded-utility
   headroom even though consequence-distinct pair support exists.
4. **Finite-update/noise headroom.** The ordered consequence is present and
   policy-expressible, but sparse delayed full-return credit leaves a
   prospectively measurable gap after the same bounded update budget. It
   predicts oracle-over-GAE allocation and utility headroom without implying
   that an association relay closes it.
5. **Representational or training deprivation.** A gap appears because GAE is
   denied pair-interaction features, adequate samples, a competent baseline,
   or comparable optimization. It predicts disappearance under an
   information- and capacity-matched GAE control and invalidates the host as a
   credit discriminator.
6. **Request/update geometry or evaluator saturation.** Scores move but do not
   cross the one-token rank boundary, or discretization fixes the same choice.
   It predicts probability differences without allocation or bounded-utility
   headroom and therefore fails the prerequisite.
7. **Constructed-host non-informativeness.** Three identities are present but
   no lawful ordered waiter action changes a bounded downstream consequence.
   It predicts a zero within-stratum pair contrast or a consequence that is
   independent of the waiter mechanism.

## Smallest discriminator and outcome branches

The frozen discriminator is a support-then-headroom gate, not a relay
comparison. Neutral source analysis and the fresh Innovator first test whether
the balanced three-agent construction is scientifically coherent and whether
repository evidence already answers it. Only if an executable observation is
decision-changing will a later EM milestone freeze exact source/test paths,
configuration, data, RNG, command, thresholds, predictions, and artifacts for
one CM.

- **Support absent or confounded:** record the bounded null, stop before CM,
  and recommend no relay experiment in this host family. This does not prove
  association credit impossible elsewhere.
- **Support present, oracle headroom absent:** record that the host exposes
  identity consequences but does not leave scarce-allocation or utility room
  beyond competent same-information GAE; stop before relay.
- **Both pass with valid controls:** establish only an informative host
  prerequisite and recommend a subsequent, separately frozen identity-only
  relay comparison. No relay arm is run in this cycle.
- **Evidence incomplete but executable observation can change the investment
  decision:** freeze one bounded EM-to-CM discriminator and interpret its
  returned observation scientifically.
- **Scope or technical validity fails:** retain no scientific recommendation;
  name the missing premise or recovery fact.

No branch is selected before the fresh Innovator is accepted and interpreted.

## Neutral retained grounding

- B1 established a real ordered source-to-waiter execution path, bounded
  consequence exposure, later-record expressibility, and a small selectivity
  change. Those facts motivate the mechanism but do not establish physical
  identity support or utility value.
- Across all twelve B1 confirmation roots, intact and ordinary GAE had exactly
  equal fixed-token allocation and bounded utility; the registered
  opposite-type binding cut does not isolate identity because it changes
  JOINT/SOLO consequence sign.
- B1's two-agent world is invariant to physical waiter identity once type and
  lag are fixed. This explains why the new question prospectively requires a
  different, balanced, at-least-three-agent host rather than a B1 rerun.
- These are historical premises and failure risks, not the result of this
  cycle. The new cycle must independently establish whether its proposed host
  and oracle comparison are scientifically informative.

## Claim ceiling

At most, this cycle may establish or refute balanced ordered-pair consequence
support and same-information oracle fixed-token allocation or bounded-utility
headroom in one prospectively frozen three-agent, finite-horizon, bounded,
single-update microhost. Passing both gates would justify only a later
identity-only relay discriminator; it would not show that a relay, estimator,
or learned policy closes the gap. No end-to-end variable-agent-count,
fixed-`k` superiority, multi-update stability, learned estimator or gate, UAV
or continuous-control value, safety, or general credit claim may be imported.
