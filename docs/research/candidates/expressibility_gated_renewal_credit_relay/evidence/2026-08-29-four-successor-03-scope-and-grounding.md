## Decision question

This cycle performs a bounded pre-relay search, not a T3 rerun. It asks whether
any of three prospectively fixed, genuinely different multi-waiter and
multi-step host families can simultaneously establish:

1. at least two consequence-distinct eligible waiters within one generic
   action-time information stratum, with balanced identity marginals;
2. exact, material scarce-allocation and normalized bounded-utility headroom;
3. a material noncollinear association-aware decision update; and
4. source-localized excess that is not reproduced by a static identity or
   generic conditional-value denoiser.

The decisive comparison is under equal action-time information and useful
work among ordinary GAE, a competent generic pair-aware conditional-Q or
Rao-Blackwellized estimator, and one association-aware credit candidate. A
relay comparison becomes admissible only after one family passes every gate.
If no family survives, that bounded no-survivor result answers this cycle.

## Protected evidence and non-goals

The clean T3 result is valid terminal science. It established balanced,
source-localized ordered-waiter support but found oracle-minus-GAE actual
allocation and normalized utility exactly zero at every confirmation root. Its
positive favorable-allocation probability shift was only
`2.2238206119185033e-05`, and source-localized native excess was about
`0.000543`. T3 remains an exhausted failure-mode fixture. This cycle does not
rerun, extend, reseed, tune, relabel, weaken, or reinterpret its host,
comparators, evaluator, thresholds, roots, prompts, operations, conversations,
or result.

This cycle is not a claim about general impossibility, learned-critic failure,
multi-update superiority, variable populations, fixed-`k` superiority,
end-to-end relay value, UAV or continuous-control relevance, safety,
deployment, or general credit. It does not weaken the generic estimator, hide
pair/history information from a comparator, add task-specific reward shaping,
or give the association arm counterfactual or causal labels unavailable to the
other arms. It does not run sampled training, allocate relay credit, or collect
result-bearing observations before an exact survivor is frozen and writer
ownership is transferred to a fresh CM.

## Scientific object and estimands

Each family is a finite-horizon stochastic game with at least three persistent
agents, at least two eligible waiters for one source decision, and at least two
causally relevant transitions after that decision before bounded utility is
resolved. The action-time history is `H`, the source action is `A`, the
external bounded return is `U`, and the predeclared scarce decision statistic
is `L`. The exposure clock begins at the source decision and ends only after
the candidate waiter path can affect `U`.

The three learning objects share one policy class, ancestor, behavior data,
action support, information, batch, update count, trust map, and native
evaluator:

- `GAE`: ordinary full-external-return GAE with a competent value function for
  the frozen state/history representation;
- `GENERIC-Q`: a competent pair-aware conditional-Q or Rao-Blackwellized
  estimator trained or computed with the same raw histories, downstream
  observations, capacity, and useful-work budget available to the candidate;
- `ASSOCIATION`: one edge-aware source-to-waiter credit construction that
  replaces rather than adds to the source target and has no extra action-time
  input, reward, counterfactual, model call, or optimization exposure.

For every family, exact enumeration integrates over the finite history,
behavior-action, and exogenous-tape distribution. It reports each arm's
population update, the common-trust post-update policy, native scarce
allocation, normalized bounded utility, and a matched source-severed panel.
Exact enumeration is a feasibility certificate, not an algorithm experiment.

## Fixed three-family search law

The search has exactly three ordered family slots. Each receives one canonical
minimal construction and one prospective analytic audit. A failed family is
not tuned, repaired, replaced, reseeded, or re-entered under another name. The
first family to pass all gates ends the search and is the only possible
survivor. If none passes, no fourth family is admitted in this cycle.

### Family 1: overlapping many-to-one contention

Multiple source records recruit or defer different eligible waiters whose
service windows overlap at a shared downstream queue, port, or capacity token.
The correct source-waiter edge changes later congestion or service, while
unrelated overlapping returns create attribution noise. This family tests
whether causal edge matching can beat a generic conditional-value estimator
when several live handoffs interfere at the same scarce resource.

### Family 2: serial two-hop complementarity

The source decision changes one of several eligible waiters, and that waiter's
later action enables or blocks a second downstream hop. Bounded utility depends
on the ordered two-hop path through an AND, threshold, or complementary-service
condition rather than one immediate compatibility lookup. This family tests
whether delayed relational complementarity produces a decision direction that
ordinary or generic conditional value does not already supply.

### Family 3: censored substitution chain

After the source decision, an eligible waiter may persist, expire, or be
replaced before a later service opportunity, and a substitute may complete the
same generic task. The observed history contains the same identities,
survival/replacement events, and service outcomes for every arm. This family
tests whether correct source-to-original-waiter association under censoring has
material credit value beyond a competent history- and pair-aware generic
estimator.

The families differ in causal topology: simultaneous contention, serial
complementarity, and censoring/substitution. Parameter changes inside one
topology do not create another family.

## Canonical-construction rule

For each slot, use the smallest finite instance satisfying the family
definition: the fewest agents above two, binary source action, two eligible
waiters, the fewest post-action steps above one, and bounded external utility
in `[0,1]` after common additive costs. Generic pre-action cue, sign,
propensity, readiness, budget, clock, and action support are held fixed across
the favorable and unfavorable waiter alternatives. Persistent IDs may index a
relation but cannot directly pay reward. Every physical consequence must be
derived through the stated transitions.

Balance each identity across favorable and unfavorable relations and across
nonparticipant roles. Use the uniform behavior policy unless a family has a
unique symmetry-preserving alternative; any such alternative must be fixed
before evaluating updates. Exogenous branches are crossed with pair and action
cells. A family may choose its minimal bounded transition probabilities and
utility table only by symmetry and causal-support requirements, never by
observed comparator performance. If those requirements do not identify one
canonical construction, the family is rejected as underspecified rather than
tuned.

## Equal-information and useful-work contract

At action time all arms see exactly `H`, including any ordered IDs, public
event history, survival/replacement state, and generic features admitted by
the canonical host. None sees future outcomes, a hidden compatibility label,
counterfactual worlds, or an association answer. During learning, every arm
receives the same raw trajectories and downstream observations. If the
association operator consumes an edge locator, event key, decomposition, model
prediction, or sufficient statistic, `GENERIC-Q` receives the same object and
may condition or Rao-Blackwellize on it within the same work budget.

The arms use equal source rows, trace rows, actor/value/model evaluations,
full-batch passes, updates, parameterization, displacement or KL trust budget,
and evaluator calls. Candidate-specific useful work cannot be hidden as free
metadata processing. An advantage that requires more information, a richer
head, a privileged counterfactual, a weaker critic, or extra model/optimizer
work is an invalid comparison, not a survivor.

`GENERIC-Q` is competent only if its exact conditional target is internally
consistent on the declared finite information set and its population residual
is zero within exact arithmetic or `1e-12`. When finite-data estimation is part
of the family, enumerate the estimator's full finite-sample distribution under
the same data budget; do not replace it with an intentionally underfit critic.

## Prospective gates

A family survives only if every gate below passes in order.

### Structural support

- two eligible waiters in the same generic stratum have different controlled
  bounded consequences for the same source action;
- waiter and joiner identity marginals cancel, and single-agent main effects
  cannot explain the contrast;
- changing the source action changes the selected true waiter's physical
  transition before utility resolves; and
- severing only that transition collapses the ordered consequence contrast.

### Comparator and representation competence

- the shared policy class represents every required scarce choice;
- ordinary GAE and `GENERIC-Q` use competent declared values/conditionals and
  the same action-time information as `ASSOCIATION`;
- all work, exposure, trust, and evaluator controls pass; and
- no arm obtains a reward, label, model, capacity, or information advantage.

### Material noncollinearity

Let `g_A` and `g_Q` be the exact population source-update vectors for
`ASSOCIATION` and `GENERIC-Q` after the same preconditioner. The association
update passes only if its normalized orthogonal residual

`||g_A - proj_{g_Q}(g_A)|| / ||g_A||`

is at least `0.10` and the post-trust-map policies make at least one different
predeclared scarce decision. A zero vector, sign-only diagnostic, magnitude-only
change removed by the common trust map, or probability change that never
reaches a decision is a failure.

### Native headroom

Against both GAE and `GENERIC-Q`, `ASSOCIATION` must achieve:

- exact mean favorable scarce-allocation gain at least `0.10` of the full
  favorable/unfavorable span;
- exact mean normalized bounded-utility gain at least `0.10`;
- a strictly positive exact lower bound for both quantities over every
  predeclared balanced symmetry panel; and
- competent baseline allocation above chance or its family-specific symmetric
  null, so a broken baseline cannot manufacture headroom.

### Source-localized excess and generic-denoising falsifier

Preserve histories, pair contexts, outcomes, work, and evaluator, but sever the
physical waiter path and pay an action- and context-conditioned static outcome.
The native association-minus-`GENERIC-Q` normalized-utility gap minus the
severed gap must have exact mean at least `0.05` and a strictly positive exact
lower bound over every balanced panel. If `GENERIC-Q` matches the association
target or update, if the severed panel reproduces the gain, or if a generic
conditional-value estimator obtains the same native decisions and utility, the
family stops as generic denoising or representation rather than relay support.

## Result-blind branch map

- **Full survivor:** the first family in fixed order passes every gate. Freeze
  only that family. If an executable exact observation remains answer-changing,
  create one fresh Sol/high CM from the exact EM commit; otherwise synthesize
  the analytic certificate. Recommend `CONTINUE` only toward one separately
  frozen pre-relay observation. A relay arm remains unauthorized.
- **Generic estimator wins materially:** GAE leaves at least `0.10`
  allocation/utility headroom, but competent `GENERIC-Q` closes it or matches
  `ASSOCIATION`. This supports at most `NARROW` toward a distinct generic
  denoising/representation question; it does not support association relay.
- **Support without decision headroom:** consequence-distinct waiters exist,
  but updates are collinear or actual allocation, utility, or localization
  margins fail. Restore the `PARK` recommendation for EGRCR.
- **No admissible construction:** a family cannot satisfy its own canonical,
  balance, information, or work contract without tuning or privilege. Reject
  that family. If all three fail, return a bounded no-survivor synthesis and
  recommend `PARK`.
- **Invalid evidence surface:** an exact premise cannot be established, a
  comparator is incompetent, or the scope must change. Do not infer science;
  retain the same WORK with the exact missing premise or end with no
  recommendation if the required review cannot be completed.

## Resource envelope, observation rounds, and stop conditions

The search uses at most three family audits, one fresh strict Innovator, one
fresh strict Convergence, and at most one CM exact-enumeration observation if a
family first survives the analytic gates. Each Pro operation receives one send
and ordinary observation windows of at most 45 minutes per call until natural
completion or a nonterminal transport state. No provider fallback is allowed.

EM performs no result-bearing command. If a survivor requires CM, its later
contract may authorize one exact-enumeration command using one CPU worker, at
most 20 wall minutes, and at most 2,000,000 enumerated
`(history, action, exogenous branch)` tuples. Wall time and tuple count are the
acceptance-critical resource metrics; no universal RSS cap is imposed. CM must
prove those counters on a non-result fixture before the sole material command.
No sampled training, relay arm, sweep, restart, threshold repair, family
replacement, or post-result enlargement is permitted.

Stop a family immediately on missing support, extra information/work,
incompetent comparator, generic-target equality, update collinearity,
evaluator-margin failure, or failed source localization. Stop the family
search at the first full survivor or after all three fixed slots. The scope is
invalidated if any route reuses T3 coordinates, weakens a comparator, changes
the external reward to encode the answer, requires unavailable private state,
or cannot be represented as a finite exact pre-relay audit.

## Claim ceiling

At most this cycle can show that one of three frozen abstract host families
does or does not admit a prospectively specified pre-relay certificate under
the stated information, work, trust, evaluator, and margin contracts. A
no-survivor result means only that these three canonical families did not
justify association-aware relay investment. It is not a theorem over every
multi-agent host or credit method. A survivor would justify only one bounded
exact pre-relay observation, not algorithm superiority or integration.
