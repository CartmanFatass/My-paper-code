## Frozen scientific question

Can a scientifically justified, bounded same-information discriminator expose
association-specific ordered waiter credit beyond ordinary GAE in the current
EGRCR object? Or do the retained exact zero intact-versus-GAE utility and
allocation contrast, together with the apparent lack of consequence-distinct
waiter identities within a matched generic context, exhaust what this host can
identify?

This cycle does not repeat B1 merely to improve attribution. A new executable
discriminator is eligible only if neutral analysis first establishes both of
these premises independently of relay-credit outcomes:

1. at least two eligible waiter identities have different bounded
   consequences while sharing one generic pre-action context, cue/sign,
   propensity band, lag, action, and observable information set; and
2. a prospective same-information oracle association has actual scarce-renewal
   allocation or bounded-utility headroom over ordinary GAE in that stratum.

If either premise is absent, the smallest answer-changing observation is the
static support/headroom audit itself and no CM work is warranted.

## Scientific object and estimand

The scientific object is the registered finite EGRCR-B1 two-agent,
single-later-record-update renewal host. An earlier waiter requests at physical
tick 2; a later joiner request occurs at lag 1 or 2; legal same-snapshot worlds
hold information fixed; outcomes are bounded through tick 8 inside ten-tick
blocks. The treatment is replacement of the later joiner's ordinary GAE atom
by the correctly associated, centered ordered waiter interaction. The primary
comparators are unchanged ordinary GAE and a scientifically valid wrong-waiter
association cut that preserves generic cue/sign, pre-action information,
action, propensity, lag, marginal consequence distribution, work, count, and
update scale.

The decision estimand is a paired root-level difference in native bounded
utility and actual fixed-token scarce renewal allocation, not probability
movement alone. The registered confirmation has 12 independent roots and 128
fixed-token evaluation dyads per arm/context; root is the inferential unit.
Source-to-target action change and bounded waiter consequence are necessary
mechanistic checks, but acceptance requires a downstream allocation or utility
contrast. The exposure clock begins at the ordered earlier-waiter request,
passes through the later joiner record, and ends at the bounded outcome
boundary; no later or reverse credit is imported.

## Neutral competing explanations and distinct predictions

1. **Association-specific incremental credit.** Correct waiter identity carries
   action-changing information unavailable to ordinary GAE even after generic
   cue/sign and marginals are held fixed. It predicts a legal within-stratum
   wrong-identity derangement, prospective oracle headroom, and a correct-binding
   advantage on actual allocation or utility rather than only selectivity.
2. **Generic dense or lower-variance shaping.** The relay re-encodes favorable
   context already present in return. It predicts probability or covariance
   movement without intact-over-GAE allocation or utility gain; an
   information-equivalent generic target should match the apparent benefit.
3. **Registered-cut anti-supervision.** B1's opposite-type cut changes the
   generic cue-to-consequence sign as well as association. It predicts intact
   can beat that cut even when physical waiter identity is irrelevant, while a
   cue/sign-preserving identity-only cut becomes inert or unsupported.
4. **Request/update-rate geometry.** Arm-local normalization, fixed
   displacement, discretization, or common-uniform request changes alter
   selectivity without crossing the scarce allocation boundary. It predicts
   exact allocation and utility equality despite proximal probability changes.
5. **Missing downstream identity exposure.** Within every matched generic
   stratum, nominal waiter IDs have identical consequences or ordinary return
   already exposes all consequence variation. It predicts no legal
   consequence-changing identity derangement and no association-oracle
   headroom.
6. **Non-informative or saturated host.** GAE and intact rank every scarce
   choice identically in the finite evaluator. It predicts an exact
   intact-versus-GAE allocation/utility tie even when the relay is expressive;
   progress would require a prospectively justified different host, not a
   repeat or additional seeds.
7. **Technical or observational artifact.** The recorded null could reflect a
   broken action path, missing edges, unequal work, invalid RNG pairing, or an
   incomplete result. It predicts a traceable contract violation in the source
   or retained result. Absence of such a violation leaves this explanation
   unsupported; the historical wrapper receipt limitation alone does not erase
   a complete scientific surface.

## Smallest discriminator and decision branches

The frozen smallest discriminator is a two-part support-and-headroom test. The
support part asks whether the current host contains a cue/sign- and
information-matched pair of eligible waiter identities with different bounded
consequences. The headroom part asks whether, before fitting or inspecting a
relay outcome, a same-information oracle identity assignment changes a scarce
renewal allocation and improves bounded utility over ordinary GAE. Only if both
parts pass may the cycle define one identity-key derangement that changes no
generic cue, sign, marginal label distribution, count, work, scale, data, or
RNG and request one bounded EM-to-CM observation.

- If both premises pass and the identity-only contrast could reverse the
  investment judgment, freeze one executable observation. A positive result
  can support only continued or narrowed work on this finite host; it does not
  establish a general estimator advantage.
- If the current source/result already refutes either premise, complete the
  cycle statically and make the no-CM basis explicit. The supported decision
  impact may be to narrow, park, close, fuse, or spin off a prerequisite host
  question, but no branch is selected at scope freeze.
- If evidence is internally inconsistent or technically incomplete in a way
  that changes the estimand, retain the scientific question without inferring
  a lifecycle action and name the exact recovery evidence.
- If association improves only probabilities, beats only a generic-cue-broken
  cut, or ties GAE on allocation and utility, it is not answer-changing under
  this cycle's acceptance.

## Neutral repository grounding

- Calibration established an ordered source-to-execution and first-action
  path, later-record expression, useful signed interaction, and actual
  common-uniform action changes. These checks show expressibility; they do not
  by themselves show allocation value.
- Across all 12 confirmation roots, intact and ordinary GAE had exactly equal
  native bounded utility (`D_IG_N = 0`) and exactly equal fixed-token allocation
  (`Psi_G = 0`), both with zero-width intervals. Native selectivity still moved
  in favor of intact by mean `0.004711` with 95% interval
  `[0.002960, 0.006462]`.
- Intact exceeded the registered binding cut in native bounded utility by mean
  `0.144444` with 95% interval `[0.076664, 0.212225]`, but the cut maps JOINT
  packets to SOLO packets and conversely within older-ID/lag/action/propensity
  groups. It therefore changes generic consequence sign as well as binding and
  is not an identity-only comparator.
- Static source inspection finds `older_id` controls the physical role but not
  the bounded world consequence. For a fixed type and lag, both waiter IDs have
  identical interaction values; consequence varies with JOINT/SOLO type and
  lag. This is current grounding, not yet the successor's terminal judgment.
- The retained B1 result exposes the complete registered scientific surface,
  matched counts/work, 306,910 physical ticks, cap compliance, and no recorded
  anomaly. Its historical wrapper lacks a separate child-exit and peak-RSS
  receipt, a limitation to report rather than authorization to rerun B1.

## Claim ceiling

At most, this cycle may make a finite-host, two-agent, one-update statement
about whether same-information ordered waiter association changes the named
bounded utility and fixed-token allocation contrasts. It cannot import
association-specific algorithm value, an end-to-end variable-`k` result,
fixed-`k` superiority, multi-update stability, a learned estimator or gate,
UAV or continuous-control value, safety, or general source-to-target credit.
An exhausted current host would be a bounded negative result, not a general
impossibility theorem.
