## Objection 1: comparator overreach

**Objection.** Giving the generic arm the public edge key and allowing it to
Rao-Blackwellize the candidate's transformed scalar may absorb the treatment
by definition.

**Disposition: bounded and not surviving.** The objection correctly identifies
the narrow estimand. This cycle does not test whether discovering or exposing
the relation is useful; it tests whether specialized source-to-waiter credit
adds irreducible decision value once representation, evidence, useful work,
and optimizer geometry are common. The prospectively frozen contract expressly
gives the generic arm every relation, transformation, routing object, and
sufficient statistic consumed by the candidate. Under that containment test,
record-wise conditional expectation is the competent comparator, not a
post-result strengthening.

This makes the conclusion conditional on a common represented relation. It
does not show that relation extraction is valueless. A representation question
would be a distinct cycle and cannot rescue relay credit here.

## Objection 2: biased finite-step coordination

**Objection.** A relation-indexed biased surrogate can be noncollinear with
external-return Q and can outperform the true-gradient direction at a finite
radius in a nonconcave coordination objective.

**Disposition: real alternative, wrong object for this comparison.** If the
same biased scalar and relation object are available to the generic arm, it can
condition that scalar and reproduce the exact population score vector. If the
candidate alone receives the surrogate, a gain identifies candidate-specific
target or optimizer geometry, not irreducible association credit. A target
constructed directly from the favorable-action table is also task-specific
shaping unless derived by a prospective generic rule; a generic rule is again
available to both arms.

No admissible smallest construction remains that can approach the `0.10`
decision margins or `0.05` localization margin without withholding an object,
using candidate-only curvature or preconditioning, differentiating through a
candidate-only graph, or hard-coding external utility. The finite-radius
counterexample remains useful as a future optimizer question, not as an EGRCR
survivor.

## Objection 3: canonical minimality

**Objection.** The smallest construction may be too simple; a larger topology
could introduce a nonseparable association interaction.

**Disposition: claim limit, not repair.** The minimal hosts do not fail from
weak support. The uncensored effect spans the full `0` to `1` utility range,
both signs and all six ordered assignments are balanced, two eligible waiters
are present, the relation key is explicit, and the serial family already
contains a true two-hop consequence. They fail at exact decision sufficiency.

The frozen search law permits one smallest construction per family and forbids
post-result enlargement. A larger host with constrained compute, imperfect
relation inference, simultaneous nonseparable actions, limited capacity, or
adaptive feedback is a new scientific object. This synthesis makes no claim
about its result.

## Objection 4: the exact generic estimator is too strong

**Objection.** A learned or capacity-limited critic may leave headroom that an
association factorization can exploit.

**Disposition: strongest surviving future alternative.** Exact conditioning is
intentionally strong because this cycle asks whether irreducible population
decision content exists before sampled training. A win caused only by an
imperfect generic critic is evidence about approximation or sample efficiency,
not information absent from conditional Q. Weakening the current comparator
would manufacture the requested gap.

This alternative remains scientifically live outside the present claim. The
smallest distinct reentry is a finite-data, finite-compute relational-
approximation comparison with prospectively matched parameters, trajectories,
edge access, optimizer state, updates, FLOPs, memory, trust geometry, and
native allocation/utility endpoints. The generic critic must be able to
represent the factorized solution. Any gain would be described as
representation, denoising, or approximation efficiency rather than relay
value.

## Objection 5: shared parameters and nonlinear learning

**Objection.** Parameter sharing, normalization, Adam/RMSProp state, clipping,
stochastic thresholds, or multiple adaptive updates can separate methods even
when population first moments match.

**Disposition: excluded here and retained as a claim limit.** The equality is
record-wise before shared gradients are summed, so ordinary parameter sharing
does not break it. The frozen cycle uses exact population quantities, a common
preconditioner, one common trust map, and one update. Arm-local second moments,
samplewise normalization, candidate-specific clipping order, stochastic
threshold crossings, and multiple updates are outside scope. Introducing them
would turn the question into optimizer or finite-sample dynamics.

## Objection 6: censoring or substitution may preserve unique identity value

**Objection.** The original waiter can disappear or be replaced, so the
realized path carrier contains identity information not available at the
source boundary.

**Disposition: not surviving.** The realized target is indeed heterogeneous,
but its source-time conditional mean is `(2/3)c` in the canonical balanced
surface. Generic Rao-Blackwellization produces the same `(0,1/6)` population
update and removes lifetime-mode variance. History-dependent censoring merely
changes the conditional survival probability. A positive association gap
would require withholding a mode or binding fact from the generic arm, which
violates parity.

## Objection 7: source severing proves specialized credit

**Objection.** A path that collapses under source-to-waiter severing may imply
that only association-aware credit can localize it.

**Disposition: causal support, not algorithmic uniqueness.** Severing proves
where the physical effect came from. Conditional Q at the same source action
localizes the expected effect to the same policy score. Candidate and generic
are equal separately in native and severed panels, so their native-minus-
severed excess is exactly zero. T3 already demonstrated the same separation:
strong balanced path support coexisted with exact allocation and utility
equality.

## Objection 8: execution might reveal a missed effect

**Objection.** Encoding and enumerating the three hosts could verify whether
the analytic zeros survive implementation.

**Disposition: no answer-changing observation.** Once the finite surfaces and
record-wise equality premises are fixed, execution can only reproduce the
zeros or reveal that the implementation violates the stipulated object. The
latter is an engineering defect, not positive science. No CM, result command,
relay arm, sampled token, or new source/test namespace is warranted.

## Strongest evidence against the null

Population-update equality is not a theorem about finite-sample nonlinear
adaptive learning. B1 showed selectivity movement, and T3 showed a positive
sampled favorable-allocation probability shift of about `2.22e-05` even while
actual allocation and bounded utility remained exactly equal. These are real
counterexamples to literal sample-path identity.

They do not satisfy this cycle's decision gates. T3 actual allocation and
utility gaps were `[0,0]`, its localized interval crossed zero, the probability
movement was orders of magnitude below `0.10`, and the present canonical
surfaces remove sampling. The counterevidence motivates only the separate
finite-approximation discriminator above.

## Recommendation mapping

The Convergence reviewer recommended closing this exact association-specific,
exact-population search and treating any approximation work as a separately

The direction-level recommendation remains `PARK`, not `CLOSE`, for two

Second, the frozen result-blind branch map prospectively assigns `PARK` when

automatic continuation of EGRCR.

## Final scientific resolution

Every material objection is resolved. No family is a full survivor. The
strongest observation is not absence of physical association but its exact
decision absorption: unit ordered effects in contention and serial hosts yield
candidate and generic update `(0,1/4)`, while heterogeneous censoring yields
`(0,1/6)` in both. All orthogonal, scarce-decision, allocation, utility, and
localized-excess differences are zero.

The common failure risk is confusing a large, balanced, severable causal
association with unique decision information. Once the relation and evidence
are common, a competent conditional estimator can absorb the effect; residual
movement can instead come from denoising, gradient scaling, comparator
corruption, arm-specific optimizer geometry, or a probability shift that never
changes a scarce decision.

The maximum supported claim remains confined to the three frozen smallest
balanced, exactly-three-agent, fixed-population, finite-horizon,
exact-population, single-update abstract hosts under the parity and
common-trust contract. No learned-estimator, finite-sample, multi-update,
variable-population, relay, fixed-`k`, transfer, UAV, safety, deployment, or
general credit conclusion is available.
