## Strongest observations

### 1. Physical waiter identity has no consequence support in B1

`Opportunity` stores `older_id`, and `joiner_id` is deterministically
`1 - older_id`. The bounded four-world function `_world(opp, u, v)` uses the
event type, lag-derived timing, the two execution indicators, and fixed host
constants. It never reads `older_id`, `joiner_id`, event key, or cue. Therefore
at fixed type and lag, substituting physical waiter identity leaves every
reward, execution, generation, packet-success path, `Y^{uv}`, and `kappa`
unchanged.

The two-agent topology makes the support failure stronger: for any fixed later
joiner there is exactly one possible older waiter. There is no second physical
waiter to which the same later record could be legally associated. Variation
in retained `kappa` comes from type and lag, not identity. The calibration
means of approximately `+0.183333` for JOINT and `-0.183333` for SOLO confirm
signed type variation but do not create identity variation.

The source-to-execution, first-action, downstream exposure, and later-record
expression gates remain valid. They establish that a waiter-role effect can
reach the later record and policy. They do not establish that which physical
agent occupies the waiter role changes the consequence.

### 2. The registered cut changes type, not physical waiter identity

The cut stratum contains `older_id`, lag, sampled action, stored propensity,
and fixed nuisances. Within that stratum, `_cut_mapping` pairs JOINT sources
with shifted SOLO targets and SOLO sources with shifted JOINT targets. The cut
therefore retains physical waiter identity and reverses latent type. Because
the cue sign is not part of the stratum and the cue reports type with accuracy
`0.90625`, the intervention also reverses the dominant cue-to-consequence
relationship.

Exact preservation of global signed-label, centered-target, and
action-conditioned-advantage multisets does not preserve the conditional
target given the actor's cue. The positive intact-versus-cut native utility
effect can consequently be explained by correct type/cue supervision beating
opposite-type anti-supervision. It cannot identify a correct physical waiter
against a consequence-distinct wrong waiter.

### 3. The retained allocation-valued comparison is an exact null

Across all twelve confirmation roots, INTACT and ordinary GAE have exactly the
same native bounded utility and fixed-token scarce allocation:

- `D_IG_N = 0` with mean, lower bound, upper bound, and standard deviation all
  zero;
- `D_IG_Y = 0` and `Psi_G = 0` with the same exact equality; and
- the actual fixed-token decisions are identical root by root.

The policies are not numerically identical. INTACT minus GAE native request
selectivity is `0.004711` with 95% interval `[0.002960, 0.006462]`, and the
common-uniform panel detects corresponding request changes. INTACT also beats
the registered cut in native utility by `0.144444`, 95% interval
`[0.076664, 0.212225]`. Those are the strongest observations against treating
the relay as inert.

They do not answer the successor estimand. Probability movement alone is below
the frozen acceptance boundary, and the cut contrast is not identity-only.

### 4. The fixed-token evaluator has no current same-information headroom

Each held-out dyad contains one JOINT and one SOLO opportunity at the same list
coordinate. The two opportunities share lag, ordered role, later joiner ID,
time, pending state, and every non-cue actor feature. Their logits can differ
only through the cue coefficient and the two cue values. Any two arms with the
same cue-coefficient sign therefore choose the same member whenever the cues
differ; when cues tie, both use the same seed-fixed tie choice.

The exact GAE/INTACT allocation equality at every root is the observed instance
of this ranking-class geometry. Increasing cue selectivity without crossing
the coefficient sign cannot change one-token allocation. The common-uniform
panel is probability-sensitive because it thresholds opportunities
independently, but it is not the scarce one-token allocator.

More importantly for the proposed identity oracle, the actor has no
consequence-distinct identity signal to exploit. Physical identity does not
enter the consequence, and a fixed joiner has no alternative waiter. Thus an
identity-aware same-information oracle cannot define a different
utility-improving allocation inside this host. A softer probability evaluator
could expose generic shaping, but it could not manufacture an association-only
contrast.

three agents, in which one later joiner can pair with either of two waiters.

identity marginals, work, update scale, source observations, suffixes, counts,
data, and RNG. Correct binding must improve actual allocation/utility over both
