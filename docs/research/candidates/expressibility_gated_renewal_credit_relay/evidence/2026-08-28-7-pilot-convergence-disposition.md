**Disposition: accepted; claim narrowed.** The cut holds physical `older_id`
fixed while mapping across `JOINT/SOLO`; type determines interaction sign and
is predicted by the source cue. The positive intact-versus-cut effect is
evidence that the registered corruption is harmful, not that true waiter

This objection does not rescue the mechanism against ordinary GAE:
INTACT/GAE fixed-token allocation and bounded utility were exactly equal at
every root. Repairing attribution to the cut cannot manufacture an

### 2. A cue-preserving cut might be a smaller executable repair

**Disposition: rejected as a current-host discriminator, with the premise
accepted for a future host.** Conditional on type and lag, both physical

Adding such consequences now would construct a genuinely different host after
seeing B1, not repair a missing run. It can be considered only in a new
material cycle after independent motivation and oracle headroom are established.
