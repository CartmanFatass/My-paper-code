The decisive criticism is accepted. The cut was matched within action,
propensity, and declared nuisance strata but intentionally excluded cue sign
and rotated to the opposite type. Because cue sign predicts type and type fixes
the sign of `kappa`, the cut breaks both:

- the record-to-waiter label association the experiment intended to test; and
- the generic cue-sign-to-favorable-context supervision ordinary GAE can use.

Consequently, `INTACT>CUT`, even with a positive native/yoked utility
interaction and exactly preserved label/work/update marginals, establishes only
that native source-label alignment is better than this anti-aligned corruption.
It does not isolate the identity of the true older waiter.

This is the strongest alternative already compatible with the local result.
The local intake concluded that ordinary GAE already learned the cue/outcome

does not invalidate the observed zero-utility comparison. It further limits
