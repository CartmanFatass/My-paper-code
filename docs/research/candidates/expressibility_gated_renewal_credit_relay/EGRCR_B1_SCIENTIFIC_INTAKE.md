## Conclusion

EGRCR-B1 answered its enabling question with an identifiable but
algorithm-negative result. The constructed host contains the intended causal
joiner-to-waiter edge, a positive/negative waiter-interaction target, downstream
utility exposure, an expressive later-joiner update, and a legal association
cut. Correct waiter binding materially outperformed deliberately wrong binding
in native utility and that difference disappeared under suffix yoking.

However, correctly bound relay credit did not improve bounded utility over
ordinary GAE at any root. `INTACT` and `GAE` also made exactly the same
fixed-token allocation at every root. The small proximal selectivity difference
therefore changed some common-uniform requests but supplied no task-value gain.
The registered association-specific criteria fail, and this direction supplies
no basis for adding the relay to a variable-`k` learner.

Direction-local recommendation: delete this association-specific relay from
current algorithm investment; do not construct the learned gate/estimator
successor, repeat B1, or progress it to a full variable-`k` or UAV experiment.
This is a bounded deletion of the present mechanism and evidence surface, not a
general theorem that ordered cross-agent credit can never help.

## Scientific interpretation

The result distinguishes three statements:

1. **The causal edge and label are real in this host.** The later request
   changes a specific waiter, the waiter change affects bounded outcomes, and
   the four-world `kappa` is usefully signed.
2. **Correct association matters relative to a wrong association.** The intact
   versus cut native/yoked interaction shows that assigning opposite-type
   waiter labels to matched source records is consequential rather than a
   work, count, optimizer, or gross-update artifact.
3. **The explicit relay is not useful beyond ordinary GAE.** Exact equality of
   GAE and intact utility at every root, together with identical fixed-token
   allocation and `Psi_G=0`, means correct association did not deliver an
   incremental decision or task-value benefit over the simpler learner.

Statement 2 cannot substitute for statement 3. The binding cut is a
falsification control, not the deployable baseline. Beating a deliberately
misbound target while tying ordinary GAE supports neither relay necessity nor
algorithm value.

## Strongest alternative explanation

The strongest sufficient explanation is cue supervision already present in
ordinary return. In this small deterministic handoff host, the observed cue and
ordinary GAE already identify the useful `JOINT` versus harmful `SOLO`
allocation. The centered `kappa` target supplies a dense, low-variance version
of substantially the same decision signal, so it slightly changes probability
and score-credit covariance without changing fixed-token choices or utility.
The opposite-type binding cut instead turns that target into matched
anti-supervision and can push the one-step policy toward the wrong allocation.
Its native utility loss—and its disappearance when suffix association is
yoked—therefore shows that poisoning the cue/outcome association is harmful;
it does not show that the intact relay adds information or value unavailable to
ordinary GAE.

This explanation fits all observed facts, including positive proximal
selectivity, `INTACT>CUT`, yoked absence, equal counts/work, and exact
`INTACT=GAE` task utility.

## Outcome map, next action, and revisit condition

The observed branch is the science card's conjunction of:

- `INTACT>CUT` while `INTACT` does not beat `GAE`: the cut is harmful, with no
  demonstrated value over the simpler learner; and
- proximal selectivity separation without bounded-utility separation: credit
  reaches the policy but has no demonstrated task value at this boundary.

No unchanged-science repair or extra seed can answer a remaining B1 question;
the complete registered surface already separates the alternatives. No direct
algorithm candidate or full variable-`k` successor is warranted.

Reconsider ordered renewal relay only as a new treatment in a prospectively
different regime where (a) ordinary GAE demonstrably lacks access to a delayed,
identity-specific consequence, (b) an independent oracle establishes usable
utility headroom over GAE, and (c) the binding control breaks identity while
remaining neutral rather than becoming an opposite-type anti-label. Without
all three facts, another relay experiment would repeat the present answer or
compare against a poisoned control.
