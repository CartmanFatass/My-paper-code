1. **Authentic association has direct finite value.** A valid carrier identifies the independently
   relevant record; the shared controller learns the payload-to-lane rule; authentic training
   improves both selection and return over reassociation; forcing the relevant record improves
   return; and both natural effects survive at `M=10`, reduced-roster, and first-post-rejoin
   windows under every hidden polarity.
2. **Competent bit-blind containment.** The same-information reassociated or masked policy and the
   payload controller absorb all usable structure. Correct selection remains exactly `0.5` under
   reassociation/masking and no authentic natural advantage survives.
3. **Selection-only credit.** Authentic association improves correct reads, but common-team credit
   does not turn them into native return despite a positive forced-record contrast and competent
   controller. This localizes value to the selector rather than the full cooperative edge.
4. **Controller or host failure.** Forced relevant reads do not support the required safe rate, or
   the relevant-versus-decoy intervention lacks two-sided return headroom. No association efficacy
   conclusion is then identified.
5. **Finite-transfer or geometry failure.** Effects exist only at trained envelopes, one hidden
   polarity, full-roster windows, or a side channel. The variable-axis or semantic interpretation
   fails at the smallest implicated scope.
6. **Attribution ambiguity.** The carrier truth is declared from the target table rather than
   generated from a prior publisher/record event, reassociation changes information or work, or a
   decoy's cross-pair origin creates common-reward credit not localized by the forced branches.
   The prospective object is invalid and no result-bearing execution is warranted.
