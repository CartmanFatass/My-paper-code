## Scientific question and differentiating prediction

The mechanism-to-capability chain is:

`authentic carrier-to-record association -> correct capped peer-record read ->`
`the relevant reservation enters a shared anonymous policy -> safe paired`
`deconfliction -> common team return under membership change and held-out N`.

The primary comparison is the total effect of training and executing with
authentic rather than conditionally deleted carrier-to-record association.
Everything else is paired: physical worlds, churn, active masks, roles,
records, record payloads, validation-success marginals, interface classes,
presentations, learner class, initialization, random-coordinate schedule,
updates, transaction counts, physical windows, and resource caps.

The differentiating prediction is joint:

1. `AUTHENTIC` improves correct relevant-record selection over
   `REASSOCIATED`;
2. the same arm improves common deconfliction return;
3. forcing the relevant rather than decoy record changes return in the
   predicted direction, establishing that selection is a live mediator; and
4. the selection and return advantages remain at the untrained roster envelope
   `M=10` after one parameterization is trained jointly at `M in {6,8}`.

Record selection alone is insufficient. Team return alone is insufficient.
Fixed-roster performance alone is insufficient.

## Exact finite cooperative/UAV host

### UAV abstraction and episode law

The host is a finite reservation/deconfliction toy. Physical agents are UAV
lineages approaching repeated two-lane merge windows. A publisher has already
committed to one of two reservation lanes. Its paired subscriber must choose
the other lane. Opening the correct sealed peer record reveals the publisher's
committed lane; opening a decoy record may reveal another publisher's lane.

The publisher commitment is an exogenous mission reservation, not a learned
action in this first edge assay. Every physical lineage occupies publisher and
subscriber roles across the frozen world schedule, while the subscriber
decision is made by one learned policy shared by every lineage. This isolates
the carrier-selection-to-coordination edge without mixing it with publisher
policy competence.

This is a cooperative UAV-motivated toy, not flight dynamics. A merge window is
one simultaneous team-credit window. Each subscriber makes one capped read and
one lane choice; all subscribers receive only the same common team return.

### Varied quantity, support, and churn

`M` is the episode's physical roster envelope. `N_t` is the active count at
window `t`. The only registered supports are:

- training envelopes: `M=6` and `M=8`;
- in-support frozen evaluation: `M=6` and `M=8`; and
- held-out frozen evaluation: `M=10`.

Every episode has five windows `t=0..4`. A counter-keyed hidden permutation
`h` of the `M` physical lineages fixes:

- `A_0 = all M lineages`;
- `A_1 = A_0 minus {h_0,h_1}`;
- `A_2 = A_0`, with `h_0,h_1` rejoining;
- `A_3 = A_0 minus {h_2,h_3}`; and
- `A_4 = A_0`, with `h_2,h_3` rejoining.

Thus `N_t=(M,M-2,M,M-2,M)`. Every registered episode contains leave and
rejoin events, not merely a comparison of fixed agent counts between episodes.
At least `M-4` lineages survive every event. Survivor physical state and
reservation counters continue across all five windows. A departing lineage's
physical state is held by the environment and resumes at its next counter when
it rejoins.

Every join or rejoin receives a fresh opaque public slot token. Tokens,
physical-lineage keys, hidden permutation positions, previous slot tokens, and
join history are absent from learner features. The learned policy is
feed-forward and has no per-agent hidden or optimizer state; the only learned
state is the one shared parameter vector. Consequently there is no fixed
identity slot through which cooperation can be carried. The activity log must
still prove that environment-owned survivor and rejoin state followed the
declared lineage rather than an array position.

### Pairing, roles, and peer composition

At each window a separate counter-keyed permutation of the active lineages
forms ordered pairs. The first member is publisher and the second is
subscriber. Across each complete frozen schedule, every lineage has equal
publisher and subscriber exposure at each occupancy type. Training episodes
come in `32` role-complement pairs per envelope and evaluation episodes come
in `16` role-complement pairs per envelope and branch. The second episode in
each pair retains the first episode's active sets and unordered peer pairs but
reverses publisher and subscriber within every pair; it uses fresh
counter-keyed reservation and carrier worlds. Only complete role-complement
pairs enter analysis.

The pairing permutation is renewed after each membership event. The retained
world set must include survivor lineages whose peer and role both change after
leave/rejoin. Pairing, role, partner lineage, and pair position are not learner
features.

There are `P_t=N_t/2 >= 2` pairs. Each subscriber receives two sealed records:

- the relevant record contains its own publisher's reservation `c in {0,1}`;
- the decoy record contains the reservation of another active publisher,
  assigned to pair `j` from publisher `(j+1) mod P_t` in the current ordered
  publisher list.

Worlds are arranged in exact eight-world superblocks. Each superblock contains
every `(t,c,c_decoy) in {0,1}^3` cell once. It is partitioned into two
four-world carrier blocks, one with `c=0` and one with `c=1`; within each
carrier block `t=(0,0,1,1)` and `c_decoy=(0,1,0,1)`. Thus relevant and
decoy reservations have the complete four-cell joint table at both physical
slots. Slot, payloads, interface class, lineage, role history, and lane labels
are mutually balanced on the retained finite support.

## Carrier treatment and semantic deletion

Each subscriber receives one finite carrier with outcome-neutral public
interface classes `i,r in {0,1}`, one surface bit, and a private opaque serial.
A finite registry performs the same single provenance/integrity lookup in
every arm. The learner sees only `i`, `r`, the surface bit, and
`auth_ok=1` before selecting a record. Serial, registry state, validation
evidence, arm, donor, pair, lineage, roster position, block, hidden slot, record
payload, and future return are private.

- In `AUTHENTIC`, semantic bit `z=t`, the physical slot of the relevant
  publisher record.
- In `REASSOCIATED`, whole valid carriers are assigned from other worlds
  inside the exact stratum
  `(split,M,N_t,window_type,i,r,presentation,block)`. Each four-world block
  has `t=(0,0,1,1)`; the donor permutation is drawn uniformly before learning
  from
  `[(1,2,3,0),(1,3,0,2),(2,0,3,1),(3,0,1,2)]`.
  Every resulting `(t,z')` pair occurs once. The comparator carrier is valid,
  balanced, and conditionally uninformative rather than predictably inverted.

The two arms use the same sealed records in the same physical slots. Only the
carrier-to-subscriber-world association differs. No donor or arm marker reaches
the learner.

For paired seed `s`, the hidden presentation tuple is fixed exactly as:

`[(11,0,0,0),(23,0,0,1),(37,0,1,0),(53,0,1,1),`
`(71,1,0,0),(89,1,0,1),(107,1,1,0),(127,1,1,1)]`,

where each row is `(seed,kappa_s,mu_s,lambda_s)`.

- visible carrier bit: `b = z XOR kappa_s`;
- opened reservation payload: `p = c XOR mu_s`;
- learner lane action `a` maps to physical lane `y = a XOR lambda_s`;
- correct selector action: `s_star = b XOR kappa_s`; and
- safe lane action:
  `a_star = 1 XOR p XOR mu_s XOR lambda_s`.

This fully counterbalances carrier copy/complement, payload polarity, and action
label polarity. Both arms of a seed use the same presentation.

## Boundary, reward, and analytic mediator

The subscriber's legal boundary transaction is:

1. choose exactly one of `OPEN_0` or `OPEN_1`;
2. receive only that record's one-bit reservation payload; and
3. choose exactly one lane action.

It consumes exactly
`(payload_reads=1,reservation_services=1)`. Opening either slot has the same
work. `OPEN_BOTH` requires at least
`(payload_reads=2,reservation_services=1)` and is denied before either
payload is exposed. Validation uses one matched registry lookup outside this
cap. Publishers issue the same two sealed records and one carrier in both arms.

For pair `j`, let subscriber physical lane be `y_j` and its relevant
publisher reservation be `c_j`. Pair score is:

- `g_j=+1` when `y_j != c_j` (safe deconfliction);
- `g_j=-1` when `y_j = c_j` (collision).

The only learning reward is the common team mean
`R_t = (1/P_t) * sum_j g_j`. Pair score is retained for analysis but is never
an update input. As `N_t` grows, one subscriber's contribution to its update
target shrinks as `1/P_t`; this is the exact cooperative credit exposure being
tested.

Let `S_j=1` when the relevant record was opened. With a perfect payload-to-lane
controller and the exact balanced relevant/decoy table:

`E[g_j | S_j=1]=1`, `E[g_j | S_j=0]=0`, and therefore
`E[R_t]=P(S_j=1)`.

This identity is an analyzer check and a mechanistic ceiling, not an assumed
result. The forced-record branches below determine whether controller failure
or selection failure breaks it.

## One anonymous shared learner

Each arm and seed trains independent zero-initialized parameters. No parameter,
transition, update, checkpoint, or optimizer state crosses arms or seeds.

The learner has two shared linear contextual-bandit heads.

Selector features are
`x_sel=(1,2b-1,2i-1,2r-1)`. For record action `u in {0,1}`,
`Q_sel(u)=w_u^T x_sel`.

After one record is opened, controller features are
`x_ctl=(1,2p-1,2i-1,2r-1)`. For lane action `a in {0,1}`,
`Q_ctl(a)=v_a^T x_ctl`.

Both heads use epsilon-greedy choices. Ties and exploration use separate paired
counter-keyed coordinates during training. Frozen greedy evaluation uses one
seed/head/feature-context-fixed paired tie rank, never a world-coordinate tie
rank, so an uninformative balanced bit cannot acquire selection value through
tie variation. Epsilon decreases linearly from `0.40` at completed subscriber
decision zero to `0.05` at decision `1984`; all subscribers in one window
use the epsilon at that window's pre-decision completed count.

All decisions in one team window are sampled from the same pre-window
parameters. After the common return is known, updates are accumulated without
agent order and applied once:

`w_u <- w_u + 0.05/(4*P_t) * sum_j 1[u_j=u] * (R_t-Q_sel_j(u)) * x_sel_j`

`v_a <- v_a + 0.05/(4*P_t) * sum_j 1[a_j=a] * (R_t-Q_ctl_j(a)) * x_ctl_j`

There is no bootstrap, discount, recurrence, replay, local reward, centralized
critic, regularization, identity embedding, roster-size input, active-mask
position, partner identifier, block clock, or task-specific shaping. The same
`w` and `v` are used by every subscriber at both training envelopes and all
evaluation envelopes.

This small learner is deliberate. It tests whether the semantic selector can
be credited through a common return whose individual contribution changes
with roster size. A larger set, graph, attention, recurrent, or identity-aware
module would add an unneeded alternative mechanism to this first edge assay.

## Training, evaluation, seeds, and exact workload

For each arm and seed, native training has `64` complete episodes at `M=6`
and `64` at `M=8`, interleaved by a frozen counter-keyed schedule. An
`M=6` episode has `13` subscriber decisions and an `M=8` episode has
`18`; training therefore has exactly
`64*(13+18)=1984` subscriber decisions per arm and seed.

At decision `1984`, parameters are frozen. Evaluation has `32` complete
episodes at each of `M=6,8,10` for each of four equal-world branches:

- `NATURAL`: greedy learned selector and controller;
- `MASKED`: replace `b` by an independently balanced bit within every exact
  evaluation stratum, then use the greedy learned heads;
- `FORCE_RELEVANT`: force the relevant one-record read, then use the greedy
  learned controller; and
- `FORCE_DECOY`: force the decoy one-record read, then use the greedy learned
  controller.

Forced reads consume the same `(1,1)` resource and are tagged as
interventions. No evaluation branch updates parameters. An evaluation episode
has `13`, `18`, or `23` subscriber decisions at
`M=6`, `8`, or `10`; each branch therefore has
`32*(13+18+23)=1728` decisions per arm and seed.

## Positive, null, failure, and inconclusive map

All interpretation is conditional on the complete gate, exact counts, matched
resources, and all frozen paired evaluations.

### Positive edge evidence

The exact R01 edge is supported only if:

1. `FORCE_RELEVANT` safe rate is at least `0.90` for every seed at each
   `M in {6,8,10}`, establishing controller competence;
2. every seed has positive `AUTHENTIC-REASSOCIATED` `NATURAL` effects on
   both selection and team return at each of `M=6,8,10`;
3. at held-out `M=10`, mean selection effect is at least `0.20`, mean team
   return effect is at least `0.10`, and `AUTHENTIC` mean relevant-record
   selection is at least `0.70`;
4. mean `FORCE_RELEVANT-FORCE_DECOY` team-return effect is at least `0.40`
   at each envelope, proving that the selected record is action-relevant;
5. `AUTHENTIC NATURAL` exceeds its own `MASKED` branch on both selection
   and return at `M=10`; and
6. the effect direction is positive under both carrier polarities, both payload
   polarities, both action-label polarities, reduced-roster windows, and
   first-post-rejoin windows.

This supports only a finite held-out-`M=10` carrier-selection-to-coordination
edge for the exact shared learner and churn host. It is sufficient to ask
Portfolio whether a second surface or UAV simulator is worth scoping; it does
not enact that investment.

### Localized nonpositive outcomes

- If forced-relevant controller competence passes but authentic association
  does not improve selection, the exact carrier-credit realization is
  unsupported under this learner and budget.
- If selection improves but natural team return does not, while the
  forced-record return contrast is positive, the learned
  selection-to-coordination path is unsupported under common team credit.
- If both selection and return pass at `M=6,8` but not at `M=10`, the exact
  held-out roster transfer fails. A later revision must change the shared
  interface or credit mechanism before claiming variable-`N` robustness.
- If correct selection is preserved but forced relevant versus decoy does not
  change return, the proposed reservation-information-to-coordination edge is
  absent on this task.
- If every competence and validity condition passes and both primary mean
  effects lie inside `[-0.05,0.05]` at all three envelopes, record a bounded
  null for this learner, budget, host, and support.

These outcomes do not delete all carrier-aware policies or all cooperative UAV
families.

### Inconclusive or invalid outcomes

An incomplete support/activity gate, wrong exact selection control rate,
identity or arm leakage, unmatched records/work/resources, failed forced-record
controller competence, incomplete paired seed/arm/branch output, result-based
retry, resource-cap breach, or any semantic deviation is inconclusive. It
supports neither a positive nor a null edge claim.

An advantage confined to one carrier, payload, or action-label polarity is an
optimization-geometry falsifier. An effect attributed to fixed identity slots,
lineage tokens, roster position, `M`, or `N_t` is outside this treatment and
invalidates the semantic interpretation.

## Strongest alternatives and claim ceiling

The strongest simpler explanation is ordinary learning of a local binary
payload-to-lane rule after an arbitrary one-of-two read. The
`REASSOCIATED` arm retains exactly that opportunity, carrier validation,
records, controller capacity, common reward, and work while deleting only
carrier-to-relevant-record association. The forced-record branches separate
payload-to-lane competence from selector credit. `MASKED` excludes a stable
nonsemantic carrier code at evaluation. Presentation balance excludes
copy/complement and action-label bias.

A positive result does not establish:

- arbitrary-`N`, open-support, or larger-than-`10` generalization;
- superiority of graph, set, attention, recurrent, or centralized-critic MARL;
- learned publisher behavior, partner choice, communication topology, or
  skill-lifetime handling;
- cryptographic security, issuer truth under compromise, authentication
  necessity, or production carrier prevalence;
- continuous flight dynamics, sensing, latency, packet loss, safety,
  certification, deployment, or aircraft value; or
- that a semantic carrier is better than every equally predictive
  resource-matched feature.

It supports acquisition and held-out finite transfer of one anonymous,
shared-policy, carrier-gated deconfliction rule under the exact churn and common
credit law. A null or failure remains bounded to this object.
