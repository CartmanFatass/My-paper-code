## 3. Binding mechanism, competent alternative and source use

The binding structure is **temporal abstraction or termination**. Two fixed
controller/job identities make SUBMIT/CONTINUE choices at offset legal clocks.
Both see the same 14 public own/partner event, readiness and clock features.
SUBMIT occupies the shared slot for eight transitions and can remove the
partner's last opportunity; CONTINUE reserves no capacity. Both target processes
advance through t=40, including waiting and missed deadlines. Each valid action
receives the actual remaining team return. Both controllers co-adapt the shared
G during training; evaluation freezes that G and executes strict logit>0.
This traces environment event → job ownership → public information → submission
and team credit → real optimizer exposure → native success, attempts and waiting.
There are no membership changes, private observations, replacement or evaluation
partner adaptation. This host does not identify a decentralized or MARL-specific
causal benefit merely because its actions couple two jobs.

control rule**, and current G training/sample variation changes a small
waiting/attempt tradeoff without making replacement worthwhile. R0 is the
same-information readiness reference; R adds the existing yielding condition.
Their observation, action, information, utility and evaluation budget match the
retained use, so reuse them. Neither is a tuned optimum. The trained G remains a
