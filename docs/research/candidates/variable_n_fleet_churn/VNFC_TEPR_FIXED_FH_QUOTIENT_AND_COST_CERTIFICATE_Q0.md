## Reduced-horizon exhaustive-agreement domain

The registered horizons are exactly `h in {1,2,3}` remaining twenty-second
decision intervals. For each `h`, the domain is every state reachable in the
finite Q0 fixture graph from each declared fixture root by alternating one
declared legal action and one declared successor edge for at most `h` intervals.
The graph must contain, at minimum, fixtures that exercise:

- one and multiple `A_bid` collisions into the same matching;
- distinct `X_20` and `X_40`, plus their coincidence with an `A_bid` action;
- nonzero and unequal `D_past` values with the same physical numerator state;
- all sixteen successor labels, including unequal rational probabilities;
- action-dependent successor classes;
- equal-value actions resolved by the complete-command lexicographic tie;
- histories that are physically equal now but produce different future
  `A_fix` or values and therefore must not merge;
- states that differ only in a field proved irrelevant and therefore may merge;
- terminal ratios with unequal denominators; and
- a deliberately invalid proposed merge that the independent checker must
  reject with a concrete counterexample.

Every scalar used by the reference evaluator is an exact integer or
`fractions.Fraction`-equivalent reduced rational. Floating point is forbidden in
keys, transition probabilities, values, comparisons, certificate quantities,
and ties. The reference and quotient evaluators must agree for every reachable
`(h,key)` on:

1. the ordered distinct action list;
2. immediate numerator and denominator increments;
3. the exact successor probability distribution;
4. every exact action value;
5. the exact optimal value;
6. the exact selected complete-command serialization; and
7. the complete set of maximizing actions before the canonical tie is applied.

Pass requires identity on every quantity above and independent acceptance of
every proposed class. Any mismatch returns the first canonical counterexample
`(fixture,h,state-pair-or-state,action,quantity,reference,quotient)` and Q0 does
not complete. Tests may not delete, reorder, or weaken the failing fixture.

## Bellman-bisimulation obligations

For each proposed equivalence class and remaining horizon, the independent
checker recomputes from unquotiented records and proves:

1. **Candidate preservation:** the ordered distinct `A_fix` set is identical,
   including the `A_bid` image and the exact identities/values of `X_20` and
   `X_40`.
2. **Objective preservation:** current/past delivered data, `D_past`, every
   future delivered-data increment, every future-demand increment, and the
   terminal expected ratio are preserved exactly. An expectation of ratios may
   never be replaced by a ratio of expectations.
3. **Transition preservation:** for each common action and each successor
   quotient class, summed rational probability is identical; the physical and
   history update feeding the next `A_fix` is represented.
4. **Tie preservation:** equal bids, equal `X_20`/`X_40` expectations, equal
   Bellman values, duplicate candidate actions, and the complete-command
   lexicographic serialization produce the same unique executed action.
5. **Induction:** terminal values agree at `h=0`; obligations 1-4 plus agreement
   at `h-1` imply exact action-value, value, maximizing-set, and selected-action
   agreement at `h`.

A hash collision, sampled comparison, tolerance, decimal rounding, unverified
canonicalization, or policy-only agreement is not a proof.

## Full-query analytic bounds to certify

The cost calculator must accept declared nonnegative integer parameters and
emit exact integers/rationals plus assumptions. For the parent-card envelope it
must instantiate:

- decision horizon `H=6`;
- exogenous successor fanout `B=16` at each nonterminal boundary;
- distinct fixed candidates per state `A<=129`;
- legal variable matching count `F<=1,961` at held-out `N=7`;
- `A_bid` source weight vectors `W=127`;
- twenty replicates and 128 held-out worlds per replicate.

It must report both:

1. **Unquotiented upper envelope.** For one root, candidate-action evaluations
   `Q_raw=sum_(d=0)^(H-1) A^(d+1) B^d`; action-successor edges
   `E_raw=sum_(d=0)^(H-2) A^(d+1) B^(d+1)`; and the corresponding panel
   multiplier. `X_20`/`X_40` inner enumeration is reported separately using
   `F`, never hidden inside `A`.
2. **Quotient-parametric envelope.** For declared reachable quotient-key counts
   `K_h` at each remaining horizon and declared distinct action counts `A_h`,
   report `Q_q=sum_h K_h*A_h`,
   `E_q=sum_(h>1) K_h*A_h*B`, key bytes, edge bytes, value bytes, certificate
   bytes, peak memory, retained storage, and exact reduction factors against the
   unquotiented envelope. No empirical `K_h` is fabricated in Q0; absent a full
   graph, it remains a named parameter and the certificate reports a range or
   symbolic expression.

The rational-bit certificate must propagate a conservative exact bound. With
reduced input probability numerator/denominator bit limit `b_p`, increment bit
limit `b_i`, and at most `H-1` stochastic transitions, it must bound numerator
and denominator bits for transition products, sums of at most `B^(H-1)` paths,
terminal delivered/demand ratios, and Bellman weighted sums. It must distinguish
mathematical bit bounds from implementation allocation. Arbitrary-precision
integer storage is estimated as `ceil(bits/8)` plus a declared object overhead;
no fixed-width overflow assumption is allowed.

Work bounds must separately expose canonical-key construction, candidate
generation (`W` RDA calls plus `X_20`/`X_40` enumeration), transition generation,
exact rational arithmetic, partition refinement, independent checking, backward
induction, certificate serialization, and panel multiplication. Memory/storage
bounds must separate live frontier, memoized keys/edges/values, checker duplicate
state, and durable certificate.
