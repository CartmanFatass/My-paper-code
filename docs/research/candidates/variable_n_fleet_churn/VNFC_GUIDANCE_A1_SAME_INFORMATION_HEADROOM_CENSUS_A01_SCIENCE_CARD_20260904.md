## 1. Authority, question, and claim ceiling

The accepted K=1024 intake records that the controller-headroom result is not the requested

defects are constitutive rather than cosmetic:

1. `ORACLE-BEAM-FAIL60-K1024` sees the complete future exogenous tape, whereas `BCRH-PERSIST` and

   `DIRECT-SET-AR` result was produced at one prospectively fixed 64-update configuration and is
   not accompanied by a tuning/selection record that qualifies it as a tuned generic baseline.

The sole question of A01 is:

> On the exact first-primary R02 sixteen-world `heldout-N7` host, do the accepted records already
> contain both (i) an upper reference using the same causal decision-time information and native
> action law and (ii) a valid, competent, tuned generic learner baseline evaluated on the identical
> worlds, endpoint, and support, so that their raw `R_fail_60` difference can be transcribed without
> training, tuning, search, replay, or another result-bearing invocation?

**Claim ceiling.** This object can establish only an evidence-path and measurement-availability
fact: the required matched pair is present and yields one raw finite-panel difference, or one or
both exact evidence slots are absent. Even a numeric value would be bounded to the named simulator
host, sixteen worlds, causal information boundary, physical action law, evaluation support, and
historical learner budget. It would not establish an algorithm effect, MAPR value, a useful
investment threshold, stable competence, arbitrary-`N`, repeated churn, transfer, safety, flight,

## 4. Eligibility slots, strongest candidates, and live explanations

### Same-information upper reference

`J_upper` is eligible only when accepted evidence explicitly designates a return or tight
achievability bound as the best legal performance under the section 2 causal information set on
the exact sixteen worlds. An exact causal DP/POMDP solution, exhaustive same-information policy
census, or a saturation-qualified legal controller could qualify if its assumptions, native return,
and population match.

The following nearby quantities do **not** qualify automatically:

- K=256 or K=1024 full-tape search, because it is information-advantaged;
- `PERSIST-MAX-C60`, because it also uses the complete tape and covers only a fixed command class;
- the structural ceiling `R_fail_60 <= 1`, because it is not an accepted achievable
  same-information controller return; or
- `BCRH-PERSIST`, because competence and causal legality do not make it an upper reference or
  establish optimality.

### Tuned generic baseline

`J_generic` is eligible only when an accepted generic learner/controller:

- receives the identical causal decision-time information and uses the identical physical action
  and evaluation laws;
- has enough generic history/set capacity to represent non-MAPR reassignment without receiving a
  VNFC-specific target label or future tape;
- has a declared development/tuning population disjoint from the frozen held-out `N=7` worlds, a
  bounded configuration/selection budget, a selection rule, and a selected checkpoint rule;
- reports a valid final evaluation on the exact sixteen worlds; and
- reports training transitions/episodes, gradient-bearing optimizer updates, model-selection
  exposure, initialization scale, parameter displacement, and evaluation exposure.

The strongest current candidate is the accepted `DIRECT-SET-AR` learner because it strictly
contains MAPR and uses the same causal information. Its existing 64-update final checkpoints and
held-out evaluations remain valid B/EXPLORE evidence under their original meaning. They fill the
A1 slot only if the bounded re-read finds an explicit prior tuning/selection record meeting the
criteria above; fixed budget and competence alone are not silently relabelled as tuning.

Live explanations preserved by the census are:

1. a same-information causal optimum may leave substantial headroom above DIRECT and BCRH;
2. the generic learner may be optimization- or budget-limited rather than structurally limited;
3. BCRH may already be close to the causal optimum even though the privileged physical ceiling is
   loose;
4. headroom may remain zone- or world-specific; and
5. missing upper/tuning records are evidence-availability facts, not scientific negativity.
