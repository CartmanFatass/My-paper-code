parameter bytes.

This can change the state panel, stable keys, fitting presentations, initial
checkpoints, learned treatments, invalid branch and activity/work claims.

Minimum correction class: prospectively bind one fresh immutable `M` by an
outcome-independent law before any counter-address or diagnostic is evaluated;
bound every rejection scan and `stiefel_attempt` to `0..65535`; define
`COUNTER_EXHAUSTION` and `INITIALIZATION_EXHAUSTION` without fallback or seed
substitution; and state that the registered SHA-derived counter tape is a
constitutive finite law rather than an unproved probabilistic-uniformity
theorem. A future complete revision must also freeze how each exhaustion enters
the static-invalid disposition and atomic manifest.

### 2. Stable keys influence physical-state generation outside their four registered roles

different key assignment can therefore change physical prehistory, energy,
routes, token occupancy, failed-executor identity and acceptance without a
physical or teacher tie.

Minimum correction class: use a separate proposal-only command serialization
based on the fixed public latent tags `UAV1,...,UAV8` solely for counter
unranking of prehistory commands. Preserve the stable-key serializer for
evaluation identity and equality, and keep stable keys restricted to the four
registered plumbing/tie roles. Treating proposal ordering as a fifth key role
would instead require a wider claim and alternative-explanation revision; the
proposal-only order is the narrower repair.
