## Decision first

The adversarial review sharpens, but does not overturn, the local B1 result
interpretation. `B-REBIND - G-MEAN` remains a valid success against the registered
weak factorized comparator. It is not evidence that B1 produced a useful learned
variable-`N` algorithm: the simpler reward-complete `GREEDY-ORACLE` materially
dominates B on both project-facing outcomes, and the exact reward inside the
decoder is sufficient to explain the B-versus-G gain.

The precise four-level disposition is:

1. **Registered contrast:** B cleared the frozen B-versus-G thresholds on `P`,
   `H`, and `X`.
2. **Comparator-relative package:** B outperformed that particular factorized
   learned baseline.
3. **Non-dominated package:** unsupported; the simpler greedy rule is materially
   better on `P` and `H`.
4. **Learned algorithm value:** unsupported and locally contradicted; the closest
   observed zero-bid rule outperforms the learned-bid package.

Accordingly, the earlier owner phrase “positive structured variable-N allocation
package” must be read only as a registered comparator-relative observation. It
must not be expanded into a claim that B1 met the project destination with a

reward-blind scalable discriminator.

This review changes no B1 data, technical acceptance, arm, or result. It authorizes

## What the review correctly converges

### Explicit mass bundle

The review agrees that `A-MASS - G-MEAN` is a registered practical null, not merely
an underpowered non-significant effect. It applies to the complete sum-plus-pressure
bundle under the factorized action mechanism. It neither isolates pressure from
sum aggregation nor proves that extensive population information is universally
useless.

### Exact joint decoder

`A-JOINT - A-MASS` identifies the value of a bundled intervention: assignment-level
coupling, exhaustive `4^N` search, and direct access to the known coverage/waste
objective. Additive learned bids alone remain factorized; the nonfactorized
structure is supplied by the hand-coded assignment objective. Therefore the large
contrast supports exact reward-aware assignment construction, not learned joint
coordination or scalable allocation.

The strongest matched current-host control would clamp A-JOINT bids to zero while
preserving enumeration and tie rules. The existing greedy result already makes the
corresponding rule-shortcut explanation dominant. A cleaner same-history clamp
would reduce tie-history ambiguity, but that ambiguity cannot rescue learned value
from the large negative B-versus-greedy intervals and is not worth a new B1 run.

### Keep/switch algebra and learned bids

The review retains the pre-result correction:

`keep(a)=1-switch(a)` and  
`score_B(a)=bid(a)+reward(a)+0.04`.

Thus `B-REBIND - A-JOINT` cannot identify learned persistence. The observed
`B-REBIND - GREEDY-ORACLE` diagnostic is the closest available test of whether
learned bids add information beyond the complete immediate reward. Its negative
effects, `P=-0.2174020` and `H=-0.1920236`, with both 95% intervals wholly below
zero, make a positive learned-bid interpretation unavailable on B1.

Different tie-equivalent assignment histories keep this diagnostic from being a
perfect same-history intervention. That qualification limits exact causal
decomposition; it does not support the opposite sign.

### Cardinality and true expansion

The shared parameterization at unseen even sizes shows held-out-cardinality
interpolation within the trained range, not beyond-range extrapolation. Positive
`X` for A-JOINT/B does not establish learned use of population mass: the exact
decoder directly consumes the changed capabilities and demands, while the explicit
mass bundle itself was practically null.
