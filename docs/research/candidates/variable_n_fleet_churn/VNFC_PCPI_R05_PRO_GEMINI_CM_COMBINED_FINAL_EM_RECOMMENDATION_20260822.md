## Decision

Recommend one bounded, result-blind, TEST-only native proof and measurement
gate. Do not move directly to full r05 construction and do not classify the
direction as no-current before buying this smaller discriminator.

This choice preserves the finite architecture-deletion value of a Pro-closed,
technically single-valued object while avoiding a direct commitment to the
central 246-engineer-day construction estimate. The gate is the lowest-cost
known way to replace the dominant weak facts: exact-numeric feasibility,
literal containment, information leakage, exhaustive-trace throughput and
atomic lifecycle cost. It is not a scientific run and must create no r05
master, identity, coordinate, accepted state, parameter, checkpoint or result.

This EM milestone recommends the later gate; it does not authorize or start
it. Full construction, any scientific identity and empirical activity remain
no-current until the gate returns, this EM intakes its accepted technical
artifact, and Portfolio makes a fresh object-specific decision.

## Independent evidence intake

### Mathematical/causal definition

The complete r05 composite is `CLOSED` with zero remaining science-bearing
defects, and the same-direction EM accepts that closure. R05 is the immutable
definition. There is no reason to create r06.

### Gemini innovation boundary

The exact mutually blind Gemini operation produced no response, conversation
identity or scientifically interpretable advisory. Its only usable fact is the
operation-local permanent no-resend fence. The absence of an advisory is not
evidence against PCPI, does not revise r05, does not support a mechanism or UAV
bridge, and does not delay this decision. No retry or continuation of that
operation is recommended or authorized.

### Static engineering evidence

The EM accepts the owner-CM return without redoing technical acceptance:

- r05 is statically single-valued, bindable and observable;
- invariant-in-free containment can be literal at byte and work surfaces;
- key/tag/slot/coordinate/teacher/analyzer information boundaries can be
  enforced through explicit native types and ABIs;
- no science-bearing implementation ambiguity remains; and
- no conforming PCPI r05 source, registry, exact-numeric trainer/optimizer,
  exhaustive evaluator, intervention/analyzer or atomic lifecycle exists.

Missing substrate is engineering work, not scientific failure. Existing BPCR
r09 components are design patterns only and cannot be adapted in place as r05.

## Scientific value

PCPI remains aligned with the variable-`N` project destination as a bounded
architecture prerequisite. It can determine whether one current MAPR-compatible
association/decoder path commutes internally and physically across a held-out
roster size, whether only the strictly broader free family finds an output-
commuting solution, or whether both current paths should be deleted before a
larger direct-value experiment.

That deletion/retention information is real but upstream. PCPI does not test
task return or recovery value, and the missing Gemini advisory adds no new
mechanism or target-UAV rationale. The scientific value is therefore sufficient
for one fail-fast technical gate, but not sufficient for the full 137/246/424-
day construction envelope without measured feasibility and cost.

## Identifiability and strongest alternative

R05 has strong finite-family identification for its narrow question: a literal
containing comparator, equal information and work, held-out `N=7`, exhaustive
presentations, public-record choice diversity, exact command equality,
per-size competence/sensitivity gates, matched-prefix commutation and a
first-true branch map.

Its strongest surviving alternative is:

> A finite-template ETA/radio teacher imitator operating through centralized
> stable-key canonical ordering can satisfy exact presentation commutation and
> registered sensitivity without learning a decision-relevant variable-roster
> coordination mechanism.

Closely related alternatives remain deterministic tie effects, free-family
finite-budget optimization geometry and unavailable or costly shared physical
keys in distributed deployment. Neither static bindability nor a later
finite-family positive can remove these alternatives or establish recovery
value.

## Total-cost interpretation

The accepted unmeasured static envelope is:

| Quantity | Low | Central | High |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Experienced-engineer effort | 137 d | 246 d | 424 d |
| Typical CPU-only object | 128 CPUh | 416 CPUh | 1,420 CPUh |
| Eight-worker wall time | 30 h | 75 h | 260 h |
| Aggregate RSS | 8 GiB | 16 GiB | 32 GiB |
| Peak scratch | 60 GiB | 180 GiB | 500 GiB |
| Durable storage | 18 GiB | 60 GiB | 160 GiB |
| Total I/O | 180 GiB | 700 GiB | 2,200 GiB |

The hard exhaustion envelope is much larger than the typical projection. It
can require 58,342,768,640 correctly rounded inverse-normal values; at 10
microseconds each that primitive alone is at least about 162,000 CPUh, before
QR or state simulation. The scientific caps cannot be reduced. This tail risk
is a reason for the bounded gate, not a reason to alter r05 or silently accept
the central estimate.

## Exact bounded TEST gate recommended

Portfolio should separately authorize Operational Root and the same-direction
CM to freeze one compute-bounded TEST package containing only:

1. a source/ABI/numeric-library identity that contains no r05 master,
   coordinate, state, parameter or scientific manifest;
2. deterministic nonproduction fixtures at prospective widths
   `1/8/32/128` proving public-law, tensor, command, permutation, literal
   containment, work-count and leakage conformance;
3. one bounded state-attempt batch, every distinct matrix-shape inverse-normal
   plus QR path, one 24-state forward/backward/scalar-AdamW update, one
   synthetic 61,992-presentation exhaustive unit, compression and atomic
   resume at workers `1/4/8`;
4. exact output digests, logical counters and baseline/optimized CPU, wall,
   per-worker/aggregate RSS, uncompressed/compressed bytes and total I/O; and
5. one CM-authored full-object projection with low/central/high assumptions and
   explicit hard-exhaustion primitive bounds.

The gate must fail closed on any byte, order, logical-work, leakage,
containment, trajectory, worker-count or resume mismatch. Its source, fixture,
test, command, compute and resource envelope must be separately frozen before
execution. This recommendation itself creates none of them and requests no
lease.

## Gate return branches and revisit conditions

After a technically accepted gate artifact returns through Operational Root:

1. **Exact conformance failure or science ambiguity:** no-current for full r05
   construction. Return any genuine scientific ambiguity to this EM; do not
   repair it by tolerance, approximation, cap reduction, alternate comparator
   or automatic r06.
2. **Exact conformance but disproportionate measured cost:** no-current for
   full construction when measured exact-numeric, trace/lifecycle or hard-tail
   burden remains disproportionate to the architecture-only decision value.
3. **Exact conformance and materially credible bounded cost:** return the CM
   artifact to this EM for scientific/value intake, then ask Portfolio for a
   fresh full-construction-versus-no-current decision. Gate success does not
   itself authorize construction, identity, coordinate, model, training,
   evaluation or empirical activity.

Revisit a no-current outcome only when at least one exact decision-relevant
fact changes:

- a new exact-numeric/native route materially lowers measured engineering or
  resource cost without changing r05;
- a technically accepted gate closes the current conformance/throughput/
  storage uncertainty;
- new independent mechanism or target-UAV evidence materially increases the
  value of the finite architecture question; or
- a different lower-cost variable-`N` path makes PCPI's deletion information
  newly necessary or clearly redundant.

Completion order, provider availability and the unverified Gemini operation
have no ranking or revisit meaning.

## Claim ceiling

The largest possible future claim remains limited to the frozen 576-state,
256-update, sixteen-paired-role family with fitting `N={3,5}`, held-out `N=7`,
exhaustive registered presentations, public-record decoder-choice diversity,
per-size state-action and association-row sensitivity and exact physical-command
commutation. Only `RETAIN_INVARIANT_DECODER` could add finite-family internal
commutation. `SELECT_FREE_PRESENTATION_FAMILY` could claim only output
commutation plus registered residual activity.

No definition, static return or TEST gate supports a present architecture
result, task return, post-loss recovery value, robustness, arbitrary `N` or
roster composition, repeated churn, general invariance, unique mechanism,
distributed stable-key availability, UAV or real-aircraft transfer, flight,
safety or deployment.
