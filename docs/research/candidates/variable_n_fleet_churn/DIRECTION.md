## Controlling conformance result

R01 required one presentation-independent inverse-mapped physical action for a fixed physical
state and checkpoint. A source-only finite counterexample at the committed R01 implementation
found an `N=5` reverse co-presentation for a synthetic finite, nonzero near-tie parameterization:

- aligned physical-agent encoding, pooled-summary, and score-logit differences were respectively
  `2.220446049250313e-16`, `1.3877787807814457e-16`, and `5.551115123125783e-17`;
- the canonical agent-minus-null logit delta was `0.0`, so the opaque-rank exact-tie decoder chose
  the physical agent;
- the reverse physical-aligned delta was `-5.551115123125783e-17`, so it chose null; and
- agent rows, legal masks, fixed occupants, opaque ranks, legal physical support, inverse mapping,
  and source/native identity remained correctly co-permuted or bound.

This directly contradicts R01's constitutive predicate
`inverse_mapped_physical_command_equal`. It proves only reachability of this finite-precision
mechanism for the named `N=5/reverse` synthetic witness. It does not identify the quarantined
attempt's arm, address, checkpoint, return, frequency of near ties, or algorithm polarity.

No same-R01 repair exists: restoring equality must change the finite action denoted by the same
parameters on at least one presentation. Canonical sorting, an alternate arithmetic kernel, a
tolerance/near-tie quotient, rounded logits or probabilities, presentation aggregation, or a new
physical categorical sampler would define new finite action, gradient, checkpoint, or RNG coupling
semantics. Correcting the validator alone improves observability but cannot repair the action flip.

The stochastic path independently requires revision. Its candidate-row CDF and one token-level
uniform do not define a presentation-free physical categorical sampler. R02 must therefore freeze
deterministic and stochastic physical-action semantics together.
