## Scientific question

Can a presentation-safe shared MAPR-4 policy learn post-loss recovery from unshaped external return
at training rosters `N={3,5}` and show preliminary recovery direction on untouched `N=7` worlds
relative separately to a strictly containing same-information `DIRECT-SET-AR` learner and the
competent fixed `BCRH-PERSIST` controller?

## Current scientific object

The complete `em:variable_n_fleet_churn:innovator` decision selected
`VNFC-BPCR-BEXP-PRESENTATION-SAFE-RETURN-R01` at `B/EXPLORE`.

- Treatment: `MAPR-4` with only meaning-preserving presentation/relabel-path repair.
- Learned null: strictly containing `DIRECT-SET-AR`, with identical observations, worlds, reward,
  interaction count, PPO exposure, checkpoint schedule, and bitwise-copied homologous base
  initialization. Because `DIRECT` contains MAPR, any MAPR advantage can support only finite-budget
  factorization, optimization, or regularization—not expressivity or information necessity.
- Fixed comparator: `BCRH-PERSIST` on the same held-out worlds and public state. Its model knowledge
  and decision cost are reported; optimizer parity is inapplicable.
- Host: the exact two-zone, one-unannounced-executor-loss simulator; train only at `N={3,5}` and
  keep `N=7` outside training, tuning, architecture/checkpoint selection, and seed-count decisions.
- Objective: `J_ext = 0.5 R_fail_60 + 0.5 U_total`, without intermediate shaping. The primary
  held-out endpoint is `R_fail_60`; `U_total`, `U_intact`, recovery latency, reacquisition time, and
  zero-service seconds are context measurements.

Before result-bearing execution, `PS-B0` must test the actual runner at initial and debug-final
checkpoints for both learned arms on 18 states spanning all `(N, failed-zone)` cells. Canonical,
reverse, cyclic, and one seed-fixed random presentation must preserve legal support and the exact
inverse-mapped physical command. Internal score equality is diagnostic only. Any mismatch is an
incomplete engineering attempt, not algorithm evidence.

The selected sequence is:

- debug seed `2026090101`, eight updates per learned arm;
- primary seeds `2026090111`, `2026090121`, and `2026090131`, 64 updates per arm; and
- optional seeds `2026090141` and `2026090151`, added only for training-side variance or a technical
  issue before held-out `N=7` values are revealed.

Each update uses 16 balanced training episodes, 96 joint-decision transitions, and 16 optimizer
steps per arm. Each exploratory seed evaluates learned-arm update 0 and update 64 on eight common
fresh worlds in every `N={3,5,7}` by failed-zone cell, plus BCRH on the same sixteen `N=7` worlds.
All checkpoints and the final seed count are fixed before any `N=7` endpoint is opened.

## Interpretation boundary

Revision-09 remains consumed and invalid for value attribution: its 277/1024 consistent-relabel
failures forced `ASSOCIATION_INVALID -> INVALID`. Its later return, competence, training-gain, and
comparator coordinates carry no positive or negative polarity into this object.

PCPI R05 remains closed prerequisite evidence for physical-output commutation and a future revisit
boundary. Its full 576-state construction, exact-numeric dependency, oracle, and architecture
selection are neither required by R01 nor substitutes for return-bearing evidence.

The strongest live alternative is a simple ETA/radio/clearance/retention rule that BCRH already
executes: MAPR may only regularize finite-budget discovery while DIRECT spends the same update
budget on unnecessary prefix interactions. If DIRECT is inactive, the factorization comparison is
nonidentified; if BCRH is incompetent, only that comparison is nonidentified.

The exact-budget proposition is falsified only when common validity, presentation safety, action
sensitivity, and comparator competence hold and either MAPR has nonpositive final-minus-initial
recovery on at least one training roster in every exploratory seed, or MAPR learns at `N={3,5}` but
has nonpositive paired `N=7` failed-zone recovery against both DIRECT and BCRH in every seed. Mixed
signs or zone reversal mean instability/heterogeneity. Telemetry, resource, host, relabel, or
comparator failures mean incomplete/nonidentified.

The maximum claim is preliminary learnability, direction, instability, and finite-budget held-out
`N=7` return/recovery evidence on the exact simulator support and named implementations. It cannot
establish stable superiority, a confirmatory C result, arbitrary-`N`, repeated churn, general MARL,
general permutation invariance, unique mechanism, UAV/hardware transfer, safety, flight, or
deployment.
