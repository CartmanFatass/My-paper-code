Require complete evidence, all hard validity and answerability facts, either

action-response gates, and both inert-equivalence gates. The maximum claim is

### B. Equal-class FREE comparator removes metric need

If FREE is materially better, practically equivalent, or oracle-saturated and
tied across both held-out sizes under complete valid evidence, delete the
metric-specific parameterization on this support. A generic feasible allocator
may remain useful. Beating only the public-load diagnostic changes nothing.

### C. Generic optimization effect, not metric grounding

If held-out value clears but the binding cut fails to attenuate it, legal

most generic conditioning, regularization, memorization, or noise. Do not
activate the second surface.

### D. Training-only or size interaction

Value only at `N={4,8}` is not direct variable-`N` value. A valid held-out size
interaction is reported without averaging and may motivate only one new
prospective boundary discriminator. Neither branch is positive MGTAP evidence.

### E. Nonidentified or incomplete

underoptimization, FREE cut leakage, incomplete evidence, or undefined
inference supports no positive, negative, or equivalence conclusion. No seed
replacement, threshold change, checkpoint selection, or automatic rerun is
