Let `C` denote all calibration evidence. For each cell

unchanged train-size-only panel and the four fresh calibration seeds. Select

\[
S_c=\operatorname*{argmax}_{h}V(c,h,256),
\]

with the inherited deterministic tie break: smaller learning rate, then larger
`lambda`. Selection may use no held-out roster, final seed, binding/action
estimand, revision-04 value, or result-responsive checkpoint.

For every selected cell, compute

\[
d_c=|V(c,S_c,256)-V(c,S_c,224)|,

\[

\]

its grid point lies on a boundary.

paired final-seed blocks; `F` is independent of `C` under Section 4. Conditional
