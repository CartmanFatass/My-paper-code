### Inference

The combination of a recurring low-rate gap, much larger within-actor rate gains, a positive FREE-to-low-rate-METRIC bridge on every seed, and a shrinking cross-actor contrast supports the **simpler finite-optimization explanation**. The earlier fixed-rate comparison is no longer a persuasive basis for investing in distinctive coordinate-family value at MEI \(0.01\).

This is not a claim that scalar rate explains every trajectory difference. It is a judgment that the competitive premise motivating the current family has weakened substantially under the most relevant completed comparator intervention.

## STRONGEST_CONTRADICTION AND LIVE_ALTERNATIVES

**The strongest contradiction to parking is that the residual scientific opportunity is not zero.** METRIC’s mean AUC remains positive relative to FREE at all four common rates; the low-rate pattern recurs on fresh seeds; both selected rates sit at the upper grid edge; and selected FREE retains endpoint headroom. The recorded oracle is \(0.66875\), selected FREE’s endpoint is \(0.628151222512\), and their difference is \(0.040598777488\), with seed gaps ranging from \(0.038161892361\) to \(0.042347547743\). An extension could therefore reveal consequential finite-training behavior not seen here.

That contradiction limits the decision to **reversible parking**. It does not itself select the next experiment, for two reasons.

First, the \(0.040598777488\) quantity is **oracle-minus-FREE endpoint headroom**, whereas the card’s MEI is **METRIC-minus-FREE normalized learning-curve AUC**. The endpoint gap is neither a forecast of recoverable METRIC advantage nor an interchangeable AUC effect.

Second, headroom says that the observed FREE endpoint is below the stated reference. It does not identify which mechanism can recover that gap, whether METRIC can recover more of it than FREE, or whether the difference would change an allocation decision at the declared scale.

The live explanations remain:

* **Generic effective step size and finite-budget optimization:** most directly supported by B03.
* **Anisotropic conditioning, clipping interactions, and implicit finite-training regularization:** unresolved by a scalar-rate sweep.
* **Ground-binding or population-dependent coordinate effects:** possible, but not established by the completed comparison.

The actors have the same reachable score class and public information; the maps are invertible, while the decoder, action support, team-return feedback, and per-fit work are shared. Zero explicit weight decay does not remove implicit finite-training effects. Those facts permit different learning trajectories without establishing a representational advantage or pure metric cause. Source: B03 card, “Treatment, competent comparator and surviving explanations.”

## OPTIONS_AND_SELECTION

### Selected: park this family reversibly

The reason is **not** missing confirmation, absent held-out splits, lack of global baseline optimization, failure to prove convergence, or insufficient runtime exposure. The accepted methodology explicitly permits adaptive B work and does not make C-time obligations launch conditions. The reason is that no sufficiently specific remaining native-value question is established here that makes another coordinate-family experiment the justified next investment. Source: evidence specification §§5.2, 7, and 11.1–11.4.

### Runner-up: one targeted B binding/conditioning discriminator retaining tuned FREE

This is the strongest alternative because it could distinguish scalar-rate absorption from a residual binding-dependent or anisotropic learning effect. It remains scientifically admissible.

It loses **now** because “test binding” or “test anisotropy” does not specify which event, information-to-action relationship, or credit path should let the treatment improve native return beyond competent FREE. An intervention that only makes a binding control worse can explain treatment sensitivity without restoring competitive value.

For a common native estimand, the distinction is explicit:

$$
J(M_{\mathrm{intact}})-J(M_{\mathrm{cut}})
=
\bigl[J(M_{\mathrm{intact}})-J(F_{\mathrm{tuned}})\bigr]
+
\bigl[J(F_{\mathrm{tuned}})-J(M_{\mathrm{cut}})\bigr].
$$

A large intact-minus-cut gap can arise entirely because CUT is worse than FREE, while intact METRIC still offers no material advantage over FREE. Likewise, changed gradient alignment, path length, or conditioning statistics are not enough unless connected to a native-return or competent-action consequence.

A symmetric extension beyond rate 3.0 is also affordable and legitimate B exploration. It could change the result. But the observed trend does not provide a particular reason to expect a distinctive METRIC advantage there, and resolving grid coverage alone is not the mechanism-level next question selected by this node.

### Recast: not selected

The present host already has real capacity coupling and scalar team-return credit, but it has no persistent ownership, roster replacement, hidden-role inference, partner co-adaptation, or delayed temporal-credit intervention. Merely renaming the existing allocation problem “multi-agent credit assignment” does not create a new discriminator. A genuine recast must supply the new structural event and its information/action consequences rather than inherit them by assertion. Source: B03 card’s treatment trace and the B02 intake’s “Prediction check and observation that bounds the result.”
