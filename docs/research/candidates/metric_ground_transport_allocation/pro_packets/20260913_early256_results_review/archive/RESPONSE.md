## 4. Assessment of the no-third-pair and reopening rationale

**The strongest continuation case is real:** 8241 demonstrated a local beyond-MEI contrast, 8214 preserves support at a different exposure, and only two early256 training instances have been observed. A new unscreened matched pair would add independent training evidence about recurrence. Another evaluation panel of an existing checkpoint would not serve that purpose. Mixed signs do not make replication information-free, and the absence of stable superiority is neither a universal stopping rule nor a requirement to keep sampling. [B01 E0][S6]; [B02 intake, stated alternative][S3]; [specification, §11.8.3][S12].

The DM nevertheless has a coherent reason not to choose that alternative now: the explicitly selected recurrence question returned a substantial adverse realized contrast, while the broader same-family history already shows that isolated favorable results coexist with unfavorable ones. There is no demonstrated incremental development opportunity in the current packet that makes another unchanged draw especially valuable. Maintaining the simpler generic development default while suspending this line is a defensible response to that record. This is a marginal-value judgment under uncertainty, not a quantified proof of zero value or a scientific requirement to stop after exactly two pairs.

One qualification should remain explicit in the final rationale: **a third pair is not rejected because it lacks mechanism identification or population power; it is declined because its expected practical contribution is judged insufficient at this boundary.** The intake already supplies the practical-value judgment, so this is a clarification of its valid basis, not a defect requiring a new run. If an actual recurrence-sensitive development decision later arises, the same unchanged comparison may again be worthwhile; an entirely new architecture or customer is not required. [B02 intake, “DM action and independent review”][S3]; [specification, §§11.8–11.9][S12].

The card's unrealized branches are also appropriately limited. Two positive points would have supplied a second local recurrence observation, not stable superiority or automatic C promotion. An inside-band second point would have preserved its sign without equivalence. A damaged primary would have supplied no paired polarity and no scientific evidence for failure. The actual adverse result must not acquire stronger inferential authority merely because it supports stopping. Both positive and adverse results receive the same evidence ceiling. [B02 card, §3][S1].

The proposed reopening conditions are scientifically appropriate when they mean a concrete new development question, comparator or mechanism hypothesis, or credible new evidence that changes the value of the next observation. They must not be reduced to a positive-first gate. Unused time or a revised watchdog is not itself a scientific reason, although actual cost or feasibility changes can affect the value of an already meaningful question. No new diagnostic, third pair, replacement design or consultation is selected here. The DM owns any final PARK and its later revision; the empirical conclusion remains the two local results, not the lifecycle label. [B02 intake][S3]; [AGENTS, §2][S15].
