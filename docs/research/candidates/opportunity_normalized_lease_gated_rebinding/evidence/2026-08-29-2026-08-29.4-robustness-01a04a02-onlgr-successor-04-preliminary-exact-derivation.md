`E[min(4K,7)]=4q+7(1-q)=7-3q`,

## Limitations

This is exact finite-grid arithmetic for one frozen host, not a stochastic or
learned observation. `RATE` no longer attains both unrestricted optima, so the
predecessor's exact coincidence does not literally repeat. Its remaining
`5/2048` ceiling gap and its win over `EXP` are specific to this grid, cost,
clock, ordering, and equal weighting. The payload-null shell matches direct
charges rather than computation. The large age-conditioned gap shows that all
three deployed families remain competence-restricted.

Most importantly, the mandatory fresh Innovator has not completed: strict
transport is currently `ZERO_SEND_FAILED`. These calculations therefore remain
an in-flight EM derivation and cannot be raised to accepted synthesis or used
for a lifecycle recommendation.
