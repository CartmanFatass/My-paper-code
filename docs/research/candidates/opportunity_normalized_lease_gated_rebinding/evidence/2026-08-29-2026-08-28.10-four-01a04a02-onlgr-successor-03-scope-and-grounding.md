## Question and non-goals

The fresh question is whether a prospectively defined family with equal total
physical-time exposure but different opportunity partitions can give a
material direct-return reason to prefer one global per-opportunity event
probability over one global exposure-linked policy, or conversely show that
physical-time linking is the smaller competent family. The first burden is
structural: the policy families must not be reparameterizations on the chosen
host, useful renewal must beat competent no-action and payload-null real-action
controls, and the two predeclared clock strata must require materially
different event probabilities before any learned comparison is considered.

This cycle does not repair or repeat B3. It does not read, relaunch, reinterpret,
or calibrate from the quarantined B3 raw package, its branch, policies,

does not reuse an old prompt, key, operation, provider conversation, material

## Valid neutral grounding

The retained valid record is mixed. The complete finite-budget ONLGR package
beat RAW on the registered worst schedule and on post-action RAND-IID return
and service. It did not demonstrate a registered-material increment over
task-content-blind TIMING-ONLY, and B2 did not demonstrate material
timing-flexible value over one learned global exposure-linked rate. ROAD beat
RAW but lost to NEVER and barely beat SHAM. These facts preserve a local
global-rate or regularization hypothesis while making competent nulls and
action-shell controls mandatory. They do not decide whether the correct global
parameterization is per opportunity or per unit physical exposure.

The B2 source supplies one structural fact, not a result for this cycle. At a
legal boundary its constant-rate family uses
`u(e)=1-exp(-lambda*e)`, and every actual routine opportunity, including
`KEEP`, starts the next exposure interval. On an event-free sequence the
survival factors therefore multiply over the partitioned eligible exposure.
The current cycle isolates that policy-link geometry from B2 learning,
two-agent task content, marks, safety, and checkpoints.

## Exact structural host

The host is a single regenerative renewal unit with direct physical-time
service. It is deliberately smaller than the B2 host so that policy geometry
can be derived exactly.

- A renewed plan supplies service `1` for exactly `L=8` physical ticks after
  renewal and service `0` afterward until the next renewal.
- A real `RENEW` event resets the service age and pays action cost `c=2`
  service-tick units. `KEEP` preserves age and pays no cost. Renewal is always
  legal; there is no task content, mark, hidden state, safety action, or
  terminal censoring.
- Direct return is the infinite-horizon regenerative average
  `(service ticks - 2 * renewal count) / physical ticks`. There is no discount,
  reward shaping, optimizer, checkpoint, or sampling error.
- `DENSE` opportunities occur every `Delta=1` physical tick. `SPARSE`
  opportunities occur every `Delta=4` physical ticks. Each stratum is observed
  in steady state over equal physical time; their opportunity partitions are
  intentionally different. The service lifetime is an integer multiple of
  both spacings: `m_DENSE=8`, `m_SPARSE=2`.
- At a real opportunity, a policy event is an independent Bernoulli draw with
  the family's event probability. All expectations are evaluated exactly, so
  no RNG coordinate exists. For work accounting, both families receive the
  same physical-tick slots, exposure values, real-opportunity flags, and dummy
  no-action slots. Only real opportunities can act or contribute probability;
  dummy slots carry no score, action, cost, reset, or evidence.

For one constant boundary-event probability `q` in a stratum with spacing
`Delta`, the event-free opportunity count is geometric. The proposition to be
proved or refuted before use is

`J_Delta(q) = 1 - (1-q)^(8/Delta) - (2q/Delta)`, with `J_Delta(0)=0`.

This is a claim about the frozen host, not an observed result.

## Exact policy families and controls

Use the rational base grid

`Q = {0/16, 1/16, ..., 16/16}`.

The unrestricted stratum diagnostic chooses a separate `q` from `Q` for each
stratum. It is not a deployed family and exists only to test whether the
physical strata require different optimal probabilities.

The two deployed global families are:

1. `PER-OPPORTUNITY(p)`: one `p in Q` and `q_DENSE=q_SPARSE=p`.
2. `EXPOSURE-LINKED(r)`: one base per-tick event probability `r in Q`,
   equivalent for `r<1` to `lambda=-log(1-r)`, with
   `q_DENSE=r` and `q_SPARSE=1-(1-r)^4`. The endpoint `r=1` is the limiting
   always-event policy.

Both families receive identical information and exact evaluation work. Both
are told the current exposure and opportunity flag; the per-opportunity family
is definitionally constant in exposure, which is the treatment axis rather
than missing information. Both use the same single real renewal action, cost,
age reset, direct-return function, stratum weights, and lower-activity tie
break.

Competent controls are:

- `KEEP`, which never renews and has long-run direct return `0` after the
  initial plan ages out; and
- `PAYLOAD-NULL-SHELL`, which executes events with exactly the candidate's
  stratum probabilities and action costs but does not reset service age. Its
  long-run service is zero and its exact direct return is `-2q/Delta` in each
  stratum. It is a real cost-bearing action shell, not forced inactivity.

## Estimands, materiality, and exact branch map

The observational unit is one predeclared clock stratum, not an opportunity or
tick. All quantities are exact expectations. Each global family's primary
estimand is the equally weighted mean of its `DENSE` and `SPARSE` direct
returns. Exact ties select lower mean physical event rate, then the lower grid
index.

Freeze these margins before any answer:

- direct-return materiality `M_R=1/32` per physical tick;
- event-probability heterogeneity `M_Q=1/4`; and
- near-optimal tolerance `T_Q=1/64` direct-return units.

For each stratum let `q*` be its unrestricted grid maximizer and let its
near-optimal set contain every `q in Q` within `T_Q` of the stratum maximum.
The structural gates and branches are:

1. `FAMILIES_COLLAPSE` if the two global families induce identical
   `(q_DENSE,q_SPARSE)` pairs over all nondegenerate grid settings. This stops
   before direct-return comparison and before CM.
2. `NO_DIRECT_HEADROOM` if either stratum's unrestricted best fails to exceed
   both `KEEP` and its matched payload-null shell by more than `M_R`. This is a
   bounded structural null and stops before learning.
3. `ONE_GLOBAL_OPERATING_POINT` if either best is an endpoint or if the
   minimum separation between the two strata's near-optimal sets is below
   `M_Q`. This stops before learning because robust probability heterogeneity
   is absent.
4. After the first three gates pass, optimize each global family on the equal
   stratum mean. `PER_OPPORTUNITY_ADVANTAGE` requires its optimum to exceed the
   exposure-linked optimum by more than `M_R`.
   `EXPOSURE_LINKED_ADVANTAGE` requires the reverse by more than `M_R`.
   Otherwise the branch is `NO_MATERIAL_LINK_DIFFERENCE`.

Every branch reports service and action-cost decomposition. A gain that exists
only because one family suppresses real work is invalid; the dummy-call and
real-opportunity contract prevents that interpretation. A payload-null shell
matching the candidate prevents action cost or nominal activity from being
called renewal value.

## Competing explanations and predictions

- Reparameterization predicts family collapse when every legal exposure is
  constant. On the frozen two-partition object, a nondegenerate exposure link
  predicts event-free survival `exp(-lambda * sum e)`, whereas a constant
  per-opportunity policy predicts survival by opportunity count. Exact algebra
  must decide whether that difference survives the finite grids.
- A single global operating-point explanation predicts overlapping robust
  near-optimal probability sets despite different opportunity geometry.
- Physical-time renewal predicts that a useful event intensity should scale
  with `Delta`, favoring exposure linking when dense and sparse strata require
  different boundary probabilities.
- Opportunity-count renewal predicts that the same probability per real
  decision is the smaller useful rule and can favor `PER-OPPORTUNITY`.
- Generic inaction, cost avoidance, or shell regularization predicts failure
  against `KEEP` or the matched payload-null action shell.
- Grid artifacts predict endpoint optima, tie sensitivity, or near-optimal-set
  overlap rather than stable material separation.

No route is favored before the exact derivation and independent Innovator.

## Outcome-to-claim and Portfolio branches

- Collapse, no headroom, or one global operating point supports a scientific
  stop. It can narrow the surviving interpretation to generic global-rate
  regularization or support parking, but EM will choose the recommendation only
  after independent review.
- A material per-opportunity advantage establishes only a constructive
  single-unit, two-spacing reason to study opportunity-normalized policy
  linking. It does not validate learned ONLGR or `REBIND`.
- A material exposure-linked advantage establishes only the corresponding
  constructive physical-clock result and weighs against replacing the link by
  one constant per-opportunity probability on this host.
- No material family difference preserves structural distinctness without a
  decision-relevant value difference and stops before learning.
- A mathematical ambiguity that can change a branch permits at most one exact
  CM enumeration after an exact writer transfer. It does not permit a
  stochastic experiment or broader host search inside this cycle.
