  supplies useful history-independent cadence handling that the matched RAW
  head does not acquire under the frozen budget.
- **Timing/tenure sufficiency:** the observed advantage needs elapsed, lease,
  age, busy, cause, and work coordinates but not binding, mismatch, or partner
  task content.
- **Fixed/global-rate sufficiency:** lease plus a state-blind operating rate or
  action shell explains the useful regularization; learned exposure dependence
  adds no decision-relevant value.
- **Deterministic-cadence leakage:** realized preceding intervals predict later
  intervals on deterministic schedules, so the seven-cell contrast does not
  isolate history-independent future-cadence handling.
- **Task-state-conditioned marked action:** current task content changes useful
  voluntary refresh/rebind decisions beyond timing-only behavior.
- **Finite-budget optimization/initialization geometry:** ONLGR-versus-RAW is a
  local parameterization package effect, not evidence for a unique mechanism.
- **Direct-value mismatch:** the broader direction may contain a valid
  constrained transform that mainly avoids a bad RAW update while still losing
