  cells.
- RATE-FLEX learned a nonconstant mapping in every seed. Its 20-cell diagnostic
  lambda range was positive but small, `0.0001473..0.0009183`, around rates near
  `0.03`.
- Every seed/arm cell passed the prospective voluntary-action support rule, and
  all mandatory conformance, safety, KEEP, filtration, identity, and work facts
  passed without a missing fact or anomaly.

Thus literal head collapse, missing support, initial-policy mismatch, incomplete
panels, and implementation nonconformance do not explain the branch. The
nonconstant grid is enough to rule out an exactly identical final rate function;
it was not a prospectively registered, trajectory-weighted minimum-dose gate and
does not establish large or decision-relevant rate separation.

## Strongest alternative explanation

The strongest surviving alternative is weak realized functional dose or finite-
budget optimization geometry. Under eight updates and this host's geometry,
RATE-FLEX learned only a small residual modulation around essentially the same
global operating rate. Because both arms already turn eligible exposure into a
strongly exposure-dependent boundary probability, there may have been little
remaining useful heterogeneity for the flexible head to exploit. Another host,
budget, or optimization path could produce and benefit from stronger timing
dependence; the fixed `rho=0.5` mark law and reward/lease geometry may also limit
its marginal value.

This alternative prevents claims that one global rate is generally sufficient,
equivalent, or noninferior; that timing coordinates have no causal value; that
an optimally trained flexible rate has only the observed effect; or that more
training and other environments must remain null. It does not prevent the
frozen absorption action for this exact package.
