## Conclusion first

Exact r05 is **semantically single-valued and architecturally finite**, and its
host, information boundaries, learned arms, controls, analyzers and atomic
lifecycle can be bound literally. No science-bearing ambiguity remains. The
five r04 defects are closed by the r05 card, and this assessment preserves the
Pro-closed/Gemini-retained object, strongest alternative and claim ceiling
unchanged.

It is **not currently construction-ready or cost-bounded**. Direct repository
inspection found no r05 native host, numeric-certificate substrate, policy/
trainer, exact ANALYTIC solver/checker, analyzer, runner, registry entry or
resume chain. The existing
`experiments/candidates/voronoi_quadrature_field_policy/` package is the
superseded `VQFP-B1-MATH-CLOSURE-20260812-04` periodic, float32, categorical,
two-arm, 12-seed, 375-update, 32-tick serial-Python object. It cannot be adapted
in place or treated as r05 evidence. Only the shared source-keyed native
loader/cache and fail-closed registry patterns are reusable substrate.

Four exact-certificate families are technically realizable with new code:

1. exact rational spatial/effort geometry and conservation;
2. correctly rounded binary64/binary256S/binary256 functions with directed
   proof objects and fail-closed subnormal handling;
3. literal FERL/FREE/NO_PORT/reassociation and exact binary64 update graphs;
   and
4. exact rational seed reduction plus certified Student inference.

The fifth family, the exact finite-lattice `ANALYTIC-ONE-STEP` global optimum,
has a sound certificate architecture but **no credible worst-case tractability
bound**. The objective is separable before its final reduction, but it is not
globally convex: its backlog hinge can make the exponential coefficient have
either sign, and the canonical binary64 graph adds plateaus and exact-bit ties.
A structured interval branch-and-bound may be fast on ordinary states, yet in
the worst case it can degenerate toward the complete integer simplex. This is
an engineering/cost fact, not a definition ambiguity and not permission to use
an approximation.

The card's certificate requirement is itself the other dominant cost. The
frozen workload entails approximately 1.90 trillion correctly rounded neural
activation calls, 32.5 billion Adam square roots, 20 billion working-score/
entropy special-function calls, 5.43 billion binary256S exponentials and 1.18
billion certified Gamma-CDF inverses. A proof-reusing universally certified
kernel could keep the transcript compact; materialized per-call directed
brackets have a tens-to-hundreds-of-terabytes floor. The repository currently
contains neither route.

## Exact-certificate feasibility

### 1. Exact spatial geometry, piecewise integrals and conservation

**Proof argument: feasible.** `Q_x`, `2*Q_x`, `Q_E` and every count fit in
unsigned 64-bit storage, while products and rational integral numerators need
at least 128-bit or arbitrary-width integers. Geometry is a finite sequence of
integer sums, midpoint numerators and ordered comparisons. Triangular plume
integrals split at a finite set of exact rational breakpoints and reduce to
degree-at-most-two rational polynomials. Largest-remainder allocation compares
cross-products exactly and has a fixed ascending tie rule.

Prospective implementation uses checked 64-bit coordinates, `unsigned
__int128` where statically sufficient and an arbitrary-width integer/rational
fallback for products/certificates. The independent checker replays only
integer/rational identities: positivity, partition endpoints, unit sum,
piecewise coverage of the cell, integral numerator/denominator and exact count
sum. Complexity is `O(N log N)` per apportionment and `O(N)` per tick for the
two-front piecewise field. Certificates are kilobytes per reset/block, not a
dominant cost.

Remaining uncertainty is engineering only: the smallest safe fixed-width bound
for every intermediate should be proved during construction; arbitrary-width
fallback cannot select precision from results.

### 2. Correctly rounded binary64 functions and conversions

**Proof argument: feasible but no repository substrate exists.** A Ziv-style
loop can evaluate each exact rational/dyadic input with outward-rounded
high-precision intervals until both endpoints round to the same binary64 bit
pattern. The certificate contains function ID, exact input, directed enclosure,
working precision, output bits and proof that the enclosure lies inside the
ties-to-even rounding cell. The checker uses a separately implemented interval
kernel and integer decoding of the target rounding cell.

Candidate library boundary:

- GMP/MPFR or equivalent for arbitrary integers and directed elementary
  evaluation;
- Arb/FLINT, MPFI or a separately pinned interval implementation as checker;
- a custom monotone root/bracket engine for Gamma-CDF inverse and Student-CDF
  inverse, because ordinary Boost/SciPy inverse functions are candidates, not
  certificates; and
- a pinned fenv/MXCSR boundary plus bit-level subnormal tests so FTZ/DAZ,
  contraction, reassociation and `-0` retention fail closed.

`exp`, `log`, `sin`, `cos`, `tanh`, `sigmoid`, square root, `lgamma`,
`digamma` and `trigamma` are covered by interval refinement. Gamma-CDF inverse
uses monotonicity: bracket the exact midpoint probability between certified CDF
enclosures at adjacent candidate boundaries until one binary64 rounding cell
is proved. Student inverse is analogous and is low volume. Difficult-to-round
inputs make precision and time data-dependent, but never output-dependent in a
scientific-selection sense; exhaustion is the frozen numeric invalidity.

Dependency licensing and distribution must be reviewed before construction.
Boost's permissive surface is not enough for the proofs; GMP/MPFR/Arb/FLINT or
equivalents introduce separately pinned native and licensing obligations not
present in the current lock.

### 3. binary256S

**Proof argument: feasible with a custom type.** Store sign, a normalized
256-bit significand and an arbitrary-width signed exponent. Exact promotion
from binary64 is mechanical. Subtraction, exact dyadic sum and division use
wide integers with guard/sticky bits and one ties-to-even rounding. `exp(x)` is
computed by directed range reduction and interval refinement until one
binary256S rounding cell is isolated. The unbounded exponent is necessary
because a finite binary64 logit difference can have magnitude near `1e308`,
well beyond a fixed machine exponent even though `x<=0` after stabilization.

Each certificate records the exact source dyadic, normalized output, rounding
mode, enclosure and precision. The checker verifies normalization, exact sum
coverage, division enclosure, positivity and final binary64 conversion. The
graph has `O(K)` operations per softmax, but its 5.43-billion-exp volume is a
major cost. No overflow/underflow fallback or clamp is permitted.

### 4. Exact ANALYTIC global minimizer and lexicographic tie

Let `x_i` and `y_i` be SENSE and RELAY counts for agent `i`. With current-state
denominators fixed, each agent contributes the canonical rounded form of

```text
c_U * h_i * exp(-k_i*x_i)
+ c_B * max(B_i + g_i*(1-exp(-k_i*x_i)) - d_i*y_i, 0),
```

subject to nonnegative integer counts and `sum_i(x_i+y_i)=Q_E`. This proves
three useful facts: the pre-reduction objective is agent-separable; every local
term has one backlog hinge; and the feasible set is a finite integer simplex.

The strongest credible exact route is:

1. partition by backlog-active/saturated status and exact count-simplex boxes;
2. derive outward interval bounds for each local exponential/hinge term over a
   box, including every binary64 rounding node;
3. use the continuous relaxation, KKT/water-filling only for coordinates whose
   coefficient proves convex, and endpoint bounds for concave coordinates;
4. enumerate only lattice points in surviving narrow boxes;
5. emit a disjoint cover DAG whose leaves are exact candidates or excluded
   boxes with lower bounds strictly above the chosen objective bits; and
6. separately cover every lexicographically larger prefix, proving either a
   larger objective bit pattern or infeasibility.

The checker verifies root-simplex coverage, child disjointness, feasibility,
directed bounds, exact candidate objectives and lexicographic exclusion. This
is materially stronger than conceptual enumeration.

The assumptions that **do hold** are finiteness, separability before final
reduction, positive fixed denominators, monotone coverage, a single local
backlog hinge and exact physical-coordinate ordering. The assumptions that do
**not hold generally** are global convexity, strict convexity, monotone sense
benefit, unique optimum, a lower-bounded objective-bit gap or small proof size.
Inside an active backlog piece, the coefficient of `exp(-k_i*x_i)` is the
difference between unserved-length benefit and acquired-backlog cost and may
have either sign. Canonical rounding adds flat steps and exact ties.

Therefore the only unconditional worst-case bound is complete enumeration:

```text
number_of_vectors = binomial(Q_E + 2N - 1, 2N - 1), N<=12,
```

which is astronomically large (about `10^293` at `N=12`). No credible static
upper bound on one certificate or all 1,572,864 solves follows. Typical
certificates may be small when interval margins are strong; that is precisely
what the later TEST-only probe must measure. Any local optimum, tolerance,
relaxed action, altered objective or uncertified timeout is forbidden.

### 5. Exact seed inference

**Proof argument: feasible and low volume.** Every seed statistic is a finite
binary64 dyadic. Means and unbiased variances use arbitrary integers/rationals;
binary256 square root and arithmetic use the same rounding-cell certificates as
binary256S. The Student critical value uses a directed monotone CDF bracket at
exact probability `7199/7200`. Reverse intervals are bit-exact `[-U,-L]` and
all margin comparisons reduce to exact binary256 integer/exponent comparisons.

The checker consumes 180 fixed rows, their 24 dyadics, exact rational
mean/variance, quantile/square-root certificates, interval bits and literal
predicate operands. Complexity and storage are negligible relative to
training and ANALYTIC.

## Comparator, containment and controls

Literal FERL-within-FREE is feasible with one parameter layout and one arm
multiplier. FERL and FREE execute the same residual affine; FERL multiplies the
output and gradient by canonical `+0`, while FREE uses one. Both allocate the
same residual tensors and Adam slots. Residual-zero FREE and FERL then share
the same history, logits, binary256S graph, shapes, midpoint vector, Gamma
values, exact `z`, counts and physical action. A nonzero reachable residual
bias proves strict containment without modifying the FERL subspace.

NO_PORT replaces only self/present actor length inputs by `1/N`; its critic
continues to receive true measure. EQUAL is an exact direct count constructor.
ANALYTIC is current-state only and isolated from the trainer. Reassociation
substitutes cyclic lengths but preserves true cells/service and recursively
owns its action, backlog and GRU descendants.

Required later TEST-only proof obligations are:

1. **Residual-zero containment:** identical tensor/optimizer schema and exact
   bits for hidden state, logits, softmax, shape, Gamma, `z`, counts and action;
   FERL residual gradients, moments and parameters remain canonical zero.
2. **Strict residual reachability:** one fixture residual coordinate changes a
   FREE logit/action law while every common tensor remains fixed.
3. **Permutation equivariance:** every supported handle permutation restores
   actor state/action by physical rank.
4. **Handle/storage invariance:** storage-lane and worker-order permutations
   leave physical allocation and raw record order unchanged.
5. **Effort conservation:** boundary and adversarial share vectors always emit
   nonnegative counts summing exactly to `Q_E` with the frozen tie order.
6. **Address/coupling identity:** every worker width returns identical words,
   transforms and certificates; `AFER/AFRE/ANOP` remain disjoint; each intact/
   reassociated pair shares one complete vector.
7. **binary256S/action equivalence:** independent checker reproduces every
   intermediate and final binary64 target share/count for difficult-rounding
   fixtures.
8. **NO_PORT qualification:** true-length changes cannot affect evaluation
   actor output when content and `1/N` ports are held, but may affect the
   training critic exactly through its registered route.
9. **Closed-loop reassociation:** after tick zero, no intact backlog, prior
   action or hidden state can be copied into the reassociated lane.
10. **ANALYTIC proof:** checker rejects one missing box, weak bound, smaller
    objective and lexicographically larger equal-bit candidate.
11. **Analyzer completeness:** fixtures cover sentinel/event gate, all 180
    row positions, strict/non-strict equalities, decisions 1--15, decision-6
    harm annotations and descriptive-only downstream rows.

The central certificate floor follows from approximately 1.9 trillion
activation calls times a compressed 40--48-byte function/input/output/bracket
record, before Adam and other special functions. The low case requires a
machine-checked universal kernel proof whose applicability to each transcript
row is compactly committed. If the card is implemented with materialized
per-call brackets, low storage does not apply.

All writes must be chunked and content-addressed. Per-tick JSON, repeated
intact rollouts, duplicate actor forwards and scanning every historical chunk
on resume are construction defects. An update-atomic frontier limits ordinary
recomputation to less than one update (expected half an update): approximately
0.9 minutes / 5.3 minutes / 1.2 hours per interrupted job in the low/central/
high planning cases. Certificate chunks already committed remain immutable and
are referenced, not recopied. Publication overhead is estimated at 2--5% low,
8--20% central and can dominate high.
