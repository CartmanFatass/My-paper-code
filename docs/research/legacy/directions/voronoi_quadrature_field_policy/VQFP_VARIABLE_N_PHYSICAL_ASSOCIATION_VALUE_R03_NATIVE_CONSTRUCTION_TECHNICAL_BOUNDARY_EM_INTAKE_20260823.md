## Why construction should not be selected again yet

The earlier static planning evidence placed the expected case at 16
engineer-days, 820 CPU core-hours, 32 wall hours, 18 GiB RSS, 56 GiB scratch
and 11 GiB durable storage. Its high case was 20 engineer-days, 1,750 CPU
core-hours, 70 wall hours, 30 GiB RSS, 112 GiB scratch and 22 GiB durable
storage. The frozen pointwise ceiling is 20 engineer-days, 1,800 CPU
core-hours, 72 wall hours, 32 GiB RSS, 120 GiB scratch, 24 GiB durable and no
GPU.

Those static totals were explicitly conditional and left little high-case
margin. The present CM work validates useful foundations but shows that seven
integrated production families, including the full measurement seams, still
do not exist. The synthetic benchmark cannot tell how much of the old estimate
has been retired, how much residual engineering remains, whether integration
or exact-arithmetic fallback dominates the full chain, or whether cumulative
engineering and runtime still fit every ceiling.

A further construction selection on this record would therefore commit to an
unquantified residual that includes nearly all conclusion-bearing surfaces.
That is not a scientific reason to abandon r03, but it is insufficient
Portfolio cost evidence for selecting continuation now.
