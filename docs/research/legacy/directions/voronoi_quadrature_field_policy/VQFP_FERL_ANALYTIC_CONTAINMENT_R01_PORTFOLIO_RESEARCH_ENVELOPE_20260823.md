## Objective and value

Replace the unbounded numeric/certificate tail of the no-current r05 path with
one bounded theorem-or-counterexample decision. The route asks whether, on a
nondegenerate fixed-total-effort variable-`N` physical task, physical-measure
binding can be made numerically decision-preserving and exactly nested inside
a strict learned `FREE` comparator. It does not resume or partially validate
r05.

## Current authorized stage: freeze before analysis

The EM must prepare one complete pre-analysis packet that fixes:

- the shared variable-`N` domain, including training and held-out roster sizes;
- one named unsaturated fixed-total-effort target, legal allocation actions and
  task-value endpoint;
- one nondegenerate geometry class `G`;
- at most one nested, question-preserving fallback class `G'`;
- the exact physical measure and numeric routine;
- an independent oracle law;
- command-preserving reassociation/equal-mass/action-sensitivity controls;
- a constructive embedding of the treatment in the strict `FREE` comparator;
- the exact uncertainty-to-legal-action/physical-command/assay decision rule;
- the finite theorem-or-counterexample work boundary and prospective total-cost
  reporting law; and
- the three terminal branches: retain unrestricted, retain only `G'`, or
  delete.

The EM may use the existing r05 science and no-current tail evidence only as
direction-local provenance. It must not import another direction's thresholds,
results or acceptance. The proposed packet returns to Portfolio before any
theorem search. Portfolio, not the EM, freezes `G/G'` for the next stage.

## Prospective next stage, not yet authorized

After a separate freeze, the only admissible outputs would be:

1. a finite uniform tail/error certificate whose uncertainty interval cannot
   change any registered legal-action ordering, physical command or assay
   decision; or
2. an explicit geometry sequence showing divergence, certificate failure or
   action-order reversal.

If `G` fails, the already-frozen `G'` may be tested once. No third subclass,
result-dependent precision rule, `N`-specific tuning, saturated target,
degenerate geometry, removed action sensitivity, weakened strict containment,
fixed-fixture extrapolation or approximate substitute is permitted.

Fatal alternatives are generic allocation regularization and finite-budget
optimization: if command-preserving reassociation/equal-mass controls preserve
task value, or oracle-accurate `FREE` recovers the same allocation, physical-
measure binding is not identified as the mechanism.

## Claim and stop boundary

The maximum pre-efficacy claim is mathematical testability or non-testability
on the frozen geometry class, decision-preserving numeric validity and exact
comparator containment. It cannot establish task improvement, robustness,
held-out-`N` generalization, physical-measure necessity, UAV performance or
deployment.

The current stage stops after one freeze-ready packet. It creates no theorem
result, construction, CM request or provider turn. If no nondegenerate
question-preserving `G/G'` and bounded protocol can be defined, return a
complete stop/revisit milestone.
