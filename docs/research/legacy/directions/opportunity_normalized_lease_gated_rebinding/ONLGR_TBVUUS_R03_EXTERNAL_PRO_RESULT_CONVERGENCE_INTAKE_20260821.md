## Scientific intake

- mean: `-0.04778320312500001`, nominal 95% interval
  `[-0.04844332953877,-0.04712307671123001]`;
- tail: `-0.04633789062499999`, nominal 95% interval
  `[-0.04961886512947887,-0.04305691612052111]`.

For this exact full panel, those intervals provide strong nominal sign evidence
that the fixed-`t=0` ROAD package produced less valid target service than
BOOT-only KEEP. This remains the card's registered positive-utility nonpass,
not a general reverse-effect or physical-harm branch.

Against matched SHAM, the payload effects were only:

- mean: `+0.0010717773437499993`, interval
  `[0.0004137479323603788,0.0017298067551396196]`;
- tail: `+0.0019775390625000003`, interval
  `[-0.001216802377989227,0.005171880502989228]`.

The mean has nominal positive-sign evidence and the tail sign is unresolved,
but neither effect approaches its registered materiality margin. At the point-
estimate level, the matched decomposition implies SHAM-minus-NEVER effects of
approximately `-0.04885498046875` in mean and `-0.04831542968750` in tail.
Thus almost the entire ROAD-minus-NEVER loss is attributable to the registered
shell at this instant, while the ROAD payload recovers only a small fraction.

ROAD-minus-RAW is very large (`+0.80469482421875` mean and
`+0.79208984375` tail). The defensible descriptive interpretation is that the
road-constrained estimator avoided the severe degradation of the exact raw
two-sample patch. It does not establish utility over the incumbent estimate or
matched shell, identify road regularization as a unique ingredient, make RAW
claim-bearing, or change the registered branch.
