## Availability, completeness, and coherence

These terms govern all branches below.

### Scientific activity

The delta D11 definition controls. Scientific activity begins at the earliest
retained actor, critic, or optimizer-state update using any learned-arm task
transition, or retention of any learned-arm trajectory/statistic for a
claim-bearing result. Critic-only, stochastic-`KEEP`-only, partial-schedule,
and incomplete-mark learning count. A discarded dry run with no retained
learned state or result does not.

- `activity=false`: there is no learned-treatment evidence. A launch or
  preactivity engineering fact cannot support, contradict, or rank ONLGR.
- `activity=true` with incomplete output: scientific work occurred, but no
  missing endpoint may be treated as zero, failure, or a negative result.
- `activity=true` with a complete coherent package: apply the outcome map.

### Complete coherent package

A package is complete and coherent only when CM returns one technically
accepted, version-coherent result for the exact composite with:

1. the sole final checkpoints for all three learned arms and all eight seeds;
2. every registered native seven-schedule row, IID row, analytic identity and
   operational partition probe, safety row, resource fact, and fixed-rate row
   at its frozen count;
3. the activity fact, paired identities, denominators, support/ineligibility
   reasons, anomalies, and all required behavioral diagnostics; and
4. no mixed scientific revision, per-`k` checkpoint, post-result repair of a
   gate, arm, threshold, seed, schedule, episode count, or selected analysis.

A predeclared support failure is a complete scientific outcome when the
original denominator and structural failure reason are present. Examples are
`|C_s|<12`, fewer than `15/16` supported yoke pairs, a zero yoke exposure
denominator, or insufficient yoke materiality. Exposure-clamp, degenerate/
oracle, and yoke rows remain finer diagnostics: engineering-missing rows remove
only their named interpretation and, where used below, second-surface
qualification; they do not make an otherwise complete core package incoherent
or delay the Pro trigger. Any core row listed above that was never executed,
lost, silently reduced, or analyzed under another revision is engineering-
missing, not a support failure.

### Partial but interpretable native result

All eight seeds and all seven ONLGR/RAW native schedule cells, with one coherent
checkpoint/version and valid safety/resource/conformance facts, are sufficient
to compute the bounded native `P/W` contrast even if a secondary panel is
engineering-missing. Such a result is `NATIVE_COMPLETE_PACKAGE_INCOMPLETE`:
its native interpretation may be recorded, but every unavailable stronger
claim is suppressed and the terminal same-conversation Pro trigger waits for
the complete coherent package. An incomplete native endpoint, cap stop before
complete primary output, missing seed/cell, or mixed version is scientifically
inconclusive and returns to CM for unchanged-science repair.
