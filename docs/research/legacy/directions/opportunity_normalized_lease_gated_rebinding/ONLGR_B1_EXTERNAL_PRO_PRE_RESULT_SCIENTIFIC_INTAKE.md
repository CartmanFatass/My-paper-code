## Intake conclusion

The dedicated Pro review found the core three-action distribution coherent but
identified two design-changing defects that must reach CM before scientific
activity:

1. weighting the current PPO row by the following physical duration changes
   the episodic-return objective and imports the magnitude of an interval drawn
   after the current action; and
2. deterministic constant, midpoint, and alternating schedules allow realized
   `delta_t` and its state consequences to predict later cadence even though no
   explicit schedule label or future interval is observed.

A third finding is an exact algebraic identity:

```text
1-(1-sigmoid(g))^e = 1-exp(-softplus(g)*e).
```

`PROB-EXP-LEASE` is therefore not a distinct learned mechanism or an independent
confirmation. Before activity, the science card replaces that redundant learned
arm with `TIMING-ONLY-ONLGR`, retains probability-exponent only as a float64
analytic conformance check, removes every duration weight, and adds an unchanged-
checkpoint IID future-`k` panel without exceeding the frozen resource cap.

The local card and same-direction intake remain authoritative. Pro supplied
advice, not scientific acceptance, technical acceptance, implementation
authority, result evidence, or portfolio authority.

## Accepted mandatory corrections

### Event and censoring object

- B1 is now explicitly an **eligible-exposure-offset boundary link**, not an
  identified literal continuous-time hazard.
- The per-agent integer interval is frozen as `(b_prev,t]`, with the current
  endpoint included once, adjacent intervals disjoint, lease-masked slots
  excluded, and the terminal residual censored without a terminal action or
  likelihood.
- A forced safety action has zero current actor score but never terminates the
  return, critic bootstrap, GAE recursion, or prior-action credit. The affected
  agent gets exactly one transition/cost/lease reset; the unaffected dummy is
  neither a sampled `KEEP` nor an exposure reset.
- Each stochastic agent uses the complete three-action categorical likelihood.
  The team row sums stochastic-agent log-probabilities, forms one PPO ratio,
  and clips it once. Event and mark ratios are never clipped separately.

### Return-preserving optimization

- All following-duration actor, critic, and entropy weights are removed.
- Every update contains eight complete episodes from each training schedule.
  The actor loss averages schedules and episodes but sums genuine joint score
  terms inside each episode, using only the common constant `1/256` scale.
- There is no pooled-row mean, per-episode row mean, duration weight, row-count
  advantage normalization, or advantage normalization.
- The separate critic averages value losses within episode and then applies the
  same schedule/episode outer weights.
- Four epochs are four full-batch Adam steps per update. Optimizer work remains
  identical across learned arms.
- Entropy coefficient is exactly zero throughout training. The correct marked
  entropy `H_Bernoulli(u)+u*H_Bernoulli(rho)` remains diagnostic only.

### Comparator and schedule-shortcut closure

- `PROB-EXP-IDENTITY` has no checkpoint, trajectory, return, optimizer, or
  scientific vote. Float64 event and marked cumulative probabilities must agree
  with ONLGR to absolute tolerance `1e-10` in the frozen-score probe.
- The third learned arm is `TIMING-ONLY-ONLGR`: same dimensions, link, critic,
  actions, lease, safety, work, and communication, but actor binding, mismatch,
  and partner task-summary coordinates are frozen to zero. It tests whether a
  learned cadence/tenure heuristic is sufficient.
- `RAND-IID-4-16-32` evaluates every unchanged final checkpoint on 32 paired
  episodes per seed. Every next interval is sampled after the current action,
  uniformly and independently of complete visible history, with common tapes
  across arms. It is separate from the seven deterministic `P/W` cells.
- A seven-cell positive result without the IID contrast supports only a
  deterministic-schedule finite-budget package. Schedule-identity-free exposure
  value requires ONLGR to beat RAW in the IID panel by `0.02` with a paired 95%
  lower bound above zero. Task-state timing additionally requires the same
  threshold versus TIMING-ONLY.

### Partition, yoke, and inference

- The partition probe separates a frozen-score identity check from the learned
  operational marked-composition probe. The latter computes cumulative no-event,
  first-refresh, and first-rebind probabilities and their total-variation
  instability, with common nonsaturation and one-block-activity matching.
- The yoke preselects exactly one counter-keyed nonzero cyclic shift before
  legality. It never falls through to another shift, recomputes transformed
  masks/lease/busy/exposure, preserves exact time-weighted binding occupancy,
  uses all original pairs as the support denominator, and measures exposure-
  partition reassignment rather than generic age/signature change.
- `Psi` is only support-conditioned sensitivity to that one transformation. It
  cannot explain the native arm difference or establish an alignment mediator.
- Since either `P` or `W` independently triggers project value, both claim-
  bearing sign tests use Bonferroni-adjusted two-sided 97.5% paired Student-t
  intervals. The original materiality semantics remain: point estimate at least
  `0.02` or `0.03`, respectively, with adjusted sign evidence. This does not
  claim that the true effect is lower-bounded by the materiality margin.
- Ordinary 95% intervals, exact paired sign-flip p-values, leave-one-seed-out
  point estimates, and within-cell episode uncertainty are reported as
  non-gating sensitivity facts.

## Advice accepted only as claim boundaries

- A tickwise predictable integrated intensity would be needed for a literal
  continuous-time hazard claim. It is not added to B1 because that would replace
  the candidate. The boundary-link terminology and claim ceiling are narrowed
  instead.
- No no-lease factorial or refresh-versus-rebind action ablation is added.
  Consequently B1 cannot identify the causal value of the lease or of `REBIND`
  separately from `REFRESH-SAME`.
- Initial action probabilities are matched only at the declared `e=8` anchor.
  The full initialization curve is part of the treatment's finite-budget prior;
  it is reported over `e={1,4,8,16,24,32}` rather than falsely called matched.
- The stronger rule “confidence lower bound exceeds the materiality margin” is
  not adopted because the registered scientific statement concerns an estimated
  material effect with sign support, not a confidence lower bound on
  materiality. Multiplicity for the either-outcome decision is corrected.
- Extra seeds are not added. Sign-flip and leave-one-seed-out summaries expose
  eight-seed fragility without post-review budget expansion.

## Revised claim ceiling

finite budget, the eligible-exposure exponential boundary parameterization
outperformed the input-matched RAW Bernoulli parameterization on the named

remained useful when the next interval was unpredictable from visible history.

host. No B1 branch proves a literal hazard, RAW incapacity, lease causality,
REBIND causality, arbitrary-`k` robustness, variable `N`, UAV transfer, or an
