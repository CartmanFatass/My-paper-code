## Preliminary conclusion

The recovered finite panel supports one narrow algorithm result and does not
identify the proposed mechanism. ONLGR improves the registered worst-schedule
estimand over the matched RAW learner and improves return over RAW when every
next interval is drawn after the current action independently of the visible
history. It does not improve the registered seven-schedule mean-return
estimand. The task-content-blind TIMING-ONLY ONLGR ablation matches ONLGR on the
IID panel, the operational partition estimand is unavailable under its frozen

aggregates.

Accordingly, the current evidence supports at most a finite-host,
finite-training-budget and finite-recovery-panel worst-schedule plus IID return
advantage of the complete ONLGR package over RAW. It does not support an
eligible-physical-exposure event-link inductive-bias claim, task-content use,
lease or REBIND causality, a literal hazard, arbitrary `k`, UAV causality or
