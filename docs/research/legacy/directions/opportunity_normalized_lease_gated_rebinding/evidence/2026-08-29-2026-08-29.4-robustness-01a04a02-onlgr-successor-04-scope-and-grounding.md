## Exact host and action/service ordering

The host is one regenerative renewal unit. A real renewal costs `c=2`, resets
a nonstacking plan, and makes the *following* exactly `L=7` physical ticks
serviced. Opportunities are periodic with spacing `Delta=1` or `Delta=4`.
Starting immediately after a renewal, the next opportunity occurs after
`Delta` physical ticks. Service for that interval is accrued before the
opportunity action; a renewal sampled at the boundary pays its cost and resets
service for the next interval. A `KEEP` boundary leaves age unchanged and pays
no cost. Early renewal discards residual service.

Equivalently, if `K` is the number of opportunity intervals from one renewal
through the interval ending at the next renewal, a cycle has length `Delta*K`,
service `min(Delta*K,7)`, and renewal cost `2`. This convention is the
predecessor convention with only `L` changed from eight to seven.

Direct return is the infinite-horizon regenerative average

`(serviced physical ticks - 2 * renewal executions) / physical ticks`.

There is no discount, terminal censoring, task content, hidden state, safety
action, optimizer, checkpoint, or sampling error. The two strata receive equal
physical-time weight. Both policy families receive identical physical slots,
spacing, age/exposure, opportunity flags, and dummy no-action slots. Only real
opportunities can sample an event, reset service, or incur cost. Dummy slots
carry no probability, action, reset, cost, or evidence.

For a constant stratum boundary probability `q`, exact evaluation must derive

`J_Delta(q) = q * E[min(Delta*K,7)] / Delta - 2q/Delta`,

where `K` is geometric on `{1,2,...}` with success probability `q`, and
`J_Delta(0)=0`. No divisible-clock shortcut may be assumed for `Delta=4`.

## Base grid, policy families, and controls

The base grid is frozen as

`Q = {0/16,1/16,...,16/16}`.

Only free base parameters lie in `Q`; transformed probabilities are evaluated
exactly and need not lie in `Q`.

1. `RAW(p)`: one `p in Q`, with `q_1=p` and `q_4=p`.
2. `EXP(r)`: one `r in Q`, with `q_1=r` and
   `q_4=1-(1-r)^4`, evaluated exactly without projection.
3. `RATE(h)`: one `h in Q`, with
   `q_Delta=min(1,Delta*h)`. This is the prospectively registered common
   executed-rate family, not a post-result comparator.

All three families use the same information, action shell, cost, reset,
physical-time weights, and lower-activity tie break. Each family's primary
estimand is the equal mean of its spacing-one and spacing-four direct returns.
Exact ties select lower mean executed events per physical tick, then the lower
base-grid index.

The unrestricted diagnostic chooses a separate `q in Q` for each stratum. It
is not a deployed family and supplies the exact finite-grid competence ceiling
for the three global memoryless families.

Controls are:

- `KEEP`, which never renews and has long-run direct return `0`; and
- `PAYLOAD-NULL-SHELL`, which samples exactly the candidate's stratum event
  probabilities and pays the same event costs but does not reset service. Its
  long-run direct return is `-2q/Delta` in each stratum. It matches the frozen
  direct action charge, not an unclaimed computation or optimizer workload.

The age-conditioned competence ceiling lies outside the memoryless families.
It renews deterministically at the first legal opportunity boundary at or
after the seven serviced ticks have elapsed. It uses the same cost and ordering
and is evaluated separately in each stratum and at equal physical-time weight.

  headroom. This rejects link specificity at this claim ceiling. Generic-rate

## Competing explanations and predictions

- A divisible-clock grid artifact predicts that changing only `L=8` to `L=7`
  breaks the exact shared-rate coincidence and may create a material
  exponential-versus-rate separation.
- A robust generic-rate law predicts that the common-rate family still reaches
  or nearly reaches the unrestricted ceiling and is not materially worse than
  exponential linking.
- Survival-intensity specificity predicts `EXP-RATE > 1/32` because exact
  no-event survival composes over unequal opportunity partitions even when
  executed event rates do not.
- Generic clock correction without link specificity predicts that both
  physical-time families beat `RAW` but remain within the material margin of
  each other.
- Inaction, action-cost avoidance, or shell regularization predicts failure
  against KEEP or the charge-matched shell.
- Memoryless-family restriction predicts a substantial gap to the
  age-conditioned ceiling regardless of which global link wins.

No route is favored before exact derivation and the independent Innovator.
