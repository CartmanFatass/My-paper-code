## Competing explanations and distinguishing predictions

- **Decision-relevant exposure-link bias.** It predicts a positive B1 IID
  ONLGR-minus-RAW contrast together with a qualifying operational partition
  estimand and value beyond the simpler timing/global-rate controls. If task
  content matters, ONLGR should also materially beat TIMING-ONLY with adaptive
  headroom and voluntary marked support.
- **Timing/tenure sufficiency.** It predicts ONLGR and TIMING-ONLY agreement
  under post-action IID cadence even when both beat RAW. Task-content removal
  should not reduce service materially.
- **Global-rate or shell regularization.** It predicts fixed/global-rate,
  NEVER, or SHAM-like controls matching or beating the more elaborate package.
  B2 timing flexibility should add little, and ROAD's payload should add little
  over its matched shell even if both constrained treatments avoid RAW failure.
- **Deterministic cadence leakage.** It predicts a deterministic-panel benefit
  that weakens or vanishes when future intervals are drawn after action and are
  history independent. Persistence versus RAW on IID rejects this as a complete
  explanation of that pairwise gap, but does not identify task content or the
  exposure link.
- **Finite-budget initialization or optimization geometry.** It permits a
  local ONLGR-over-RAW IID effect even without partition support or value over
  timing/global controls. It predicts sensitivity to parameterization and
  training budget rather than a unique causal lease-gated mechanism.
- **Direct-value mismatch.** It predicts a constrained estimator that avoids a
  pathological RAW update yet fails to improve over a competent incumbent or
  matched shell. ROAD supplies a direct example on its separate object.

No explanation is favored at scope freeze.
