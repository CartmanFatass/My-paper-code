## EM scientific judgment

The repository evidence and fresh Innovator agree on the answer-changing gap: existing B1 and B2
results do not establish prospective direct-return headroom over competent global exposure-linked,
genuine inaction, and association-severed shell controls, and they do not estimate a supported
direct-return-optimal event-probability contrast across predeclared physical-time strata. B1 forced
KEEP and B2 forced KEEP are conformance and pairing observations, not this headroom screen. The new
question therefore is not already answered.

One executable observation is warranted, but it must be evaluation-only. Training another ONLGR-like
learner before proving headroom and heterogeneity would reverse the frozen gate order. The smallest
decision-changing object is a finite grid of fixed policies evaluated on result-blind discovery and
independent confirmation banks, with no new checkpoint and no learned second-stage comparison.

The Innovator's central design principles are retained: direct return is primary; service and action
cost are decomposed; a true KEEP policy and a real-action marginal-rate-matched shell are required;
grid selection and confirmation are separated; policy assignment occurs at episode root; the unit of
inference is a fresh root, not an agent, episode, boundary, or event; margin `0.005` is frozen; and a
positive screen remains a finite-host total-policy result.

EM makes three deliberate corrections. First, the response's reference to “16 recovered
seed/checkpoint units” is not a traceable fact in B1 or B2: both registered objects have eight root
seeds, and arms or checkpoints are not independent units. This screen therefore uses 16 newly
predeclared evaluation roots. Second, three equal bins across the capped `[0,32]` range risk making
the upper bin a support artifact under the 4/16/32 interval and 12-tick lease geometry. The smallest
prospective contrast uses two equal 16-tick eligible-exposure bins and treats support failure as a
no-go; tenure is not allowed to rescue exposure. Third, the competent global exposure-linked primary
control is the best discovery-selected member of a finite global-`lambda` grid, not a finite-budget
learned checkpoint. The retained B2 learned RATE-CONST values determine only an outcome-blind center.

### Outcome-blind grid construction

The retained B2 RATE-CONST lambdas are
`[0.029139509643148372, 0.029675171967434282, 0.02953697449602964,
0.029094204916531825, 0.029941797091303225, 0.02921486406352556,
0.029115472072202886, 0.028947621023704066]`; their frozen median is
`lambda_ref=0.029177186853336964`.

Run a forced-KEEP discovery support policy first. From legally non-KEEP-capable rows only, construct
the outcome-blind empirical exposure distribution `F0` and set
`q0 = mean_F0(1-exp(-lambda_ref*e))`. Define five probabilities
`G(q0) = {sigmoid(logit(q0)+r): r in (-2,-1,0,1,2)}`. The algorithm, not the realized `q0`, is frozen;
no reward, service, cost, value, arm identity, or confirmation fact enters it.

Evaluate on discovery:

- all 25 exposure-stratified pairs `(p_low,p_high)` in `G(q0)^2`;
- all five homogeneous global probabilities `p` in `G(q0)`;
- five global lambdas, each solved uniquely so that
  `mean_F0(1-exp(-lambda*e))` equals the corresponding member of `G(q0)`;
- forced KEEP.

The discovery-best member of each family maximizes the mean of the 16 root-level direct returns.
Exact ties prefer lower activity, then smaller high-minus-low separation, then lexicographic grid index.
The selected stratified policy's matched shell is one global probability
`p_shell = w_low*p_low + w_high*p_high`, where `w` is the forced-KEEP discovery legal-row bin mass.
It takes real legal actions, uses the same uniform and conditional mark rule, and differs only by
severing the exposure association. It is not an effector-null cost-only sham.

Only five policies run on confirmation: discovery-selected stratified, discovery-best global `p`,
discovery-best global `lambda`, forced KEEP, and the frozen matched shell. No confirmation outcome may
change a grid, policy, root, margin, bin, or selection.

### Support and conformance gates

For each confirmation exposure bin require legal non-KEEP-capable occupancy in at least 12 of 16 roots,
at least 25 legal rows in every contributing root, at least 300 pooled legal rows, no root contributing
more than 20% of pooled support, event-free reachability in at least 12 roots, and at least 200 pooled
event-free legal rows. For the selected candidate require at least 100 voluntary non-KEEP events, at
least 20 in each bin, and marginal voluntary activity at least `0.02` among legal opportunities.

The shell's confirmation event rate must be within the larger of `0.01` absolute or 10% relative of the
candidate rate; conditional REFRESH/REBIND proportions must be within `0.05` absolute unless the legal
mask makes a mark unavailable. Direct return must reconcile exactly as service minus action cost.
Episode identity, root pairing, post-action IID ordering, terminal censoring, action legality, conditional
marking, resource accounting, and reuse-free counter namespaces must all pass. Any failure makes the
screen `INVALID`, not negative evidence.

### Inference and frozen branches

For each confirmation policy and root, average direct return over its 64 episodes. All claim-bearing
contrasts are paired over the 16 root means. Report the mean, two-sided Student-t 95% interval, one-sided
95% lower and upper bounds, exact paired sign-flip p-value, and leave-one-root-out means. Episodes,
agents, callbacks, and actions are nested observations and never increase `n`.

Material margin is `M=0.005` direct normalized-return units. Gate 1 `HEADROOM_PASS` requires valid
support/conformance and one-sided 95% lower bounds above `0.005` for all three selected-stratified
contrasts: minus best global `lambda`, minus KEEP, and minus matched shell. This is an
intersection-union gate; all component intervals are also reported. If any required one-sided upper
bound is below `0.005`, branch `BOUNDED_NO_HEADROOM`; otherwise a nonpass is `HEADROOM_UNRESOLVED`.
Neither nonpass unlocks Gate 2 or any later learner.

Gate 2 is evaluated only after `HEADROOM_PASS`. It requires all of:

- selected stratified minus best global `p` one-sided 95% lower bound above `0.005`;
- `p_high - p_low >= 0.10` and `p_low <= p_high`;
- at least 12 of 16 leave-one-root-out discovery selections are monotone with separation at least `0.10`;
- the discovery near-optimal set, defined as within `0.005` mean direct return of the best stratified
  vector, contains neither a homogeneous vector nor a vector with separation below `0.10`;
- neither selected bin probability is an outermost member of the five-point grid.

If all pass, branch `HEADROOM_AND_EXPOSURE_HETEROGENEITY`; otherwise branch
`HEADROOM_WITHOUT_IDENTIFIED_EXPOSURE_HETEROGENEITY`. Nonmonotone, edge-dependent, flat,
near-homogeneous, or unstable heterogeneity does not justify the learned `lambda`-versus-`p` stage.

For every contrast, report root-level service and action-cost differences alongside direct return. A
direct-return gain wholly due to lower action cost is cost avoidance, not useful rebinding. A service
gain consumed by action cost is not headroom. Mark-specific value remains unidentified.

### Predictions and interpretation

- Global `lambda`, KEEP, or shell spanning the selected candidate predicts no direct headroom and stops
  the executable branch.
- Headroom with a flat or weakly separated grid predicts a global operating point or shell effect and
  stops before learned links.
- Stable, monotone high-minus-low probability separation plus direct value over global `p` supports only
  a finite-host exposure-conditioned policy comparison and permits, but does not answer, the later
  learned-link question.
- Learned-checkpoint weakness cannot create passage because the primary exposure-linked control is a
  finite-grid ceiling.
- Sparse bins, one-root domination, shell mismatch, grid-edge selection, or nonmonotone optima produce
  invalid or no-go branches, never threshold repair.
