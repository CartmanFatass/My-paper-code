## Frozen object

A regenerative plan produces eight physical service ticks after a real
renewal. Renewal resets rather than stacks service lifetime and costs two
service-tick units. Direct return is long-run service minus renewal cost per
physical tick. Opportunities are every one tick in `DENSE` and every four
ticks in `SPARSE`; strata receive equal weight in physical time. Policies are
state-blind memoryless Bernoulli rules on `Q={0/16,...,16/16}`. KEEP never
renews. A fixed-probability payload-null shell pays identical action cost but
does not reset service age.

The global per-opportunity family uses `(q1,q4)=(p,p)`. The global exponential
exposure family selects a base `r in Q` and uses
`(q1,q4)=(r,1-(1-r)^4)`, with the transform evaluated exactly. A diagnostic
may select a separate `q in Q` in each stratum. The frozen direct-return margin
is `1/32`; near-optimal tolerance is `1/64`; robust boundary-probability
heterogeneity requires `1/4` separation between near-optimal sets.

## Material audit issues

The owner prompt's phrase that all probabilities use `Q` could be read as
requiring the transformed spacing-four probability to lie in `Q`, even though
the durable scope calls `Q` the base grid and defines the exact transform. The
literal reading leaves only exposure bases `{0,1/2,1}` and reverses the
P-versus-E winner because of grid feasibility. Review must decide whether the
scope-authoritative exact transform is coherent and whether the prompt
ambiguity limits the claim.

Physical service age makes cumulative physical exposure an aligned positive
control. Opportunity-aged service would align the per-opportunity diagonal.
The exponential base is not the executed slow-stratum event rate because at
most one action can occur per boundary. Dummy calls match physical-slot
invocations but not the intentionally different count of actionable
opportunities. An age-conditioned deterministic controller outside the frozen
families renews every eight physical ticks and achieves direct return `3/4`.

Independent review should decide whether the physical-rate family is a
required competent simple null or an impermissible post-scope comparator;
whether raw `q` heterogeneity or equal executed physical rate controls the
“one global operating point” stop; whether the direct-return and shell gates
are decision-relevant rather than constructed tautologies; whether any exact
fact needs an executable certificate; and which bounded scientific and
investment branch follows without exceeding the claim ceiling.
