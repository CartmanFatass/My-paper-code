## Treatment and filtration

At a legal routine boundary, ONLGR maps a learned scalar through
`u = 1 - exp(-softplus(g) * e)` and then, conditional on acting, selects
`REFRESH-SAME` or `REBIND`. RAW maps its learned scalar through a per-opportunity
sigmoid. Both primary arms receive the same task and elapsed inputs, capacity,
action surface, lease, critic, PPO work, optimizer, and ordinary physical-time
SMDP-GAE. TIMING-ONLY retains the ONLGR link and timing, tenure, role, cause,
lease, busy, and own-action state while zeroing binding and mismatch content.

In `RAND-IID-4-16-32`, the next routine interval is keyed by seed, episode,
and boundary ordinal and drawn after the current joint action. It is independent
of the visible history, current state, action, reward, and preceding intervals.
Persistence on this panel can reject predictable-next-cadence leakage as a
complete explanation, but cannot by itself isolate task content, the link's
causal contribution, or its training geometry.

## Frozen observations

### B1 primary and history-independent contrasts

The ONLGR-minus-RAW seven-schedule mean is `+0.0065377`, with Bonferroni-adjusted
97.5% interval `[-0.0026772, 0.0157526]`; it does not pass. The within-seed
worst-schedule contrast is `+0.0405534`, interval `[0.0329355, 0.0481712]`; it
passes. Effects are heterogeneous: ONLGR loses on `CONST-4`, wins on
`CONST-16` and `CONST-32`, and has intervals spanning zero on the four switch
or alternation schedules.

On post-action IID, ONLGR-minus-RAW normalized return is `+0.0348194`, with 95%
interval `[0.0245868, 0.0450520]`. The service contrast is `+0.0351990`, while
ONLGR incurs `+0.0003796` more explicit action cost. The positive return is thus
not an action-cost-only inaction effect.

On the same IID panel, ONLGR-minus-TIMING-ONLY return is `-0.0001714`, with 95%
interval `[-0.0052189, 0.0048762]`; the service contrast is likewise near zero.
The interval's upper endpoint lies below the registered `0.02` materiality
margin. All 64 native-plus-IID ONLGR seed/schedule cells pass the post-startup
voluntary marked-activity requirement.

### Mechanism qualifications and simple controls

The analytic exposure-probability/Jacobian identity passes. The operational
marked-partition estimand does not exist: every seed has `0/16` common valid
cells, and all 128 cells fail because the unsplit ONLGR/RAW first-event
probability gap is above `0.05`, ranging about `0.4117` to `0.4725`. Excluded
cells cannot support an MPI/HPI estimate, and the unavailable gate is not a
negative stability estimate.

Validation selected the state-blind control `lambda=0.125`, `rho=0.5`. Its
held-out deterministic aggregate is descriptively stronger than ONLGR on every
schedule. ONLGR-minus-fixed is `-0.0366342` for the seven-schedule mean, with
ordinary 95% interval `[-0.0525154, -0.0207530]`; its worst-schedule contrast
is `-0.0244677`, with an interval spanning zero. This is a sufficiency
diagnostic, not a resource-matched learned superiority test.

The recovery lacks current-state oracle, degenerate-policy, exposure-clamp,
and yoke observations. These absences remove adaptive-headroom, live-exposure,
and action-sequence qualifications; they do not reverse an observed contrast.
The retained recovery also exceeded the original seven-million-tick cap, so no
within-cap claim is available.

B1 matched initial action probability only at zero features and exposure
`e=8`. At initialization ONLGR's event probability rises from about `0.0275` at
`e=1` to `0.5904` at `e=32`, whereas RAW remains `0.20`. The links also induce
different exposure-scaled gradients. The registered finite-budget comparison
therefore identifies the complete link-plus-initialization-plus-optimization
package rather than the transform alone.

### B2 and ROAD separate objects

B2 prospectively compares a timing/tenure-dependent but task-content-blind
RATE-FLEX arm with a zero-input learned global RATE-CONST arm. Both retain the
same exposure transform, fixed mark law, shell, initialization criterion,
critic, PPO work, and IID coordinates. RATE-FLEX learned a small nonconstant
mapping in every seed, but RATE-FLEX-minus-RATE-CONST IID return is only
`+0.0009783`, with 95% interval `[-0.0013578, 0.0033143]`. The frozen branch is
`ABSORB_TO_GLOBAL_RATE`.

ROAD is a different host and treatment. Its constrained patch strongly beats
nominal RAW, but ROAD-minus-NEVER is about `-0.0478` on mean service and
`-0.0463` on tail service, with intervals wholly below zero. ROAD-minus-SHAM is
only about `+0.0011` mean and `+0.0020` tail, below the prospective margins.
This is a local direct-utility nonpass and a comparator warning, not a B1
exposure-link estimate.

## Live explanations and discriminating implications

1. A task-content-aware exposure mechanism would require material value over
   TIMING-ONLY plus usable support/headroom evidence. The retained IID contrast
   does not show that increment, and operational partition support is absent.
2. A task-blind timing or tenure rule is compatible with ONLGR matching
   TIMING-ONLY, but B2 does not demonstrate material value for timing-flexible
   rate learning beyond one global rate.
3. A global exposure-linked operating point, action-shell regularization, or
   favorable initialization/gradient geometry is compatible with every B1 and
   B2 observation, including positive ONLGR-minus-RAW IID service and the
   fixed-rate diagnostic.
4. Deterministic cadence leakage can contribute to native schedule effects,
   but cannot be the sole explanation of the post-action IID ONLGR/RAW gap.
5. Small task-content or timing effects below the registered margin remain
   possible. They are not evidence for a material investment claim.
6. Host-specific reward, lease, mark, action-shell, and short-budget geometry
   are shared dependencies. ROAD independently illustrates the risk that a
   large win over nominal RAW can coexist with little payload value over a
   competent incumbent or matched shell.

## Open objections for independent Convergence

Convergence must decide whether the positive IID service contrast against RAW
supports more than a finite-budget package claim once TIMING-ONLY, B2, and the
fixed-rate diagnostic are considered; whether any unavailable control could
change the investment branch rather than merely cap attribution; whether the
fixed-rate diagnostic is being given too much inferential weight; whether B2
and ROAD are being improperly transported across distinct objects; whether an
evaluation-only clamp or fixed-rate IID panel would genuinely distinguish the
live explanations; and whether the surviving global exposure-link question is
a narrow continuation, a separate prospective question, or no current
investment target.

Any proposed discriminator must be one unchanged-checkpoint,
history-independent observation inside the frozen resource and semantic
boundary. Extra seeds, retuning, changed thresholds, repaired support
denominators, longer training, or a new host do not qualify.

## Claim ceiling

At most, the current record can support a local statement about the complete
ONLGR package relative to the named RAW learner on the constructed two-agent
host, frozen checkpoints, paired coordinates, and finite training/evaluation
budget. It cannot establish RAW representational impossibility, a literal
hazard, causal lease value, causal `REBIND` value, arbitrary or continuous
callback periods, variable population size, UAV performance, transfer, safety
certification, or general PPO superiority.
