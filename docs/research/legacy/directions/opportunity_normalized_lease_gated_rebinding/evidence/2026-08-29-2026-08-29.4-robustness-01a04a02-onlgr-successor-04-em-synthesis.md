## Scientific judgment

The frozen branch is `GENERIC_RATE_SUPPORTED`, not `EXP_LINK_SPECIFIC`.
Changing eight ticks to seven breaks the predecessor's exact shared-rate
coincidence: the unrestricted optimum physical rates differ by `1/64`, and RATE
misses the sparse-stratum grid optimum by one sixteenth. The investment-relevant
conclusion nevertheless survives. RATE directly beats EXP, lies within
`1/32` of the unrestricted memoryless ceiling, and materially beats RAW.

This is a bounded null for distinct exponential link value, not a negative
claim about all exposure links. It supports narrowing or parking the distinct
exponential/opportunity-normalized link question while retaining, at most, a
generic physical-rate regularization hypothesis. Final recommendation remains
review-contingent until independent Convergence is resolved.
