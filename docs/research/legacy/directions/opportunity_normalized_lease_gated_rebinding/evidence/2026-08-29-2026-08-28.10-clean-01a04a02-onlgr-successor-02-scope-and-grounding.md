## Frozen question

The first question is whether a result-blind prospective screen on the existing finite two-agent host
establishes both of the following gates:

1. direct normalized-return headroom over a competent learned global exposure-linked policy, a genuine
   no-voluntary-action policy, and a matched action-shell control; and
2. a material difference in direct-return-optimal voluntary event probability across predeclared,
   equal-width physical-time exposure or tenure strata with adequate prospective support.

Only if both gates hold is the second question admissible: under identical initialization, optimizer
work, action shell, post-action `RAND-IID-4-16-32` coordinates, direct-value controls, and conditional
marking, does a learned global exposure-linked rate
`u(e) = 1 - exp(-lambda e)` differ materially in direct normalized return from a learned global
per-opportunity event probability `u = p`?

The event is voluntary non-`KEEP` activity at a legal routine boundary. Physical exposure is the host's
eligible tick exposure, not callback count. Tenure is physical plan age in ticks. The primary outcome is
paired-seed direct normalized return. Service and action cost are required decomposition outcomes; they
cannot replace direct return. Voluntary event counts, event-free survival, exposure, stratum support,
and action-shell conformance are operational validity facts rather than scientific endpoints.

## Result-blind prospective boundary

Any executable screen must choose its strata, event-probability grid or learned parameterization,
support floor, material margin, seed set, multiplicity rule, and primary contrast before reading a new
outcome. Candidate strata must be equal-width intervals on the physical tick clock and must come from
the already declared host ranges: eligible exposure through 32 ticks and plan age through 64 ticks.
The result-blind support audit may use boundary counts but not rewards, service, cost, learned values, or
arm identity. A predeclared hierarchy may select exposure strata first and tenure strata second solely
when the first choice fails its frozen support floor; it may not switch clocks or cut points after seeing
returns. Synthetic diagnostic-grid probabilities alone do not establish direct-return-optimal strata.

The competent global exposure-linked comparator must learn one state-blind `lambda`; the no-action
comparator must take no voluntary non-`KEEP` action; and the matched-shell comparator must preserve the
same boundary calls, masks, dummy work, action availability, and accounting while severing the proposed
informative link. The candidate headroom arm must be defined without access to prospective results and
must not receive more optimizer work, favorable initialization, or privileged exogenous coordinates.
An oracle is admissible only as a predeclared finite-grid headroom diagnostic and must be reported as an
upper-bound construction, not as a learned deployable policy.

## Retained grounding

The registered B1 package selected fixed-rate diagnostic parameters `lambda=0.125` and `rho=0.5` on
four training schedules and separate validation roots. On post-action IID coordinates, ONLGR minus RAW
had mean direct-return difference `0.03481940746307373` with 95% interval
`[0.02458681710870283, 0.04505199781744463]`, while ONLGR minus TIMING-ONLY had mean
`-0.00017138481140136635` with 95% interval
`[-0.005218943755004485, 0.004876174132201753]`. The first passed its registered materiality gate and
the second did not. B1 did not supply a current-state oracle, exposure clamp, yoke, or prospectively
powered competent no-action and matched-shell headroom screen.

B2 compared a state-dependent exposure-linked RATE-FLEX learner with a learned state-blind
exposure-linked RATE-CONST learner under matched initialization and work. RATE-FLEX minus RATE-CONST
had mean IID direct-return difference `0.0009782886505126948`, 95% interval
`[-0.0013577707092971786, 0.0033143480103225683]`, and sign-flip p-value `0.359375`; its registered
branch absorbed to a global rate. B2 records a forced-`KEEP` replay and extensive conformance facts, but
that replay is a pairing diagnostic rather than the prospective direct-return headroom test frozen
above. B2 also does not estimate stratum-specific direct-return-optimal event probabilities.

The older ROAD/RAW/NEVER/SHAM record remains important but non-interchangeable evidence: ROAD beat RAW,
lost to NEVER, and barely beat SHAM. It warns that a competent no-action or shell-like regularizer may
explain apparent value. It does not by itself answer the current host-specific prospective gates.

The retained record therefore constrains the design but does not already answer the frozen question.
That statement is neutral: a small prospective observation could support either headroom and
heterogeneity or a bounded null, and a clean Innovator review must precede any executable freeze.

## Competing explanations and discriminating predictions

- **No direct headroom.** A competent global exposure-linked policy, no-action policy, or matched shell
  spans the candidate's direct return. Prediction: the prospective candidate-or-oracle margin is below
  the frozen material threshold or its interval includes the null. The executable branch stops.
- **Shell or generic exposure reduction.** Boundary work, downtime, action suppression, or optimizer
  regularization explains the apparent benefit. Prediction: the matched shell or no-action control
  closes the return gap, or service gains are offset by action cost. The executable branch stops.
- **Homogeneous global optimum.** Some voluntary-event headroom exists, but the direct-return-optimal
  event probability does not differ materially across supported physical-time strata. Prediction: a
  shared global probability is adequate within the frozen margin. The link-versus-probability comparison
  is not justified.
- **Physical-exposure dependence.** Headroom and prospective heterogeneity both survive, and the optimal
  event probability varies with eligible physical exposure in a pattern that a single `lambda` can
  capture. Prediction: the matched learned exposure link may beat `u=p` on post-action IID direct return.
- **Opportunity-normalized dependence.** Both preliminary gates survive, but a constant event
  probability per legal opportunity is adequate or superior after matching marginal initialization and
  work. Prediction: `u=p` matches or beats the exposure link.
- **Finite-budget selection or optimization artifact.** A contrast depends on initialization, work,
  seed selection, unsupported strata, or favorable shell details. Prediction: conformance or support
  fails, paired-seed uncertainty is broad, or a result disappears under the matched prospective design.

## Outcome-to-claim and decision branches

If competent controls eliminate direct-return headroom, the scientific outcome is a bounded null on this
host and budget; the executable branch ends without comparing the two learned global links. If headroom
survives but supported stratum optima are not materially heterogeneous, the result narrows the surviving
object to a global operating point or shell effect and the executable branch also ends. If either gate
is under-supported or ambiguous, no positive or negative mechanism claim is allowed and the next action
is a smaller support or design discriminator, not a repaired threshold.

Only if both gates pass may a matched `lambda`-versus-`p` comparison be frozen. A positive second-stage
contrast would support only a finite-host, finite-budget difference between those two global policy
families. A null would support only adequacy within the frozen margin. Recommendation is formed only
after independent Convergence review; none of these branches directly changes Portfolio lifecycle.

finite-budget direct-return contrast among the explicitly matched policies, or a bounded null within its
frozen margin. It cannot establish RAW representational impossibility, a literal hazard, causal lease or

heterogeneity would justify only the matched second-stage comparison, not its conclusion.
