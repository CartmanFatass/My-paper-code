## Strongest alternative explanation

The strongest alternative to the local simplicity action is weak realized
functional dose or finite-budget optimization geometry. RATE-FLEX acquired only
small rate variation around the learned global operating point; another host,
training budget, or optimization path might exploit timing inputs more strongly.
The experiment was designed to test the complete finite-budget package, so that
alternative does not rescue this registered treatment or authorize post-result
retuning. It prevents interpreting absorption as a theorem that timing-dependent
rates can never help.
