## Frozen decision question

Within at most three prospectively specified physical and causal construction
families, can two evidence histories collide not only in scalar effective
sample size and ordinary provenance counts, but also in a competent frozen
second-order relational summary, while their posterior covariance orientations
make different scarce physical-time actions uniquely optimal?

If such a pair exists, does explicit covariance computation retain material
action or utility value after comparison with the strongest invariant
relational statistic derived before any learned result and with a
replication-safe permutation-invariant learner that strictly contains the
frozen summaries under the same raw information and useful work? If no pair
survives the causal, equality, or exact-headroom gates, the bounded negative is
the result; no learned discriminator will be manufactured.

The scientific object is conditional information value for one public
`SENSE`, `RELAY`, or `COMMIT` decision at a declared physical time. It is not
covariance calibration accuracy by itself.

## Posterior and action object

Each family defines a finite-dimensional latent terminal task state `x`, a
prior fixed before the history, a prospectively sampled public evidence law,
and an immutable origin/lineage relation structure fixed before latent draws,
measurements, actions, or outcomes. For a realized public history `H`, let
`m_H` and `P_H` be the valid posterior mean and covariance for `x`. The exact
Bayes action minimizes declared acquisition cost plus posterior terminal loss.

The primary terminal loss is squared estimation error in fixed public task
coordinates. A family may use a fixed positive-semidefinite action-specific
loss matrix only when that matrix and every acquisition direction are declared
before histories are sampled and are identical across the paired arms. Under
quadratic Bayes risk, the value of an additional linear observation depends on
`P_H`, so realized measurement values need not be conditioned to equality.
This avoids a measure-zero zero-value anchor and makes any surviving pair a
positive-support consequence of the ex ante history law.

`SENSE` and `RELAY` must deliver different, prospectively available, not-yet-
assimilated origins with equal declared latency and accounting unless the
family explicitly gives both the same other cost. A literal copy of an
assimilated composite key has zero information and cannot be relabeled as the
relay. `COMMIT` terminates at the current physical time. All three actions and
their costs are common across the pair.

## Frozen coordinate-equality law

A candidate pair is admissible only if exact derivation establishes all of the
following before any sampled or learned outcome is inspected.

### Scalar and spectral coordinates

The pair has the same number of distinct composite origin keys, the same
receipt and duplicate-quotient law, the same prior, action set, costs, and
clock, the same scalar information coordinate `tr(J_H)/dim(x)` under the
family's declared terminal-state information convention, and the same complete
unordered spectrum of `P_H`. Equality therefore includes posterior trace,
determinant, rank, and every rotation-invariant spectral polynomial. A scalar
that encodes task-axis orientation or the action values is treated as a
renaming of the structured answer, not ordinary scalar ESS.

### Unary typed coordinate `U1`

After exact composite-key quotienting, `U1` is the complete empirical measure
of public single-origin types. A type contains the public measurement label or
gain class, signed loading class and exact rational magnitude when present,
quality, capture-time class, channel class, receipt state, lineage degree,
factor/group size, and every other public single-row field used by any route.
Arbitrary numeric lineage or group names are excluded because they carry no
semantics. The pair must have exactly the same `U1`, including all joint unary
histograms, not merely the same marginal counts.

### Frozen relational hierarchy `R2`

`R2` is the complete permutation- and lineage-renaming-invariant empirical
measure over ordered public row pairs. Each pair type includes both `U1` row
types; same-factor, same-lineage, parent/child, same-capture-batch, and declared
time-lag relation classes; direction of a causal ancestry edge; and all degree-
at-most-two monomials of public signed loadings and gains used by the family.
Counts are stratified by the full relation signature rather than pooled after
seeing a candidate.

This hierarchy strictly contains the preceding unsynthesized same-group
cross-label product statistic

`T = sum over same-factor cross-label pairs of loading_i * loading_j`.

The candidate pair must have identical `R2`, so merely rediscovering `T`, a
typed pair count, a lag-one transition count, or another second-order moment
cannot pass. Higher-order group composition, temporal word structure, or
global lineage topology may differ, but only when it is public, causal, and
available equally to all containing routes.

No regime label, oracle covariance, posterior, latent state, future receipt or
measurement, reward, action outcome, or arbitrary group name is part of any
input. A candidate selected because of realized values or future actions is
invalid.

## Three bounded construction families

The search ends after these three materially distinct families. A family that
cannot meet every equality and exact-headroom gate is retired without learned
work.

1. **Static higher-order factor groups.** Public observations of a two- or
   three-coordinate latent state share prospectively sampled physical nuisance
   factors. Group composition or multiple factor loadings may differ beyond
   `R2` while all row and pair types match. The route must check whether a
   Woodbury or Schur-complement reduction proves that `R2` already determines
   the posterior; if so, the family is a negative rather than a candidate.
2. **Noncommuting dynamic measurement channels.** A linear-Gaussian state
   evolves through fixed physical transitions while identical time-labeled
   measurement rows are connected by different prospectively wired sensor
   lineages. Only the lineage relation, not channel/time unary content, may
   differ. The route must establish that any order effect is a classical
   filtering consequence of transition/update composition, not a future-action
   or hidden schedule label, and that the paired posterior spectra are equal.
3. **Cross-time lineage coupling.** Correlated disturbances or calibration
   errors follow a prospectively fixed finite lineage graph across capture
   times. Paired graphs may collide on `U1` and every `R2` motif while differing
   in higher-order cycles or connectivity. The route must test whether exact
   lag-aware precision or a canonical invariant relational statistic already
   reconstructs the Bayes action.

These labels delimit mechanisms, not a quota for positive examples. A single
valid reduction proof may close a family. No fourth family enters this cycle.

## Estimands and material margins

For an admissible balanced pair, the static primary estimand is the mean Bayes-
risk difference between the exact full-history oracle and the best policy
measurable with respect to `(scalar spectrum, U1, R2)`. Because the pair
collides on those coordinates, that restricted policy must use one common
possibly randomized action law, solved exactly rather than represented by a
weak learner.

Exact decision headroom is material only when:

- the two histories have different unique optimal actions;
- in each history the optimal action beats its runner-up by at least `0.05` in
  the family's normalized Bayes loss; and
- the balanced full-history oracle beats the best `(scalar, U1, R2)`-
  measurable policy by at least `0.05` normalized loss.

Failure of any threshold stops that family. Calibration, covariance-estimation
error, or a nonzero but subthreshold action-value change does not pass.

Before any learned observation, EM must derive and freeze the weakest
group-renaming-invariant statistic `R*` found to separate a surviving pair. If
an exact policy using `R*` matches the covariance oracle's action and Bayes risk
to `1e-12`, explicit covariance has no decision increment in that host; the
cycle stops at relational reduction. `R*` may be a higher-order motif, a
canonicalized finite relation graph, or an exact sufficient precision
contraction, but it cannot use forbidden information.

If, and only if, exact evidence leaves learned specificity answer-changing, a
later committed EM-to-CM card must freeze the selected finite population,
train/evaluation split, data and counter-addressed RNG, model widths, update
counts, useful-work audit, and simultaneous paired interval before the first
result-bearing command. The material learned effect is a structured-route
paired normalized-risk advantage of at least `0.03` over the containing learner
with the simultaneous 95% upper bound below `-0.03`. Equivalence is available
only when the whole corresponding interval lies within `[-0.01,+0.01]`.
Anything wider, work-mismatched, undertrained, or shortcut-sensitive is
unresolved and cannot support specialized covariance investment.

## Competent comparators and matched information/work law

1. **Exact scalar/spectral policy.** Receives only the frozen scalar and
   spectral coordinates, clock, costs, and action support; its best balanced
   action law is solved exactly.
2. **Exact unary-plus-`R2` policy.** Receives `(U1,R2)` as well and is also
   solved exactly. It is the prospective low-order relational null.
3. **Strongest invariant `R*` policy.** Uses the weakest separating invariant
   derived before learned results. It is not handicapped by omitting a known
   causal public relation.
4. **Exact covariance-aware Bayes oracle.** Uses the same public history to
   construct the valid posterior and exact action values. It is an analytic
   ceiling, not evidence that a learned covariance estimator succeeds.
5. **Learned structured route, conditional.** Learns or computes structured
   covariance from the common raw public rows and relations and uses one common
   action head.
6. **Replication-safe containing learner, conditional.** Receives the exact
   quotient row set and public relation-tuple set. It has an explicit frozen
   `(U1,R2)` summary channel plus an output-connected permutation-invariant
   within-lineage/within-relation residual and global symmetric pooling, so it
   strictly contains the declared summaries. It is invariant to row order,
   literal-copy replication after quotienting, and arbitrary lineage-name
   permutation, and can express every legal action.

Conditional learned routes receive identical histories, raw fields, targets,
action head, one invocation per decision, batches, updates, training seeds,
selection opportunity, and held-out evaluation. Output-connected trainable
parameters, scalar operations, and peak live state must each be within ten
percent per active invocation mode. Dummy arithmetic, ignored outputs, hidden
second evaluations, regime labels, post-result architecture edits, and
deliberate comparator undertraining invalidate the comparison. If honest
architectures cannot meet this law, the learned specificity question is
unresolved rather than favorable to CCIC.

## Strongest alternatives and result-blind branches

- **No physical pair.** Prospectivity, positive support, or causal lineage
  fails. Record a bounded null and stop.
- **No coordinate collision.** `U1`, `R2`, spectrum, or scalar ESS separates
  every candidate in the three families. The old scalar counterexample is not
  enough; stop with no specialized claim.
- **No material exact action headroom.** Covariance orientation changes a
  number but not a scarce decision or material utility. Stop before learning.
- **Low-order relational sufficiency.** `(U1,R2)` reconstructs the posterior
  action. Narrow the scientific object to competent relational provenance; do
  not claim covariance specificity.
- **Higher-order invariant sufficiency.** A pre-result `R*` matches the oracle.
  The finite host establishes a need for richer relation structure, not an
  explicit covariance-calibrated clock.
- **Containing learner sufficiency.** Under a valid conditional learned test,
  the containing learner is equivalent or superior. Specialized covariance is
  unsupported; retain generic relational set learning at most.
- **Structured learned increment.** Continue bounded CCIC investment only if
  every causal/equality/exact gate passes and the structured route clears the
  frozen learned margin against every competent comparator under valid matched
  work.
- **Execution or identification failure.** No scientific branch is inferred
  from transport, code, training, work-audit, observer, or interval failure.
