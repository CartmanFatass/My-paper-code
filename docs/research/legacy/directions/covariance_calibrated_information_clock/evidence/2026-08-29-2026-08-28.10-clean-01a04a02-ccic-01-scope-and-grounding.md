## Frozen question

Can two prospectively generated cooperative evidence histories be physically
and causally valid while matching in all of the following and nevertheless
requiring opposite optimal scarce actions?

1. four distinct immutable evidence origins, the same per-axis, per-loading,
   per-quality, and shared-factor group-size counts, and no duplicates;
2. the same declared scalar effective information, posterior trace,
   determinant, eigenvalues, and ordinary rotation-invariant summaries;
3. the same action support, action cost, receipt law, available raw fields,
   optimizer exposure, and useful-work opportunity; and
4. opposite optimal `SENSE_LOCAL` versus `RELAY_PARTNER` actions because the
   relational shared-nuisance lineage rotates which task direction remains
   uncertain?

If the pair is valid, does an analytic or exact-enumeration covariance-aware
oracle have material expected-loss headroom over the best policy restricted to
the matched scalar/count summaries? If so, does any decision-level distinction
remain after comparison with both a competent relational lineage-count route
and a replication-safe flexible hierarchical set learner that receive the
same raw history and are matched in useful work and learning opportunity?

The question concerns conditional information value and physical action time.
It does not ask whether covariance merely improves calibration, whether a
metadata flag can be decoded, or whether arbitrary extra capacity can fit two
labels.

## Candidate causal object held neutral at scope freeze

The candidate is a two-coordinate Gaussian cooperative estimation microhost.
The latent task state is

\[
\theta=(\theta_1,\theta_2)^T\sim N(0,I_2).
\]

At one public decision epoch the team has four valid, already received,
distinct origins. There are two independently sampled physical nuisance groups
`g in {a,b}`. Each origin measures one task coordinate and has a public signed
loading `l_i in {-1,+1}` on its group's physical nuisance:

\[
z_i=e_{axis(i)}^T\theta+\sqrt{r\rho}\,l_i W_{g(i)}
     +\sqrt{r(1-\rho)}\,\epsilon_i,
\]

where `r=2`, `rho=0.8`, and all `W_g`, `epsilon_i`, and `theta` components are
independent standard normals before this equation. Group identity and loading
are sensor-wiring/geometry facts fixed before `theta`, measurements, actions,
or outcomes. They are not inferred from reward or future data.

The two equally likely wiring histories are fixed prospectively:

```text
POSITIVE-PAIRING:
  (a,axis1,+), (a,axis2,+), (b,axis1,-), (b,axis2,-)

NEGATIVE-PAIRING:
  (a,axis1,+), (a,axis2,-), (b,axis1,-), (b,axis2,+)
```

They have exactly the same four unique origins, two rows per task axis, two
positive and two negative loadings, two groups of size two, marginal noise
variance, quality, receipt, and all unary typed counts. They differ only in
the within-group axis-loading incidence. Renaming groups or permuting rows
cannot change either history's meaning.

For the zero-value analytic anchor, and by continuity on a positive-density
neighborhood around it, the posterior mean is matched while the posterior
covariance has the same two eigenvalues with their task directions swapped.
With the frozen constants, the posterior variances along
`u_plus=(1,1)/sqrt(2)` and `u_minus=(1,-1)/sqrt(2)` are exactly `9/14` and
`1/6`, in opposite order between the two histories. The data-information trace
is `50/9`, a scalar per-coordinate ESS convention is `25/9`, posterior trace
is `17/21`, and the determinant and complete unordered spectrum are identical.
No claim is made that every possible task-aware scalar must collide; a scalar
that already encodes orientation or action values has absorbed the structured
object rather than reduced it to ordinary ESS.

Three common public team actions are available:

- `SENSE_LOCAL` obtains one new unique measurement of `u_plus^T theta` with
  independent noise variance `1/4`;
- `RELAY_PARTNER` delivers one prospectively captured, not-yet-received remote
  unique measurement of `u_minus^T theta` with the same noise variance,
  latency, packet accounting, and cost; and
- `COMMIT` terminates immediately with the posterior-mean estimate.

The two acquisition actions each cost `1/5` and then terminate with the Bayes
posterior-mean estimate. Terminal loss is squared estimation error plus action
cost. A literal retransmission of an assimilated key has zero information and
is not this `RELAY_PARTNER` action. Trustworthy lineage is therefore retained
without conflating delivery of a new remote origin with duplicate replay.

At the analytic anchor, acquiring the high-variance direction reduces Bayes
risk by `81/175`, while acquiring the low-variance direction reduces it by
only `1/15`. Immediate-commit risk is `17/21`; the high-direction action has
risk `287/525`, and the low-direction action has risk `33/35`. Thus the
candidate predicts `SENSE_LOCAL` for `POSITIVE-PAIRING` and `RELAY_PARTNER`
for `NEGATIVE-PAIRING`, with a `46/175` margin over immediate commit. A policy
forced to act identically on the matched scalar/count state has prospective
mean regret at least `104/525` under the balanced pair. These arithmetic facts
are candidate derivations to be independently checked before they become
evidence.

## Estimand and observational unit

The primary static estimand is the balanced-pair expected Bayes-risk difference
between the full structured-information oracle and the best policy measurable
with respect to the frozen scalar/count summary. The observational unit is one
prospectively selected wiring history plus its complete latent/noise tape; the
two wiring histories are balanced before generation. The exposure clock is one
public post-history decision followed by zero or one matched acquisition and a
terminal commit.

If a learned discriminator is scientifically required, its primary estimand
will be held-out balanced-pair excess Bayes risk relative to the exact oracle,
reported separately for structured covariance, unary lineage-count,
relational-incidence count, and hierarchical set routes. Training seeds, not
generated rows, will be inferential replicates. Exact thresholds, data roots,
RNG, models, update counts, and intervals will be frozen in a later EM-to-CM
science card before any result-bearing command.

## Competent comparators and information law

All comparators receive the same immutable row table after exact composite-key
deduplication. No comparator may receive a regime label, oracle covariance,
future measurement, latent state, reward, action outcome, held-out statistic,
or represented feature withheld from another route.

1. **Scalar ESS.** Receives the matched posterior mean anchor, unique-origin
   count, declared scalar effective information, rotation-invariant spectrum
   summaries, clock, costs, and action support, but no relational orientation.
   Its best balanced action law is solved exactly rather than represented by a
   deliberately weak learner.
2. **Unary lineage count.** Deduplicates composite keys and retains all
   competent unary typed counts: axis, loading sign, quality, group size,
   marginal values, and their histograms. It does not retain within-group
   incidence. The frozen pair deliberately collides under this summary.
3. **Relational-incidence count.** Retains permutation- and group-renaming-
   invariant within-group axis/loading pair counts but performs no Gaussian
   covariance inversion. This is the strongest simple alternative: if it
   attains the same action and risk, the surviving object is relational
   provenance structure, not uniquely covariance computation.
4. **Replication-safe hierarchical set learner.** Quotients literal copies,
   encodes rows, pools within shared-nuisance groups, encodes groups, then pools
   across groups before one action head. It must be invariant to row order,
   duplicate replication after quotienting, and nuisance-group renaming. It
   receives the same raw features and must be able to express both actions.
5. **Structured covariance route.** Uses the same public loading incidence to
   construct or learn a valid covariance and maps the resulting posterior to
   the same action head. The exact analytic route is a ceiling, not evidence
   that a learned estimator succeeds.

For any learned comparison, one forward invocation per decision, identical
training histories, seeds, targets, update count, batch exposure, action head,
and hyperparameter-selection opportunity are required. Parameter count, scalar
operation count, and peak live state must be prospectively matched within ten
percent by output-connected useful computation; dummy arithmetic, ignored
outputs, hidden second evaluations, regime labels, or deliberate comparator
deprivation invalidate the comparison. If honest architectures cannot satisfy
this useful-work relation, the learned specificity question is unresolved
rather than favorable to CCIC.

## Competing explanations and distinct predictions

1. **Structured conditional information has decision value.** Both histories
   are valid, scalar/unary summaries collide, the exact action reverses, and
   the structured route retains material held-out risk headroom after competent
   relational-count and hierarchical-set comparisons.
2. **Scalar ESS is sufficient.** Once posterior mean, clock, cost, and an honest
   scalar information statistic are matched, exact optimal actions do not
   differ or the balanced oracle regret is below the material threshold.
3. **Ordinary provenance counting is sufficient.** A competent unary typed
   lineage-count summary already distinguishes the histories without a
   relational object. This would falsify the proposed pair as an equal-count
   discriminator.
4. **Relational counting is sufficient.** Pair incidence changes action, but a
   simple invariant relational count matches the covariance oracle. The
   direction narrows to structured lineage incidence rather than covariance
   calibration.
5. **Flexible set learning is sufficient.** The replication-safe hierarchical
   learner matches the structured route under equal information and useful
   work. Explicit covariance has no demonstrated decision increment in this
   host, though the equal-ESS counterexample remains valid.
6. **Optimization or representation deprivation creates a gap.** A learned
   comparator fails only because it cannot express group incidence, receives
   less useful work, is undertrained, or is selected after results. No
   covariance-specific conclusion is available.
7. **Metadata-label shortcut.** A public regime bit or arbitrary group name
   predicts the action without tracing the causal loadings. Group-renaming and
   unseen-incidence tests should destroy that shortcut; failure invalidates the
   learned comparison.
8. **Calibration-only benefit.** Covariance estimates improve while action,
   Bayes risk, or physical commit time does not. This does not pass the
   decision-level gate.

## Result-blind branch map and stop rule

- **Causal or arithmetic invalidity:** stop before CM and return a bounded null
  or corrected definition; do not infer lifecycle from failed execution.
- **Valid pair but static headroom below `0.10` balanced expected loss, or
  either action margin below `0.05`:** stop at scalar/count reduction for this
  host. This does not prove global scalar sufficiency.
- **Valid pair and material exact headroom, but unary count distinguishes it:**
  reject this construction as an equal-count discriminator and redesign only
  if a genuinely new causal relation exists within this cycle's bounds.
- **Valid pair and material exact headroom, relational count matches:** retain
  the equal-ESS counterexample but narrow the surviving mechanism to relational
  lineage incidence; explicit covariance specificity is unsupported.
- **Structured and relational routes beat scalar/unary summaries, while the
  competent hierarchical set learner is equivalent within a prospectively
  frozen `0.01` risk margin:** retain flexible relational set learning and do
  not claim explicit covariance value.
- **Structured route beats every competent comparator by at least `0.05`
  held-out balanced risk with all validity and useful-work gates passed:**
  support continued bounded CCIC investment in this host family.
- **Wide, unstable, shortcut-driven, undertrained, or work-mismatched learned
  result:** keep the specificity question unresolved; neither non-rejection nor
  an incompetent baseline licenses continuation.

One exact executable discriminator is the maximum. It is scientifically
required only if static analysis and the fresh Innovator establish causal
validity and material exact-oracle headroom but cannot decide relational-count
or flexible-set sufficiency. A result-bearing command is prohibited until EM
freezes and commits a meaning-complete EM-to-CM assignment. The cycle stops
after that command's terminal witness, a preactivity validity failure, the
frozen resource bound, or any branch above. No adaptive rerun, seed
replacement, threshold repair, or post-result architecture change is allowed.

## Scope invalidation and non-goals

The scope is invalidated by choosing pair wiring after measurements or
outcomes; leaking a regime label or oracle covariance; using a retransmission
as new evidence; changing receipt, latency, cost, action support, or raw
information across arms; hiding task-axis or factor-lineage fields from a
comparator; treating arbitrary group IDs as semantics; matching work with
dummy computation; or changing thresholds, data, RNG, model, seeds, optimizer,
or evaluator after a result.

This cycle cannot establish arbitrary covariance value, universal superiority
to scalar summaries, general MARL coordination, dynamic-roster benefit,
variable skill-lifetime benefit, real communication reliability, learned
semantic duplicate detection, UAV transfer, safety, deployment, or end-to-end
HMASD value. It does not compare against or import RCLE. It does not reopen B1
or authorize the historical B1 experiment.

## Claim ceiling

At most, this cycle may establish that one prospectively frozen, finite,
two-coordinate Gaussian cooperative timing host contains physically valid
equal-scalar-ESS and equal-unary-count histories with opposite scarce
sense-versus-relay actions, and that a specified structured covariance learner
does or does not retain material held-out Bayes-risk value over competent
scalar, lineage-count, relational-count, and replication-safe hierarchical-set
comparators under matched raw information and useful work. Any positive is
limited to the tested nuisance-incidence family, costs, action clock, models,
data, and resource bounds. A static oracle separation alone cannot establish a
learned covariance advantage.
