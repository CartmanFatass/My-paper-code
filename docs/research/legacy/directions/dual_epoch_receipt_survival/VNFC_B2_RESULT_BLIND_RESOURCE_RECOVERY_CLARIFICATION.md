# VNFC-B2 result-blind resource and recovery clarification

Owner: `direction:dual_epoch_project_bridge` Explorer Manager  
Treatment: `VNFC-B2-TYPED-CAPSULE-RETENTION-v1`  
Status: prospective owner ruling made without inspecting any partial B2 metric  
Authority boundary: scientific treatment identity, observable authority, claim
boundary, and no-rescue semantics only; recovery implementation and technical
acceptance remain CM work

## Ruling first

The registered three-hour value is a **technical engineering resource envelope**,
not an algorithm-success criterion, statistical stop, scientific estimand, or
negative-result gate. Exceeding it is a resource failure and material cost fact for
the original realization. It does not by itself support or contradict typed
retention.

One guarded same-treatment recovery is authorized under the already-existing
project formal-compute rule that caps the cumulative wall clock of one formal
train/evaluate/analyze iteration, including resumes, at eight hours. This does not
erase or redefine the original three-hour envelope and does not create a fresh
eight-hour allowance: original execution wall plus all recovery work must remain
within one cumulative eight-hour maximum.

Atomic final update-32 checkpoints from the original process instantiate the same
training treatment and may be reused when CM establishes their registered
checkpoint contract. Every reusable model, plus every deterministically
reconstructed missing model, must be evaluated anew under the clarified observable.
Historical old-process evaluation rows or summaries are preserved as historical
facts but cannot be mixed into or treated as authoritative for the clarified
eight-seed conclusion.

## 1. Meaning of the three-hour envelope

The frozen science card registers the algorithm, worlds, arms, seeds, 32-update
training exposure, evaluation population, estimands, thresholds, and claim ceiling.
It does not make completion within three hours a support condition. The accepted
source contract labels the three-hour field `engineering_resource_envelope`, checks
it as a runtime resource condition, and separately reports time, memory, and
latency as diagnostics. The project evidence-complexity policy already states that
a formal iteration may include resumes while its cumulative wall remains capped at
eight hours.

Therefore:

- a natural three-hour resource termination is not a scientific observation about
  treatment efficacy;
- it does not select a checkpoint from learning performance and cannot be used as a
  favorable or unfavorable outcome branch;
- it remains a reportable material engineering anomaly and practical algorithm
  cost even if recovery later yields complete data; and
- a complete clarified eight-seed result obtained within the cumulative formal
  eight-hour bound retains the original treatment identity and scientific claim
  ceiling.

The permitted recovery is one bounded continuation of the same formal iteration,
not an extension or silent replacement of the original three-hour fact. CM must
retain the original overrun in resource accounting, sum original and recovery wall
for the formal cap, and project the recovery to fit the remaining allowance before
starting it. Idle calendar time between processes need not be called algorithm
compute, but all train, reconstruction, validation, evaluation, analysis, and
recovery execution wall belonging to this formal iteration counts. No user-granted
exception to the eight-hour cap exists in this ruling.

## 2. Checkpoint identity and clarified re-evaluation

The pre-result SCR/support clarification changed the definition and aggregation of
an evaluation observable. It did not change the training worlds, observations fed
to the policy, actions, reward, arm semantics, architecture, optimizer, exposures,
random namespaces, seeds, or final-checkpoint selection. The revision chronology
also records the clarification as future evaluation/analyzer instrumentation while
the already imported training process remained unchanged.

An original-process checkpoint is reusable only if CM can establish, from the
ordinary checkpoint/source contract rather than metric values, that it is an atomic
`VNFC-B2-PPO-CHECKPOINT-v1` final checkpoint for the named arm and base seed, update
32, exactly 4,096 training episodes, the registered optimizer-step completion and
parameter shape/count, and that it loads into the frozen arm architecture. Hash,
byte, line-ending, or Git identity is neither required nor sufficient. A temporary,
partial, intermediate, mismatched, ambiguous, or non-loadable checkpoint is not
reusable.

Recovery must use this rule for every arm/seed:

1. reuse each contract-valid atomic final checkpoint without further optimization;
2. treat each absent or nonreusable arm/seed checkpoint as missing and reconstruct
   it from its registered initialization using the exact frozen training treatment;
3. never continue an incomplete optimizer trajectory or select an intermediate
   checkpoint;
4. after the complete 8-by-3 model panel exists, re-evaluate **every** model on all
   four registered evaluation panels, event cells, worlds, and row-order replicas
   with the clarified SCR eligibility/counting and same-metric support rule; and
5. build the retained analysis only from that one coherent clarified evaluation.

Deterministic reconstruction of a missing final model is a repeat of the same
arm/seed treatment, not a new treatment, because its seed, initialization,
exposure, optimizer, architecture, and data-generating process are frozen. Recovery
order must be fixed from the registered seed/arm ordering before looking at any
metric and may not favor models or cells suggested by historical partial output.

Historical evaluation rows and summaries produced by the old imported module may
be kept for audit, but they cannot supply unchanged cells, fill gaps, reduce the new
evaluation workload, or enter a clarified contrast. Re-evaluating only SCR while
reusing old `J`/`RR3`, or mixing old and clarified arms/seeds, would create a
version-confounded analysis and is outside this ruling.

## 3. Consequence when a checkpoint cannot be reused

Checkpoint nonreuse is not evidence against the arm or treatment. The affected
arm/seed simply lacks a valid final training object.

- If CM can deterministically reconstruct it under the exact frozen treatment and
  finish the complete clarified evaluation within the remaining cumulative
  eight-hour allowance, treatment identity and claim ceiling are unchanged.
- If any required arm/seed cannot be reconstructed, loaded, or fully re-evaluated
  within that allowance, the registered eight-seed result is incomplete. Complete
  seed blocks may remain descriptive observations, but the registered paired
  intervals, five-condition treatment pattern, typed-over-raw attribution, and
  variable-`N` project-facing claim are unavailable.
- Such incompleteness is a resource/technical non-completion, not a negative result,
  equivalence finding, contradiction of lifecycle masks, or reason to reinterpret
  missing arms as poor performers.
- Reaching the one-seed activity boundary establishes only that question-relevant
  activity occurred. It does not lower the eight-seed analysis population or
  authorize a conclusion from fewer seeds.

If exact reconstruction would require a different training world, reward,
observation, architecture, optimizer, exposure, seed, random namespace, or
checkpoint choice, that object is a new treatment and receives no B2 claim under
this recovery.

## 4. Missing training curves

Training curves are diagnostic and not claim-bearing for B2. The science card
selects the final update-32 checkpoint only; validation is reporting-only and may
not select a checkpoint, threshold, seed, width, or hyperparameter. Therefore a
reusable final checkpoint does not become scientifically invalid merely because
its per-update learning curve is unavailable.

CM must report curve unavailability and may not fabricate, interpolate, or recreate
a historical curve from the final model. No claim about learning trajectory,
stability, intermediate convergence, or validation behavior may be made for that
arm/seed. The final checkpoint's ordinary metadata and contract must still
establish the registered update, episode, optimizer-step, and model completion; if
those completion facts are themselves unavailable or ambiguous, the checkpoint is
nonreusable under section 2. Missing curves do not excuse missing final-exposure
evidence.

## 5. Exact no-rescue boundary

This ruling authorizes exactly one guarded recovery pass after the original process
terminates. It authorizes only contract-valid final-checkpoint reuse, exact
deterministic reconstruction of missing final arm/seed models, and complete fresh
clarified evaluation/analysis within the remaining cumulative eight-hour bound.

It does **not** authorize:

- a second recovery pass or a fresh full restart without another same-direction
  ruling;
- more than eight cumulative formal hours, resetting the cumulative clock, hiding
  the original three-hour overrun, or obtaining a new allowance by renaming the
  process;
- fewer or substituted seeds, early stopping, additional updates, changed episode
  counts, a different checkpoint, resuming an intermediate optimizer state, or
  tuning from partial outputs;
- changes to any arm, architecture, optimizer, observation, action, reward,
  lifecycle mask, world, schedule, roster size, random namespace, evaluation count,
  row-order tape, estimand, threshold, confidence rule, SCR definition, oracle, or
  claim ceiling;
- using partial metric values to decide which checkpoint to reuse, which missing
  model to reconstruct, which panel to run, or when to stop;
- combining historical old-definition evaluation with clarified evaluation,
  imputing missing arm/seed/cell output, or treating an undefined SCR as zero;
- presenting recovered wall time as if the original realization met three hours;
  or
- interpreting resource exhaustion, missing data, or a nonreusable checkpoint as
  scientific treatment failure.

If the guarded recovery cannot be projected within the remaining cumulative time,
or reaches the eight-hour maximum without a complete coherent 8-by-3 clarified
panel, CM stops and returns the concrete resource/non-completion facts. The B2
registered conclusion is then unavailable. Only the user may authorize a named
exception to the project formal-compute maximum; neither Root, CM, EM, Operator,
nor External Pro may rescue it by altering the bound or scientific object.
