The mathematical closure makes claim-bearing `C2` SCR, support condition 4,
and the full five-condition conjunction unavailable for every possible outcome
of the current B2 run. The original interpretation map made that full positive
conjunction a necessary gate for the frozen 2-D kinematic valid-but-stale
successor. Therefore no current B2 metric value or pattern can activate that
successor. Relaxing the gate, deleting condition 4, or selecting an
implementation-specific `C2` old-command definition after activity began would
be a post-hoc change and is prohibited.

This amendment changes no treatment, arm, seed, threshold, observable,
analysis rule, recovery contract, or active run. It records only the necessary
prospective routing consequence of the result-blind mathematical closure.

## Exact same-conversation Pro convergence trigger

Same-conversation ChatGPT External Pro result convergence remains warranted
if and only if CM returns one complete, scientifically usable endpoint object
with all of the following facts established without interpretation by Pro:

1. all eight seeds and all three final arm models were evaluated completely;
2. all models were freshly evaluated under one coherent clarified evaluation
   version, with no historical/clarified row mixing;
3. the relevant joint-held-out `J` and `RR3` package summaries and paired-seed
   contrasts are complete and are not invalidated by a wrong lifecycle mask,
   contaminated world, missing cell, or other contrast-breaking defect; and
4. the result packet carries the closure's finite-package claim boundary and
   labels any implementation-defined `C2` SCR as non-claim-bearing.

The trigger is deliberately independent of outcome sign. If the endpoint
object is complete and coherent, each of the following exhaustive outcome
families warrants reuse of the same dedicated B2 Pro conversation:

- **Favorable typed-package separation:** one registered metric (`J` or
  `RR3`) clears the frozen same-metric typed-versus-reset components in both
  `C1` and `C2`, with any replacement-referenced, `C3`-equivalence,
  typed-versus-raw, and oracle-headroom component outcomes reported separately.
- **Comparator-attribution split:** typed separates from reset but not raw,
  separates from raw but not reset, or raw `C0` access adequacy fails. This is
  informative about the strongest alternative even though it cannot identify
  factorization alone.
- **Cell, metric, or transfer split:** only one retention cell qualifies,
  `J` and `RR3` qualify in different cells, marginal panels and joint holdout
  disagree, or replacement/equivalence components disagree with direct arm
  ordering.
- **Null, equivalence, or registered-resolution saturation:** arms are
  practically indistinguishable, reset matches typed, or the registered oracle
  headroom gate is not met. Pro convergence may distinguish lack of measured
  package value from lack of resolving room, but may not infer true saturation
  from gate failure.
- **Adverse typed-package result:** typed is worse than reset and/or raw on an
  unambiguous registered endpoint. Pro convergence may assess the strongest
  package-level counterexplanation and next discriminator, but may not infer
  that fail-closed lifecycle masking is generically harmful.

Thus Pro reuse is not a favorable-result reward and is not conditioned on the
unavailable SCR/full-conjunction branch. It is a once-only result-convergence
step for any valid complete endpoint, including positive, mixed, null, or
negative findings. Incomplete seeds, mixed evaluation versions, a broken
contrast, or an SCR-only artifact do not trigger result convergence because
they do not provide the required B2 endpoint object.

The Pro request must ask only for convergence on the bounded package
interpretation, strongest alternative, and next discriminator. It must not ask
Pro to certify code/runtime facts, restore the full support conjunction, choose
a `C2` old-command definition, or authorize the frozen second surface.

## Maximum contribution of the current B2 run

Even under the most favorable possible complete `J/RR3` outcome, B2 can
contribute only bounded evidence of the following form:

> On the registered realized finite joint-held-out `N=5,S*` panel, the named
> independently trained typed package exhibited the reported `J` and/or `RR3`
> endpoint relationship to the named reset and raw packages under the frozen
> finite budget and implemented observation convention.

Unambiguous registered components may be described individually as met or not
met. A favorable collection of components may be called a consistent bounded
package-performance pattern, but not full selective-retention support, a
carrier-only causal effect, or activation evidence for the existing kinematic
successor. A null or adverse collection is bounded evidence about these named
packages and host, not a universal rejection of lifecycle masks.

## Successor and portfolio consequence

The frozen 2-D kinematic surface remains scientifically conceivable only as a
future, separately prospectivized discriminator with a meaning-complete safety
observable and a new pre-activity decision rule. It is not an activated B2
continuation, and no current B2 result can be cited as satisfying its old gate.

Under the current frozen research graph, B2 is therefore an endpoint-evidence
contributor rather than the source of the next algorithm discriminator. ONLGR
and B3 remain the already independent next algorithm-discriminator routes.
This statement transfers no B2 evidence to either direction and expresses no
acceptance of their mechanisms; Root retains portfolio scheduling authority.
B2 result convergence may inform a later prospectively new question, but it
cannot displace those routes or revive the old second-surface activation.
