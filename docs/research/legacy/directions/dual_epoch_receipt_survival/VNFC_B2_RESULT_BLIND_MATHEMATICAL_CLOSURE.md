`treatment=VNFC-B2-TYPED-CAPSULE-RETENTION-v1`

The frozen object is mathematically closed for complete, version-coherent,
finite-panel endpoint comparisons of the three independently trained algorithm
packages on their realized evaluation worlds. It is **not** closed for the full
five-condition treatment-support conjunction. In particular, the written
object does not uniquely define the `C2` SCR "old command" after a role change.
Consequently `C2` SCR, support condition 4, and the full five-condition support
statement are unavailable. No observed or future B2 result may be used to
choose between plausible old-command definitions.

This ruling does not amend the active treatment, arms, seeds, thresholds,
observables, analysis rules, or recovery contract. It changes no run and
authorizes no rerun. It prospectively narrows what can be inferred from any
complete output. Independently defined `J`, `RR3`, oracle contrasts, and other
unambiguous endpoint summaries remain interpretable at the limits below.

## Maximum available claim language

Conditional on all eight seeds and all three final models being evaluated
completely and coherently under one clarified evaluation version, the maximum
claim is:

> On the registered realized finite evaluation panel at the joint held-out
> roster size `N=5` and churn schedule `S*`, under the frozen training budget
> and implemented observation convention, the independently trained named
> algorithm packages exhibited the reported `J` and/or `RR3` paired-seed
> differences. A registered arithmetic component may be described as met or
> not met from its observed paired mean and nominal interval. The comparison is
> an endpoint comparison of the full trained packages, not an isolated causal
> effect of one capsule type and not a population, safety, UAV, arbitrary-`N`,
> or adaptive-`k` guarantee.

Where applicable, it is also permissible to say that an unambiguous registered
operational component was met or not met. It is not permissible to call the
compound conclusion globally 95%-calibrated, to say that all five treatment
conditions passed, or to treat a failed component as proof of the opposite
scientific hypothesis.

## Principles-to-Critic-to-owner reconciliation

Each disposition below is prospective and affects interpretation only.

1. **Formal stochastic closure — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Several supports are
   declared, but the complete joint law for energy costs, training roster/role
   allocation, focal selection, permutation, and other couplings is not.
   Consequence: realized panel averages remain calculable; population-DGP and
   source-only replication claims are unavailable.

2. **Missing laws eliminate all estimands — REJECT.** Once a coherent panel and
   actions are realized, its registered means are deterministic summaries.
   Consequence: complete `J`/`RR3` finite-panel summaries remain available even
   though population generalization does not.

3. **State transition and tick order — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Cue/service
   ticks, leave/return ticks, return-tick inclusion in `RR3`, and mask-before-
   actor intent are substantially constrained, but the entire within-tick
   kernel is not written. Consequence: recorded endpoint rewards are available;
   timing-specific mechanism claims are limited to the realized implementation.

4. **Typed actor information set — ACCEPT.** The object does not establish
   whether focal capsule fields can enter other actors' DeepSets summaries.
   Consequence: package performance remains measurable; entity-private and
   decentralized-memory claims are unavailable.

5. **Reset information set — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Its clearing behavior is
   clear but inherits the unresolved actor-visibility boundary. Consequence:
   typed-versus-reset is only a package comparison under the implemented
   observation convention.

6. **Raw information and external memory — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Raw has
   primitive relations plus persistent entity-keyed recurrent state; parameter
   matching does not equalize persistent storage, deterministic computation,
   optimization burden, or effective capacity. Consequence: typed-versus-raw
   compares the named finite-budget packages, not representational necessity.

7. **Actor-private versus centralized information — ACCEPT.** Critic inputs,
   privilege, reset behavior, and recurrent sequencing are underspecified.
   Consequence: CTDE and credit-mechanism claims are unavailable, while final
   policy returns remain measurable.

8. **Independent arm training — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Each arm changes the
   trained policy as well as the carrier; pairing reduces variance but does not
   hold weights fixed. Consequence: the causal object is the full train-and-
   evaluate algorithm package, not a per-decision capsule restoration effect.

9. **Recurrent PPO specification — ACCEPT.** Sequence length, burn-in,
   truncated-BPTT boundaries, hidden-state handling across PPO epochs,
   recurrent minibatching, advantage normalization, and related details are
   incomplete. Consequence: exact learning dynamics and source-only algorithm
   replication are unavailable; coherent endpoint descriptions survive.

10. **GAE and clock semantics — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** The documents do not
    uniquely fix absent-agent bootstrapping, gap discounting, critic resets, or
    all terminal handling. Consequence: learning/credit interpretations are
    unavailable; evaluation `J` and oracle-defined `RR3` are not thereby
    undefined.

11. **`D_E` and `D_ER` algebra — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Their orientation is
    correct, but they are arm-by-cell interactions across separately trained
    policies and nonexchangeable cells. Consequence: report the numeric
    replacement-referenced interactions, never isolated entity/role memory
    effects without an unregistered exclusion assumption.

12. **`C0`–`C3` control geometry — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** The lifecycle masks
    are coherent, but cells also differ in owner continuity, fresh facts, role
    assignment, and realized worlds. `C3` is a replacement reference, not a
    unit-level counterfactual; `C0` is no-churn access, not post-absence access.
    Consequence: cell results survive; matched-isolation wording does not.

13. **Typed/raw attribution — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Raw `C0` equivalence can
    remove an obvious ordinary-access failure but not unequal optimization,
    state geometry, or rebind learning. Consequence: only advantage over the
    named recurrent comparator is available.

14. **`C3` equivalence — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** The paired 90% interval wholly
    inside `[-0.03,+0.03]` is a coherent operational equivalence rule under its
    model. Consequence: it concerns the two analyzed metrics on this panel;
    failure is non-demonstration, not proof of generic confounding or harm.

15. **SCR definition and conditioning — ACCEPT; CLAIM-BEARING `C2` SCR
    UNAVAILABLE.** `K/E` pooling and the zero-denominator rule are coherent, but
    `C2` old-command semantics are not unique. SCR also conditions on a policy-
    affected choice to serve, so arm rates compare selected decision sets and
    can change through abstention or probing. Consequence: condition 4 and the
    full conjunction are unavailable; any other SCR is a conditional behavior
    diagnostic, not an unconditional safety effect. `J`, `RR3`, and oracle
    contrasts remain independent.

16. **Oracle and headroom — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** The registered gaps are
    calculable, but `OracleGap_RR3` reduces to averaged regret because oracle
    regret is zero; any one arm can satisfy the OR gate, including an arm
    irrelevant to typed/reset headroom. Failure does not prove saturation.
    Consequence: report only whether the registered operational gate was met,
    not actual saturation or typed/reset-specific room.

17. **Four panels — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** They separate seen, size-only,
    schedule-only, and joint held-out conditions. Consequence: the joint panel
    supports finite transfer only to exactly `N=5,S*`; marginal panels diagnose
    localization and do not establish a formal interaction or general range.

18. **Paired-seed inference — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Seed-first pairing avoids
    pseudoreplication, but eight fixed seeds are not a declared IID population
    and normality is not established. Consequence: paired means, standard
    deviations, and nominal model-based `t` intervals are reportable; population
    coverage is not.

19. **Thresholds, equivalence, noninferiority, and multiplicity — ACCEPT WITH
    NARROWING.** A point mean above a materiality threshold plus an interval
    lower bound above zero establishes the registered operational rule, not
    that the population effect exceeds the margin. The metric/arm OR branches
    lack familywise adjustment. Consequence: say "registered component/rule was
    met," never "globally significant at 5%" or "95%-confidence compound
    support."

20. **Lifecycle, variable-`N`, safety, UAV, and adaptive-`k` ceiling — ACCEPT.**
    One shared policy at held-out `N=5` is a legitimate one-point variable-`N`
    test. Zero hard errors is finite sampled compliance, and the UAV mapping is
    conceptual. Consequence: claims stop at the constructed host and tested
    point; broad safety, UAV, arbitrary-`N`, and adaptive-`k` claims are
    unavailable.

21. **Recovery/version coherence — ACCEPT WITH NARROWING.** Contract-valid final
    checkpoints may preserve the same training treatment, and a complete fresh
    reevaluation of all models under one clarified observable version can
    support endpoint inference. Missing curves only remove trajectory claims.
    Consequence: this is conditional on CM establishing checkpoint identity,
    deterministic missing-arm reconstruction, complete `8 x 3` evaluation, and
    no old/new mixing. Incomplete seeds or mixed versions make paired and
    compound inference unavailable, not negative.

22. **Evaluation action law — ACCEPT AS AN ADDITIONAL GAP.** Categorical versus
    greedy evaluation and action-randomness coupling across row replicas are
    not source-identified. Consequence: realized return is descriptive; an
    expected-policy-return interpretation is unavailable.

23. **World-construction weights and feasibility — ACCEPT AS AN ADDITIONAL
    GAP.** Energy-cost, focal-entity, initial-role-multiplicity, and active-role
    laws are incomplete. Consequence: realized-world weighting is usable, but
    no unique population weighting follows.

24. **Replica dependence and hard-error scope — ACCEPT AS AN ADDITIONAL GAP.**
    Four row orders are correlated views of a base world; pooled SCR is a
    replica-weighted conditional rate, not four independent safety trials.
    Zero observed hard errors is not an invariant proof. Consequence: seed-level
    analysis remains appropriate, while safety/generalization language is
    unavailable.

25. **Resource-contract provenance — ACCEPT AS AN ADDITIONAL BOUNDARY.** The
    recovery document asserts the three-hour and eight-hour contract facts, but
    the allowed science sources do not independently establish their technical
    provenance. Consequence: the recovery ruling is internally coherent for
    science interpretation; CM remains the owner of whether its technical
    predicates actually hold.

## Compound-rule consequence

Conditions 1–3 and 5 remain arithmetically defined and can be reported
individually, subject to the limits above. Condition 4 cannot be adjudicated as
a prespecified confirmatory condition because its required `C2` SCR is not
unique. Therefore, regardless of any observed values:

- do not output `all five conditions passed` or its semantic equivalent;
- do not infer treatment-level support by silently dropping condition 4;
- do not select an old-command definition after seeing results;
- if an implementation produced `C2` SCR, label it
  `implementation-defined conditional diagnostic, non-claim-bearing`;
- preserve all unambiguous metrics and report their component-level outcomes;
- treat incomplete recovery, incoherent versions, or undefined required
  denominators as unavailable inference rather than failure, equivalence, or
  evidence against the mechanism.
