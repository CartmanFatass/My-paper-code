## Conclusion first

The current `>=778050`-second conflict-free single-window premise **cannot be
replaced now** by shorter lease slices. Shorter atomic resumable slices are
scientifically admissible in principle without creating a new SGSP treatment,
but only if CM proves an exact result-blind multi-command/multi-lease lifecycle
that is observationally identical to the accepted uninterrupted B continuation.
The current evidence accepts the explicit A-to-B lineage package and names a
fresh lease; it does not accept resumable-slice semantics, lease-expiry
atomicity, repeated command/Operator lifecycle, or one B continuation across
multiple fresh administrative leases.

Therefore the existing safe-window gate remains in force until the
same-direction CM returns exact technical acceptance or an exact incompatibility
and Portfolio then makes a separate evidence-bound adaptation decision. User
intent for immediate execution does not itself supply the missing technical
acceptance and does not authorize Root to infer a bypass.

Separately, the external same-thread heartbeat is a Root control-plane launch
prerequisite, not a Portfolio scientific or allocation parameter. For the
current long or unknown-duration SGSP command, Root policy requires the current
Operational-Root thread target, an estimate-driven cadence within 15–60
minutes, ACTIVE coverage, and one real same-thread scheduled canary after the
relevant configuration change before launch. Portfolio has no authority to
waive, replace, or deem that prerequisite satisfied. The current daily RCLE
heartbeat is not SGSP coverage.

A slice design is science-neutral only if every condition below is technically
accepted:

- one unchanged scientific object, master, coordinate/RNG law and B
  continuation lineage govern every slice; per-slice lease or command
  identifiers, if required, are administrative provenance only and cannot
  become new treatments or reset the run;
- slice boundaries are fixed from time/resource/control-plane facts independent
  of actions, returns, quantities, endpoints, model values or inferential rows;
- an expiring or interrupted slice publishes only a fully committed frontier,
  rolls back any incomplete update, and the next slice executes exactly the
  next uncommitted update without repetition, omission or reordering;
- actor, critic, optimizer, recurrent, rolling-origin, audit and all other
  accepted state are restored exactly; wall time and slice count cannot alter
  RNG addresses, seed order, update order or arm behavior;
- intermediate durable frontiers are lifecycle state, not new scientific
  checkpoints or inspectable observations; the sole update-512 checkpoint and
  complete 24-seed inference law remain unchanged;
- no slice receipt, lease transition, stop decision or restart exposes or uses
  partial value, changes the seed roster, truncates the panel, or permits
  survivor/availability selection; and
- only the final complete technically accepted panel can enter scientific
  interpretation and the mandatory same-conversation Pro result convergence.

Under that contract, slicing changes administrative wall-clock occupancy, not
treatment, comparator, observable, estimand or claim ceiling. If any condition
fails—especially state discontinuity, repeated/skipped updates, value-dependent
stopping, an added science checkpoint, altered ordering or a new empirical
identity—the adaptation is science-bearing and must return to this EM before
activity, with same-conversation Pro reclosure where required.

## Strongest current technical unknown

The strongest unknown is whether lease expiry and command termination can form
an idempotent, fail-closed boundary around the last fully committed B frontier
and later resume the exact next update under the same empirical lineage across
fresh leases and Operator commands. The current acknowledgments do not establish:

1. whether the accepted B identity and owner-authenticated lease relation can
   span multiple fresh slice leases without aliasing or re-instantiation;
2. the minimum safe slice size and exact legal stop boundary;
3. atomic stop/receipt behavior when a lease expires during an update;
4. exact-next-update resume with no duplicate or omitted update, seed,
   checkpoint or evaluation cell;
5. non-overlap and no-duplicate launch semantics across successive Operators;
6. persistence and cross-validation of complete lineage/state provenance through
   multiple slice resumes; or
7. preservation of the no-partial-value firewall and sole update-512 checkpoint
   across per-slice receipts and cleanup.

Technical acceptance of restart-stable explicit lineage is not, by itself,
acceptance of this multi-lease command lifecycle. This EM must not infer the
missing equivalence.
