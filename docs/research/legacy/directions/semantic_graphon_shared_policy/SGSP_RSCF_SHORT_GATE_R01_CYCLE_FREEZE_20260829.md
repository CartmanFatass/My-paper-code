pass/fail meaning is a one-way-valid screen for the Pro-closed RSCF value
object: it preserves the object needed to test the reward-independent physical
interaction prior against the strictly containing EDGE learner, but can change

## Protected invariants and meaning-changing reductions

A surviving gate must preserve or conservatively relax with a proved one-way
implication all of the following:

- one shared recurrent execution policy across all four rosters;
- reward-independent `P0`, `K0`, load normalization, and the same 18 residual

  support, counterfactual work, optimizer opportunity, and checkpoint
  opportunity whenever either is present in the gate;

  actions, branch-specific closed-loop recurrence, immutable policy state, and
  a common branch-independent future tape;

  rosters `N=9` and `N=15`;

  attribution.

roster-specific controllers, weakening literal containment, giving either arm
different information/work/opportunity, changing the full-suffix intervention
into a one-step or open-loop proxy, adapting at held-out rosters, replacing
direct endpoints by a proxy, using an asymmetric cut, or treating class
expressibility as learned comparator competence changes the meaning. Such a
reduction cannot be called the requested gate unless it is explicitly a
conservative outer bound and the required one-way implication is proved.

## Current claim ceiling, nulls, and competing explanations

The current ceiling is static/exact pre-purchase decision evidence for this
exact toy, role set, policy class, intervention semantics, roster set, and
endpoint/cut family. Even a successful gate cannot establish physical-prior
value, EDGE training competence after 512 updates, credit causality, kernel
truth, joint-action credit, post-clip unbiasedness, another roster or task,
UAV efficacy, safety, or deployment value.

The strongest simple null is that no gate can jointly preserve these meanings
and be materially cheaper: the expensive parts may be exactly the optimizer
exposure and full-suffix observations needed to distinguish a narrow prior
from its containing learner. Competing explanations for a later favorable
comparison are finite-budget regularization, projection and optimizer geometry,
recurrent preconditioning, retained critic/global-clip interaction,
sampled-origin variance, and task-specific alignment of `K0`; a static support
certificate cannot distinguish them.

Predictions differ as follows. If an exact necessary-condition certificate can
bound any required support, competence, action-sensitivity, direct-headroom,
or cut-attribution predicate below its registered requirement, the expensive
RSCF purchase is scientifically dominated. If every necessary condition has a
cheap witness but learned comparator competence or PHY-versus-EDGE separation
still depends on finite-budget optimizer dynamics, the gate can justify at
most a bounded optimizer-geometry discriminator. If exposing the five classes
requires recreating the production suffix/training/evaluation package, there
is no materially smaller meaning-preserving gate.
