# OEER project-alignment addendum

Owner: `direction:optimizer-entropy-exposure-boundary-relay` Explorer Manager  
Applies to: `OEER-B1-BOUNDARY-RELAY-v1`  
Relation to the science card: interpretation and portfolio bridge only; it does
not change the frozen treatment, host, arms, observables, budget, or outcome
rules.

## Classification

OEER is **not a variable-`N` or variable-`k` algorithm candidate**. Its host has
one binary policy, no roster variation, no skill lifetime, and no UAV task.

It is a bounded **answer-changing enabling experiment** for the variable-`k`
family in which a high-level policy uses `KEEP/SET`, a duration head, or learned
termination and thereby changes the states and future learning data it will see.
The nearest repository family is the exogenous-opportunity F0/F1 commitment
editor, where repeated `KEEP` decisions produce variable realized lifetimes;
the stronger bridge is to its deferred learned-termination/hazard successor.
OEER is not currently enabling evidence for variable `N`.

The bridge is about *training dynamics*, not runtime identity. `CARRY/RESET`
means carrying or resetting Adam across a training boundary. It does **not**
justify giving each agent or skill an optimizer, or resetting Adam whenever a
skill terminates. `SELF/YOKED` is a small causal analogue of a duration or
termination decision changing later visitation; it is not itself a duration
model.

## Outcome to algorithm-decision map

| OEER observation | Variable-`k` decision |
|---|---|
| `D_M` is material and entropy-direction-specific, while `A_M` is negligible | Entropy can leave a persistent parameter-displacement effect, but this host gives no evidence of duration-driven amplification. Keep the current exogenous-opportunity `KEEP/SET` design unchanged. Any entropy schedule remains a training hyperparameter and must earn value in a variable-`k` toy before entering the algorithm. |
| `D_H` is material under `YOKED`, while `A_H` is negligible | Hidden Adam history can survive matched parameters on common data. A later fixed-`k` to adaptive-`k` curriculum must treat optimizer history as an experimental factor: compare carried versus freshly initialized optimizer state, or isolate a new duration/termination head's optimizer. Do not reset optimizers at runtime skill boundaries. |
| `A_M` is material, its exposure check is realized, and it clears the generic envelope | A small entropy-induced boundary mutation is specifically amplified through policy-controlled future exposure. Before promoting learned duration/termination, test an exposure-stabilized variable-`k` toy against the ordinary on-policy version—for example matched opportunity schedules or balanced boundary data during the causal comparison. If stabilization removes a harmful effect, retain exogenous opportunities; if the effect is beneficial and replicates across held-out `k` schedules, an adaptive entropy schedule becomes a candidate component. |
| `A_H` is material and its exposure check is realized | Carried optimizer history interacts with endogenous exposure beyond matched first parameters. Do not combine a warm-started optimizer and learned termination as an unexamined package. The next variable-`k` toy must cross optimizer carry/reset with exogenous versus learned timing; a robust benefit under learned timing is required before choosing carry or reset as part of training. |
| A `SELF-YOKED` difference appears without an attributable `D_M`, `D_H`, `A_M`, or `A_H`, or the required exposure check is not realized | The relay did not identify a design-bearing channel. Do not modify the variable-`k` algorithm; first repair identifiability with a duration-shaped toy only if Root judges that question worth its cost. |
| The generic same-size perturbations explain `D_M` or `A_M` | Delete the entropy-specific design story. Treat the finding as generic basin/early-update sensitivity and require ordinary perturbation and seed robustness; it does not justify an entropy or termination component. |
| All four primary effects meet the card's practical-absence rule | Close this optimizer/entropy relay for project navigation. Continue variable-`k` work without an OEER-derived component or optimizer fence. |
| Effects are finite but submaterial, heterogeneous, or imprecise | No algorithm change and no automatic larger OEER run. A follow-up is warranted only if a variable-`k` toy supplies a stronger, duration-specific discriminator. |

Signs must be interpreted with the reported trajectories and utility direction;
a material contrast alone does not say that carry, reset, pulse, or zero is the
better algorithm choice.

## Continue/stop judgment

Continue the already assigned CM construction and the single registered real
run. The experiment is small, already frozen, and can either delete an
optimizer/entropy embellishment or identify the one training interaction that a
variable-`k` toy must test. Unchanged-science engineering repair remains CM-owned;
if its cost becomes disproportionate, CM can return that cost fact for Root's
portfolio judgment. The scientific run should not be enlarged merely to obtain
significance. After the result and same-EM intake, only a material identifiable
channel may create a new duration-shaped toy direction; null, generic-envelope,
unexposed, or unresolved outcomes end the OEER project branch.

## Claim ceiling

Even a positive result establishes only that, in the frozen four-root
full-feedback toy, an entropy-induced displacement or inherited Adam state can
persist and possibly be amplified by action-controlled exposure. It may justify
the design of a variable-`k` discriminator. It does not establish variable skill
periods, learned termination, MARL coordination, variable agent count,
robustness/performance improvement, or transfer to a UAV simulator or real UAV.
Those require a later shared-parameter variable-`k` toy with held-out period or
timing schedules, followed by the normal toy-to-UAV evidence ladder.
