### 1. Ready To Design: Does Distribution Estimation Beat a Properly Conditioned Scalar Baseline?

**Question and contribution.** On the existing decentralized UAV task, does estimating the complete return distribution improve finite lower-tail learning over directly estimating the scalar score needed by the same actor update, once both methods receive the current threshold? This is a concrete continuation of TRDL, not an untouched idea or another unchanged-recipe replication.

Crucially, the source gives Q32 the current threshold through its output transformation, whereas SCALAR predicts `b(c)` and follows changing thresholds only through refitting. [Direction record](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/570fd45646ea62f4e28d86c74770e835b2e0a963/docs/research/candidates/tail_return_distributional_learning/DIRECTION.md), [actual learner](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/570fd45646ea62f4e28d86c74770e835b2e0a963/experiments/candidates/tail_return_distributional_learning/trdl_b01/learner.py#L54).

### 2. Conditional Reserve: Is Simultaneous Radio Activation a Valuable Control Resource?

**Question.** Does choosing which UAV service transmitters are active improve delivered service enough to justify telemetry, coordination and airtime costs, compared with competent ordinary scheduling? This changes service-radio actuation, not fleet availability, private history or learned message content.

**Source-grounded reason.** The native radio calculation includes every UAV as an interferer irrespective of whether it serves users. Conversely, replacing the association algorithm alone offers no alternate-link opportunity under the retained non-FDMA, positive-noise, nonnegative-threshold contract: ACG B17 recorded zero multiply eligible user-steps over 288,000 steps. Turning transmitters off changes that physical restriction, but also removes their ten-user capacity. That tradeoff is substantive; another matching algorithm is not. [SINR source](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/570fd45646ea62f4e28d86c74770e835b2e0a963/envs/pettingzoo/uav_env.py#L913), [retained accounting](https://github.com/CartmanFatass/My-paper-code/blob/570fd45646ea62f4e28d86c74770e835b2e0a963/docs/research/candidates/agent_count_generalization/NOTES.md#L17528).

**Concrete decision and comparison.** On the five-UAV base host, a joint controller selects one of 31 nonempty transmitter masks at each native clock, retaining bandwidth, on-state power, flight limits and native association. First compare all-on, greedy single-transmitter removal, and exhaustive 31-mask ordinary optimization. Give all three identical current channel telemetry and activation rights. Fixed motion and separately specified sensing/control-radio behavior are essential. No learner is initially needed: with accurate current channels and no intertemporal radio cost, enumeration is the competent solution, not a weak baseline to beat.

**Bounded prospective purchase.** A 64-world/H256, three-program comparison is **192 episodes, 49,152 native steps, zero fits**. With at most 15 mask scores for greedy removal, 31 for enumeration and one for all-on, the bound is **770,048 mask-score evaluations**. Engineering is conjecturally 8–16 hours. Native transition cost is probably small relative to support, but no activation-host timing or memory measurement exists; multiplying old environment throughput by 31 would be misleading because path losses can be shared.

**Prediction and branch.** Silencing can create eligible links while sacrificing serving capacity. Under identical geometry, exact current-channel scoring and free instantaneous switching, exhaustive J cannot underperform all-on because all-on is a candidate. Therefore a positive idealized score is unsurprising. The consequential outcome is whether service gains survive declared airtime, observation and switching costs. If not, decline the resource; if they do, retain ordinary scheduling. A learner becomes interesting only after a genuine uncertainty, latency or computational constraint is specified.

**Why conditional, not fourth-DM ready.** The existing host has no implemented activation contract, and we lack a defensible specification for pilot airtime, channel-estimation error/latency and what an inactive service transmitter can still observe. Assuming perfect free counterfactual CSI would mostly manufacture an optimization benefit. The local GSAC paper’s main-body §7–8 explicitly limits its empirical support to tabular, fully observed benchmarks; its wireless appendix uses packet collisions, not our SINR model. It supplies no missing UAV telemetry contract. [Primary PDF, arXiv 2510.21427](/mnt/c/Projects/My-lib/.local-formal-capture/corpus/papers/neurips-2025/e933b487df6bdf4e4fa17e079114786d/arxiv-2510.21427.pdf).
