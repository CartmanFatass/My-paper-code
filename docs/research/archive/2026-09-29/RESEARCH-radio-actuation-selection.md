## Radio Question

**Question:** Given a provisioned low-rate control link and a registered static-user map, can ordinary joint transmitter activation improve complete native service under delayed execution and observation-dependent motion? Is exhaustive mask search worth its additional computation over greedy removal?

**Contribution:** A finite ordinary resource-package comparison, not a new scheduling algorithm, learning advantage, physical battery-saving claim or optimal joint flight planner.

**Source-grounded opportunity:** Native non-FDMA interference currently includes every UAV, whether or not it serves anyone. Silencing can create eligible links but removes that transmitter’s ten-user capacity. This changes physical eligibility, unlike another association algorithm. [Native radio and assignment](/home/fires/hmasd-wsl/envs/pettingzoo/uav_env.py:919).

**Constructive conjecture:** Temporarily silencing a poorly placed transmitter can restore more useful links than its lost capacity costs. Intermediate consequences should include threshold crossings, changed connections and altered local visibility. The consequential prediction is positive full-episode J **and service** after subsequent motion responds to those observations. The serious alternative is that lost capacity, stale predictions and blind-motion intervals erase the gain, or that simple greedy removal captures everything useful.

### Proposed Contract

- **Host and motion:** Existing N5/U50, H256, uniform static users, free-space/no-shadowing, non-FDMA, 3 dB threshold, cap10 host. Retain `LocalController(history=False)`, its 27 velocity commands and four-tick holds. Its code stays fixed; its trajectories do not. [Factory](/home/fires/hmasd-wsl/experiments/candidates/ucope/uav_motion_prefix_b01/environment.py:8), [ordinary controller](/home/fires/hmasd-wsl/experiments/candidates/uav_local_history/b01/controller.py:136).

- **Declared use case:** Managed line-of-sight UAV service for registered stationary sites. Preinstall their XY coordinates rounded to one metre, a 400-byte position-table payload, and the known propagation/power/noise specification. This is an added static prior. It is not an online measurement of hidden channel state. No claim covers unmodelled fading or unknown propagation.

- **Lawful measurements:** Each UAV reports its existing own-position observation and chosen held command. Reports are encoded at one-metre position resolution, with sequence/time/hold fields. No simulator SINR matrix, connection truth, future positions or other agents’ private controller state reaches the scheduler.

- **Added communication resource:** A dedicated out-of-band **2 kbit/s effective control link**, not borrowed silently from the service band. Every four ticks: five 24-byte reports and one 16-byte mask command, at most **136 bytes/round; 8,704 bytes/H256 episode**, averaging 272 bit/s. All programs have the same resource rights; all-on may leave them unused. The rate and packet sizes are proposed provisioning assumptions, not measured hardware facts.

- **Timing and error:** At `t=0,4,…,252`, report position and the newly committed motion command. A mask takes effect at `t+1` for four transitions, truncated at termination; the first transition is all-on. The 1,088 transmitted bits require `.544 s` at the stated rate, leaving `.456 s` of the one-second round for scheduler/routing. Record scheduler latency; a missed deadline leaves the previous mask in effect. One-metre encoding contributes at most half a metre per coordinate. Predict motion by native clipping and command persistence: the first three scored positions follow the known command, while the fourth crosses the next motion decision and is explicitly extrapolated, not known.

- **Inactive sensing and switching:** A silent service transmitter exposes no user-discovery rows; peer-discovery eligibility requires both endpoints active. Own position, flight control and the out-of-band reports continue. No free passive CSI is added. Switching occurs at frame boundaries with **zero additional modelled switching energy or guard time**; report flips and transmitter-on time, but claim neither zero hardware cost nor net battery savings. There are no new channel pilots or fabricated scalar cost penalties.

The missing resource contract is therefore resolved by a transparent abstract service specification. What remains unknown is its empirical usefulness, not whether hardware measurements must precede a study.
