No DM/Root conversation was inherited. Assignment framing and historical navigation memory were available, so this was not blinded review. I reconstructed frozen protocols, source code, native summaries, selected per-world outputs and training/readout records before reading the current Root plan and result dispositions. I did not repeat remote trace audits or review the other two studies.

The consequential evidence is:

- **Roster memory:** the [source note](../../candidates/uav_roster_memory/NOTES.md) and G0 controller at `27551e616` show real leave/return with existing assignment and handoff memory. They do not establish the proposed private-history problem. Challenging that premise without a fit was useful scientific work.
- **Energy:** [B01](../../../../runs/uav_energy_coordination/b01_analytical_coordination_a01/summary.json) gave I−H J `+145.24`, but C−I `−42.60`. - **Active sensing:** [training](../../../../runs/uav_active_sensing/b01_native_j_a01/training.json) completed one fit and 1,600 optimizer steps. The minimum worthwhile changes are these:

Roster reconstruction is a successful example. Root must not partition away the important decision merely to obtain disjoint assignments.

2. **Choose the comparator for the decision being purchased.** If the purpose is package reuse, include the strongest applicable *existing* ordinary alternative in the initial comparison. If the purpose is a component contrast, retain that narrower interpretation. Energy H was useful as a competence reference, but could not answer whether I deserved investment beyond P. This requires neither exhaustive baseline search nor proved headroom. Central-information P and lawful-information H_BS/P_BS remain different comparison settings.

3. **Design learning around behaviorally distinct choices and their experience.** Extend “Use an informative horizon” to consider eligibility, requested versus executed actions, initial sampling probabilities and credit duration using available configuration and evidence. The 1-versus-256 service/scout representation made scarce service experience foreseeable; its realized magnitude was learned from the run. Balancing that choice would predict more service experience, but would not remove the unknown-BS restriction or guarantee native benefit. No universal exposure threshold, preliminary learnability run or positive-rule prerequisite follows.

4. **Use outcome branches to distinguish scientific reading from investment.** A behavior change without native benefit weakens the tested usefulness prediction. Nonactivation limits the mechanism test. A one-instance gain can justify retention or a specifically valuable replication, without automatically purchasing either. A loss can justify stopping the recipe while leaving the parent question open. DM continuity means making that reasoned choice, not producing a repair quota.

5. **Keep one consequential independent review, with Root’s allocation inside its scope.** Existing ResearchCritic can examine the question, comparator and learning design together before substantial implementation. Reuse applicable reviews; changed interpretation or investment may need focused follow-up, but ordinary completed batches do not automatically require another pass. Scout can resolve a specific source or comparator gap. No new role or skill is justified.

The smallest next observation is **no new run for this workflow task**. A subsequently selected learning comparison should preserve the important choice, include initialization and a competent applicable reference where informative, and reach complete native endpoints.
