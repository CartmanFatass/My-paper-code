What changed: finite ordinary commitment is a useful new conditional asset.
The motion study also prevents a misleading learned-benefit claim: L-O's
positive difference is exactly R retention, with fitting and additional compute.
What did not change: neither study identifies a dominant energy, guard,
optimization or observation bottleneck; neither establishes a useful learned
increment, sustainability, population-optimal control or exhausted questions.

The learner results share unchanged deterministic deployment, but not a proven
common cause. Persistent-service training executed 1,155 commitments; 17
charged without the arrival event that starts dwell, changing experienced
duration. That limits the intended learning interpretation without invalidating
O/P or explaining L=P. Joint-motion training executed 7,086 non-D choices and
1,201 simultaneous changed-motion windows; its strong initial D preference
remained the argmax throughout saved training and evaluation. Training exposure
is real, yet general learnability is unanswered. No stochastic deployment,
detector-patch retry, bias adjustment, extra seed or predictor expansion is owed.

Favorable ordinary means do not erase tails. Persistent O has one complete
loss, nine longer below-half-service spells, lower early service and net stored
energy withdrawal in every world. Motion O improves both outcomes in worlds
02/03 and avoids R's severe world-06 reserve tail, but introduces seven-member
terminal reserve tails in 07/08. R/P also have adverse tails. The corrected
forecast's causal benefit was not measured against the rejected predictor.
The two O programs and their world panels differ; no cross-panel ranking or
combined-component claim follows.
