Shared knowledge did affect decisions: B05 matched information, model and action rights; DM3 used the stronger ordinary selector and a competence reference; DM1 treated training instances as the replication unit; DM4 separated ideal score identities from finite PPO. This is substantive uptake. The recurring weakness is turning a local result into the next repair or a broad explanatory exclusion. The current closures interrupt that pattern appropriately.

The next Codex design should target temporary coverage handoffs during UAV transit, with H1's destination generator and final assignment fixed. This is narrower than Claude's service-adaptive anchors/deployment-design scope. It asks whether anticipating the joint consequence of one UAV leaving can improve complete service through a temporary supporting trajectory by another UAV.

The concrete comparison to converge on is:

- Decision: at the existing ten-step clock, choose no handoff or one eligible replacement UAV to visit a temporary support waypoint while another returns or transits. Preserve final H1 destinations, original native reward, production shield and guard. Freeze how a handoff opportunity and waypoint are generated using current permitted information.
