### Answer

Complete independent answer, with only local link targets rebased for archival:

Recommend **retain both amended comparisons**: A as a complete learning-package test, and B as a comparison that can change the ordinary reference. The additions of R50 and B’s corrected uncertainty branch resolve my consequential objections. Neither study needs another preliminary diagnostic or scientific review.

I received no inherited DM/Root conversation. The assignment disclosed candidate routes, so this was not a blinded review. I reconstructed the original records before reading current RESEARCH and the subsequent design proposals. I read committed native summaries, per-world records, training/reading outputs and source; I did not rerun experiments or bulk-data audits.

The supported diagnosis is:

- **Active-sensing B01 did not establish useful learning.** At source `38b7f132a`, the complete 480k-step fit produced L1−L0 J/step −0.003262 and L1−A −0.009755. Ten of sixteen evaluation worlds followed exactly H’s trajectory. Training contained 1,129 eligible decisions, only seven service requests; deterministic L0 and L1 always requested sensing when eligible. This establishes sparse service-choice exposure and a disappointing finite package, not its cause. The 1-versus-256 label structure and near-uniform sampling are consistent with the imbalance, but do not prove that balancing categories repairs learning. [Native reading](../../../../runs/uav_active_sensing/b01_native_j_a01/reading.json), [complete outcomes](../../../../runs/uav_active_sensing/b01_native_j_a01/summary.json).
- **Ordinary lawful information already has consequential uses.** Information B01 at `ee9c6c8ac` found a much clearer BS-source benefit than a user-source increment under its fixed controller. B02 at `d6151ff51` then produced P_BS−H_BS J/step +0.132724 and QoS +0.086515, with 17 improvements, three losses and twelve ties. Its useful-package prediction received support; default replacement failed its declared risk rule. In world `28100224`, final low-reserve UAVs increased from zero to seven despite higher J and service. [Native summary](../../../../runs/uav_information_value/b02_station_prior_a01/summary.json), [paired records](../../../../runs/uav_information_value/b02_station_prior_a01/perworld.json).
- **The remaining interpretation is ordinary controller competence.** Supplying P_BS coordinates also enables relay assignments that H_BS otherwise omits. B02 therefore does not isolate coordinate content. The successful transit-hold P from energy-coordination B02 is a central-information package, and cannot serve unchanged as A’s lawful-information comparator. [Planner source](../../../../experiments/candidates/uav_information_value/controllers.py), [energy comparison contract](../../candidates/uav_energy_coordination/NOTES.md).

**A should retain the revised four-program design:** H/L0, L1, always-eligible A, and untuned R50 on sixteen fresh complete H3000 worlds after one 480k-step fit.

Common P_BS support is appropriate for this new question. It prospectively permits decisions before genuine sighting, while explicit absent/inferred/observed states preserve information meaning. It does not establish that the old gate was mistaken. Preserve the existing availability, return-margin and service-assignment constraints, thirty-step commitment and native shield; increased eligibility remains an observation to measure inside the study.

The hierarchical gate makes initial service/sensing sampling explicit. Learning both gate and waypoint answers whether a **joint when-and-where package** improves complete outcomes. It cannot isolate timing, category balance or information acquisition as the mechanism. H/L0 can share one evaluation because the frozen zero gate and deterministic tie-to-service rule make their executed policies identical. This alias supplies no additional empirical replication.

R50 addresses the strongest simple alternative: a sensible amount of ordinary sensing may explain any apparent advantage over always-sensing. Its alternating rule should remain untuned. Outcome implications are:

| Complete observation | Scientific and investment consequence |
| --- | --- |
| L1 improves over H/L0, A and R50 with a useful service/risk tradeoff | Evidence of conditional package value from one fit; consider whether independent replication is worth its cost. No automatic confirmation. |
| L1 beats H/L0 but does not improve on A or R50 | Training changed behavior without earning its incremental cost against the applicable ordinary alternative. |
| Substantial choices execute, but native benefit fails | Adverse evidence for this finite package; retaining the ordinary controller and stopping is justified without identifying a repair. |
| Choices remain sparse or largely ineffective | Complete deployment remains unhelpful or unresolved, while the active intervention receives limited exposure. Do not discard inactive worlds or automatically widen the gate again. |

Read absolute native risks as well as contrasts. Shared P_BS support does not erase its inherited reserve counterexample. Sixteen evaluation worlds describe this fitted instance; they are not sixteen independent training replications.

**B should retain P_BS, S0_BS and H_BS on the proposed thirty-two fresh worlds.** The host law makes station 0 a substantive comparator: it already combines BS and initial service-center geometry. The inverse transformation removes that offset but amplifies station noise; clipping and rejection prevent an exact inverse interpretation. [Host generator](../../../../envs/pettingzoo/relay/energy_aware.py).

Match the controller rules and genuine-sighting precedence, not realized activation counts after trajectories diverge. Distinguish inferred input, relay generation, assignment, submitted command and realized movement. The existing B02 ties demonstrate why those distinctions matter.

P_BS superiority over S0_BS would support incremental decision value of the transformation in this controller. S0_BS superiority with favorable service/risk evidence would favor that alternative. If both help against H_BS but their difference remains decision-wide, retain unresolved conditional alternatives and consider ending attribution investment. That outcome establishes neither equivalence nor a reason to demote P_BS for negligible algebraic simplicity. Root has adopted this correction. Preserve the inherited P_BS replacement restriction and each prospective anchor-versus-H_BS risk rule.

The proposed complete observations cost **one fit, 480k training transitions and 480k evaluation transitions combined**. A’s estimate is 70–95 node minutes; B’s is 27–40, before engineering, queueing, review and readback. Historical A consumed 72.85 runner minutes, including 51.19 training minutes; B02 consumed 17.95 runner minutes and 18.59 accepted-operation minutes. New eligibility, optimizer behavior, contention and implementation effort remain uncertain. Existing assets make both comparisons feasible, subject to actual engineering checks and node admission.

This round can produce more decision-relevant knowledge through these explicit outcome branches. It cannot causally establish that the workflow revision caused better research, or that earlier failures arose from model limitations. Preserve this review’s fixed-rate objection, uncertainty objection and Root’s adopted dispositions in the existing records. This review itself authorizes no run.
