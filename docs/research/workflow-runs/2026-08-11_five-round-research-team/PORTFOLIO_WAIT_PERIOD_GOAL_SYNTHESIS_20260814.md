   MARK_SUPPORT_OK`. Insufficient voluntary support is neither branch and

4. Fusion does not require prior positive main effects, but it does require a
   prospectively interpretable `NEITHER/A/B/BOTH` interaction, a joint failure
   mode, cross-direction isolation and an answer that would change the family
   choice. Pure complementarity remains admissible under that design.

   beyond toy evidence requires an explicit observation/action/credit/timing
