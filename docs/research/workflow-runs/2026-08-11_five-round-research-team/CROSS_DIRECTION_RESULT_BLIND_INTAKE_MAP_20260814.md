  next discriminator.

### Common intake gate

An intake is portfolio-admissible only when it identifies:

1. exact direction, revision, frozen scientific object and same-direction EM;
2. complete, version-coherent, technically accepted evidence required by that
   object's atomic panel—never selected partial metrics;
3. the EM's plain-language conclusion and key observation;
4. the strongest surviving alternative and maximum defensible claim;
5. prohibited claims, next scientific discriminator and possible portfolio
   effect;
6. the exact cross-direction decision requested; and
7. a provenance statement confirming that no rows, seeds, thresholds,
   acceptance or claims were transferred from another direction.

Incomplete or no-data engineering work returns to CM for unchanged-science
completion. Scientific ambiguity returns to the same EM. Runtime streams,
elapsed time, attempt count, resource-slice state, hashes, receipts, provider
mechanics, child labels and archive/Git state are not scientific intake.

Same-conversation ChatGPT External-Pro result convergence remains required
before a positive mechanism claim opens a second surface. It informs the
direction EM and portfolio; it does not own portfolio allocation.

## Cross-direction portfolio comparison after admissible intake

For every admissible owner packet, compare—not automatically score—the
following:

1. whether the exact mechanism, only the full package, or neither was
   identified;
2. direct project value under variable `N` or `k`, including held-out change;
3. maximum claim and the distance/cost to a second surface or UAV bridge;
4. the strongest surviving shortcut, optimization or host-specific alternative;
5. prospective total cost and time to the next family-dividing discriminator;
6. new redundancy, competition, complementarity or fusion hypotheses; and
7. what information would actually change the investment decision.

Effect sizes, margins and thresholds are not commensurate across the four
hosts. There is no result-blind winner, fixed ranking, first-result-wins rule,
fixed `N`/`k` balance, top-`k`, WIP slot or automatic backfill.

SCDMP stability-first is only eligible for reconsideration when a complete
same-direction EM intake establishes that a direct variable-`k` route is
nonqualifying/nonidentifying, or when an invested algorithm needs its distinct
ordered transition/reward composition model. Eligibility is not activation.

## Forbidden automatic routing

Never use any of the following as a scientific or portfolio command:

- `FAILED`, `TERMINAL`, `BLOCKED`, `READY`, `no retry`, `attempt consumed`, a
  child recommendation or a provider status;
- wall time, resource-slice expiry, missing serialization, evaluator failure,
  archive/commit state or local engineering absence;
- fixed direction counts, top-`k`, winner-take-all, first-result-wins,
  one-action-per-cut, WIP slots or automatic SCDMP backfill;
- evidence pooling, shared thresholds, transferred acceptance or scientific
  updating of one direction from another's result;
- automatic fusion merely because two directions share variable `N`, variable
  `k`, or search/relay language; or
- post-result threshold edits, result-dependent branch changes, seed addition,
  partial-result selection or retrospective claims that an amended branch was
  result-blind.

Holding scientific evidence fixed, changing labels, elapsed time, attempt
count, resource state, arrival order or the number of directions must not
automatically change a scientific conclusion or allocation. Substituting a
complete EM-intaken milestone opens portfolio judgment; it never predetermines
the outcome.
