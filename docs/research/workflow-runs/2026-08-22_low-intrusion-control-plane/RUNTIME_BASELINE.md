This record is a calibration placeholder for the low-intrusion pilot. No formal
training was launched. The resource preflight was captured on the current host
for the native continuous-roster toy route, with CM selecting two workers from
the observed CPU/memory state. The manifest records the matching C++/parallel
route and one thread per worker.

no speculative duration is used for scheduling or scientific disposition.
