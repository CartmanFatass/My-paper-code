## Candidate portfolio and CDC dispositions

### C-ALPSW — selected

Conjecture: each lifecycle owns fast recurrent state `h_i` and a bounded slow predictive state `z_i`. The fast state may update on every active step; the slow state changes only on an endogenous agent-local write learned from environment-agnostic prediction.

Derivation target: sparse persistence may separate fast control from slow predictive information, give lifetime explicit ownership and replay meaning, and avoid the failed external-return renewal estimators.

Strongest counterexamples: ordinary recurrence can absorb the same finite state; always-write and never-write are degenerate; membership events can fake lifetime; nuisance dynamics can be predictively easy but behaviorally irrelevant; latent coordinates are non-identifiable.

Correction: require an online predictive compression objective with explicit write cost, exact structural nulls, latent-reparameterization invariance, causal primitive-action mediation and G8 as complete comparator.

Disposition: retained and selected only for S1 exact derivation.

### C-ALH — parked

An explicit categorical hazard exposes a semi-Markov factor, but evaluating KEEP/RENEW every active step is the prior shared opportunity mechanism with `k=1`. Reactivation requires a new identified source and a scientifically new source of boundary supervision, not a clock change.

### C-ATS — parked

Continuously adaptive timescale recurrence may reduce interference, but supplies no objective segment boundary. Reactivation requires a threshold-free survival or causal-persistence estimand.

### C-SEPM — parked

Set-equivariant persistent population memory may help complementary allocation, but changes coordination and individual lifetime simultaneously; TEAM_REC and ordinary set encoders remain simpler explanations. Reactivation requires an identified source where lifecycle recurrence and TEAM_REC are insufficient.

## Selected C-ALPSW scientific contract

### State, write and lifetime

For lifecycle `i`:

- `h_i,t`: fast lifecycle-owned recurrent control state;
- `z_i,t`: bounded slow predictive state;
- `age_i,t`: active primitive steps since structural initialization or the last learned write;
- `w_i,t`: latent write indicator evaluated only on an active transition.

At genuine join, sample one structural `q_join(z | c)` initialization with no Bernoulli write factor. On an active existing lifecycle, sample `w_i,t ~ Bernoulli(lambda_phi(c_i,t))`; a write installs a candidate and sets age to zero, while no-write preserves `z` exactly and increments age. Lifetime is the number of active primitive steps between writes. Terminal leave and evaluation horizon right-censor; temporary absence freezes the state, age, writer RNG position and open segment.

### Online information and forbidden fields

Writer context may use current local observation or a uniform embedding, `stopgrad(h)`, `stopgrad(z)`, task-independent active-step age, anonymous membership-event type, a permutation-invariant active-set summary and the executed primitive action only as a dynamics condition.

It may not use lifecycle identity, role, task name, goal, named object, external reward, success or progress, distance-to-goal, task phase, oracle change points or durations, future observations, or global cycle phase. Routing keys remain ledger/provenance only.

### Objective and probability factorization

The writer and predictor minimize a generic observation/membership predictive negative log likelihood plus `beta` times active-transition write count and registered variational regularization. External reward is absent. Oracle regimes are audit-only.

Writer transitions factor in parallel over active lifecycles and are permutation-equivariant. Candidate density exists only when `w=1`; structural join has its own factor. Primitive action probability is a separate factor conditioned on `stopgrad(z+)`.

### Gradients and credit

Predictive and variational losses update only the writer, candidate encoder and predictive decoder. Any consumed G8 representation is stop-gradient at the writer boundary. Primitive actor/critic gradients may train an adapter from `stopgrad(z)` but may not enter `z`, write probability or decoder. No lifetime reward, switch penalty, renewal bonus or external-return writer advantage is legal.

### Membership, replay and invariances

Genuine join creates fresh `h=0`, one structural slow-state sample and age zero. Temporary leave freezes ownership. Rejoin restores state and permits an ordinary endogenous write only after current observation processing. Terminal leave records censoring then deletes state. Other survivors retain their states and respond only through anonymous active-set context.

Future checkpoint/replay must preserve separate structural initialization, learned write, persisted no-write, primitive likelihood, predictive evidence, censoring, masks, lifecycle epochs and all RNG positions. Checkpointing and rollout chunking never create lifetime events.

The contract is invariant or equivariant to active-member order, lifecycle-key relabeling, inactive padding, environment packing, inactive-time insertion, rollout/checkpoint boundaries and invertible latent-coordinate relabeling.

### Honest claim ceiling

Ordinary recurrence can represent any finite construction used here. C-ALPSW can therefore support only an optimization or temporal-factorization advantage, stable held-out transport, causally used persistence or a cleaner individual-lifetime object. Predictive fit and persistence alone do not establish a skill.

Future mechanism-matched null is ALPSW-DUM: identical writer, predictor, slow state, predictive updates and RNG, but zero slow-state contribution to primitive control. Complete comparator is exact G8. Future causal quantities include same-state action-distribution intervention on `z` and same-snapshot CRN forced write/no-write continuation, not natural-group contrasts.
