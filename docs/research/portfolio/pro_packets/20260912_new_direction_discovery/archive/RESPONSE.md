## 3. Fund ACPS-B01: physical heterogeneity and partial sharing

### Scientific object and practical use

**Question:** on a fixed five-UAV team with explicitly different actuation capabilities, does limited capability-specific specialization improve the final native return obtained by a real finite-budget recurrent learner over a fully shared learner with the same information? Binding structure is multi-agent partial observability and co-adaptation under heterogeneous action consequences. The practical decision is whether a mixed-capability fleet merits this extra parameter organization instead of retaining the generic shared controller. This is a new physical-control/learning question, not another initialization, seed or pooling variant of an existing direction.

Use the existing five-UAV, fifty-uniform-user, H256 native service task as the base, with a **new, declared heterogeneous-actuation variant**. At each episode reset, draw one uniformly random assignment of multipliers `{0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5}` to the five physical UAVs. Hold it fixed throughout that episode. The multiplier changes the native integration's speed scale for the corresponding UAV; it is not a reward multiplier, hidden task label, change in elapsed time or a substitution of requested displacement for realized movement. Normal world/altitude boundaries still apply. Both arms receive exactly this variant and the same exogenous assignment law.

Both actors receive the unchanged native local input/history plus the same public five-entry capability assignment and own five-entry physical-ID indicator. This makes the original 108-feature actor input a proposed 118-feature input for **both** arms. The public capability list exposes capabilities, not hidden positions, user assignments or another actor's state. A physical ID is not sufficient to identify a capability across resets because the assignment is redrawn. The treatment cannot look up a private capability that its comparator cannot observe. No membership, replacement, latent-duration or communication intervention is included.

The treatment retains a common DENSE/GRU64 actor and adds, to the three pre-tanh velocity means, the capability-indexed residual `A_c tanh(B_c h)`, with `B_c` of shape4×64 and `A_c` of shape3×4, no adapter biases, for each of the five capability values. This is 1,340 proposed additional scalar parameters. Initialize A to zero and B with a separate private initialization stream after common parameters have been initialized; train both. Shared log standard deviations and the remainder of the actor remain ordinary learned components. Index the adapter only by the acting UAV's publicly known capability.

The comparator is **intact fully shared DENSE/GRU64 with the identical 118 legal inputs**, including capability and ID, and its full nonlinear velocity head. It can already produce different actions for different agents and histories. It is not an anonymous, historyless or constant-action null. Extra adapter parameters and their optimization are part of the tested package; no equal-capacity or isolated-gradient-interference claim is made. No independent-per-agent third learner is required for this first question. The supplied native DENSE observations support it as a serious starting architecture, not already measured competence on this newly defined heterogeneous host. [MGTAP accepted comparison and limitations][m-intake]; [MARL topic, shared policies need not behave identically][marl].

**Native chain:** different actuator responses change motion and joint service geometry; each actor observes its own permitted history and capability; shared plus specialized parameters change its primitive command; real trajectories supply team reward and recurrent PPO data; final native return tests whether that organization was useful. More specialization might reduce harmful interference, but it may instead waste data, overfit capability labels or make optimization worse. That is the strongest structural countercase. FRRIE's and MGTAP's mixed architecture results warn against equating structure with benefit, but do not score ACPS. [FRRIE question][d-frrie]; [MGTAP question][d-mgtap]; [FOUNDATIONS §§3–4][foundations].

## 4. Fund CADC-B01: communication contention as part of control

### Scientific object and competent null

**Question:** when communication is an actual shared, delayed resource, can a decentralized learned transmission policy improve cost-adjusted native service over a collision-free fixed schedule whose motion controller is fully learned? Binding structure is multi-agent partial observability and joint information-action consequences. The practical decision is whether to develop learned decentralized transmission control rather than retain a simple predictable schedule. It is not a claim that communication is always beneficial or that a more informed actor beats a less informed one.

Use the homogeneous five-UAV/fifty-uniform-user/H256 native motion task, with a **new explicit communication wrapper** shared by both arms. The physical task advances one primitive tick irrespective of messaging or waiting. Add a public two-state network process, initialized with equal probabilities, with probability.95 of retaining its state each tick. GOOD gives a newly accepted packet a one-tick delivery delay; BAD gives five ticks. Both arms see the current network state, own physical ID, tick modulo5 and own pending-packet indicator. This process is a proposed task law, not a measured network model.

At each tick, each sender not already awaiting delivery may attempt one fixed-size broadcast. Simultaneous attempts by two or more senders collide and all those attempted packets are lost; exactly one attempt enters transit. A successful sender has at most one packet in transit. At the beginning of a tick deliver packets whose delay has elapsed, then construct observations, sample motion/transmission actions, resolve new attempts and execute physical motion. Delivery cannot inform the action that originally created the packet. Future network states and undelivered contents are unavailable to actors.

Payload is fixed, not learned: seven normalized numbers consisting of the sender's currently observed own3D position, centroid of its currently visible user positions and visible-user count. An empty visible-user set supplies zero centroid and count. The payload is computed only from the sender's legal observation; the environment's full user set is forbidden. Add sender label and send-time metadata. Each receiver retains the latest delivered payload per sender, with validity and age; these are communication-environment records, not another agent's recurrent state or an FOLR-style trained lifetime memory bank. No global ground-truth map, privileged user assignment or hidden intent is sent.

Charge **.001 native reward units per attempted packet**, including collisions, identically in both arms. The objective is `J_net = (sum of original native team rewards − .001×number of attempts)/256`. Report original physical return and communication cost alongside J_net. This is explicitly a modified reward objective for this new task; it is not silently backfilled into an old UAV result. A fixed packet has one unit of channel cost; no real-radio energy or bandwidth-efficiency claim follows from that abstraction.

Treatment: one shared DENSE/GRU64 motion policy with a Bernoulli transmission head. Append the same ordered received-message records and public channel inputs to the actor in both arms. Jointly train the primitive velocity and legal send decisions from J_net. If the sender is pending, transmission is forced to zero by its public action mask and contributes no sampled-send likelihood. Preserve every legal sampled decision, including collisions; do not learn from a fictitious successful delivery or resample a collided decision during replay.

Comparator: **collision-free round-robin transmission, sender ID=t mod5 when eligible, plus a freshly trained motion DENSE/GRU64 with exactly the same packet, channel, mask, clock and observation license**. Its motion policy is not frozen or degraded. This schedule supplies regular reports and avoids attempt collisions without seeing another agent's private state. The treatment can in principle learn a periodic policy from the same inputs, but that does not guarantee it will. Differences in learning a send head and its parameter/optimizer burden belong to the complete package. Do not use no-message or always-collide as the primary null.

The strongest alternative is that round-robin already captures the useful communication, while decentralized send learning causes collisions, stale packets and additional credit noise. The sender most able to transmit may not have useful information, and the receiver may already have enough local observations. This first comparison does not identify message-content causality, optimal scheduling or general communication necessity. Its difference from VSP03 is consequential: that study chooses access to a service slot, whereas here transmissions modify several independently moving actors' information before later native control; the motion task never stops while the channel is busy. [VSP03 scientific question][d-vsp03]; [CBSC question][d-cbsc]; [UCOPE question][d-ucope]; [knowledge, DACOM limits][knowledge].

The null uses motion likelihood only, since its schedule is not a policy sample. The full channel-augmented team return trains both critics; all extra critic information remains training-only. ## 5. Register TRDL, but do not fund TRDL-B01

**Programme:** learning for lower-tail cooperative return with a distributional training baseline. Binding structure is team-return learning under joint-action uncertainty. Its practical use is whether an operator who explicitly values poor-service episodes should develop a distributional learner rather than a scalar learner aimed at the same risk objective. It is not formal safety, expected-return superiority or adding an adverse-tail statistic after looking at old results.

**Concrete unfunded first object.** Use the unmodified homogeneous five-UAV/fifty-user/H256 native task. No failure simulator or hidden privileged hazard is needed to create the first question: variation from resets and stochastic policies already defines an episode-return distribution. Let G be a complete episode's native J. Target the lower-tail functional at alpha=.25, written `max_eta {eta − E[(eta−G)_+]/alpha}`. This is a proposed risk-sensitive objective distinct from expected J; both treatment and null target it.

Both retain the same legal DENSE/GRU64 stochastic actor. For each16-episode training batch, form the detached empirical lower-quartile threshold eta and the trajectory score weight `W=(G−eta)×1{G≤eta}/alpha`. Use the same per-agent clipped recurrent policy surrogate and four fixed update epochs in both arms, with frozen behavior inputs, eta and advantages across those epochs. Do not add per-batch advantage standardization or an entropy reward that silently changes the named risk-oriented comparison. The empirical threshold and PPO approximation mean this is a **finite CVaR-oriented learning package**, not a proof of unbiased CVaR gradients or convergence.

The null uses a scalar training-only critic fitted to W. Treatment uses32 fixed quantile outputs for full episode G, and forms its detached baseline by averaging the same tail-score transform over those quantile predictions. Give both critics the same legal centralized training context, including elapsed time and accumulated reward needed to describe full-episode G; none of that extra context enters the execution actor. Quantile losses are trained on the factual episode return. This is a distributional baseline versus a same-objective scalar baseline, not an unfair comparison with a mean-return learner. Quantile calibration, finite tail samples and actor/critic optimization can defeat the treatment. RiskQ motivates examining distributions, not this particular estimator. [Knowledge, RiskQ extract and limits][knowledge]; [MARL topic, critic information][marl].

Propose512 training episodes per arm,32 batches of16 and four joint actor/critic Adam epochs per batch:128 joint calls per arm,256 for the pair. Evaluate only the final policies on256 matched reset episodes per arm, with fresh private policy randomness and no adaptation. Proposed total is `2×(512+256)×256 =393,216` team ticks and1,536 episodes. Quantile loss has32 outputs per factual training target; no simulated counterfactual trajectory or tail-policy search is introduced. Rates and actual memory are unknown.

Primary is **the difference between the two arms' empirical lower-quarter means**, each using its own64 lowest J values among256. It is not the lower quarter of the paired differences and not a mean over only mutually adverse worlds. Keep all512 returns, ordinary means and tail thresholds. Select proposed **MEI .01 lower-tail J**, an absolute service difference for this risk use. Tail uncertainty is conditional on the fitted policies and order-statistic sampling; an ordinary paired-mean SE must not be mislabelled as tail uncertainty. No inference calculation is performed here. Above/inclusive-band/adverse readings apply to the tail difference, with ordinary-mean harms disclosed and no unqualified overall-benefit language.

Proposed offer:900 seconds per complete arm,1,800 native,1,200 support,3,000 future invoked seconds. **Decline this offer now; numerical and implementation allowance are zero.** A full tail-oriented learner and its estimator/readout risk are a less attractive first purchase than the two selected native questions. Reconsider when this explicit poor-service-use decision warrants that complete cost, or a materially better justified equally risk-oriented baseline/implementation reduces it. A positive pilot, optimal tail bound or safety proof is not required to propose re-entry. Any eventual funded first object ends after its one pair/intake or concrete limitation; no automatic expansion of tail samples or tuning follows.

The practical question is whether a counterfactual action baseline improves finite native learning beyond a competent scalar centralized value baseline when actors retain exactly the same execution information.
