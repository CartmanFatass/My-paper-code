learning comparison needs, then preserving it as a prerequisite through successive
engineering rounds. Finite support, no learner, and a small timeout do not establish
low cost. Existing specifications already rejected ritual exact prerequisites in
several traced cases: this is also a failure to apply the specification while
authoring prompts and taking in Pro decisions. The owner-directed skill/renderer
and intake changes at 5a4265cd2 address those specific insertion points.

This evidence does not establish that every direction incurred hundreds-fold
measured inflation, that every census is wasteful, or that changing a model alone
repairs the process. Cost-cap ratios are not savings relative to an unmeasured B.
Real implementation defects and valid scientific negatives remain operative.
