## Cross-direction insight

Fixed addressed roots provide reproducibility, not a sampling population. The same defect appeared
independently in CBSC, CRTO, FRRIE, SCDMP, and UCOPE: small-block Student-t or bootstrap/max-t laws
were being asked to carry population conclusions without iid, exchangeability, normality, or an
exact randomization law. The accepted repairs differ by estimand:

- CBSC and UCOPE could make honest, narrower complete-panel claims because every registered slot is
  itself decision-bearing.
- CRTO, FRRIE, and SCDMP require a new inference/power investment because their intended claims are
  population means, equivalence, or expected disturbance-law value; silently relabeling a realized
  panel would change the scientific object.

The wave also separated three distinct failure types that must not be conflated:

- structural impossibility (`VQFP`);
- definition-level nonidentification (`RISP`, `CRTO`);
- technically implementable but scientifically held execution (`FRRIE`, `SCDMP`, `DISH`).
