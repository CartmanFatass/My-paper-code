## Purpose

The complete 33-direction Portfolio audit closed eighteen labels as independent investment units.
Nothing is deleted. Durable direction evidence remains at its original paths so later work can audit
the decision. Thirteen closures preserve a useful primitive, control or negative result by naming
where it may be reused; they are still `CLOSE`, not protocol `FUSE`, because no new synthesis
question or shared-core integration was created. Five closures end the current scientific object
directly.

Closed source labels do not occupy advancing capacity and must not receive successor WORK merely
because an old task, prompt, implementation or evidence packet exists. They also do not block future
progress: a genuinely new independent question may be registered by a later Portfolio action or
spinoff, and a fused primitive may be used immediately inside its named surviving authority without
reopening the source direction or inheriting its scientific polarity.

## Noninterference rule

Future Portfolio, EM and CM tasks must use current authority and the surviving direction's own
current evidence. This legacy report may be cited only to explain provenance, a control, or why a
standalone label was retired. It cannot:

- keep a slot occupied;
- create a join, WAITING state, Effect or reentry dependency;
- force a new task to read or reconcile every historical source package;
- reopen or cancel a direction;
- transfer a positive or negative scientific conclusion into the surviving authority; or
- authorize code movement, shared-core work, provider transport or an experiment.

The surviving owner may restate the smallest relevant primitive in a new complete WORK and cite the
source evidence directly. This keeps useful insight available without allowing legacy labels or old
task state to impede current allocation.
