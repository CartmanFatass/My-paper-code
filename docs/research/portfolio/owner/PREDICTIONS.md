# Prediction queue（预测队列）

One row per ladder, appended by the direction's DM when the ladder's first card is frozen. The
owner fills the `owner prediction` cell when convenient; launch never waits. At intake the DM
scores the owner's cell if filled and records `not taken` otherwise. Scored rows stay here as the
calibration record.

所有者填法：在 `owner prediction` 里写一句话（哪个机制赢、差距大概多大），或写 `unclear`。
