## Corrections to the supplied report

- The Shoham--Leyton-Brown manuscript’s copyright page says 2009; some
  publisher metadata says 2008. This index preserves the conflict and uses
  2009 for the local filename.
- Fox et al. is a 2021 arXiv preprint and a 2022 AISTATS/PMLR paper.
- Carmona--Lauriere--Tan is a 2019 preprint and a 2023 journal article.
- “Sample Complexity of Markov Games (Huang et al., 2024)” conflates at least
  two sources. `P11` is the Huang--He--Krause P-MBED/MFG paper; `P20` is a
  direct 2024 Markov-game sample-complexity paper by Dai--Cui--Du.
- Graphon MFC in `P12` gives `O(N^-1/2)`, not `O(N^-1)`. The `O(N^-1)` result
  in `P22` concerns a different uncontrolled interacting-particle setting.
- The 2026 STORM/Armijo item is an arXiv v1 preprint named VR-SDA-A, not an
  established MARL convergence result.
- “DEDA-FP” is identifiable as the algorithm in Magnino et al. (NeurIPS 2025),
  *Solving Continuous Mean Field Games: Deep Reinforcement Learning for
  Non-Stationary Dynamics*; it is now retained as `P24`.
- The corpus contains no direct variable-`k` theorem or algorithm. Do not turn
  fixed episode-level hierarchy, two-timescale learning, or asynchronous
  updates into a variable-skill-period claim without new evidence.
