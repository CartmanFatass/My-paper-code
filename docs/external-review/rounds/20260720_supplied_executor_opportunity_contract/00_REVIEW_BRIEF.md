# Supplied-Executor Opportunity-Contract Review Brief

## Purpose

The formal `CLEAN_SUPPLIED_EXECUTOR_HIGH_PATH_G0` run is terminal. Its
implementation contract is valid, but its registered scientific branch is
`INVALID_CLEAN_SUPPLIED_EXECUTOR_OPPORTUNITY_CONTRACT` because the routing
oracle failed the opportunity floor. At the same time, the learned high path
strongly outperformed its frozen initialization. This round must determine what
scientific information survives the invalid comparator contract and what the
smallest next evidence source is, or explicitly stop.

This round does not authorize code, compute, threshold changes, oracle repair,
or relabeling. The two blind open reviewers independently construct and attack
a plural causal portfolio. The controller reconciles only facts. Convergent Pro
validates the evidence, weighs the portfolio, and recommends one next evidence
source or stop.

## Open scientific ambiguity

The open reviewers must consider at least these non-exclusive possibilities and
may add structurally distinct ones:

- the routing oracle is not a valid upper-bound opportunity comparator under
  the actual lifecycle/frontier semantics, so the experiment identifies a
  contract defect while leaving the learned high signal informative;
- the learned actor exploits a real state-dependent assignment opportunity,
  but the supplied-primitives diagnostic is too narrow to support an algorithmic
  claim without a corrected information-matched comparator;
- the utility metrics or opportunity process favor learned stochastic routing
  differently from the constructive rule, so the apparent learned gain is
  inseparable from a metric/comparator mismatch;
- the supplied-executor high path is only a task-specific controller reduction,
  suggesting replacement by a simpler direct recurrent or options-like
  formulation rather than expansion of the hierarchy.

Do not collapse these into a unique legal route during divergent review.

## Decision quality

Each serious candidate must give its causal mechanism, evidence explained,
strongest contradiction, ordinary-MARL reduction, replacement ledger, and one
separating observation. Proposed evidence sources must specify comparator,
estimand, mutually exclusive branches, prohibited rescues, and exact minimal
implementation boundary. Prefer reanalysis or a small constructive diagnostic
over another long training run when it is more informative.

Convergent Pro must distinguish evidence validity from scientific promise,
retain a weighted two-to-four-candidate portfolio when uncertainty remains,
select only one next evidence source or stop, and preserve valuable unselected
ideas for later reconsideration.
