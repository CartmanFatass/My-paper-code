## Verified oracle and opportunity facts

The repository supports the Open-Pro diagnosis more specifically than the
Gemini spatial account:

- `constructive_actions` is a full-step rule: one active member is PERSIST;
  during an active short wave, uncontributed non-owners are SHORT; otherwise
  they are IDLE.
- The registered routing oracle recomputes that rule but teacher-forces only
  owners currently on the private opportunity frontier. Non-frontier members
  retain their incumbent primitive.
- Each short wave lasts four primitive steps and needs a two-step SHORT streak.
- A new private opportunity gap is sampled uniformly from integers `1..19`.

Therefore the routing oracle is a myopic frontier-filtered projection of the
full-step constructive rule. It is not established as an upper bound for the
opportunity-constrained event process. A learned recurrent actor may in
principle use pre-positioning and long KEEP commitments, but the existing
result does not yet contain a trace decomposition proving that explanation.

## Reconciled causal portfolio without ranking

### O — Opportunity-comparator misspecification

The current oracle may fail because a full-step constructive rule was projected
onto sparse owner opportunities without causal planning or feasibility
accounting. Separating evidence is an opportunity-feasibility audit comparing
the current oracle with a same-information causal planner and a clearly labeled
hindsight ceiling, without changing the original status or thresholds.

### R — Simpler scheduled recurrent allocation suffices

The learned gain may require only a time-aware recurrent mark policy with the
same lifecycle, opportunities, fixed executors and owner credit. F1
working-prefix dependence may be unnecessary. Separating evidence is either
existing-trace behavior decomposition or an architecture-matched F0/F1
comparison after comparator validity is understood.

### P — F1 applied-prefix has assignment value

Later frontier owners may use earlier applied commitments to avoid duplicate
PERSIST assignments and form a useful composition. The current learned-versus-
frozen comparison cannot identify this. Separating evidence requires a matched
F0 arm and common-support directional/utility effects, especially on
multi-owner frontiers.

### B — Learned executor remains the downstream bottleneck

The success of zero-parameter primitives may mean the high/event graph is
trainable while the historical shared learned executor failed to form naturally
used skills. This remains a low-confidence downstream explanation. It cannot be
tested before a valid high-path comparison and must replace, not stack onto, the
failed shared executor.
