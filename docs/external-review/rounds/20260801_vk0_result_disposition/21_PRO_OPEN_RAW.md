Is the proposed random-policy differential required?

No.

It is neither required for the present invalidity ruling nor sufficient to distinguish the live explanations.

An untrained random autoregressive policy is not guaranteed to have symmetric scores under the two serializations. The policy contains identity-indexed roster features, and the second token conditions on the first token’s realized edit. Random weights can therefore be order-sensitive before any learning occurs.

A random-policy difference would not prove an instrument defect; random-policy equality would not prove the trained factorization is equivariant.

A better positive control is an exact, deterministic order-conjugacy test:

prescribe the same final joint skill assignment under both serializations;

verify that token-to-agent mapping, primitive actions, and environment rewards are exactly identical;

separately enumerate the learned policy’s complete joint edit distribution under both orders at the same pre-decision state.

That distinguishes instrument mapping from policy factorization without relying on a statistical expectation about a random network.

CORRECTIONS
1. “The result system executed as frozen” is false

Mandatory actual-exposure fields were unavailable, and neither the manifest nor the analyzer rejects their absence. The formal first-match result therefore skipped a frozen row-1 condition.

2. “Full actual exposure” is not supported by the listed durable evidence

The listed evidence supports checkpoint identity, common configuration identity, return-code success, and zero low-level optimizer exposure. It does not contain the required high-level optimizer-step, high-check, token, skipped-batch, or aborted-batch records.

The PM’s reported nominal totals may be correct, but they are not auditable under the frozen evidence contract.

3. The row-4 output must not be converted into a broad carrier failure

The formal result stopped before every urgency-access statistic. Only natural task competence under the two evaluation serializations was read.

4. The order intervention is serialization, not pure anonymous relabelling

It is scientifically relevant because R30’s deployed joint policy is autoregressive, but it does not by itself test a complete permutation of environment slots, policy state, and outputs.

5. A random-policy symmetry expectation is not a valid null

The architecture can be order-sensitive at random initialization. Use an exact prescribed-assignment positive control and finite joint-distribution enumeration instead.

6. The trace did not implement every frozen semantic field

Current/previous targets are absent, and segment origin is stored under an ending-authority name. These are implementation corrections, not evidence that the toy source failed.
