## Adopted scientific boundary

- Keep `G = U_EHC - U_DUM`, OR/DUM/EHC, reward, task, seeds, budget and
  thresholds unchanged.
- Treat natural KEEP/RENEW counts and opportunity spread as support only.

  not by itself that the change is useful.
- Interpret continuation effects only as same-state, exact-snapshot,
  common-random-number forced-event effects at eligible naturally reached
  decision states.
- Report timing, mark, representation-only and selection explanations
  separately before applying the existing joint result branch.

checkpoint. The unchanged formal training creates that checkpoint once; the
audit is an evaluation stage and does not authorize retraining, rescue or a new
experiment family.

seed, budget or post-result threshold change. No aborted checkpoint is valid
input. No result may be promoted beyond the claim ceiling in the reconciliation.
