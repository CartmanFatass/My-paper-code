## Final target and non-negotiable boundaries

The final target is one shared MARL algorithm supporting anonymous within-
episode join, temporary leave, rejoin and terminal leave while learning
heterogeneous individual skill lifetimes and preserving survivor state.

- Intrinsic reward must be environment-agnostic and cannot read task fields,
  identity, assigned role, named objects, success, progress, contact, phase,
  distance-to-goal or external reward.
- Variable membership requires event-time masks and survivor continuity;
  cross-episode fixed-N generalization is insufficient.
- Variable lifetime must distinguish physical clock, service opportunity,
  realized segment and credit window. Do not return to a duration catalogue,
  scheduler-only method or shared renewal period.
- Prefer replacement and simplification. Do not stack a graph, field, slot,
  latent, posterior, scheduler, critic or reward without a unique causal role.
- A clean-carrier direct pass is an access prerequisite, not evidence that
  temporal abstraction helps. Any hierarchy proposal needs a matched direct
  reduction on a task where heterogeneous temporal abstraction is load-
  bearing.
