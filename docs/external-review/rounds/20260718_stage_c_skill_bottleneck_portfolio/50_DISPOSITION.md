## Authorized Reanalysis

Use the serialized Stage C final checkpoints, stored natural ledger and
registered forced branches. Freeze metrics, splits, support floors and
interpretation branches before reading results. The audit has three linked
reads:

1. **Same-snapshot intervention.** Hold focal observation, recurrent state,
   legal action support, exogenous ledger and boundary state fixed; intervene
   only on `z`. Compare exact primitive-action distributions and paired
   fixed-active-time trajectory distributions.
2. **Nuisance-matched stability.** Test whether generic action/process
   signatures retain their relative meaning across held-out lifecycle
   instances, active-age bins, ordinary versus join/rejoin strata and active
   team size. Age, duration and membership state are matching variables, not
   semantic features.
3. **Natural overlap.** Test whether naturally assigned segments occupy the
   same process regions as the corresponding forced branches. Natural skill
   frequency must not solve the comparison.

Required nulls are context-only, shuffled-label, skill-prior, age-matched and
duration-matched controls. Only active execution is scored. Inactive leave
intervals, task reward, task phase, role names, progress, contact and success
fields are excluded. Unsupported strata return `UNDERPOWERED`.

R29/R31-style effect statistics may be reused only as diagnostic instruments;
they do not become rewards, advantages or gradients. The audit cannot by
itself determine transfer or commitment advantage over Stage B.
