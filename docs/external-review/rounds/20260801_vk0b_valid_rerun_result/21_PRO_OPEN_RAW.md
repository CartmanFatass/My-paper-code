CORRECTIONS
1. The row-4 result is valid, but causal wording requires qualification

It is correct to call the result a valid adverse competence finding for the registered reversed-order stratum.

It is not yet correct to say:

reversing the serialization caused the entire observed gap.

Canonical and reversed orders were assigned to different halves of the episode-index bank rather than paired on every identical state. V-K0C supplies the causal matched-state comparison.

2. “Training reproduced the original training exactly” is slightly too strong

Byte-identical final checkpoint files across all six seeds establish byte-identical final serialized training states and strongly support noninterference. They do not, by themselves, logically prove that every transient token, action, gradient, and reward was identical throughout 640,000 interactions.

The proof-sized counters-on/off witness supplies direct trajectory-level noninterference; the full-scale checkpoint equality supplies a strong endpoint replication. The durable wording should be:

Instrumentation produced byte-identical final checkpoint artifacts for all six seeds and passed a direct proof-sized trajectory noninterference witness.

3. Equality of summary numbers is not caused by checkpoint identity alone

The repeated competence values follow from the conjunction of:

byte-identical policy checkpoints;

the unchanged environment and action table;

the frozen episode and policy seed streams;

the unchanged order assignment;

the same analysis and bootstrap;

and zero replay mismatches.

Checkpoint equality is necessary but not the sole reason.

4. The interim analyzer schema correction does not invalidate the result

The correction occurred on a schema-shape refusal, before a scientific branch could be interpreted, and the row files were unchanged. It aligned the analyzer with the frozen producer representation and did not move a threshold, exposure identity, estimator, or result predicate.

The quantitative exposure fields remain source-labelled; the empty skip/abort reason arrays are non-quantitative status annotations protected by the hashed source manifest. The valid rerun remains authoritative.
