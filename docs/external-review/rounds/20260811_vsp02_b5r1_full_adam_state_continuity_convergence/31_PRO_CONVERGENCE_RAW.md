Overall conclusion

The frozen branch requires exactly the proposed narrow interpretation: the complete-Adam carry/reset intervention was nonvacuously active and changed the optimizer-to-parameter path, but it did not localize the binary exact-mapping endpoint on any of the five registered paired root/tape units. The result supports neither equivalence nor a directional Adam-state effect. Treating this exact treatment as exhausted and parking the B1–B5R1 line without a result-selected successor is scientifically justified.

Strongest remaining alternative

The stated alternative is appropriately bounded. The result is compatible with a combination of:

root- and tape-specific closed-loop learning paths;

treatment-induced trajectory differences that remain on the same side of the final exact-success boundary;

a shared final threshold classification despite differences in logits, margins, or intermediate learning dynamics.

Pairing rules out root or tape imbalance between CARRY and RESET within a unit. It does not allow the design to decompose why U03 succeeded in both arms while the other four units did not. In particular, the design cannot distinguish:

initialization contribution from tape contribution;

one downstream mediator from another;

genuine broad-basin acquisition from endpoint proximity;

latent quantitative treatment effects that do not change the binary classification.

The phrase “compatible with … threshold crossing shared across arms” should be read as compatibility with a shared final threshold classification, not as evidence that a temporally observed crossing event occurred. Because the draft presents this only as an unranked compatibility statement, no textual repair is required.

Exhaustion and park

The exact B5R1 treatment is exhausted under its registered:

common-ancestor and update-1 boundary;

complete carry/reset definition;

five roots and paired tapes;

finite training budget;

final-only checkpoints;

exact-success evaluation panels;

one-full/no-rescue rule.

A successor selected from the observed common U03 success, scalar differences, or mediator traces would be adaptive post-result selection. The same is true of choosing a different boundary, moment component, threshold, checkpoint, root count, or optimizer modification because B5R1 did not separate the endpoint.

Parking is therefore justified. NO_SUCCESSOR is correctly bounded to mean no successor licensed by this result or its traces; it does not assert that an independently motivated future scientific question is impossible.

No draft sentence requires rejection or substantive correction. The status fragment COMPLETE_ADAM_AXIS_EXHAUSTED must remain interpreted as shorthand for the exact registered B5R1 complete-Adam treatment under this boundary, budget, roots, tapes, and endpoint, not as exhaustion of all Adam-state questions across other boundaries or designs. The surrounding draft already supplies that limitation.
