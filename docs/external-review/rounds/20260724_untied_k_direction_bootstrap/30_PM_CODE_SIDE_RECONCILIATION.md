## Adopted scientific decisions

These are Pro's, restated for repository use, not renegotiated.

1. **Verdict on the branch direction.** Per-agent variable skill period is a
   **restatement as posed**, not a resolution of the G17–G19 impasse. It is
   retained as one conditional portfolio candidate (P1) and only under a
   five-part interpretation: the high-level decision is a real semi-Markov
   commitment with its own termination; immediate primitive control stays a
   separate fast decision; the high-level log-probability receives event-local
   option credit rather than inheriting a length-`k_i` trajectory window;
   post-boundary delayed consequences flow through a declared bootstrap or
   causal estimator; and the slow decision has behavioral authority over the
   agent-specific redistribution. Without all five, replacing `k` by `k_i` is
   resegmentation.
2. **Re-diagnosed root cause.** The impasse is primarily an action-authority
   and credit-factorization problem with a temporal manifestation. The fast
   requirement is largely an aggregate service mapping; the delayed requirement
   contains an agent-specific redistribution decision (equal immediate service,
   different battery expenditure, different later service — the registered G18
   intervention). One primitive action can carry both effects at once, so a
   residence time is not automatically a causal window.
3. **Coupling audit.** Of the four things sharing one constant today:
   - *skill reassignment = period boundary* — load-bearing only at the
     individual-decision level; agent `i`'s own termination governs its own
     renewal, under a declared agent-local clock; other agents need not renew;
   - *credit window = period boundary* — the closure relation is useful, the
     credit identity is not; close the decision record at that decision's
     termination and estimate over the realized segment plus a declared
     bootstrap;
   - *recurrent chunk length = period* — inherited convenience; delete the
     identity; recurrent state persists across chunk edges;
   - *episode length divisible by period* — pure convenience; delete it;
     permit right-censored final segments with frozen value semantics.
4. **Team skill `Z`.** In the primary untied-period candidate, periodic sampled
   `Z` is replaced by a deterministic read-time coordination context `C`,
   evaluated at each renewal event, with no holding period, no stochastic
   action, no log-probability, no entropy objective, no independent recurrent
   ownership, and no classifier-based team-skill claim. The team-code entropy
   and `q_D(Z | ·)` intrinsic-reward path is removed from this primary
   candidate. The symbol `Z` is reserved for a genuinely sampled team
   commitment, which stays a separate portfolio candidate with its own event
   clock and a strict reactivation condition (a demonstrated necessary atomic
   team consequence that deterministic context plus individual decisions cannot
   reproduce).
5. **Plural portfolio.** Four structurally distinct candidates are retained:
   P1 event-indexed asynchronous semi-Markov skills; P2 active-set functional
   decomposition into fast common mode and slow anonymous redistribution;
   P3 separate slow allocation authority over a fast executor; P4 flat
   recurrent controller with per-agent causal credit. P4 is the strongest
   information-matched reduction and is mandatory whenever P1 or P3 advances to
   behavioral evidence. The closed G18/G19 candidates stay closed.
6. **Separating evidence.** The decisive future observation is a positive
   held-out period-specific interaction `I_period` on a source with anonymous
   membership change and causally heterogeneous duties, with identical
   event-credit semantics across period arms and the information-matched flat
   reduction failing to reproduce the gain. The full freeze list in raw
   section 7 governs any such collection.
7. **G20 continues, broadened.** The active-set-centered residual derivation is
   neither dropped nor absorbed into untied period. Its scope now carries four
   questions: whether the registered constructive-minus-counterfactual action
   difference lies in an anonymous centered subspace; whether that subspace
   preserves the G17 aggregate service direction; whether shared scalar actor
   credit can orient that subspace; and a counterexample search where centering
   deletes a required delayed common-mode action. Success absorbs G20 as a
   credit/action-authority component usable by P1, P3 or P4; failure retires
   only the active-set-centered residual proposition.
8. **Scheduled evidence action.** One zero-compute timing–credit
   identifiability derivation on the frozen G18 first-action intervention pair,
   comparing at estimand level: fixed synchronous segmentation with shared team
   advantage; per-agent untied segmentation with the same shared advantage; and
   an agent-sensitive or active-set-centered redistribution advantage. The
   central proposition: does changing only the boundary schedule alter the
   information available to distinguish the constructive rotating-member
   allocation from the equal-immediate-service counterfactual? Pro
   pre-registered the outcome mapping — *no* formally excludes `k -> k_i` alone
   as a solution; *yes* obliges the derivation to name the exact probability or
   return term that changes and why matched fixed-period and flat controllers
   cannot express it. This action authorizes neither implementation nor
   compute.

## Excluded by this round

- The identity "skill holding period = causal credit window".
- Keeping skill period, recurrent chunk length and episode divisibility bound
  to one constant.
- Retaining a periodic, persistent, sampled team `Z` by default.
- Reopening exact TD(0), raw-sum, channel-normalized, actor/critic-isolated
  credit, or the G19 fast anchor by re-tuning, reseeding or renaming.
