## Review objective

Define the smallest scientifically identifiable UAV benchmark ladder for a
fixed physical fleet whose service-active membership changes at runtime due to
temporary demand coverage, charging rotation and temporary detachment/failure.

The accepted synthetic dynamic-roster algorithm is a starting hypothesis, not
UAV evidence. The review must identify the strongest fixed-agent masked MARL
reduction and prevent availability masks from being relabelled as dynamic
membership without a separating estimand.
