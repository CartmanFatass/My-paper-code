## A false claim of mine, corrected

My question §1 said both harnesses "import nothing from the audit path". **That
is false for obligation B.** `scripts/d7_s_r5_obligation_b_feasibility.py`
imports `audit_d7_s_event_aligned` and builds and steps real environments
through that module's helpers. Only the obligation-A harness is standalone.

The distinction Pro draws is the one that matters, and I collapsed it:

> B is a probe of feasibility **under the current audit/source realization**, not
> an independent verification that the realization is correct.

I had the right fact in the B document — "writes nothing into the audit path",
which is true — and then generalised it into "imports nothing", which is not.
A guarantee about writes is not a guarantee about reads, and stating the stronger
one made B look like independent corroboration when it is a dependent probe.

**Also corrected: B was not zero-compute.** It ran eight 1500-step episodes and
observed 1200 check boundaries. That is non-conclusion-bearing *development*
compute. Pro's own earlier phrase "A–G are all zero-compute" is withdrawn in this
ruling too — the accurate split is: A zero-environment-compute; B already stepped
the environment; C–E require implementation and stepping; F can be synthetic; G
is design work.

## B's claim, narrowed to what it supports

Not: *"the comparator is routinely executable on this source."*

But: **the full matching was feasible at every observed pre-action check along
eight `constructive_mixed` trajectories on development topology `20260725`.**

B does not establish feasibility on an unobserved topology, under a fresh
population, after the derangement changes future positions and assignments, or
totality of the policy over its own induced state distribution.

**That last one is the sharp point and I had not seen it.** B observes states
generated by `constructive_mixed`. Once the derangement is applied, positions,
duties and future matching graphs diverge — *a control feasible on the baseline
trajectory can become infeasible on its own trajectory.* So B closes
baseline-state development feasibility and leaves post-treatment policy totality
entirely open. The 22 checks sitting at `n_eligible = 2` show the development
realization already visits the feasibility boundary.

**The 1200/1200 must never be quoted as a source-wide probability**, nor as
evidence that the applied control stays feasible.

## Realization corrections to carry

1. **`+inf` versus finite sentinel — the derivation contradicts itself.** The
   obligation-A harness uses a large finite sentinel and explains why `inf`
   cannot carry infeasibility; the amended design still says forbidden cells are
   `+inf`. Neither numerical encoding is the scientific object. Freeze instead:

   > Forbidden edges are logically absent. The implementation must use a
   > representation that preserves the legal optimum and must reject any
   > returned forbidden edge.

   A finite sentinel is admissible only when proved larger than every possible
   legal total cost for the registered geometry. **The Hall witness owns
   infeasibility, not a sentinel-valued solver output.**

2. **The Hall refusal must carry `S`, `N(S)`, `|S|`, `|N(S)|`** — not merely the
   neighbourhood size, which is what my probe recorded.

3. **A's proof needs sparse-graph coverage.** The exhaustive tests use the
   complete non-incumbent graph; the real control has sparse graphs after
   geometric exclusion. Add feasible sparse, infeasible sparse, sparse-with-ties,
   canonical-vs-brute-force on sparse, and a check that the reported witness
   corresponds to the exact graph handed to the solver. Scientific definition of
   A is closed; its technical certificate is not.

4. **B's eligibility pruning is an undocumented two-pass approximation.** It
   removes agents whose option set empties after restricting to the eligible
   pool. One interpretation must be frozen: either iterate the pruning to a
   fixed point and apply Hall to that graph, or freeze the initial eligible set
   and classify later empty adjacency as matching infeasibility. No such
   exclusion fired in the observed 1200 checks, so the count is not overturned —
   but that invariance is to be **rechecked, not assumed**.

5. **The six-part eligibility definition must be asserted, not assumed.**
   Including failed/terminal/non-acting state, rather than relying on those
   states happening to be absent.

## The independence requirement — the R4 mistake, one obligation later

The harness must not call an `EXPOSURE_OK` function and report that the same
function returned true. The witness layer derives independently from traces:
incoming and outgoing maps, incumbent and assigned targets, actual action arrays,
check boundaries, lifecycle censoring, run lengths. The five paired negatives
must perturb production behaviour while the independent witness stays unchanged.

**Otherwise the control and its test again share one narrowed definition of
exposure** — which is exactly how R4's `full_sync_SET` certified itself with a
comment.
