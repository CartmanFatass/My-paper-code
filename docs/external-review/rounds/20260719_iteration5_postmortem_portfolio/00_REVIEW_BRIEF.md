Iteration 5 is closed as the implementation-valid registered outcome
`RETIRE_SPATIAL_CARRIER_NO_DIRECT_ACCESS`. This round must not rescue that run,
reinterpret it as an algorithm comparison, or select a successor before the
independent reviews are complete.

The unresolved scientific question is:

> After a task-neutral spatial carrier failed the ordinary-controller access
> condition and the exact segment-owned semantic objective failed its material
> behavior audit, what is the smallest next evidence source that can separate
> the remaining explanations for learning under runtime-variable team
> membership and variable individual skill lifetime?

Gemini and open Pro independently construct and attack a two-to-four-candidate
portfolio. Codex then reconciles facts and contradictions without ranking the
portfolio. Convergent Pro weighs the evidence and recommends one next evidence
source or an explicit stop. The round does not authorize implementation or
training.

## Final capability and non-negotiable boundaries

The final target is one shared MARL algorithm supporting episode-internal
anonymous join, temporary leave, rejoin and terminal leave while learning
heterogeneous individual skill lifetimes and preserving survivor state.

- Intrinsic reward must be environment-agnostic. It may not read task fields,
  identity, role, named objects, success predicates, progress, contact,
  distance-to-goal, phase, or external reward.
- Access failure must be treated as an observation, dynamics, policy-capacity,
  optimization, or evidence-substrate question, not hidden by task shaping.
- A candidate must replace or simplify a current component. Blindly stacking a
  graph, field, slot, team latent, posterior, scheduler, critic, or reward term
  is not an architecture.
- Variable `N` means within-episode membership change with masks and survivor
  continuity, not fixed agent-count generalization across episodes.
- Variable lifetime must distinguish physical time, service opportunity,
  realized segment, and credit window.
- Ordinary direct recurrent MARL remains a mandatory explanation and baseline.

## Starting explanations, not selected routes

Reviewers may merge, split, reject, or replace these, but the open portfolio
must contain two to four structurally distinct explanations.

### A. Evidence-substrate or access mismatch

Iteration 5 may primarily show that its spatial persistent/reactive task is a
poor identified substrate at the frozen capacity and budget. A constructive
toy or reanalysis may be needed before another algorithm mutation.

### B. Semantic creation is absent or incorrectly owned

The low policy may still lack a task-neutral learning pressure that creates
persistent, intervention-sensitive processes owned by realized segments. The
exact Iteration-5 posterior pressure is retired, not every possible semantic
mechanism.

### C. The fixed discrete skill bottleneck is the wrong interface

A fixed categorical `z_i` may be too rigid when membership and useful temporal
context change. The useful replacement might be a simpler set/field/composed
conditioning architecture, but it must preserve decentralized execution and
state/probability/credit semantics rather than append another controller.

### D. Hierarchy is unnecessary until a load-bearing task requires it

The current evidence may favor direct active-set recurrence until a testbed
demonstrates a task-relevant advantage from temporal abstraction. The next
evidence may therefore be a benchmark or reduction decision rather than a new
intrinsic objective.

## Required decision quality

Each viable candidate must state:

- the causal estimand and the final capability it would unlock;
- what it retains, deletes, replaces and adds;
- its strongest ordinary-MARL reduction and retired-line objection;
- one observation that separates it from at least one other candidate;
- the smallest coherent evidence source, comparator and plausible outcome
  branches;
- prohibited rescues and the precise implementation boundary Codex could later
  operationalize.

Convergent Pro must recommend exactly one next evidence source or an explicit
stop, while retaining a two-to-four-candidate portfolio if the science remains
open. Selecting one source does not make all other hypotheses illegal.

## Deadline and authority

Review output does not authorize code changes, an experiment, a threshold
change, a budget expansion, or another autonomous iteration.
