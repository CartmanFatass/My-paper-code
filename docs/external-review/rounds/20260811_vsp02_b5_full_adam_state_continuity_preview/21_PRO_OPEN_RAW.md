Overall conclusion

The B5 design is prospectively valid, causally single-axis, branch-complete, and capable of producing a decision-changing finite-set result within its explicitly narrow claim ceiling. The dated provenance supplies an outcome-independent mechanism rationale predating B4; the B5 packet then specializes that mechanism to the earliest boundary at which complete Adam memory can be nonvacuously carried or reset. Nothing in the specialization requires the B4 outcome, counts, mediator traces, or checkpoints. This establishes design prospectivity, not future implementation or result validity.

1. Prospectivity of the provenance and boundary specialization

The July 29 provenance did more than mention Adam abstractly. It prospectively registered:

reset versus exact carry of step, exp_avg, and exp_avg_sq;

equal boundary parameter bytes;

equal first post-boundary gradient bytes;

unchanged objectives, source, parameter order, and Adam hyperparameters;

a mandatory nonvacuity test on the first post-boundary optimizer transformation;

a narrow source-local, finite-budget claim ceiling.

It also identified Adam reset attribution as the scheduled next scientific action, rather than selecting it from any later VSP02 observation.

B5 does not improperly transfer G52’s host, update-100 boundary, thresholds, result, evidence, or authority. It transfers only the already-proposed causal mechanism and specializes the boundary through the deterministic rule:

EARLIEST_NONVACUOUS_ADAM_BOUNDARY_AFTER_ONE_COMMON_ORACLE_SIGN_UPDATE.

That rule is scientifically natural: before any optimizer step there is no acquired Adam memory to carry, whereas after one common step the two arms can begin from identical parameters and nonempty optimizer state. Selecting the earliest eligible boundary minimizes inherited trajectory history and does not consult a B4 outcome. The fact that the full B5 freeze is being constructed after B4 does not itself make it adaptive; the relevant issue is whether its scientific axis or settings were chosen as a function of B4’s result. The packet explicitly excludes that dependency, and the dated provenance supplies independent support for the axis.

No repair is warranted.

4. Strongest remaining alternative explanation

After valid paired roots and address-indexed paired tapes, ordinary between-arm root or randomness imbalance is not the strongest alternative. The strongest remaining account is a nonlinear interaction among:

the carry/reset treatment;

the particular initialization/root;

the particular exogenous tape;

downstream closed-loop trajectory amplification;

the thresholded exact-success endpoint on the finite evaluation panel.

For a valid discordant pair, that account does not eliminate causality: the endpoint difference remains caused locally by the registered Adam-state intervention because root, tape, pre-treatment parameters, first arm-specific batch, and first gradient were held common. It instead limits interpretation and generalization.

The design can distinguish:

local paired-root/tape endpoint discordance caused by complete Adam carry/reset;

no endpoint discordance on the registered panels;

one-directional versus bidirectional finite-set discordance.

It cannot distinguish:

which Adam component—step, first moment, second moment, or their interaction—carried the effect;

whether the decisive downstream mediator was occupancy, critic evolution, clipping, recurrence, or later gradient history;

initialization effects from tape effects when they vary jointly across registered units;

robust acquisition from a finite-panel argmax-boundary crossing;

population-average superiority, necessity, sufficiency, or transfer.

Thus the packet’s “compound root/tape-specific acquisition variability” alternative is appropriate, provided it is understood as treatment heterogeneity and mediated path dependence, not as an unpaired confounder.

5. Decision value of one fixed full

One valid registered full can materially change the status of the exact complete-Adam-continuity question, because the estimand is explicitly the finite collection of paired root/tape interventions rather than a statistical estimate over an unspecified population.

A valid result could establish:

one-directional local endpoint discordance favoring carry or reset;

bidirectional treatment heterogeneity across the registered pairs;

no exact endpoint localization on those pairs;

failure of either arm to acquire the mapping under the fixed exposure.

The first three possibilities directly adjudicate whether complete Adam continuity can alter exact acquisition under this bounded learner route. Even a nonlocalizing result can exhaust this precise treatment under the registered boundary and panels, although it cannot prove optimizer-state irrelevance. The strict activation requirement, common update-1 contract, exact activity counts, final-only checkpoints, fixed roots, and prohibition on retries or branch repair make the one-full result scientifically interpretable if every gate passes.

What one full cannot materially settle is the broader candidate, generic Adam design, B4’s variation, population superiority, component attribution, or MARL transport. Those exclusions are already explicit. No implementation or result validity follows from this design ruling.
