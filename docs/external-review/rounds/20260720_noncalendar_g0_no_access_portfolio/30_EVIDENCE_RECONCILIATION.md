## Shared evidence boundary

Both reviewers agree on the following claims:

1. The run is implementation-valid. All 22 M0 checks pass, replay and
   checkpoint round-trip errors are zero, anonymous relabeling is valid, and
   the registered interaction and optimizer exposures were completed.
2. H is a non-causal exact primitive-authority ceiling. Its
   tracking/completion/utility `0.796129/1.000000/0.892259` establishes
   structural reachability under that authority, not a learned hierarchy,
   learned skill, causal deployable policy or usable temporal abstraction.
3. C removes current demand/error information while retaining the registered
   recurrent active-set controller contract. Its held-out deterministic
   `0.500000/0.432827/0.463613` establishes the intended information null; it
   does not isolate a calendar scheduler or prove that hierarchy supplies the
   missing information.
4. S restricts command renewal to one shared four-step clock, except genuine
   JOIN/REJOIN events. The positive H-minus-S lower bounds establish that this
   restriction loses material capability. They do not establish learned
   heterogeneous skill lifetimes, skill semantics or a specific replacement.
5. D retains online demand/error and ordinary recurrent primitive control. It
   learns a real improvement over C, but its held-out deterministic utility is
   `0.616348`, its final-minus-zero utility LCB is `0.101414`, and its
   D-minus-C utility LCB is `0.132124`; it therefore remains the registered
   `NO_ACCESS_BENCHMARK_ORDINARY_CONTROL` arm.
6. The result leaves unresolved whether D failed because of terminal credit,
   recurrent training/data flow, direct primitive decision structure, task
   construction, or a genuine need for explicit commitment or temporal
   abstraction.
7. Any later hierarchy or lifetime claim requires a same-information,
   same-communication ordinary recurrent comparator and natural held-out
   external capability. H/S oracle separation is not sufficient.
8. Intrinsic reward remains environment-agnostic. Neither reviewer authorizes
   task reward shaping, identity/role inputs, progress fields or external
   reward as intrinsic inputs.

## Exact interpretation of the result

The result identifies this causal boundary:

```text
exact non-causal primitive authority is sufficient
+ current demand/error information is necessary for the registered learner
+ a shared four-step renewal restriction has material cost
+ ordinary recurrence learns some causal control
-> benchmark mechanics are informative and partially learnable
```

It does not identify:

```text
ordinary recurrent benchmark access
+ learned commitment behavior
+ learned heterogeneous lifetime
+ reusable skill semantics
+ hierarchy advantage over a matched direct controller
```

The terminal classification therefore remains
`NO_ACCESS_BENCHMARK_ORDINARY_CONTROL`. A positive D-minus-C effect cannot be
renamed access after the registered access margins fail.

- Gemini's statement that C proves naive periodic/static schedules are
  insufficient is too broad. C is primarily an online demand/error information
  removal arm; S, not C, is the registered shared-renewal restriction.
- Gemini's statement that H>S proves heterogeneous *skill lifetimes* are
  materially superior is too strong. It proves a cost of the registered shared
  command-renewal restriction under non-causal primitive authority, not learned
  skill or lifetime semantics.
- Gemini's proposed threefold budget/optimizer expansion conflicts with the
  frozen no-rescue rule if presented as a repair of D. A future source may
  study optimization only through a new, explicitly different estimand that
  does not relabel or rescue G0; budget scaling alone is not automatically a
  decision-relevant mechanism.
- Gemini's proposed split between physical movement and semantic tracking is a
  candidate architecture, not a property of the current action contract. It
  requires an exact information/action definition before it can separate
  factorization from temporal abstraction.
- Open Pro correctly states that D learns partial causal access to online
  demand, but its concluding phrase "ordinary recurrent access" must be read as
  partial causal competence, not passage of the registered access condition.
- Open Pro's demand/error, membership and recurrent-state ablations and its
  duration-shift evaluation are proposals. They are not already contained in
  the result and do not authorize new evaluation or training.
- Neither review establishes that more capacity, a different return estimator,
  a factored direct head, explicit commitments or task redesign will solve the
  benchmark.

## Reconciled plural causal portfolio

### C — Terminal credit and optimization objective mismatch

D may receive too little usable sequence-level credit from a terminal-only
utility despite nonzero learning. Relevant separating evidence would have to
inspect or change one credit mechanism under a new registered estimand without
task-shaped reward or post-result budget tuning. The explanation weakens if
the existing trace already contains healthy sequence credit or if a matched
credit change leaves access unchanged.

### R — Recurrent representation or training/data-flow limit

The active-set sum/count representation, lifecycle GRU, primitive AR prefix or
recurrent PPO exposure may be insufficient even though the information is
present. A direct recurrent structural replacement could test this without
introducing skills. This explanation weakens if an information-matched direct
controller with a cleaner sequence/data path still cannot access the task.

### F — Direct primitive decision factorization is the bottleneck

The controller may need a persistent or factored command interface while still
remaining nonhierarchical. This candidate includes explicit commitment as a
direct-policy action abstraction, not learned semantic skills. It merges into R
if the change is only additional capacity, and weakens if a matched factored
direct controller fails while a temporally owned abstraction succeeds.

### T — Genuine temporal abstraction is load-bearing

Physical-time primitive recurrence may be unable to assign credit to
heterogeneous persistent decisions under membership shocks. This remains only
a hypothesis. It gains support only if a minimal explicit commitment mechanism
beats a strongest matched direct recurrent reduction on held-out lifetime and
membership shifts while both have genuine task access. It is contradicted if
the direct reduction matches it.

### B — Benchmark construction still hides a learner-access artifact

The finite horizon, terminal-only utility, discrete dynamics, fixed duration
sets or ledger generation may make H/S informative while leaving the causal
learning problem artificially opaque. This explanation weakens if a minimal
ordinary learner reaches the access floor without altering the target
noncalendar and anonymous-membership properties. It strengthens if structurally
different matched learners all fail before any temporal comparison becomes
identified.

## Candidate next evidence sources without ranking

### A — Existing-D learning and information decomposition

Use only already-recorded traces/checkpoints if they contain the required
quantities. Separate policy movement, return/advantage signal, recurrent-state
use and observation-field dependence without retraining. This can update C, R
and B but cannot prove hierarchy or repair access.

### B — One-factor strongest direct recurrent replacement

Replace exactly one identified direct-policy or recurrent data-flow bottleneck
while preserving task, information, communication, reward, membership,
exposure and evaluation. It must be defined as a new comparator rather than a
budget/model rescue of D. Branches distinguish ordinary access, continued
no-access and a non-identifying mixed result.

### C — Matched direct versus explicit commitment prototype

Compare one strongest direct recurrent controller with one minimal persistent
commitment controller under identical actor-visible information,
communication, parameter/exposure accounting and external reward. Both-fail is
an access result; direct-match rejects a temporal-necessity claim; commitment
advantage only supports abstraction under the registered held-out conditions.

### D — Evaluation-only lifetime and membership shift

If current checkpoints and the frozen environment permit a valid held-out
intervention without retraining, compare degradation across unseen durations
and membership schedules. This can test robustness of implicit recurrence but
cannot establish that the original learner had access or that a learned skill
exists.

### E — Benchmark-access reformulation

Retain anonymous join/leave/rejoin, survivor continuity and noncalendar
heterogeneous duties while removing one identified opacity in the learning
problem. Require a constructive H ceiling, the C information null, an S-style
shared-clock restriction and an ordinary access arm. This is a new benchmark
source, never a relabeling of G0.
