# External Review Transport Self-Test Retry 3

## Purpose

Validate the complete five-stage HMASD external-review workflow after restoring
the two role-specific Luna Exchanges for Pro and narrowly enabling the registered
Antigravity state required by Gemini. This is an operational test only; it does
not review or authorize an algorithm or experiment.

## Registered Transport

- Gemini: one depth-one `gpt-5.6-terra` subagent at medium reasoning, resuming
  Antigravity conversation `111dc970-bd72-4d67-8d7a-caea65394b78`.
- Open Pro: registered Luna Exchange task
  `019f716c-3c8a-7891-8c89-c94dc94fab4c` and its fixed Pro conversation.
- Convergent Pro: registered Luna Exchange task
  `019f716c-676f-7673-9782-f37b72f200d2` and its fixed Pro conversation.
- No model override, alternate session, duplicate dispatch, heartbeat,
  automation, external browser, response-control button, or permission bypass.

## Five Stages

1. Gemini blind divergent workflow review.
2. Open-Pro blind divergent workflow review.
3. Controller synthesis from both immutable raws.
4. Convergent-Pro review of the evidence and synthesis.
5. Controller test-only disposition.

## Deadline

Each external role has a hard deadline of two hours after verified dispatch.
An incomplete or post-dispatch blocked role is not resubmitted in this round.

## Success Criteria

- all external roles use their registered role-specific session;
- each external stage has exactly one dispatch;
- both Pro stages visibly verify `Pro`;
- each completed response is archived exactly before interpretation;
- the state closes once at controller disposition;
- the workflow uses no forbidden transport or response control.

## Scientific Boundary

All outputs are workflow-test evidence only. They cannot promote, retire, or
modify any HMASD algorithm, implementation, or experiment.
