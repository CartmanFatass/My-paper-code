4. FROZEN_SCIENTIFIC_DISTINCTION
Retained G31/G32 units

G31 retained: detached realized future-tail credit plus direction-balanced full-actor optimization is usable on the registered paired immediate/delayed toy family. This remains a usability result, not a universal causal-necessity theorem.

G32 retained: one capacity-8-trained checkpoint is parameter- and behavior-compatible at separately configured capacities 6, 8 and 12; within each trajectory the packing capacity is fixed, and common-active behavior is exactly invariant to inactive padding.

Smallest new claim under test

The only new claim is:

The frozen usable G31/G32 controller transports from the registered fixed membership schedule to a bounded family of episode-random membership processes without retraining.

Strongest null

The strongest null is:

G32’s high utility is largely a direct current-demand mapping aided by normalized time and a fixed event schedule; learned-parameter capacity invariance does not imply membership-process invariance.

Explicit exclusions even after success

A positive G34 result would not establish:

arbitrary membership processes or horizons;

capacities outside 6/8/12 or active counts outside the new registered support;

live tensor-width change during a trajectory;

UAV transport;

superiority over an information-matched ordinary MARL algorithm;

causal necessity of G31’s future-tail estimator;

causal necessity of recurrent hidden state;

asynchronous skill lifetime;

intrinsic-reward value;

scalable dense physical interaction beyond the registered small exact simulator.
