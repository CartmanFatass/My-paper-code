## Purpose

Every science-bearing object should make the computation it actually requires
legible: candidate inventory, hypothetical-transition structure, asymptotic
behavior, runtime/storage projection, and the strongest scientific reason for
the chosen realization. This is decision context for the same-direction EM,
Portfolio, and CM. It is not a project-wide numerical ceiling, an automatic
implementation refusal, a review escalation, a direction stop, or an exception
process.

No cross-project fixed limit is imposed on candidate count, hypothetical
transitions, `K_search`, neighbor count, width, worker count, wall time, CPU
hours, storage, or asymptotic class. Those quantities may be frozen only by an
exact science card, object-specific Portfolio decision, or Root-issued lease.

## Object-level disclosure

Before a material construction or empirical decision, the owning EM/CM pair
records, in its ordinary owner artifact when relevant:

- whether the object evaluates a finite inventory, adaptive search, recursive
  search, tree/beam/MCTS-style expansion, or a non-trajectory analytic
  calculation;
- the object-specific candidate, trajectory, state, tape, horizon, operation,
  memory, and retained-artifact counts that are material to its interpretation
  or cost;
- the expected total cost and any semantics-preserving engineering alternatives;
- why the selected realization is scientifically valuable and identifiable
  despite its complexity, including the strongest cheaper alternative; and
- any object-local claim boundary caused by approximation, bounded support,
  sparse neighborhoods, dense reference computation, or finite host scale.

CM may optimize, batch, cache, parallelize, or construct a native realization
without changing the frozen object. If the implementation is costly, recursive,
or materially unbounded, CM reports the concrete cost, implementation risks,
and alternatives to the owning EM/Portfolio; EM and Portfolio judge scientific
value, identifiability, and allocation. The fact never automatically deletes,
rewrites, pauses, or retires a direction.

## Scaling and search interpretation

Nested, recursive, horizon-growing, exhaustive, tree, beam, MCTS, dense
pairwise, or adaptive search is not automatically forbidden. It is an
object-level scientific and engineering question: the owner must state what it
adds over a simpler witness, fixed finite inventory, analytic bound, paired
intervention, sparse realization, or exact reference computation, and state
the resulting claim boundary.

A fixed-small-`N` exact `O(N^2)` simulator may be a valid reference. A
variable-`N` algorithm may use dense, sparse, hierarchical, learned-neighbor,
or other aggregation when the object states the actual complexity and what
claim that supports. No numeric neighbor cutoff is inherited from this policy.

## Resource and evidence fences

Twenty-minute nonformal and eight-hour formal figures, where present in
historical records or owner planning notes, are review and scheduling context
only. A wall-clock or resource forecast miss pauses at most the current
Root-issued resource slice. CM preserves the same blinded coordinates and
continues unchanged-science work under a later lease when needed.

This policy does not weaken object-local activity boundaries, frozen
coordinates/seeds, treatment/comparator definitions, complete-panel claim
conditions, or the exact provider no-resend safety fence. Those protect the
meaning of a named object; none has scientific-stop authority outside its
defined scope.
