# Scientific assertion review compatibility note

```text
document_kind=legacy_name_adapter
operational_workflow=false
current_authority=AGENTS.md|.agents/roles/INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_EXPLORER.md|.agents/roles/CODE_PROJECT_MANAGER.md|.agents/roles/EXTERNAL_PRO.md
legacy_names=DESIGN_ASSERTION_AUDIT|CODE_SCIENCE_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT
```

This file no longer defines a separate two-stage workflow, readiness gate,
publication gate, compute admission rule or scientific status taxonomy. The
legacy audit names may remain in historical records, but they have no routing
or stopping authority.

Current ownership is semantic:

- the same-direction EM authors the scientific question, treatment,
  comparator, observables, activity boundary, interpretation and claim ceiling;
- ChatGPT External Pro gives rigorous scientific advice and, only for an exact
  owner-frozen pure-theory or science-definition revision, returns `CLOSED` or
  `REVISION_REQUIRED` for that mathematical/causal closure boundary;
- CM owns implementation conformance, technical evidence, runtime realization,
  performance repair and same-coordinate continuation;
- the dedicated portfolio session owns cross-direction investment, pause,
  retirement and revisit decisions;
- operational Root owns shared resources, cross-session relay, user contact and
  necessary publication/Git actions.

Review is risk-driven. EM may ask Pro one exact scientific question whenever it
can change the scientific object or interpretation. CM may provide the smallest
focused implementation evidence needed to bind that question, but neither CM
nor Pro authors a different treatment through an engineering review. Local
Principles, Critic, Verifier, readiness, six-phase, receipt and publication
steps are optional evidence, not a mandatory chain.

Raw provider reasoning returns to EM immediately after canonical transport
validation. Archive, commit, push and repository publication may follow as
non-gating audit work; publication is needed only when the provider must inspect
repository-visible evidence. A mechanical `ERROR`, timeout, missing archive,
resource-slice end or incomplete panel is an observed fact, never a scientific
disposition. With no complete question-relevant data, unchanged-science repair
and blinded same-coordinate continuation remain CM work.

Scientific safety fences remain intact: do not change a treatment after its
question-relevant activity boundary; do not select seeds, thresholds,
checkpoints or endpoints after observing outcomes; and do not make a
complete-panel claim from an incomplete panel. These fences constrain evidence
meaning. They do not convert engineering incidents into direction termination.
