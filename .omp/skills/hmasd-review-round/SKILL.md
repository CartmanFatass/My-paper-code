# HMASD Open Scientific Review

Follow `docs/project/RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md`. Do not restate the research standard
inside each prompt.

Ask Pro to follow `docs/external-review/OPEN_REVIEW_PRINCIPLES.md`, and preserve
its two-domain separation when interpreting the response.

Use Open Pro only for mechanism-family retirement, disputed benchmark
identification, repeated ambiguity, convergence around one favored route, or
full-algorithm integration. Routine lemma extraction, narrow result
interpretation and next-action selection remain internal.

The prompt provides the exact live conjecture or ambiguity, its scope, strongest
simpler explanation, frozen evidence semantics and the pushed `Claude` commit.
Ask for structurally different explanations and at least one concrete
counterexample, hidden assumption, corrected definition, retained lemma or proof
of non-identification. Do not ask Pro to choose the only legal successor, exhaust
result branches, write an implementation plan or fill a schema.

A pushed Git commit is the complete evidence identity. No manifest, hash,
receipt, marker, stable-snapshot pair, archival validator or reconciliation file
is required.

The Controller performs any BrowserMCP exchange directly, reads the natural
response, updates the smallest scientific unit and chooses the next action by
information gain, cost and reversibility. Save only scientifically useful
content.
